Objective
Develop the ability to communicate projects, problems, and recommendations clearly, briefly, and with structure, especially on technical topics.
Context
Chris's communication framework for every important decision:
Qué está pasando (what's happening)
Qué recomiendo (what I recommend)
Por qué (why)
Qué riesgo existe (what risk exists)
Qué necesitamos hacer (what we need to do)

Key rule: The conclusion comes first. Don't discover your point while talking.

Weekly practice: Select a real project and record a ~2 minute explanation. Can be an automation, architecture, Edge Function, problem, agent, new tool, or business proposal.

Self-review after each recording:
¿Expliqué el punto principal primero?
¿Repetí información?
¿Me fui por tangentes?
¿Podría haber dicho lo mismo con menos palabras?
¿Se entiende qué estoy recomendando?
¿Expliqué por qué importa?

📎 Full plan: Plan de Implementación
To Keep in Mind
Target at 90 days: Explain an important decision at 3 levels without losing clarity:
30 seconds: conclusion
2 minutes: decision + reasoning
5 minutes: complete explanation
Practice happens on REAL work, not hypothetical scenarios
Store recordings/notes as evidence for the monthly scorecard
