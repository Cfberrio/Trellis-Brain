# Hormozi - The Proof Game Framework

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: AI disrupts brands based on a Proof-to-Risk Continuum in this order: Entertainer → B2C Educator → B2C Prosumer → B2B. Higher stakes for the consumer require more proof, making B2B the hardest for AI to disrupt.
- **definition**: Proof consists of two types: Third-party proof (claims like revenue, headcount, or follower counts) and Demonstration (doing the work live/real-time). Demonstration is the stronger moat as it is harder to fake.
- **process**: The 'Self-Licking Ice Cream Cone' is a business loop where serving the customer automatically generates the proof and marketing needed to attract the next customer (Sell → Serve/Capture → Market → Sell).
- **process**: To scale content volume without extra effort, 'Document while you Deliver' by capturing real work (calls, audits, builds) instead of manufacturing content from scratch.
