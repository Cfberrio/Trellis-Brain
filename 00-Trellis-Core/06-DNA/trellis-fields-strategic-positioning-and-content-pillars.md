# Trellis Fields Strategic Positioning and Content Pillars

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Trellis Fields must position itself as a translator between design and business results, focusing on how visual identity affects commercial impact rather than just aesthetics.
- **rule**: Use 'gap exposure' in content to highlight the difference between a client's current state (e.g., having a logo) and the desired state (having a strategic system).
- **rule**: Incorporate pop culture and nostalgia (e.g., Charlie’s Angels) as a shorthand for creative intelligence and a competitive differentiator.
- **process**: Implement 'comment-to-unlock' mechanics (e.g., 'Comment AUDIT for the framework') to convert authority into lead capture.
- **rule**: Present case studies as 'cases of criteria,' focusing on difficult decisions, rejections, and strategic pivots rather than just final portfolios.
