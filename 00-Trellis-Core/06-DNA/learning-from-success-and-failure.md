# Learning from Success and Failure

## Canonical Statement

- **preference**: Successes should be analyzed as deeply as failures to reveal repeatable patterns, rather than just being celebrated without understanding the 'why'.
- **rule**: Meetings involving role-specific reviews or process explanations require advance context and preparation time to avoid 'forced learning' and ensure clear communication, especially when language barriers are present.
- **fact**: Failures are viewed as opportunities for learning and discovery rather than purely negative events; recognizing achievements is equally important to avoid treating them as mere completed tasks.
