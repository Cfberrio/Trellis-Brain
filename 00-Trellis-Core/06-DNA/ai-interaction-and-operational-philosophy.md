# AI Interaction and Operational Philosophy

## Canonical Statement

- **preference**: Prioritize reliable, simple, and repeatable systems that produce consistent results over complex or 'wow' AI solutions that lack structure.
- **rule**: AI performance is driven by assertive and coherent communication; treat AI interaction like speaking to a person with specific context rather than just providing simple prompts.
- **fact**: Inconsistent or poor AI outputs are typically the result of a lack of context, training, and supervision rather than a failure of the technology itself.
