# Measurement-and-Creative-Principles

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Measurement should follow a 'progressive truth' methodology: start with aggregate benchmarks, then weight individual test results as they arrive, treating every factor as a revisable approximation rather than a permanent truth.
- **fact**: In Meta advertising, spend concentration follows a power law where a small minority of ads (3-5%) drive the vast majority of spend (70-80%).
