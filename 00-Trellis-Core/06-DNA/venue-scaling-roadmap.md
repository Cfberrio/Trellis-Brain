# Venue Scaling Roadmap

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The Venue Scaling Roadmap consists of six stages: Stage 0 (Improvise), Stage 1 (Monetize), Stage 2 (Stabilize Bookings), Stage 3 (Systemize Operations), Stage 4 (Optimize Revenue), Stage 5 (Owner Leverage), and Stage 6 (Scale/Portfolio).
- **decision**: Trellis acts as a Primary Operator during the transition from Stage 1 to Stage 2, owning inquiry capture, AI response, follow-up, and deposit collection.
- **rule**: Venues graduate from Stage 0 to Stage 1 when they define standardized event packages, establish a reliable inquiry source, and centralize calendar and communication.
- **fact**: The core business model for venue scaling involves installing an AI-driven revenue layer to move venues from inconsistent bookings to predictable cash flow, typically using a flat rate plus revenue share pricing model.
