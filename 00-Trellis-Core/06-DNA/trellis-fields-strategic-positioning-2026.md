# Trellis Fields Strategic Positioning 2026

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Trellis Fields will pivot its positioning from a 'creative agency with criteria' to an 'authority that diagnoses invisible problems' and explains branding through perception psychology.
- **rule**: Content strategy must prioritize 'constraint-proof cases' (showing results despite real-world friction) to lower sales objections before the call.
- **decision**: The 'Living Brands' concept must be transitioned from a brand vision into a repeatable, named methodology (The Living Brands Method).
- **rule**: Content hooks should utilize 'internal monologue hooks' that mirror the client's specific internal frustrations (e.g., 'My brand has something, but it doesn't feel alive').
