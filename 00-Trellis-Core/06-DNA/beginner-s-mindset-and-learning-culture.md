# Beginner's Mindset and Learning Culture

## Canonical Statement

- **preference**: Trellis prioritizes a 'learn-it-all' mindset over a 'know-it-all' mindset, valuing curiosity and the ability to question one's own certainties over the need to be right.
- **rule**: Leadership and collaboration within creative teams must be driven by curiosity and observation rather than ego or the appearance of absolute control.
- **process**: To maintain a 'beginner's mindset', team members should actively ask 'why' regarding reasoning, logic, process, operation, transition, and trajectory.
- **fact**: Defending a position solely to protect the ego limits ideas, whereas a disposition to learn multiplies them and prevents stagnation.
