# Communication and Personality Archetype Models

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: The Workplace Style Model categorizes interaction preferences into four archetypes: Director (results/speed), Socializer (energy/connection), Thinker (logic/accuracy), and Relator (trust/harmony).
- **definition**: The Conflict and Relationship Model describes behavior in disagreement through four archetypes: Gladiator (forceful), Defender (protective), Peacekeeper (de-escalating), and Sniper (indirect/guarded).
- **definition**: The Vocal/Creator Model defines how delivery shapes influence via four archetypes: Educator (clarity), Friend (relatability), Coach (accountability), and Motivator (inspiration).
- **definition**: The Leadership Voice Model explains how leaders project presence, featuring archetypes like the Commander (authority/clarity) and the Storyteller (meaning/narrative).
