# Suelos-Trellis

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: The 'Suelos Trellis' framework defines 6 stages of customer awareness (Suelo 1: Desconocido, Suelo 2: Descubrimiento, Suelo 3: Consideración, Suelo 4: Primer Contacto, Suelo 5: Recurrente, Suelo 6: Comunidad) to align content strategy with the customer's relationship level.
- **rule**: Content must be designed based on the 'Suelo' (stage) of the customer to ensure the seed (brand) grows in the correct environment.
