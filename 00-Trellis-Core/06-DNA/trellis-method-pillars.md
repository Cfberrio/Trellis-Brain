# Trellis-Method-Pillars

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: A 'Pilar' (pillar) in Trellis is a fixed, non-optional structural component of the system that performs a specific function; if it fails, the rest of the content fails.
- **definition**: Pilar 1 (Hook) is the initial promise of the content, designed to capture attention and create curiosity, and must be understandable without brand context.
- **rule**: A Trellis Hook must be expressible in one line and must include at least one of: burning question, real pain, desired result, twist/contradiction, proof/credibility, or identity/belonging.
