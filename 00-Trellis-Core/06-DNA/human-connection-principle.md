# Human Connection Principle

## Canonical Statement

- **preference**: Trellis prioritizes real human connection over mere task completion; machines and AI are viewed as vehicles to facilitate connection, not replacements for empathy and intention.
- **rule**: The effectiveness of work at Trellis is measured by the capacity to connect with people, communities, and colleagues, rather than just the technical mastery of tools.
- **decision**: Interactions with people are considered the most relevant responsibility, even when they conflict with the pace of individual tasks or technical responsibilities.
