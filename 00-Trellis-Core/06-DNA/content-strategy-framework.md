# Content-Strategy-Framework

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The process for generating original content ideas involves: 1. Defining the ICP clearly. 2. Identifying what is extremely valuable to them and what is currently poorly explained in the niche (supply/demand). 3. Validating through direct audience interaction. 4. Converting problems into content solutions. 5. Applying the 'Do more, then do better' rule. 6. Repeating successful formats for branding.
