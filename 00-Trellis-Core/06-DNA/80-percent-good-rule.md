# 80-Percent-Good-Rule

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: The '80% good' rule: Content creation should stop once a piece is 80% good to avoid diminishing returns and perfectionism, allowing for higher quantity and faster feedback loops.
- **fact**: Quantity is essential for quality improvement because it facilitates repeated feedback loops (the 'pottery class' metaphor).
