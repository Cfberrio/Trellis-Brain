# Suelo-en-Practica

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Content creation must be driven by strategy, brand moment, and the next real step, not by taste or intuition.
- **process**: The 'Suelo' (Ground) framework defines six stages of content strategy: 1. Desconocido Total, 2. Descubrimiento Inicial, 3. Consideración, 4. Primer Contacto/Compra, 5. Cliente Recurrente, 6. Comunidad/Lealtad.
- **rule**: If a creator cannot justify the choice of 'Suelo' based on the brand's strategic needs, content creation should not proceed.
