# Trellis Team Standard and AI Adoption

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: AI, automation, and new workflows are not optional; team members must use these tools with human criteria to multiply the value of their creativity and manual skills.
- **rule**: Every time a team member learns a new process, tests a tool, or improves a workflow, it must be documented to serve as a bridge for future team members and clients.
- **preference**: Trellis prioritizes a small, solid, specialized team with initiative and excellence over a large team that requires constant pushing.
- **rule**: Team members are expected to bring initiative and curiosity rather than just waiting for instructions, specifically regarding the adoption of new tools to improve efficiency.
