# Branding Workflow and DNA

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The Trellis branding workflow follows a five-stage sequence: 1. Brief & Investigation, 2. Moodboard, 3. Visual Identity, 4. Editing Guides, and 5. Brand Manual.
- **rule**: The Trellis Brand Brief must address 12 core areas: problem solved, audience, purchase motivation, emotions, value prop, mission/vision/values, audience archetype, differentiation, personality, tone, recurring messages, and visual/audiovisual style.
- **definition**: Editing Guides must define technical/visual elements, brand personality, hook structure, editing style (B-roll, SFX, music), design style, colorimetry, and sound design to ensure consistency.
