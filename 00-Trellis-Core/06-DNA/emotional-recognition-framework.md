# Emotional Recognition Framework

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Content must prioritize emotional recognition over information delivery; the audience must feel 'seen' and understood before they are receptive to solutions or teaching.
- **definition**: Communication archetypes (Educator, Friend, Authority, Challenger, Practitioner) should be used strategically to change the emotional role the creator plays in the audience's life.
- **fact**: Voice carries critical trust signals (energy, emotion, conviction, safety, authority, warmth) that the audience processes before they process the intellectual message.
- **process**: Effective communication follows a sequence: Recognition → Trust → Openness → Teaching → Conversion.
