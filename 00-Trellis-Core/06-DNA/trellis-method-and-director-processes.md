# Trellis Method and Director Processes

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: The Trellis Method uses a shared framework: Seed (brand identity), Soil (audience stage), Water (real stories), Fertilization (strategic curation), Plexus (psychological drivers), and Storytelling (narrative structure).
- **fact**: Trellis defines six 'Soils' representing audience awareness: Soil 1 (Initial existence), Soil 2 (Discovery), Soil 3 (Consideration), Soil 4 (Conversion), Soil 5 (Retention), and Soil 6 (Recommendation/Community).
- **process**: The weekly Director operating sequence consists of four steps: 1. Automated report review (findings and thesis), 2. Strategic alignment (founder insights), 3. Finalize + Feedback (video validation), and 4. Reference updates.
- **rule**: Non-negotiable operating rules: All content must respond to a clear soil; every piece must include an intentional hook and CTA; stories must be listened to before being written; automation informs but does not replace human judgment.
