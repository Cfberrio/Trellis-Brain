# Plexus-Storytelling-Framework

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The Plexus framework is a mandatory storytelling methodology in Trellis. Before writing, one must define the customer 'Suelo' (stage), select 1-2 Plexus (psychological principles), and choose 1-2 tactical techniques to ensure content is strategic rather than improvised.
- **definition**: Plexus are predictable psychological patterns (Authority, Social Proof, Liking, Commitment & Consistency, Reciprocity, Scarcity & Urgency, Curiosity) used to drive attention, trust, and action.
