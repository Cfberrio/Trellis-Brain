# Lead-DNA

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: A lead is defined as a name and email captured from an opt-in, Skool waitlist, or partner form.
- **definition**: An engaged lead is defined as someone who joins a waitlist, replies, attends a live session, clicks a join page twice, or starts checkout.
- **fact**: The weekly scoreboard tracks leads created, engaged leads and engagement rate, paid conversions, and retention (month 1).
- **process**: The core operating cadence includes daily lead response/routing, weekly live sprints/teardowns/template drops, and monthly system module updates.
