# Lead-Conversion-Strategy

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Traffic is sent to capture intention, not to sell directly; the goal is to convert traffic into a relationship before a sale.
- **rule**: A Lead Magnet must resolve a small problem, create awareness of a larger problem, and increase desire for the main offer without fully satisfying the central need.
- **process**: Lead magnets should follow one of three strategic types: Reveal a Problem (creating deprivation), Free Trial/Taste Test (creating dependency), or One Step of Many (revealing further steps).
- **rule**: CTA Structure must be clear, specify an action, and provide a reason to act immediately.
- **process**: Strategic direction requires a weekly ritual to verify lead magnet activity, packaging, CTA clarity, and post-consumption conversion measurement.
