# Trellis Method Framework

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: The Trellis Method consists of six core concepts: Semilla (Brand identity), Suelo (Customer awareness stage), Agua (Real brand stories/insights), Fertilización (Strategic curation via automation), Plexus (Psychological triggers), and Storytelling (Narrative structure).
- **rule**: The 'Six Soils' (Suelos) represent the audience's level of awareness and relationship with the brand: Suelo 1 (Initial Existence), Suelo 2 (Discovery), Suelo 3 (Consideration), Suelo 4 (Conversion), Suelo 5 (Retention), and Suelo 6 (Recommendation/Community).
- **process**: The weekly Director process follows a four-step sequence: 1. Automated report review (Fertilización), 2. Strategic alignment with brand leaders (Water Discovery), 3. Finalize + Feedback (Quality Control), and 4. Reference updates (Freshness protection).
