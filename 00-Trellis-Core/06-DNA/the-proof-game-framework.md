# The Proof Game Framework

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: To build a brand that AI cannot copy, compete on proof rather than information; AI can generate advice, but it cannot yet replicate demonstrable third-party proof or live execution.
- **fact**: AI disrupts creators on a proof-to-risk continuum in this order: Entertainer (lowest risk) → B2C Educator → B2C Prosumer → B2B (highest risk).
- **definition**: There are two kinds of proof: Third-party proof (claims like revenue, exits, or headcount) and Demonstration (doing the work live in real-time). Demonstration is the stronger moat because it is harder to fake.
- **process**: Manufacture proof at scale by: 1) Mining real work for stories via transcripts, 2) Engineering 'self-licking ice cream cones' where serving customers automatically generates proof, and 3) Documenting while delivering to capture volume for free.
