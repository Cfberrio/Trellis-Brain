# Sam-Tomlinson-Meta-Ads-Insights

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: Ad sets require a minimum volume of optimization events to stabilize; funding below this threshold makes learning mathematically impossible.
- **fact**: Fragmentation in ad accounts can be diagnosed by checking if the top three campaigns account for less than 60% of spend or if multiple campaigns produce fewer than 25 optimization events in 30 days.
- **fact**: Rising cost per unique reach (CPMr) and high 7-day frequency are leading indicators of audience saturation and rising acquisition costs.
