# Meta-Advertising-Principles-Nick-Theriot

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: Persistent low spend in a CBO campaign is a creative verdict by Meta, not a distribution bug. Diagnostic order: hook, visual, market-size, view duration, positioning.
- **rule**: Consolidate testing and scaling into a single CBO campaign to maximize learning and avoid budget fragmentation.
