# Trellis Fields Skool Community Strategy

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Trellis Fields is launching a Skool community called 'Trelling Together' for creators, editors, and brand builders to move from creative chaos to structured action.
- **rule**: The community content is organized into four pillars: AI (led by Luis), Edición (led by Char), Estrategia de contenido (led by Moche), and Estrategia de negocios (led by Luis).
- **process**: The weekly rhythm consists of Lunes (Drop de valor), Miércoles (Acción/reto), and Viernes (Feedback/revisión).
- **definition**: The ideal member is aged 20-40 with real brand responsibilities, categorized into three archetypes: The growing founder, the leveling-up creator/editor, and the creative leader/operator.
