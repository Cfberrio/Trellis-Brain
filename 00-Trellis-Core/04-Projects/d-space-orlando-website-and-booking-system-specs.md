# D'Space Orlando Website and Booking System Specs

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: D'Space Orlando will implement an automated weekday (Mon–Thu) corporate booking system for groups of 100 or fewer, while weekend events and tours remain on HoneyBook.
- **rule**: All spaces have a uniform 2-hour minimum. Pricing: Main Conference ($450/hr), Mezzanine ($250/hr), Meeting Room ($150/hr), Breakout Rooms ($75/hr).
- **process**: Stripe Connect routing: 20% management fee on base rent; 8% fee on cleaning and services. Customers absorb a 3.5% credit card processing fee.
- **rule**: Cleaning fees are automated based on guest count: $250 for under 50 guests; $350 for 50 or more.
- **rule**: All food and beverage is provided exclusively through D'Loft Kitchen; no outside caterers are permitted.
