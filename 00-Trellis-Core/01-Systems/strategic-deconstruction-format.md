# Strategic Deconstruction Format

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The 'Desarme Estratégico' (Strategic Deconstruction) format requires analyzing a content piece across five dimensions: objective (e.g., entertain, educate, sell), exact hook (visual, verbal, tension, etc.), attention-retention techniques (rhythm, zooms, sound design), visual strategy reasoning, and brand adaptation.
- **definition**: Content hooks are categorized into visual, verbal, tension, rarity, conflict, humor, and curiosity.
- **definition**: Attention-retention in editing is driven by rhythm, cuts, on-screen text, pauses, zooms, reveals, repetition, sound design, motion, POV, and plane changes.
- **rule**: Strategic deconstruction must explicitly identify elements that work in the original source but should not be copied to Trellis brands.
