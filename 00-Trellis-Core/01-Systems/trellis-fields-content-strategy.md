# Trellis Fields Content Strategy

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Trellis Fields content is organized into three main pillars: Team Culture (showing how the team works), Educational/Informative (teaching what works, tips, and community), and Storytelling (brand stories, success cases, and dramatizations).
- **decision**: Content execution will prioritize motion graphics, 'Vox' style visual storytelling, animated subtitles, and the integration of AI for specific visual elements to maintain a cinematic storytelling feel.
- **decision**: The brand will use specific 'characters' or personalities to represent different areas (e.g., editing) to build memorability and audience connection without overshadowing the content itself.
- **process**: Lead generation strategy follows a cycle: identify a large problem, provide a solution to a small subset of that problem to demonstrate value/expertise, and then invite the audience to the community (Skool) for the full solution.
