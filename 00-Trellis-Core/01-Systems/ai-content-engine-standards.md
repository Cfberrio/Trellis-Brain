# AI Content Engine Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: AI Slop (basura de IA) is generic, brandless content produced by AI when requested without sufficient context.
- **process**: An AI workflow requires three ingredients every time: Business Context (12 brand answers), Data/Training Bank (past high-performing content and examples), and a specific Prompt per channel.
- **rule**: The 'Idea is the Alpha': AI never invents the idea; the human provides the idea and criteria, while the AI handles ~90% of production work.
- **process**: The Content Engine utilizes an iteration loop where feedback on drafts improves the machine-learning output in subsequent rounds.
