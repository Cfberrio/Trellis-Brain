# Trellis e-Sign Platform

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: Trellis e-Sign is an electronic signature platform under the Trellis brand, serving as a lean alternative to DocuSign for internal use and as a free lead magnet for external users.
- **fact**: The platform is live at https://easy-sign-express.lovable.app and the MVP is fully deployed and verified.
- **fact**: The platform consists of three areas: User App (document creation and tracking), Signer Side (secure link for signing without an account), and Admin Dashboard (supervision and audit).
- **fact**: Current MVP features include Google/Admin login, individual/company onboarding, PDF field placement, Gmail SMTP integration, IP/UA capture for consent, and automated expiration.
- **decision**: Branding integration (logos, palette, typography) is pending the 'Files Extraction' task; the current style is neutral to allow for a centralized swap.
