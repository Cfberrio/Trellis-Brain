# Operational Architecture and Investment Project Phase 1

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: The investment project hierarchy is defined as: indices (e.g., S&P 500) → vehicles/products (ETFs, mutual funds) → grouping by categories (large cap, total market, mid cap, small cap, international, bonds, sectors, style).
- **decision**: Operational architecture: ClickUp Brain is used for extraction and pre-processing; Claude handles categorization and MD generation; Lobaball/Lobowe orchestrate 24/7 functions; a Windows PC automates publishing to Obsidian.
- **rule**: Heavy changes consuming credits in Lobowe/Lobaball should be grouped and executed once a week (Wednesday or Friday) unless urgent, and must be documented in a single location.
- **process**: Investment Phase 1 updates will occur every 2-3 months or up to every 6 months, rather than monthly.
- **decision**: The investment dashboard and Phase 1 results will be hosted in Trellis Hub or a shared ecosystem accessible to both Luis and Cristian.
