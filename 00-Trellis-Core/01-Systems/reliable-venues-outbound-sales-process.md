# Reliable-Venues-Outbound-Sales-Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Reliable Venues outbound sales process uses a 'micro-CTA' approach, focusing on tiny, low-friction next steps (e.g., leak map, teardown checklist) rather than selling the full ecosystem.
- **rule**: Outbound communication follows a 1-word reply rule and uses a specific angle-rotation system (Speed, Deposits, Systems, Pipeline, Calendar) to identify and address specific venue booking leaks.
- **process**: The cold call conversation path is structured into three scenarios: Gatekeeper routing, Decision maker engagement (earning 30 seconds), and handling 'already have a system' objections by focusing on what is broken.
