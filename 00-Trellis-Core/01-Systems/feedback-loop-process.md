# Feedback-Loop-Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The Feedback Loop is a mandatory process for all content creation in Trellis to ensure long-term value and prevent inertia. It consists of four stages: Action (publish/test), Result (collect data), Analyze (identify patterns/value), and Adjust (iterate in smaller batches).
- **rule**: No content creation is permitted without feedback. If the team cannot explain the value, pain, audience, and soil for a piece of content, they are improvising rather than creating with intention.
