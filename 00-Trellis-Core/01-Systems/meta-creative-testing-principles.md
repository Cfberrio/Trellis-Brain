# Meta-Creative-Testing-Principles

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: Creative diversity means concept diversity (angles, offers, proof points) first, and format diversity (static, video, carousel) second, to provide the algorithm with real signal.
- **fact**: When many ads compete under Meta's delivery system, a small minority (e.g., 2-3 of 10) typically absorb the majority of spend, while the rest receive little to none.
- **fact**: Scaling diagnostics should be judged on new-customer CPA and cold-campaign stability rather than blended ROAS, which can hide stalled acquisition by re-monetizing existing audiences.
