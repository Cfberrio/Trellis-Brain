# Colony-Cold-Call-Operating-Framework

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The Colony Cold Call Operating Framework for Reliable Venues is a diagnostic sales process focused on surfacing operational friction rather than pitching services. It follows a 10-step structure: Rep Mindset, Gatekeeper Control, Decision Maker Entry, Problem Discovery, One-Liner Positioning, Angle Branching, Handling 'Everything is Perfect', Ethical Pressure, Friend Frame, and Booking the Walkthrough.
