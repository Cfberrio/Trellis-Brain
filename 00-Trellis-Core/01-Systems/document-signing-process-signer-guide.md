# Document Signing Process - Signer Guide

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The signer process involves receiving a unique email link, providing electronic consent, signing (writing or drawing), and downloading the signed PDF once all parties complete.
- **rule**: Signers do not need an account or password; they access the document via a secure, non-guessable token link.
- **rule**: Signing links have a 14-day expiration, are single-use, and become invalid if the document is annulled.
- **fact**: The system captures legal evidence including IP address, browser/device details, and timestamps for viewing, consenting, and signing.
- **rule**: Once signed, the signer's portion is locked and cannot be re-signed or modified.
