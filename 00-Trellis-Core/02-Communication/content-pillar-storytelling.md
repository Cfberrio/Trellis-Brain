# Content Pillar - Storytelling

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: Storytelling is a content pillar that transforms real experiences, processes, brands, results, and situations into memorable narratives to generate emotional connection and demonstrate criteria.
- **process**: The base structure for storytelling content follows: Hook → Context → Tension or Problem → Development → Resolution or Learning → Remate (Closing).
- **rule**: Storytelling content can be applied through five main formats: Brand Stories (vision/intent), Success Cases (transformation/logic), Dramatizations (skits/humor), Origin Stories (how a brand relationship started), and Insight-driven observations (what was seen vs. what was done).
- **rule**: Hooks for storytelling should be categorized by type: Brand Insight, Case/Transformation, Brand Reading, Dramatization/Agency life, or Learning/Closing.
