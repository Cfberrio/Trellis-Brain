# Content Strategy and Editing Framework

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Use 'brand interrupts' (pattern interrupts like black and white effects) during important statements to sustain viewer attention and break linearity.
- **process**: Follow a standardized social media content structure: 1. Problem/Identification (Hook), 2. Educational/Scientific explanation (Nudo), 3. Solution/Value, 4. CTA (Desenlace).
- **fact**: Effective hooks can be a fusion of on-screen text (titles) and verbal introduction, creating a natural rather than forced engagement.
- **preference**: For Trellis AI content, prioritize showing actual processes (e.g., platform walkthroughs, Claude Code implementation) over simple 'talking head' (e-book) formats to teach both auditively and visually.
