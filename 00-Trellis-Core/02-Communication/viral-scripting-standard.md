# Viral Scripting Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Viral video scripts for Trellis Fields must follow an 8-step structure: Hook (0-3s), Clear Promise, Problem Context, Solution Intro, Simple Breakdown (4-6 steps), Differentiator/Insight, Result/Benefit, and a direct CTA.
- **rule**: Scripting rules: Use natural, conversational language with short phrases and a fast rhythm. Maintain a secure, direct, and slightly controversial tone. Avoid emojis, numbered lists in the final output, and sounding like a teacher.
