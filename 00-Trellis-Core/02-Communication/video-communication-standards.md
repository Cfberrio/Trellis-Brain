# Video Communication Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Every video must have a script to ensure intentionality and consistency; improvisation is considered reactive and lacks authority.
- **process**: The Trellis 'Sales Tone' requires three non-negotiable constants: sufficient volume for effortless understanding, a speed of 135–185 words per minute, and clear articulation/diction.
- **process**: Authority in video is established through controlled pauses (short for emphasis, long for processing) and downward intonation for affirmations.
- **process**: To sound natural, use the Trellis memorization method: read the script aloud perfectly articulated, then progressively redact words until the message is 'inhabited' rather than recited.
- **rule**: Authority is defined by standards and precision (e.g., 'this is the rule') rather than grandiosity or claims of being 'the best'.
