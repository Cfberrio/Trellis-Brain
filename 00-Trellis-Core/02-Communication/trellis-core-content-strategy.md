# Trellis-Core Content Strategy

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: For Trellis-Core content (e.g., Trellis AI), adopt a POV format where the camera shows the screen or tools being explained rather than just the speaker, as this feels more natural for educational/technical explanations.
- **decision**: Divide technical explanation videos into shorter parts, where each part ends with a cliffhanger to maintain retention without making the subsequent part redundant.
- **rule**: Incorporate humor and sound design (e.g., trending audio effects) into Trellis-Core video content to improve reception and retention, as observed in recent successful videos.
