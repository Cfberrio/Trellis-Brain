# Trellis Fields Content Strategy Thesis

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Trellis Fields content must prioritize showing the 'mindset shift' (before vs. after thinking) rather than just numerical results or hard diagnostics.
- **rule**: Internal tools must be packaged as simple, usable frameworks (checklists, filters, questions) to reduce viewer fatigue and increase immediate utility.
- **rule**: Operational friction (pressure, changes, chaos) should be shown as proof of the system's robustness rather than hidden to maintain a 'polished' image.
- **decision**: Strategy should be framed as 'relief' (reducing chaos and friction) rather than just high-level sophistication.
- **rule**: Content must contrast 'reactive' (impulse-driven) vs. 'designed' (system-driven) operating systems.
- **decision**: The brand voice must use 'signature phrases' (e.g., 'Publicas, pero no construyes') to name problems better than the client can.
