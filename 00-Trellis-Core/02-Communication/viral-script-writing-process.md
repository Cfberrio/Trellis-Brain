# Viral-Script-Writing-Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Viral script structure follows a 5-part format: Hook (concept/curiosity), Development (suspense/emotion/objections), Value (actionable tips/resources), Resolution (the payoff), and CTA (reciprocity-based).
- **rule**: Effective hooks must be psychologically compelling, offer a clear resolution, and have mass appeal. Avoid generic or overly specific hooks.
- **rule**: Persuasion principles for content include Authority, Social Proof, Liking, Commitment/Consistency, Reciprocity, and Scarcity/Urgency.
