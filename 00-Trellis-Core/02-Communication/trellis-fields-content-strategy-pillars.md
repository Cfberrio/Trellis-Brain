# Trellis-Fields-Content-Strategy-Pillars

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Trellis Fields will adopt a 'proof-stack' authority model, prioritizing content that stacks concrete evidence (numbers, screenshots, transformations) rather than just verbal posture.
- **decision**: Trellis Fields will use 'internal monologue mirrors' in hooks, verbalizing the specific internal frustrations or contradictions the client feels (e.g., 'My business has soul. My brand doesn’t.').
- **decision**: Trellis Fields will implement 'partial-process teaching', showing enough of a framework to demonstrate competence while leaving a key piece incomplete to capture intention and curiosity.
- **decision**: Trellis Fields will utilize 'value contrast' (Bad/Good/Excellent) to illustrate differences in brand architecture and strategic thinking, not just visual aesthetics.
- **decision**: Trellis Fields will package its 'Living Brands' philosophy as a proprietary system/methodology rather than just a conceptual vibe.
