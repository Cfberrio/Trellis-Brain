# Trellis Fields Content Strategy and Positioning Thesis

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Trellis Fields will position itself as a brand that converts strategy into saveable tools (checklists, prompts, frameworks) rather than just intelligent discourse.
- **rule**: Content strategy must prioritize attacking 'operational improvisation' (e.g., deciding what to post the same morning) as a core market pain point.
- **decision**: Trellis will use real team friction, errors, and rapid corrections as evidence of culture and criteria ('behind the pressure that proves the system').
- **rule**: Every piece of content must fulfill a specific function: authority, criteria, positioning, or distribution. At least one piece per week must be positioning-focused.
- **decision**: Trellis will adopt a 'transformation by contrast' visual/structural approach (e.g., random posting vs. system, idea vs. narrative).
