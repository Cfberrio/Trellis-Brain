# Trellis Content Creation Preferences

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Trellis content can include organic, non-pre-produced videos explaining tools and internal processes, rather than only highly produced pieces.
- **preference**: For Trellis content, use techniques like the 'It's Time' or 'Try Again' motifs to announce debuts or active status, focusing on motivational rather than conflict-based messaging.
- **rule**: Avoid excessive on-screen text and inconsistent typography that lacks alignment or uniformity; prioritize visual cleanliness to keep focus on the message.
- **process**: Improve video retention by eliminating redundancies in scripts and ensuring the timing reflects the organic nature of the digital editing process.
