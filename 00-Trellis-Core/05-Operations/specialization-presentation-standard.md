# Specialization Presentation Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Trellis editors must follow a specific structure for specialization presentations, covering why they chose the topic, research sources, main learnings, tool functionalities, and practical brand applications.
- **rule**: Each specialization presentation must include at least one practical demo or example, such as an edited reel, a functional prompt, or a tested automation skill.
- **process**: Following a specialization presentation, the editor must propose a live practice session (30–60 min) to apply the learning to a specific brand with the team.
