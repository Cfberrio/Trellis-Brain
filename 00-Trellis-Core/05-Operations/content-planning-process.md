# Content-Planning-Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Content planning in Trellis follows a mandatory 5-step process: 1. Define the 'Suelo' (strategic foundation), 2. Collect stories from the brand's people, 3. Match stories to the defined 'Suelo', 4. Execute weekly story collection tasks (Digital Director responsibility), 5. Plan content based on collected stories and defined context.
- **rule**: The 'Regla Trellis' mandates that content planning must not occur without first consulting the people who live the brand to collect real experiences. Stories must be heard before they are written.
