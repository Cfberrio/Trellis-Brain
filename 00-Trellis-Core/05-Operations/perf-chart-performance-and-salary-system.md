# Perf Chart Performance and Salary System

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: The Perf Chart is a weekly performance evaluation tool designed to provide honest, specific, and actionable feedback on key responsibilities for team members.
- **process**: Each responsibility is scored weekly on a scale of 1 to 3: 1 (needs improvement), 2 (Ninja), and 3 (Carnet de Crack).
- **rule**: Salary growth is tied to performance: every 100 points accumulated in the Perf Chart results in a 5% salary increase.
