# Founder 1-1 Meeting Process

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Trellis conducts 30-minute 1:1 'Growing Together' meetings between the founder and team members weekly on Mondays and Wednesdays.
- **process**: The 1:1 meeting agenda includes: role/responsibility catch-up, direct feedback, goal/growth discussion, future responsibilities, and alignment on support/next steps.
- **process**: Team members are expected to come prepared with achievements, frustrations, ideas, questions, and feedback for the founder.
