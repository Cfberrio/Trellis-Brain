# ClickUp Planning and Deadline Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: ClickUp task titles for content must follow the nomenclature: FECHA | TIPO DE CONTENIDO | NOMBRE DEL CONTENIDO (e.g., 05.20 | REEL | EL SUPER EJEMPLO).
- **rule**: The date in the ClickUp task title represents the publication date, not the delivery deadline.
- **rule**: The real delivery deadline is specified within the ClickUp task description/planning, typically 2-3 days before the publication date.
- **rule**: High-effort content like edited reels must be ready at least two days before the publication date to allow for a healthy review flow.
