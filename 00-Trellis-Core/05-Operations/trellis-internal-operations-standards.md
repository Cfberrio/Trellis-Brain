# Trellis Internal Operations Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Team members must implement specific time blocks in ClickUp for different brands (e.g., Cheese To Share, Discipline Rift, Trellis) to manage focus and prevent overwhelm.
- **process**: Software engineers must record step-by-step videos of their implementations for each brand to facilitate documentation and community teaching.
- **preference**: Content editors should prioritize high-quality B-roll and strategic transitions over distracting animations to tell a better brand story.
