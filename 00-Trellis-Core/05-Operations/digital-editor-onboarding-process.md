# Digital-Editor-Onboarding-Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The Digital Editor onboarding process requires adherence to a fixed weekly reporting structure (Mon/Wed/Fri) and specific ClickUp task management workflows, including a mandatory approval process where only Leadership moves tasks from 'Planificación' to 'Listo para editar'.
- **rule**: Digital Editors must submit 4-5 content ideas per brand weekly, including specific fields: Name, Idea base, Storytelling, Frase guía, Objective, Reference, and 'Suelo' (customer stage).
- **fact**: The 'Suelo' (customer stage) framework defines 6 stages of customer awareness, and content ideas must be aligned to the specific 'Suelo' of the brand.
