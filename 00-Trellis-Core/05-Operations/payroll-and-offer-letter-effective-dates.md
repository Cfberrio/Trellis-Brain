# Payroll and Offer Letter Effective Dates

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Offer letters signed during any day of the week become effective on the Monday of the following week.
- **process**: Payroll changes are reflected on the Friday of the week following the effective date, as payments operate on a one-week retroactive basis.
