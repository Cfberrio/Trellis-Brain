# Trellis Fields Content Workflow and Pillars

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The Trellis Fields Content Creation & Planning Workflow follows a weekly cycle: strategy ideation (Tuesdays), strategic curation/filtering, Content Inventory construction, planning assignment, and production.
- **definition**: Trellis Fields content pillars are: Team Culture (internal dynamics/decisions), Educational/Diagnostic (identifying brand problems), Storytelling (structured memorable stories), and Trends (adapting viral content to the Trellis voice).
- **rule**: All content tasks must follow a standard structure: Context, Content Pillar, Trellis Soil, Plexus, Value (Fruit), and Objective.
- **decision**: A 'good idea' for content must be tellable as a story, have a powerful hook, serve a strategic purpose (educate, trust, etc.), include a natural CTA, be easy to understand, and have execution potential.
