# Document Naming and Offer Letters

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: Offer letters and documents may share a common title derived from a 'mother document' (documento madre) used for their creation.
- **rule**: The shared title of a document does not affect or define the specific content of individual offer letters contained within or derived from it.
