# Trellis Fields Legal and Policy Pack

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Trellis Fields uses AI-assisted tools for analysis and drafting, but AI is not the sole decision-maker for material client decisions or final deliverables.
- **rule**: Client credentials and API keys must be stored in approved secrets-management methods and never placed in ordinary project documentation or AI prompts.
- **rule**: Standard discovery calls are free (approx. 30 mins); paid sessions require payment at booking and have a 24-hour cancellation/rescheduling policy.
- **rule**: Upon full payment, clients own final deliverables created specifically for them, while Trellis Fields retains ownership of its background IP, frameworks, and tools.
- **rule**: Meeting recording and transcription require participant notice and consent before beginning; participants may decline.
