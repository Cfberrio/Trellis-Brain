# SOP - Video Categorization Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: To categorize Trellis videos, use the columns 'Type', 'People', and 'Name'. The 'Name' column should be used for sorting (A-Z or Z-A) to verify the real order and categorization.
- **rule**: If a video appears out of order, verify three criteria: the 'Type' is correct, the 'People' field is accurate, and the 'Name' is written correctly.
- **process**: The final validation checklist for video organization includes confirming visibility of the Name column, applying the sort, and ensuring all videos are organized by Type and assigned to the correct person.
