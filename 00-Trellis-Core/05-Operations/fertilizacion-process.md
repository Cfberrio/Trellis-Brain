# Fertilizacion-Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The 'Fertilización' (Fertilization) process is a mandatory step in the Trellis method that occurs before storytelling. It uses automated reporting to curate strategic references, filter noise, and inform creative direction.
- **rule**: The Fertilization process requires a weekly workflow where Digital Editors submit ideas and references by Saturday, and the Digital Director reviews and updates the central reference sheet before the automated report runs on Sunday at 3:00 a.m.
