# Operational Delivery and Responsibility Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Every Monday, a weekly responsibility review is conducted using a three-part structure: 1. Pending tasks from previous weeks, 2. Main deliverables for the current week with dates, and 3. Tasks remaining open for corrections.
- **rule**: Corrections must include a responsible party, new date, clear priority, context of change, and urgency level (Green Flag for low, Yellow Flag for medium, Red Flag for high). Red Flag corrections take priority over new tasks.
- **process**: Before production, editors/designers must verify if information (prices, dates, copy, etc.) is final or preliminary to reduce unnecessary corrections.
- **rule**: Standard delivery times: Normal pieces (2-3 days before publication), Complex pieces (7 days anticipation with progress review at 4-5 days), Simple adjustments (1 day anticipation).
- **rule**: Early Alert System: If a task is at risk of delay, the responsible party must communicate the delay, the reason, and a revised delivery plan immediately.
