# Monthly Metrics Reporting Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Monthly metrics reports must follow a specific structure: 1. Comparison with previous month, 2. Strategic summary (max 6 lines), 3. Numerical data table (Interactions, Production, Followers, Channel Floor), 4. Additional context/events, 5. Demographics and active times, 6. Top 3 content analysis, and 7. Strategic conclusions.
- **rule**: Content analysis must classify each piece of content into exactly one dominant 'Suelo' (Floor) and one 'Plexus' (Psychological Mechanism) based on the Trellis reference list.

- **rule**: The 'Plexus' reference list consists of: 1. Authority, 2. Social Proof, 3. Liking, 4. Commitment & Consistency, 5. Reciprocity, 6. Scarcity & Urgency (real only), 7. Curiosity.
- **process**: Strategic proposals for the following month must be categorized into: Duplicate (high performance), Optimize (potential), Eliminate (no results), and two strategic Bets (experiments).
