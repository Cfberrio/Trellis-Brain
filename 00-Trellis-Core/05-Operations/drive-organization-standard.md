# Drive Organization Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Every brand must have a main Drive folder named '00_BRAND NAME' containing eight base folders: 01_brandkit, 02_raw content, 03_final content, 04_guidelines, 05_marketing, 06_funnels, 07_unknown files, and 08_TO CATEGORIZE.
- **process**: Raw content (02_raw content) is structured into 01_aroll (primary speaking/message content), 02_broll (complementary visual content), and 03_audio (voiceovers and ads audios).
- **rule**: Files must follow the naming convention: BRAND_type_content_description_YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., CTS_broll_cheesecake_table_2026-05-22). Generic names like 'untitled folder' or 'video final' are prohibited.
- **process**: Creators must upload new material to '08_TO CATEGORIZE' in a folder named with date and description, then notify the team. Editors must move published content to '03_old content' organized by year and month.
