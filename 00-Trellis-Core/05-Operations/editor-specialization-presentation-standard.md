# Editor Specialization Presentation Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Editors at Trellis must follow a 12-part structure for specialization presentations, focusing on tools, workflows, and practical applications that improve quality, speed, or efficiency.
- **rule**: Specialization presentations must include a practical project applied to Trellis brands (Discipline Rift, Cheese To Share, etc.) and a live practice session for the team.
- **definition**: The specialization presentation objective is to investigate, test, and document tools or workflows (e.g., Motion Graphics, AI, Sound Design) that solve real team problems and integrate with Claude.
