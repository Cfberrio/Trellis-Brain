**Overview**

Team members met to brainstorm how to support OCIA candidates through stronger relationship-based discipleship and improved Sunday session methodology. They reviewed attendance gaps, hospitality, grouping by life stage, a new "Traditions" segment, and concrete logistics for outreach and setup ahead of the next OCIA Sunday on the 25th.

**Key Takeaways**

*   Discipleship groups of ~10 candidates per leader, organized by season of life (e.g., college, young adult, married, older), would be formed and nurtured outside OCIA Sundays; continuity on Sundays would occur organically without mandated seating.
*   Immediate environment and hospitality enhancements would be implemented (breakfast/coffee, ambiance, flowers/lighting) with Women’s Guild support; food procurement and setup responsibilities were split.
*   A 15–20 minute "Traditions" block would be added to OCIA Sundays (e.g., Liturgy of the Hours, hymn/chant, charisms/community practices), paced pastorally (e.g., Marian devotions introduced with discernment) and aligned to weekly topics.
*   Methodology improvements for catechesis were prioritized over changing the current curriculum: shorter lectures (~30–40 min), clear objectives, visual slides, checks for understanding, richer small-group questions, journaling, prayerful closing, and selective use of media (e.g., The Chosen clips).
*   Text-message outreach for non-attending registrants was approved to improve contact rates; leaders would also receive contact info to begin group outreach.
*   Administrative clarity (e.g., convalidation needs, documentation, sponsors) would be surfaced and explained within small groups, with respectful language that communicates the “why.”
*   Christo’s continued involvement would focus on designing the "Traditions" segment and mentoring married/similar-life-stage candidates, while exploring coverage to free his Sunday availability.
