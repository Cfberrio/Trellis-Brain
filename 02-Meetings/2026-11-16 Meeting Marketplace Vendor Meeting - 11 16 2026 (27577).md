*   **Facility Odor Issue (Sewage-like Smell) Impacting Guests** Problem
    *   Multiple vendors reported a foul, sewage-like smell throughout the marketplace at closing time and in early mornings (notably near the elevator hallway and common area), with bathrooms themselves not smelling.
    *   The smell had progressively worsened; guests left events and vendors closed doors and lit candles to mask it; one vendor’s customers switched to plastic cups and departed due to the odor.
    *   Management referenced prior remediation: building and individual stalls were jetted last year; grease traps had been serviced and vented; mop/floor drains (P-traps) could dry and cause odors; they periodically fill drains.
    *   Ramzi suggested checking grease traps first, citing hotel experience; management agreed to re-inspect stalls and bring in Lampen to assess; concern noted that odors can precede drain backups.
    *   Cleaning staff had suggested the cleaning closet and Fig and Olive stall area as possible sources; management planned further investigation and would report back.

*   **Cleaner/Bussing Team Uniforms, Conduct, and Closing Procedures** Problem
    *   Vendors reported inconsistent uniforms (hoodies, hats, pajamas, clogs) making it hard to identify staff and undermining professionalism; management stated cleaners should wear black with a smock and name tag; enforcement was discussed.
    *   Complaints included loud table dragging while guests were still seated, early door locking, and chair stacking before official times, disrupting ambience.
    *   Policy restated: soft close 15 minutes after posted close (9:15 or 10:15 depending on day) with guest communication first; hard close/doors locked 30 minutes after (9:30 or 10:30). Chairs should not be stacked before soft close unless no guests remain.
    *   Management requested incident emails with timestamps to review camera footage and enforce via contract (including potential garnishments).
    *   Ramzi suggested adding inexpensive leg cushions/slides to table/chair legs to reduce noise; management acknowledged the idea.

    *   Vendors revisited the idea of extending Friday/Saturday hours by about an hour; prior attempt lacked sufficient consensus and had Yaya budget implications.
