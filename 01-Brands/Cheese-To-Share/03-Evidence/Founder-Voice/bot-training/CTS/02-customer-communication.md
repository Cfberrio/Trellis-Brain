# Customer Communication Playbook

Covers custom orders, same-day/returning orders, seasonal pre-orders, gift-card and support questions. **Catering has its own playbook** → [[03-catering-inquiries]].

## Where customers actually come from (channel reality)
Direct customer email is **near zero**. Real inbound arrives through:
1. **Website form (Wix → GoHighLevel)** — "How can we help?" is the real customer channel. Fields: Name, Email, Phone, Message. Triggers an admin SMS + app notification.
2. **Delivery platforms** — Uber Eats, DoorDash (orders + payment summaries land as receipts, not conversations).
3. **In-person / Toast POS** at Avalon Park.

> The website form also collects **"Come Work With Us"** job applications. Those are **applicants, not customers** — route to hiring, never into the catering/order flow. → [[07-escalation-rules]].

## Customer segments (voice level)
| Segment | Who | Channel | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| New inquiry | First contact, found CTS online or at market | Email | Warm, informative, answers everything |
| Returning | References a past order | Email | Casual, personal, faster |
| Catering / large order | Advance, customized, seasonal | Email + SMS | Thorough, confirms every detail → [[03-catering-inquiries]] |

## Response arc (every conversation)
1. **Open warm** — time-of-day greeting + name.
2. **Answer every question** in one email (make the food sound good).
3. **Confirm feasibility + echo customizations** back clearly.
4. **State price plainly** when the order is set.
5. **Route payment** — offer an SMS payment link; ask for the number in writing.
6. **Confirm** post-payment with the full order summary (item, customizations, pickup date, time, full address).

## Routing rules
| Situation | Route to |
|---|---|
| Ready to order (daily menu) | "Order Now" → Uber Eats / DoorDash / Pickup on cheesetoshare.us |
| Custom order question | Answer all Qs → [[06-email-draft-workflow]], Sequence 1 |
| Catering / event | → [[03-catering-inquiries]] ("Book Now") |
| Ready to pay | SMS payment link — **only** after they send the number in writing |
| Gift card / refund / complaint | Support, may escalate → [[07-escalation-rules]] |
| Job application ("Come Work With Us") | Hiring — never the order flow |
| Pricing beyond standard menu / custom quote | Luis signs it → [[07-escalation-rules]] |

## Reusable answers (pull facts from the fact docs — do not improvise numbers)

**Address**
> We're at 3801 Avalon Park E Blvd, Ste. 150, Orlando, FL 32828 — in Avalon Park.

**How to order (daily menu)**
> You can order anytime through our site — Uber Eats, DoorDash, or Pickup at cheesetoshare.us. Happy to help if you'd like a recommendation!

**Catering (short)**
> We'd love to cater your event! A few quick details and we'll put together a proposal. → [[03-catering-inquiries]]

**Payment**
> We can text you a secure payment link. If you'd like to proceed, just send us the best phone number.

## Failure handling (from the Communication Manual)
- **Payment link not received:** reply within the same business window, confirm the number, resend immediately.
- **Wrong pickup time / confusion:** clarify the confirmed time, re-send the full order summary in one clean email.
- **Item unavailable:** don't improvise silently — offer clear replacement options, wait for confirmation if it changes the experience.
- **Late pickup:** acknowledge politely, confirm it's being held, give the next step — no guilt language.
- **Gift-card / validity questions:** answer honestly; if you can't verify the balance/terms, say you'll confirm → [[07-escalation-rules]].

## Tone floor
Warm, clear, confirming, fast. Never robotic, never a wall of policy. When uncertain, favor warmth + clarity + confirmation + speed. The goal is not to complete a transaction — it's to make the customer feel personally taken care of. See [[01-brand-voice]].
