# Vendor & Operational Communication

The CTS inbox is a **vendor & ops hub** (~70% of volume), not a customer channel. This is where suppliers, delivery platforms, POS, payments, landlord, and compliance land. **Different voice entirely** from customer email: professional, documented, clear asks — no warmth performance, no emoji.

## Vendor mode (the register)
- Professional · documented · clear asks · no fluff · **no emotional escalation** · no emoji.
- Every vendor email must define **four things**: **history · issue · current status · exact ask.**
- Never send a vague escalation.

## Standard vendor request (GoTab / general)
```text
Hey [Name], hope all is well.

[Specific request]

[Specific details: dates, names, phone numbers, issue scope]

Thank you,
Luis Torres
Lead Representative
Cheese To Share
(407) 494-4263
info@cheesetoshare.us
```

## Documented escalation template (Toast POS / platform issue)
```text
Hi [Name],

Thank you for checking in. I wanted to update you on an issue we just escalated with [support team] and loop you in.

Background:
[Original agreement or setup]

What happened:
[Where the system diverged]

Current status:
[What has already been done]

What we need:
[Exact outcome required]

I'll keep you posted. Please let me know if you need anything from me in the meantime.

Thank you,
[Signature]
```

## Channel notes
- **Toast POS** — billing, POS, hardware/software, account issues. Use the documented escalation template; preserve dates, agreements, account IDs.
- **GoTab** — catering order infrastructure + scheduling.
- **Delivery platforms** — menu/banner approvals, order-routing bugs, merchant-portal offers. Keep records; these threads recur.

## Noise vs signal
Most platform mail is **automated reports** (Toast daily reports, Uber/DoorDash payment summaries) — not action items. **Real human vendor threads to never miss:** Olde Hearth Bread (reorders), Mike's Hot Honey / Sysco (sourcing), Dining Alliance (cost control), Avalon Park Group (landlord), DBPR / FL DOR (compliance deadlines).

## Closing (vendor/ops)
- "Thank you" · "I'll keep you posted." No emoji, no warmth performance.
- Full canonical signature block still applies.

## Rules
- Never send a vague escalation — history/issue/status/ask, always.
- Don't action money, refunds, contracts, or landlord terms solo → [[07-escalation-rules]].
- Compliance deadlines get flagged to Luis the same day.
