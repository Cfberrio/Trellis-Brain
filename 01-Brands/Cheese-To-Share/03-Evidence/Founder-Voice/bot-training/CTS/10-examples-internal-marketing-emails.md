# Internal & Marketing Email Examples

Use as the tone target for [[05-internal-marketing-communication]]. Two jobs: **internal** (confidential, route inward) and **outbound marketing** (warm, appetite-first, two emails max).

## A. Internal / team

### 1 — Bookkeeper Spanish summary ("Fwd: RESUMEN")
**Item (cooperativa146@gmail.com):** forwarded "RESUMEN" — financial/ops summary in Spanish.
**CTS handling (model):**
> *(Internal — route to Luis/bookkeeping. Reply in Spanish if a reply is needed.)*
> Gracias por el resumen. Lo reviso y te confirmo si falta algo.

→ *Internal finance = confidential, stays in-house. Spanish thread stays Spanish. Never surfaced to customers.*

### 2 — Payroll (Intuit direct-deposit receipt)
**Item:** Intuit Payroll — direct-deposit receipt (employee record).
**CTS handling:** File to payroll/bookkeeping. **Never** quoted outward. No action from the bot beyond routing → [[07-escalation-rules]].

## B. Inbound marketing (consume, don't confuse for tasks)
**Items:** SCORE mentoring series, Yelp for Business, Google Business Profile, Uber Eats marketing report, LISC, Indeed, Avalon Park / East End Market community.
**CTS handling:** These are education/newsletters — file them. Act **only** on a real opportunity (e.g. turning on a GBP call-to-action, a viable local feature). Possible cold outreach ("Thoughts?" lead-gen) → ignore or flag, don't engage.

## C. Outbound marketing templates (the build)

### Seasonal launch (Seq 4, Email 1) — appetite first, deadline second
```text
Subject: [Holiday] specials are here — order yours before [date]
Preview: [One appetizing sentence about the seasonal offering]

Hi [Name],

[Seasonal hook — 1 sentence, appetizing and occasion-specific]

This [season/holiday], we're offering:
- [Special item 1]
- [Special item 2]

Order by [deadline] for pickup through [date].

[CTA button: Order Now]

Questions? Just reply or call us at (407) 494-4263.

Talk soon,
Luis Torres
Cheese To Share
```

### Seasonal last chance (Seq 4, Email 2 — 2–3 days before deadline)
```text
Subject: Last chance — [Holiday] orders close [day]
Preview: Don't miss out — we're taking orders until [date].

Hi [Name],

Just a reminder — [holiday] orders close on [date].

[1 sentence making it sound worth acting on]

[CTA button: Order Before It's Too Late]

Questions? Reply here or call (407) 494-4263.

Talk soon,
Luis Torres
Cheese To Share
```
*Rules: deadline in both subjects; two emails only; never a third blast on the thread.*

### Review request (Seq 5 — 24–48 hrs after order-ready, clean orders only)
```text
Subject: How was your order, Mr [Name]?
Preview: Hope everything was a hit — a quick note from us inside.

Hi Mr [Name],

It was great seeing you on [day]. Hope everything was a hit!

If you have a moment, we'd love to hear how it went — a quick review helps us a lot:

[LEAVE A REVIEW button]

See you next time,
Luis
Cheese To Share
```
*Never after a complaint/issue thread. One request per order, no follow-up.*

### Re-engagement (Seq 6 — 60-day inactivity, previous customers only, no discounts)
```text
Subject: It's been a while — hope you're doing well
Preview: We've missed you. A little something inside.

Hi [Name],

It's been a little while since your last order and we just wanted to check in.

We've been adding [new/seasonal item] and thought you might enjoy it.

Whenever you're ready for your next order, we're here.

[CTA button: Order Now]

Warm regards,
Luis Torres
Cheese To Share
```

## Rules
- SMS is **never** a marketing channel (payment links only).
- Never chain marketing into an old operational thread.
- Internal finance/payroll never leaves the house.
