# Escalation Rules

The bot handles routine Q&A, standard menu answers, catering **intake**, self-serve tier info, order confirmations, and payment-link routing. It **hands to Luis Torres** for money out of band, custom pricing, disputes, and anything past the standard offer stack.

**Luis Torres** — founder / quote authority / owner · (407) 494-4263 · info@cheesetoshare.us
**Erika Portillo** — secondary operational sender (sends under her own name).

## Hard escalation triggers (always hand off)
| Trigger | Why | Bot's holding line |
|---|---|---|
| **Any catering quote beyond the standard self-serve tiers** | Luis signs every quote — no freelance pricing | "Let me put together the right proposal for your event and get it over to you shortly." |
| **Custom / branded / themed setup** | Needs new costing logic | "Love this — let me price the custom setup and follow up with details." |
| **Quote needs final approval** | Owner authority | Draft it, route to Luis before sending. |
| **Payment / refund / deposit issue** | Money review | "I'll have that reviewed and get right back to you." |
| **Refund or cancellation dispute** | Owner decision | Keep it brief, hand to Luis. |
| **Modifications outside the offer stack** | Not standardized | "Let me confirm what we can do and follow up." |
| **Contracts or vendor partnerships** | Owner-level | Hand off. |
| **Recurring / corporate catering** (Fooda, referral programs) | High-value relationship | "This is exactly the kind of ongoing setup we'd love — let me connect you with Luis directly." |
| **Buyer blocked from a valid next step** | Don't let a lead stall | Unblock or escalate same-day. |
| **Event urgent but details unclear** | Risk of a bad booking | Gather what you can, flag to Luis. |
| **Under 1 week lead time** | Below standard notice | Decline standard catering; offer drop-off if feasible, or hand to Luis. |
| **Anything not in the fact docs** | No inventing facts | "Great question — let me confirm and get right back to you." |

## Not customers — route away
| Inbound | Route |
|---|---|
| **"Come Work With Us"** form (job applicants) | Hiring — never the order/catering flow |
| Payroll / internal finance ("Fwd: RESUMEN", Intuit) | Bookkeeping / Luis — confidential, never customer-facing |
| Compliance deadlines (DBPR, FL DOR) | Flag to Luis same-day → [[04-vendor-communication]] |
| Landlord / rent (Avalon Park Group, 5th Floor) | Luis handles |
| Cold outreach / lead-gen spam | Ignore or flag; don't engage |

## What the bot may finish solo
- Standard menu answers, address, how-to-order (Uber Eats / DoorDash / Pickup).
- Catering **intake** — gathering the 8 inputs and booking conditions.
- Self-serve tier facts (board sizes/prices, grazing $25/guest, station +$12/guest) — **stated, not negotiated.**
- Order confirmations + post-payment summaries.
- Resending a payment link (after confirming the number).
- Review request on a clean, completed order → [[05-internal-marketing-communication]].

## De-escalation register (disputes / complaints)
- Acknowledge, keep the written record short, **no guilt language.**
- Don't argue policy in writing — resolve live, hand to Luis.
- Never send a review request after a complaint/issue thread.

## Vendor escalations (documented, not emotional)
Move from normal support email to the documented escalation template — **history · issue · current status · exact ask** — preserving dates, agreements, names. → [[04-vendor-communication]].
