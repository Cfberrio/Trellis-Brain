# Customer Email Examples

Use as the tone target for [[06-email-draft-workflow]]. Note where a reply **gathers catering details**, **echoes the order**, **routes payment to SMS**, or **hands a custom quote to Luis**.

## 1 — Catering inquiry (birthday, 40–50 ppl) — the best lead of the year
**Customer (Katie Reekie, Avalon Park):** *"I am hosting a party on 12/13 for 40-50 people and would like ideas for charcuterie trays, tequeños, and maybe flatbreads."*
**CTS (model):**
> Good afternoon Katie,
>
> Thank you so much for reaching out — a party for 40–50 sounds wonderful, and charcuterie, tequeños, and flatbreads are right in our wheelhouse. 💛
>
> To put together the best proposal, a few quick questions:
> - Confirming the date as 12/13 — and roughly what time?
> - Final guest count you'd like us to plan for?
> - A style in mind — a grazing table, individual boards, or a mix?
> - Any dietary needs we should know about?
> - Is this at your home or a venue in Avalon Park?
>
> Once we have those, we'll send options. If a photo or two of the look you love would help, feel free to include them.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Signature]

→ *Gathers the 8 intake inputs before pricing; warm; sets up a proposal. Quote itself gets Luis's approval → [[03-catering-inquiries]].*

## 2 — Seasonal pre-order (Thanksgiving cheesecake)
**Customer (Maurice Clarke):** *"We were interested in ordering one of your wonderful cheesecakes for Thanksgiving, and were wondering if that was something that we could order ahead of time? And if so, how could we?"*
**CTS (model):**
> Good morning Mr Clarke,
>
> Thank you so much — and yes, we'd be glad to prepare a cheesecake for your Thanksgiving! We do take pre-orders.
>
> Just let us know: how many you'd like, and the day/time you'd want to pick up. Once that's set, we'll confirm the total and text you a secure payment link to lock it in.
>
> Looking forward to being part of your holiday table.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Signature]

→ *Confirms pre-order is possible, gives the exact next step, routes payment to SMS.*

## 7 — Post-payment confirmation (the critical standard)
**CTS (model, after deposit/payment received):**
> Good morning Katie,
>
> Thank you! We've received your payment and your order is confirmed. 💛
>
> Order summary:
> - Grazing table for 45 + tequeños + margherita flatbreads
> - Date: Saturday, Dec 13 at 4:00 PM
> - Location: [venue address]
> - Remaining balance: $[X] due 24 hours before
>
> We'll reach out 48–72 hours before to confirm final logistics. If anything changes, just reply here.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Signature]

→ *Every confirmation names item + customizations + date + time + location + balance. No customer should follow up to learn where/when.*
