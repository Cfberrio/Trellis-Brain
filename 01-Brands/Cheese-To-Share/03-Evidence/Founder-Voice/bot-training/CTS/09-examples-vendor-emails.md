# Vendor Email Examples

Use as the tone target for [[04-vendor-communication]]. Every reply defines **history · issue · current status · exact ask**. No warmth performance, no emoji.

## 1 — Supplier reorder (Olde Hearth Bread — "Order for Wednesday?")
**Vendor (Olde Hearth Bread Co):** "Order for Wednesday?"
**CTS (model):**
> Hey [Name], hope all is well.
>
> Yes — please put us down for Wednesday. Same standing order as last week: [X loaves / items]. If you need the count adjusted, let me know by [day].
>
> Thank you,
> [Signature]

→ *Fast, concrete quantities + date. Standing reorders are real revenue threads — never miss them.*

## 2 — Ingredient sourcing (Mike's Hot Honey / Sysco follow-up) — real sent thread
**Context:** Jun 23 sent → mark@mikeshothoney.com, subject "Sysco Florida Follow Up".
**CTS (model):**
> Hi Mark,
>
> Following up on getting Mike's Hot Honey set up through our Sysco Florida account.
>
> Background: we currently use your product at Cheese To Share (Avalon Park, Orlando) and want to source it via Sysco for consistent supply.
> What we need: confirmation of the Sysco item number / distribution path for our region so we can order through our rep.
>
> Appreciate your help — happy to loop in our Sysco contact if useful.
>
> Thank you,
> [Signature]

→ *History + exact ask, documented. Sourcing threads move product cost — worth the detail.*

## 3 — Delivery platform, menu/banner rejection (Uber Eats) — real sent thread
**Context:** Jun 30 sent → merchants@uber.com, "Banner for desserts & Basque rejected".
**CTS (model):**
> Hi [Name],
>
> Our promotional banner for desserts & Basque cheesecake was rejected on the Uber Eats merchant portal and I'd like to get it resolved.
>
> Background: [what was submitted + when].
> What happened: [rejection reason shown, if any].
> Current status: banner is down; menu items are live.
> What we need: the exact requirement we missed, or a re-review so the banner can go live.
>
> Please let me know what you need from me.
>
> Thank you,
> [Signature]

→ *Documented escalation on a platform bug — states status, asks for one clear outcome.*

## 4 — POS / platform routing bug (Toast × Uber Eats) — real sent thread
**Context:** Jun 15 sent → merchants@uber.com, "Issues with Uber Eats orders on Toast account".
**CTS (model):**
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for checking in. Looping you in on an issue we escalated with Toast/Uber Eats support.
>
> Background: Uber Eats orders are set to route into our Toast POS.
> What happened: [orders not appearing / duplicating / mis-timed — specifics].
> Current status: [what we've already tried — reboot tablet, re-sync menu].
> What we need: orders routing cleanly into Toast, and confirmation the integration is mapped correctly.
>
> I'll keep you posted. Let me know what you need from our side.
>
> Thank you,
> [Signature]

→ *Cross-vendor (POS + platform) issue — the four-part structure keeps both support teams aligned.*
