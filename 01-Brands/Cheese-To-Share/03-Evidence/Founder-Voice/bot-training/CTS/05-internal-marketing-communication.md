# Internal & Marketing Communication

Two different jobs in one file: **internal/team** (bookkeeper, payroll, partners) and **outbound marketing** (seasonal, review, re-engagement). Reality check from the inbox: CTS **consumes** marketing (SCORE, Yelp, GBP, newsletters) but **sends almost none**. Outbound marketing is a build to stand up, not a backlog to clear.

**Rule:** internal financial/payroll content is confidential. Never quote it outward. Spanish internal threads are normal — keep the summary in Spanish if that's the thread's language.

## B. Outbound marketing — the sequences (Communication Manual)
Email-led. **Not** SMS-first, **not** a high-volume blast brand. Two emails per push, max.

### Seasonal / holiday (Seq 4) — launch + last chance only
- **Email 1 (launch day):** appetite/occasion hook first, deadline second.
- **Email 2 (2–3 days before deadline):** last chance.
- **Never a third blast** on the same seasonal thread.
- Deadline in **both** subject lines. Lead with appetite, not logistics.

### Review request (Seq 5) — 1 email, no follow-up
- Fire **24–48 hrs after** the order-ready notification (not same-day).
- **Only after clean, completed orders.** Never after a complaint/confusion/issue thread.
- One request per order.

### Re-engagement (Seq 6) — 2 emails
- Trigger at **60 days** of no contact from a previously active customer; Email 2 seven days later if no reply.
- Warm, **no discounts, no pressure.** Only runs on customers who previously ordered — never cold contacts.

## Subject-line rules
- Seasonal: appetite/occasion first, deadline second — "Valentine's boards are available — order before Feb 12."
- Preview text **extends** the subject, never repeats it.
- **Never chain marketing into an old operational thread.**

## Inbound marketing (consume, don't confuse for tasks)
SCORE mentoring, Yelp for Business, Google Business Profile, Uber Eats marketing reports, LISC, Indeed, community (Avalon Park, East End Market). These are **education/newsletters** — file them, act only when there's a real opportunity (e.g. a GBP CTA worth turning on). Possible cold outreach (e.g. lead-gen "Thoughts?" emails) → ignore or flag, don't engage.

## The gap to name (strategy, not sorting)
- **Customer email funnel is thin** — real customers come via the website form + platforms. If catering leads should grow, the fix is upstream (form auto-response with catering menu + quote link), not inbox sorting.
- **Marketing is inbound-only today.** Standing up Seq 4–6 in GHL/n8n is a build → owner: Luis. → [[03-catering-inquiries]].

## Never do
- ❌ Send marketing via SMS (SMS = payment links only).
- ❌ Third seasonal blast on one thread.
- ❌ Review request after any issue thread.
- ❌ Expose payroll/internal-finance content outward.
