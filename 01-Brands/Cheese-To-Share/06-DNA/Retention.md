## Two retention loops

CTS has **two distinct retention loops** that need different mechanics.

### Loop A — Everyday eaters
Repeat-purchase loop for walk-in / pickup / delivery. Mechanics:
- consistent visual and taste experience
- shareable plates that create "I want to bring someone next time" memory
- seasonal / limited menu items to create return reasons
- community presence in Avalon Park events

This loop is driven by **operational consistency**, not heavy marketing automation.

### Loop B — Event hosts
Repeat-booking loop for catering. Mechanics:
- delivered event experience exceeds quote expectation
- post-event check-in
- proactive outreach for next predictable event (birthday next year, holiday catering, corporate quarterly)
- referral / discount lever for client who books second time

This loop is driven by **memory + reminder**, not just satisfaction.

## What makes someone come back to CTS

For everyday:
- they remembered a specific plate
- they remembered the vibe
- they have someone to bring

For events:
- the event "worked" (food, presentation, timing)
- guests talked about it after
- they have another event coming up
- CTS made the next booking easy

## Retention triggers to build

These are operational moves that should generate repeat behavior:

1. **Post-event thank-you within 24 hours.** Personal message, not a generic blast.
2. **Photo / video share-back.** CTS captures media at events; sending it back gives the client UGC + emotional anchor.
3. **Next-event nudge** at 60 / 90 days. "Anything coming up we can help with?"
4. **Birthday / anniversary calendar entry per client.** Trigger preemptive outreach.
5. **VIP repeat client tag** in GHL — discount or loaded extras unlocked.

## What kills retention

- Day-of execution miss (food late, setup mismatch).
- Disappearing after the event ends.
- Treating one-time catering buyer like a stranger when they come back for round 2.
- No memory of preferences across bookings.

## Retention measurement (proposed)

- % of catering customers who book again within 12 months
- Average gap between first and second booking
- % of repeat customers whose 2nd event ticket > 1st event ticket
- Customer-source split: paid vs. organic vs. repeat vs. referral

> Repeat + referral together should target **a meaningful share** of total catering revenue. The exact target is set in [[01-Brands/Cheese-To-Share/00-Brand-Core/KPIs|CTS KPIs]].
