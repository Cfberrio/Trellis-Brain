## How CTS gets leads

CTS lead generation runs across **3 distinct entry paths**, each with its own intent shape.

### Path 1 — Walk-in / dine-in / pickup
Lead surface: physical foot traffic in Avalon Park + nearby communities. Conversion happens **in-restaurant**. No formal lead capture; immediate purchase.

**Strategic role:** local-trust feeder. Walk-in customers become catering inquirers later via familiarity + visual memory of plates.

### Path 2 — Organic social → comment / DM
Lead surface: Instagram + TikTok reels showing food, team, events, trends.

**Trigger formats:**
- Live dessert station hooks ("the dessert everyone stops for")
- Grazing table reveals
- Cheesecake cuts / texture close-ups
- The Kitchen skits (humor + recognition)
- Team / culture moments
- POV / trends

**Lead capture mechanism:** DM the brand, comment with trigger word (e.g., "SHOW"), tap link in bio.

### Path 3 — Paid Meta ads → click / DM / form
Lead surface: Reels ads with catering-focused hooks pointing to inquiry / DM.

Hero ad family (see [[01-Brands/Cheese-To-Share/02-Communication/Templates/Ads/Catering-Reels-Library|Catering Reels Library]]):
- The Dessert Everyone Stops For
- Not Just Catering
- This Is the Moment
- A Full Event Experience
- Orlando Hasn't Seen This Yet

Lead capture: link → quote inquiry. Quote authority = Luis Torres.

## What makes a lead "good"

For catering, a strong lead has:
- a real event **date**
- ≥ **30 guests**
- some sense of **format** (private / corporate / themed)
- willingness to act on the **50% deposit** rule

For everyday, the only lead test is intent to order today.

## Why leads come in

The brand reasons leads contact CTS:

1. They saw something visual that made them want it (live dessert, grazing table, cheesecake cut).
2. They have an event coming up and need a host who can handle catering + setup.
3. They want to bring people together (family, friends, partner) somewhere with personality.
4. They've eaten at CTS before and now they're hosting something.

## Lead capture rules

- Every catering inquiry gets a quote sent by Luis Torres (not by sales staff freelancing).
- Every quote includes guest count, date, package selection, and 50% deposit instruction.
- DMs / comments / form fills must be triaged within the same day where possible.

## Lead types CTS should *not* chase

- Inquiries with no date.
- Inquiries below 30-person minimum where there's no clear upsell.
- Pure price-shoppers comparing to drop-off-only competitors.
