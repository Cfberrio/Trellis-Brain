## What CTS measures

Three measurement layers, ordered by decision speed.

### Layer 1 — Daily restaurant ops
- Daily covers (walk-in / dine-in / pickup / delivery)
- Average ticket size
- Pickup vs dine-in vs delivery mix
- Most-ordered items
- Refund / complaint count

### Layer 2 — Catering pipeline
- Inquiries per week (by channel: DM / comment / link / referral)
- Quote turnaround time (inquiry → quote sent)
- Quote → deposit conversion rate
- Average booked ticket
- Setup tier mix (drop-off / standard / premium / wow)
- Station attach rate
- Lead time per booking (days between deposit and event)

### Layer 3 — Brand / content engine
- Reels reach + saves + shares (per pillar)
- Top-performing hook formats (per pillar)
- Comment / DM volume tied to specific reels
- Ad-driven inquiry volume
- Cost per qualified catering inquiry

## What "qualified" means for CTS

A **qualified catering inquiry** has:
- specific event date
- estimated guest count ≥ 30
- responsiveness to deposit instruction

Anything missing those three = unqualified or "early lead" — keep nurturing, don't count as conversion-ready.

## Hierarchy of decisions

When metrics show a problem, fix in this order:

1. **Conversion rate (quote → deposit)** — biggest revenue lever.
2. **Quote turnaround time** — closes responsiveness gap.
3. **Average booked ticket** — pushed through setup-tier + station attach.
4. **Inquiry volume** — last to scale because pushing leads without conversion fix = wasted spend.

## What CTS should *not* over-optimize

- Pure reach numbers without conversion linkage.
- Vanity follower count.
- Ad spend in isolation from inquiry quality.
- Average ticket without tracking setup-tier mix (tier mix carries the signal).

## Reporting cadence (proposed)

- **Daily:** restaurant ops snapshot.
- **Weekly:** catering pipeline review (inquiries, quotes sent, deposits in, average ticket).
- **Monthly:** content engine review per pillar (reach, top hooks, conversion-tied reels).
- **Quarterly:** revenue split between Stream 1–4 (see [[01-Brands/Cheese-To-Share/06-DNA/Money-Model|Money Model]]).

## Open metric questions

- Live-station attach rate baseline — not yet tracked.
- Repeat catering customer rate — not yet tracked, but should be (see [[01-Brands/Cheese-To-Share/06-DNA/Retention|CTS Retention]]).
- Referral source per booking — needs ClickUp / GHL field standardization.
