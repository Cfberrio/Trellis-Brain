## Revenue streams

CTS earns from 4 distinct streams that should be tracked independently.

### Stream 1 — Walk-in / menu sales
Daily restaurant operations: dine-in, pickup, in-app delivery. Margin: standard restaurant economics. **Volume driver.**

### Stream 2 — Catering base ($25/person)
Event service with 30-person minimum. **Base ticket** = $25 × headcount. This is the entry door to higher-ticket revenue.

### Stream 3 — Setup tiers
- Standard setup: **$400**
- Premium themed setup: **$600**

Setup is high-margin because the labor is amortized across the entire event and the cost is mostly aesthetic / labor, not perishable inventory.

### Stream 4 — Add-on stations & staff
- Waiter / staff: $25/hour
- Live dessert stations (tiramisu, Dubai strawberry, candy, fondue)

This is the **wow stream** — drives social proof, UGC, and word-of-mouth. Operationally premium-priced.

## Ticket ladder

| Tier | Composition | Estimated ticket (30 ppl baseline) |
|---|---|---|
| Floor | $25 × 30 (drop-off only) | $750 |
| Mid | Base + Standard Setup ($400) | $1,150 |
| Premium | Base + Premium Setup ($600) | $1,350 |
| Premium + Staff | Premium + waiter (3 hrs × $25) | $1,425 |
| Wow | Premium + Staff + Live Station | $1,800+ |

> Ticket size scales fastest by **adding live elements**, not by raising base price.

## Cash flow rule

- **50% upfront** to reserve the date.
- **50% balance 24 hours before** event.

Cash is positive **before** food cost hits. This keeps catering cash flow forward-loaded and reduces cancellation exposure.

## How CTS makes money grow

1. **Move drop-off bookings → setup bookings.** Setup is the first big margin jump.
2. **Move standard setup → premium themed setup.** $200 step with minimal extra coordination.
3. **Add staff to every confirmed booking that has > 50 guests.** Higher events justify attended service.
4. **Add a live station to anchor events.** Drives the social proof flywheel that fuels future bookings.

## What this model is *not*

- Not a high-volume catering-mill model.
- Not a discount-driven model.
- Not built to chase the cheapest catering quote.

## Risks to the money model

- Pricing on stations is incomplete (need to lock pricing for fondues, cones, candy bar). See [[01-Brands/Cheese-To-Share/01-Systems/Finance/Pricing-Logic|CTS Pricing Logic]].
- Without locked station pricing, sales can't confidently upsell on the call.
- Live-station hero ads need consistent fulfillment quality — under-execution on the moment damages future ticket size.
