# CTS Culinary and Team DNA

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Cheese To Share menu design is based on the fusion of contrasting textures (creamy vs. firm) and flavors (strong vs. weak, sweet vs. salty) to create a unique sensory experience.
- **process**: The team conducts 'pre-shift' meetings to check in on staff well-being and align on new menu items or daily objectives.
- **process**: New menu items are introduced to the team via video demonstrations followed by hands-on practice and tasting for feedback.
- **fact**: Customer loyalty is characterized by high recurrence, where regulars often bring new guests (family/friends) to experience the food.
