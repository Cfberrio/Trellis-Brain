# CTS Content Performance Benchmarks

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Cheese To Share (CTS) content output is set at 2–3 pieces per week, focusing on visual storytelling and product/service intention with less dependency on text.
- **rule**: Weekly growth benchmarks for CTS: Followers +0.5% to +1.5%, Likes +1% to +3%, Reach/Views +2% to +5%, Saves 2–4, and Shares 1–3.
- **rule**: Content quality for CTS requires 100% alignment with the brand, appearing appetizing, clear, and commercial, with at least one format identified weekly that converts best in attention/interaction.
- **rule**: Operational benchmarks for the content system include 90% on-time delivery, a maximum of one strong correction round per piece, and a goal of zero repeated critical errors.
- **rule**: During weeks with high operational load (website, menus, design), content output can be maintained at the minimum of the range while ensuring consistency.
