# CTS Website Policies v1

Five documents for `cheesetoshare.us`. Grounded facts are written as final text. Gaps are marked `[DECIDE — …]`.

**Not legal advice.** Operator-drafted from CTS's own documented rules. Have counsel review §Limitation of Liability, §Food Safety & Transfer of Risk, and §Governing Law before publishing.

**Publish at:** `/privacy` · `/terms` · `/catering-policy` · `/cookies` · plus on-form SMS copy on the contact and catering forms.

**Voice note:** CTS is warm and consumer-facing, not B2B. These pages stay plain and human — short sentences, no legalese where plain English works. `Language-Rules.md` requires that operational constraints be stated openly, not buried, so the booking conditions lead rather than hide.

### C. Not found — needs a business answer

Quote expiry / hold period (the SOP has a line item for it with no number) · exact setup duration per package (the customer email points to the website for a number that doesn't exist) · accepted payment methods beyond "payment link" · customer-facing food safety time/temperature limits · leftover handling · photo/media release for event photography · business hours · A2P 10DLC registration status · analytics/pixel inventory.

### D. Compliance flags

- **D1 — Allergens.** CTS already tells customers "trace amounts may be present." That is good. What's missing is a statement about what CTS *can't* guarantee. §2.6 adds it. Do not upgrade it into an allergen-free claim.
- **D2 — Event photography.** The SOP captures photo/video assets at client events within 24 hours and shares them back within 7 days, and CTS uses event imagery in marketing. **No media release exists.** §2.9 adds an opt-out; a signed release is better.
- **D3 — SMS consent.** The only consent mechanism documented is "customer sends their number in writing." That is not a compliant opt-in for reminder or review-request messaging. Page 5 fixes it.

**Cheese To Share Privacy Policy**
Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]

## 1.1 Introduction

`[DECIDE A1 — LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`, doing business as Cheese To Share ("Cheese To Share," "we," "us," or "our"), respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you visit cheesetoshare.us, submit a catering inquiry, book an event, or communicate with us by email, phone, or text.

## 1.2 What we collect

**You give us directly:**
- Name, email address, phone number
- Event details: date, time, venue address, guest count, event type, package interest
- Dietary needs and allergy information you share with us
- Reference photos or styling notes you send us
- Anything you write in a form, email, or text

**Automatically from the website:**
- IP address, browser and device information, referring source, and pages viewed, through cookies and analytics tools. See our Cookie Policy.

**We do not collect or store payment card numbers.** Payments are handled by our payment provider through a secure link. We receive confirmation of payment, not your card details.

## 1.3 How we use it

- To answer your inquiry, build your quote, and confirm your booking
- To plan and deliver your event: menu, setup, staffing, timing, venue access
- To handle allergies and dietary requirements safely
- To send booking confirmations, balance reminders, event-week details, and day-before setup times
- To request a review after your event
- To send promotional messages, **only where you have separately agreed**
- To improve our menu, service, and website
- For accounting, tax, and legal obligations

## 1.4 Allergy and dietary information

Allergy and dietary information you give us is used to prepare and label your order and is shared with our kitchen and event staff. Please give it to us in writing before your event so we can act on it.

## 1.5 Text messages (SMS)

If you give us a mobile number and opt in, you agree to receive SMS from Cheese To Share about your booking — payment links, balance reminders, event-week details, and day-before setup windows — and, where you separately opt in, review requests and promotions.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out, **HELP** for help, or contact info@cheesetoshare.us or (407) 494-4263. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

**We do not sell or share your mobile number or SMS consent with third parties for their own marketing.**

## 1.6 Event photography

We photograph and film our setups, including at client events, and may use those images to show our work on our website and social channels. Images focus on food, styling, and setup. **If you would prefer we not photograph at your event, or not publish images from it, tell us in writing before your event date and we will honor it.** `[DECIDE D2 — a signed release at booking is stronger than an opt-out]`

## 1.7 Who we share information with

Service providers who help us operate, under contract and only for that purpose:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Payment provider | Processing your deposit and balance |
| GoHighLevel | Booking records, email and SMS delivery |
| Supabase | Order and event data storage |
| Website host and analytics providers | Site operation and performance |
| Delivery platforms (where you order through them) | Order fulfillment |

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

**We do not sell your personal information.**

## 1.8 Retention

We keep booking, payment, and communication records as long as needed to serve you, meet tax and accounting requirements, and resolve disputes.

## 1.9 Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and opt out of marketing at any time. Florida residents may have rights under the Florida Digital Bill of Rights. Email info@cheesetoshare.us.

## 1.10 Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No system is completely secure.

## 1.11 Children

Our website and ordering services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 2.1 Acceptance

By using cheesetoshare.us, submitting an inquiry, accepting a quote, or paying a deposit, you agree to these Terms, our Catering Booking, Cancellation & Refund Policy, and our Privacy Policy.

## 2.2 What we do

Cheese To Share creates grazing tables, cheese boards, styled food stations, and catering setups for events in the Orlando area, with optional styling, florals, staffing, and live stations.

## 2.3 Inquiries, quotes, and confirmation

Submitting a form is a request, not a booking. We respond with a quote. **A quote is not a reservation — your date is held only when the 50% deposit is paid.** If two clients want the same date, the first deposit takes it.

Quotes are valid for `[DECIDE — quote expiry period]` from the date issued.

## 2.4 Booking conditions

We state these plainly rather than burying them:

- **30 guest minimum** for grazing tables and cheese service
- **1 week minimum notice** for a standard event
- **15 days minimum notice** for a themed or customized event
- **50% deposit** to hold your date
- **Balance due 14 days before your event** `[DECIDE A3]`
- **Final guest count locks 7 days before your event**
- Final quotes are issued by Cheese To Share; a planning call may be required for custom work

Orders under 30 guests, or requested inside the notice windows, may be declined or offered as a different format.

## 2.5 What we need from you on event day

- A clear surface or area for the grazing table, station, or board
- Venue access for the full setup window
- Power access if your package includes lighting or live stations
- An on-site point of contact who can let our team in and answer setup questions

If we cannot access the venue or the space is not ready within the agreed setup window, we will do our best with the time available. We cannot refund or discount for setup time lost to venue access.

## 2.6 Food, allergens, and dietary needs

Please tell us about guest allergies **in writing before your event**. We will do our best to accommodate them.

**Our food is prepared in a shared kitchen. Even when a dish is not made with a given allergen, trace amounts of nuts, dairy, gluten, and other allergens may be present. We cannot guarantee any item is free of any allergen.** Guests with severe allergies should exercise their own judgment.

We do not serve liquor. Bar service is outside our scope.

## 2.7 Outside food

Outside food may not be added to a grazing table or styled station without our prior approval. This protects the presentation you are paying for and keeps food-safety responsibility clear.

## 2.8 Food safety after setup

We prepare, transport, and set up your food following Florida Food Code and DBPR standards. **Once setup is complete and our team hands off, responsibility for the food passes to you as the host** — including how long it stays out, temperature, and handling of leftovers.

Perishable items should not remain at room temperature for extended periods. We recommend `[DECIDE — e.g. serving within 2 hours of setup and discarding perishables left out beyond 4 hours]`. We are not responsible for food consumed after the service window, taken home by guests, or held outside our recommendations.

## 2.9 Our equipment

Boards, trays, stands, florals, backdrops, lighting, and props remain the property of Cheese To Share. **Damaged, lost, or stained items are charged at repair or replacement cost.** Our team returns after the event to break down and recover equipment; please keep it accessible.

## 2.10 Photography

If you opt in, you agree to receive SMS from Cheese To Share about your booking and, where separately consented to, review requests and promotions. Message frequency varies. # PAGE 3 — CATERING BOOKING, CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY
**Publish at:** `/catering-policy` — link it from the catering page and from the quote **before** the deposit is paid

> **This page is `[DECIDE A2]`.** `Refund-Rules.md` explicitly lists cancellation, custom-package, and late-cancellation refund logic as unknown. What follows is a **recommended default** built from CTS's real cash and inventory model — deposit holds the date, balance before the event, headcount locks at 7 days because inventory is purchased. Luis must approve or change these numbers.

## 3.1 Scope

Applies to all catering, grazing table, station, and board bookings with Cheese To Share.

## 3.2 How a booking is confirmed

Your date is held when the **50% deposit** is paid. Quotes, calls, and email agreements do not hold a date. First deposit wins the date.

## 3.3 Payment

| Stage | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit | 50% of the quote | At booking — holds your date |
| Balance | Remaining 50% | 14 days before your event `[DECIDE A3]` |

Payment is made through a secure link we send you. Links expire; ask and we will resend. Applicable Florida sales tax and any service charges are shown on your quote. `[DECIDE A6]`

We do not accept payment in full after the event. The deposit is required to book.

## 3.4 Guest count changes

Your **final guest count locks 7 days before your event.**

- **Increases** after the lock date may be possible if inventory allows, at the same per-guest rate.
- **Decreases** after the lock date do not reduce the price. Ingredients and inventory for your event have already been purchased.

## 3.5 Rescheduling `[DECIDE — recommended default]`

- More than 14 days before your event: reschedule once at no charge, subject to availability. Your deposit moves to the new date.
- 7–14 days before: reschedule once, subject to availability, with a `[$___]` rescheduling fee.
- Less than 7 days before: treated as a cancellation under §3.6, because inventory has been purchased.

New dates must be within 6 months of the original.

## 3.6 Cancellation and refunds `[DECIDE A2 — recommended default]`

Send cancellations in writing to info@cheesetoshare.us. They take effect when we receive them.

| You cancel | Outcome |
|---|---|
| More than 14 days before the event | Full refund of amounts paid, less payment processing fees |
| 7–14 days before | Deposit is retained; any balance paid is refunded |
| Less than 7 days before | No refund — ingredients and inventory have been purchased |
| Day of event / no access provided | No refund |

Custom styled packages with commissioned florals, props, or branded elements may carry additional non-refundable costs. Those will be identified on your quote before you pay.

## 3.7 If we cancel

If we cannot deliver your event due to weather, supply failure, staffing emergency, or another cause on our side, you may choose a new date at no additional charge, a credit valid 12 months, or a full refund of amounts paid.

## 3.8 Board returns `[DECIDE B5]`

`Catering.md` currently publishes the line "Returning board refund may apply" with no rule behind it. Either replace that line with the terms below, or remove it from the site.

> Where a board deposit is charged on a returnable board, the deposit is refunded when the board is returned clean and undamaged within `[___]` days of your event. Damaged, stained, or unreturned boards are charged at replacement cost.

## 3.9 Chargebacks

Contact us first — we will work it out. Filing a chargeback without contacting us may result in loss of future booking privileges, to the extent permitted by law.

## 3.10 Exceptions

Any exception must be approved by us in writing.
