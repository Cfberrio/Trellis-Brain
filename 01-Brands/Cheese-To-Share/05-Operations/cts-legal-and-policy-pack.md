# CTS Legal and Policy Pack

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Catering bookings require a 50% deposit to hold the date, with the remaining balance due 14 days before the event. Availability is first-paid, first-confirmed.
- **rule**: Final guest counts and material menu reductions for catering lock seven calendar days before the event.
- **rule**: Perishable food should not remain at room temperature for more than two hours (one hour if above 90°F). Responsibility for food safety passes to the client once setup is completed unless CTS staff remain to control service.
- **rule**: CTS may photograph food and setups for marketing; clients must opt out in writing at least 48 hours before the event to prevent publication.
- **rule**: Grazing-table and staffed cheese service require a 30-guest minimum; standard bookings require 7 days' notice, while customized work requires 15 days.
