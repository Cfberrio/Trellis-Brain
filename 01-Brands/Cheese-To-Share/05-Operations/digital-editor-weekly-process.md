# Digital Editor Weekly Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The Digital Editor follows a weekly workflow prioritized by delivery date and urgency, involving the curation of three brand-aligned references, production of video/design assets, and multi-stage internal/external review.
- **rule**: Content production tools are selected by asset type: CapCut for video editing, Canva for carousels and editable stories, and Adobe Illustrator for technical flyers or professional designs.
- **rule**: All visual assets must undergo a quality control check against the brief, ensuring correct safe areas for platform interfaces, colorization to avoid saturation, and visual hierarchy before internal review.
- **process**: The approval flow for Cheese To Share content requires a primary internal filter by the Trellis team (Moche/Julián) followed by a secondary validation with the brand team via WhatsApp for specific assignments.
