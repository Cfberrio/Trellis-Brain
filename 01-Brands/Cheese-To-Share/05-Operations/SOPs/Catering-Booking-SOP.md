## Purpose

End-to-end workflow for taking a catering inquiry from first contact to confirmed event. Standardizes quote handling, deposit collection, and pre-event coordination.

## Owner
Luis Torres (quote authority)

## Trigger
Any of:
- DM inquiry on CTS social channels
- Comment with trigger word (e.g., `SHOW`)
- Form submission from link in bio / ad
- Inbound call / referral

## Inputs needed before quoting

- **Event date** (and time window)
- **Guest count** (must be ≥ 30 for catering board minimum)
- **Event type** (private / corporate / birthday / wedding / brand)
- **Setup tier preference** (drop-off / standard $400 / premium themed $600)
- **Add-ons** (live station, staff hours, fondue, etc.)
- **Reference photos** from client showing aesthetic / style goal
- **Venue / location**
- **Special requirements** (dietary, theme, branded elements)

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — Inquiry intake (within same day)
1. Acknowledge inquiry in original channel.
2. Collect the 8 inputs above (or move client onto a quick call to capture them).
3. Confirm date availability against current bookings.
4. Communicate advance notice rules:
   - **1 week minimum** for standard event
   - **15 days** for themed / customized event

### Step 2 — Discovery call (if needed)
Walk through:
- base package ($25/person, 7 cheeses + 4 meats, 30-person minimum)
- setup tier options ($400 / $600)
- staff option ($25/hour)
- live station / fondue / Dubai strawberry options
- payment schedule (50% upfront, 50% balance 24h pre-event)

Ask client for **reference photos** of desired aesthetic.

### Step 3 — Quote build (Luis Torres)
All quotes are sent by Luis Torres. Sales staff do not freelance prices.

Quote includes:
- Line 1: guest count × $25 = base
- Line 2: setup tier (if any)
- Line 3: staff hours (if any)
- Line 4: stations (if any)
- Total
- 50% deposit amount required to reserve date
- 50% balance amount due 24 hours before event
- Quote expiry / hold period

### Step 4 — Deposit collection
Deposit is the **conversion event**. Without deposit, the date is **not** held.

- Send payment link or instructions to client
- Confirm deposit receipt
- Lock date in CTS calendar / GHL pipeline
- Move lead stage to "Confirmed"
- Tag client with referral source (organic / paid / referral / repeat)

### Step 5 — Pre-event coordination (depending on timeline)
- 7–10 days before: confirm final headcount, menu, theme details
- 5 days before: lock setup details, station selection, staff hours
- 3 days before: confirm transport logistics + venue access
- 1 day before: 50% balance due + send call sheet to staff

### Step 6 — Event-day execution
Handover to event-day SOP (deferred — see [[01-Brands/Cheese-To-Share/05-Operations/SOPs/SOPs-Home|SOPs Home]]).

### Step 7 — Post-event
- Within 24 hours: thank-you message + capture asset request
- Within 7 days: photo / video share-back to client
- 30 days later: next-event nudge (per [[01-Brands/Cheese-To-Share/06-DNA/Retention|Retention]])

## Definition of done

A catering booking is "done" through this SOP when:
- [ ] Deposit collected (50%)
- [ ] Date locked in calendar
- [ ] Client has written quote in hand
- [ ] Setup tier + add-ons confirmed
- [ ] GHL / ClickUp record updated with all inputs
- [ ] Referral source tagged on record

## Common edge cases

| Scenario | Default move |
|---|---|
| Guest count under 30 | Decline catering board; offer drop-off menu only |
| Less than 1 week to event | Decline standard catering; explain advance rule |
| Less than 15 days for themed | Offer non-themed setup tier instead |
| Client asks for liquor | Decline; outside scope, partner option only |
| Client wants discount | Hold price; emphasize experience differentiator |
| Client wants to pay all at end | Decline; 50% deposit non-negotiable |
| Multiple events on same date | First deposit wins the slot |

## Escalation rules

Escalate to Luis Torres (founder / quote authority) when:
- Quote exceeds standard tiers
- Custom theme requires new costing logic
- Client requests modifications outside the offer stack
- Refund or cancellation dispute
- Anything involving contracts or vendor partnerships
