# Social Media Metrics Extraction Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: To extract Cheese To Share metrics from Meta Business Suite, log in via the Grupo Trellis linked account and select the Cheese To Share page to view Instagram and Facebook data.
- **rule**: Metric analysis must use full calendar months. Always manually adjust the date range (e.g., Feb 1 to Feb 28/29) and avoid automatic filters like 'last 7 days' or 'this month' if incomplete.
- **process**: TikTok metrics are extracted via TikTok Studio > Analytics. Key sections include Overview (views, likes, shares), Traffic Source (For You, Search, Profile), and Search Queries.
- **rule**: When reviewing audience demographics for Cheese To Share, the presence of Orlando among the top cities is considered a high-relevance indicator for analysis.
