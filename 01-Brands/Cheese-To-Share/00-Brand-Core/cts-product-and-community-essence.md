# CTS Product and Community Essence

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: The Cheese To Share cheesecake is a proprietary fusion of a Basque burnt cheesecake (torta vasca) created by Luis and Erika, characterized by extreme creaminess and unique toppings.
- **rule**: The Basque cheesecake requires a delicate 48-hour refrigeration process before it can be unmolded or sliced; it must be cut with a hot knife to maintain its creamy texture.
- **definition**: The 'Canva Cheese' is a signature artistic catering proposal where cheeses, salmon mousse, and dips are presented on a canvas/easel to resemble an oil painting.
- **fact**: Cheese To Share's operational heart is Downtown Avalon Park, leveraging community events (Farmer's Markets, Avalon Glow, Latin Night) to drive catering and social engagement.
- **preference**: Flagship menu items include the Focaccia Toscana (valued for its fusion of many ingredients) and customizable cheese boards where no two boards are identical.
