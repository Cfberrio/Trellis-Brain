# Voice and Tone — Evidencia

## Canonical Statement

- **preference**: Avoid vague descriptions like 'a plan that already feels alive'; instead, use specific experience-based language such as 'stepping into a plan to share with loved ones'.
- **rule**: When highlighting popular items, avoid 'Most recommended' as it sounds subjective. Use conversion-based triggers like 'Most ordered', 'Favorites', 'Top choice', or 'People's favorite'.
- **preference**: Marketing copy should explicitly state the 'point of sale' or specific ingredients/features (the 'backup') rather than making vague promises about a dish having 'a lot going on'.
- **preference**: The core memory of the brand experience should be anchored in 'The people'.
