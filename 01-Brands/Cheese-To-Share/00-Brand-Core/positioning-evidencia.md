# Positioning — Evidencia

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: The slogan 'We Love to Share' was not a marketing invention but an observation of customer behavior where groups naturally shared single plates with multiple spoons.
- **preference**: CTS follows a 'Simple to Special' philosophy, focusing on reinventing basic ingredients (bread, cheese, fruit) through intentional layering and presentation standards.
- **rule**: For the best tasting experience, cheese should be eaten first, followed by a sweet contrast (jam/fruit), and finished with texture (cracker/nut).
- **fact**: CTS products are designed to be 'clean' (no preservatives/additives) and include gluten-free options to ensure customers feel good after eating.
- **fact**: Catering leadership at CTS is defined by the ability to improvise and maintain the guest experience even during operational disasters, such as damaged equipment or missing decor.
