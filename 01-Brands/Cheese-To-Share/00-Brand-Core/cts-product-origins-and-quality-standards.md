# CTS Product Origins and Quality Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: The Cheese To Share Basque Cheesecake was developed internally by Erika after a previous supplier relationship ended; the key differentiator is the inclusion of goat cheese in the recipe.
- **preference**: CTS prioritizes high-quality, fresh ingredients, specifically selecting bread that undergoes a fermentation process to break down starch chains, making it less inflammatory.
- **process**: The restaurant maintains a tradition of gifting children a skewer with a strawberry and marshmallow (or just strawberry if requested) to build community rapport.
- **fact**: The brand's Basque Cheesecake has been favorably compared by customers and professionals to renowned versions like those of Alex Cordobés in Spain.
