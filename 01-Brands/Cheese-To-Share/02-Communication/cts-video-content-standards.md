# CTS Video Content Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: For CTS video content, adapt a dynamic rhythm and punctual messaging to maintain attention, particularly for showing kitchen work styles or service tips.
- **rule**: Utilize a storytelling approach with a cinematic style, B-roll, and a moderate narrative pace for brand history and emotional connection, ensuring the creator/narrator acts as a humanizing anchor.
- **rule**: Educational content for CTS should focus on recipes, using clear on-screen text and layout changes to sustain attention without visual oversaturation.
- **rule**: Cinematic cinematography, specific framing of plating and preparation, and a balance between voiceover and sound effects should be used to project a professional and high-value brand image.
