# CTS Content Strategy Framework

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: CTS content strategy follows a 'Fertilización' system: prioritizing Reels for reach and carousels for consideration, leveraging cultural calendars for legitimate promotion, and using identity-first storytelling to build community before introducing products.
- **rule**: Content structure for CTS must follow a specific flow: Hook (curiosity/pain point), Reveal/Story (cinematic/emotional), and CTA (clear, actionable, and low-friction).
