# CTS Hook Library

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: CTS social media hooks should follow specific brand signals such as 'Quick food rule:', 'CTS standard:', 'Everyone asks this...', and 'POV:' to maintain consistency across platforms.
- **fact**: The brand utilizes seven 'Plexus' psychological triggers for content: Authority, Social Proof, Liking, Commitment & Consistency, Reciprocity, Scarcity & Urgency, and Curiosity.
- **preference**: For Scarcity & Urgency hooks, always include a legitimate reason such as batch size, freshness window, or rotation to maintain brand integrity.
