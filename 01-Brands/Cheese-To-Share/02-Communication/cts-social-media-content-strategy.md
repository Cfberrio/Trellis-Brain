# CTS Social Media Content Strategy

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Social media content for CTS should prioritize an organic, enthusiastic storytelling style that feels like a conversation between people, avoiding overly formal or extended narratives.
- **proposal**: Content ideas for CTS include 'A day in the life' style videos for catering events (e.g., a Quinceañera) and identification-based hooks like 'In a world where everyone loves cheesecake, there I am'.
- **rule**: Visual strategy for social content should favor organic, selfie-style framing to create closeness and authenticity, allowing the audience to feel they could easily replicate the experience.
