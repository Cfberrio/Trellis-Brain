# CTS Narrative Scripts and Storytelling Pillars

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: The slogan 'We Love to Share' originated from observing customers naturally sharing single plates with multiple spoons, rather than being a corporate invention.
- **rule**: CTS desserts (R-Standard) must prioritize clean ingredients, balanced sweetness, and gluten-free options to ensure customers feel good after eating, rather than heavy.
- **process**: The 'perfect bite' for cheese boards follows a specific sequence: start with the cheese base, add one contrast (sweet/fruit), and finish with texture (cracker/nut).
- **fact**: New menu items are often born from real-time customer feedback and testing sessions where guests suggest modifications or additions like brownies to existing bases.
- **preference**: Brand storytelling should emphasize 'reinventing the simple until it feels special' using bread, cheese, and fruit with high standards of intention and presentation.
