## Purpose

Canonical script library for **catering-focused paid + organic reels**. All 5 reels target the catering inquiry funnel (live dessert station, grazing tables, $25/person base, custom quote CTA).

Hero hook family — use any of these as the lead ad for catering campaigns. Rotate to fight ad fatigue.

> [!info] Hero element
> The "**live dessert station prepared on-site**" is the universal differentiator across all 5 reels. Don't drop it.

## Production notes

- **Hero shot:** waiter preparing live dessert in front of guest.
- **Cutaway B-roll:** grazing table, cheeseboard, guest reactions.
- **CTA placement:** end of reel, also pinned comment.
- **DM trigger word:** `SHOW` (per Reel 04 v2 variant) — channels into catering inquiry flow.
- **Quote authority:** all inbound inquiries → Luis Torres for custom quote.

## Compliance with offer

Always anchor pricing as:
- $25/person base
- $300 / $400 / $600 setup tiers
- staff $25/hour
- 50% deposit reserves date
- 1 week minimum (15 days for themed)
