# Social Media Strategy and Benchmarks

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Social media content for CTS must prioritize being appetizing, close/relatable, visually clear, and focused on the experience rather than just a product catalog.
- **fact**: Content that is relatable and fun, such as 'food cravings' (antojos), generates higher engagement and shares for CTS compared to direct sales content.
- **rule**: Weekly social media performance benchmarks for CTS: 2–3 pieces of content, +0.5% to +1.5% follower growth, +2% to +5% reach, and 2–4 saves/shares.
- **rule**: Proven content formats to duplicate: 'food cravings' reels, 'art until you eat it' series, real customer reactions, and product-plus-occasion scenarios (e.g., 'this for a meeting').
