## What this pillar is

Content pillar that shows **the people behind Cheese To Share**.

The protagonist is not the food, not the customer — it's **the team that makes everything work**: who preps the plates, who manages orders, who solves problems, who collaborates daily inside the restaurant.

Goal: give a glimpse into **the human dynamic behind the brand**.

## How it works

Structure per piece:

```
Hook → Team situation → Interaction → Team moment → Close
```

The content captures **the team's personality and how they work together**.

## Key of the format

Strength is in showing **internal restaurant culture**:
- interactions between team members
- trends adapted to the restaurant environment
- spontaneous moments in the kitchen
- internal jokes
- small conflicts or funny situations
- collaboration to solve something
- real teamwork dynamics

Goal: viewer **sees the human side of the restaurant**.

## Each piece defined by

- **Hook** — how the team situation is introduced
- **Base situation** — what moment of work is being shown
- **Team interaction** — how people relate to each other
- **Team moment** — a gesture, reaction, or dynamic that reflects the group's culture
- **Style** — natural, humorous, spontaneous, trend-based
- **Reaction / dynamic** — gestures, jokes, collaboration
- **Close** — a phrase or moment that clarifies the team's personality

## Why this works

People don't just connect with food. They connect with:
- the people who prep it
- the team's energy
- real work dynamics
- the personalities inside the kitchen

When the public knows the team, **the brand becomes closer and more authentic**.

## Idea set

### 1) Who works here
- Hook: "The people behind Cheese To Share."
- Idea: humanize the brand by showing who's behind the restaurant.
- Style: presentation / team energy.
- Scale: person after person showing their role.
- Close: group shot.

### 2) A random moment in the kitchen
- Hook: "A normal moment in the kitchen…"
- Idea: show the spontaneity of the work environment.
- Style: natural humor.

### 3) When a big order comes in
- Hook: "The moment a huge order comes in."
- Style: dynamic / fast rhythm.
- Scale: reaction → organization → execution.
- Close: order ready.

### 4) Trends adapted to the restaurant
- Hook: trend opening.
- Idea: use trending formats to show team personality.
- Style: trend-based / light.

### 5) The one who always saves the day
- Hook: "The teammate who always saves the day."
- Style: character humor.
- Scale: problem → intervention → resolution.

### 6) Team dynamic under pressure
- Hook: "How the team works when it gets intense."
- Style: dynamic / real.
- Scale: simultaneous work → interaction → result.

## Principle of the pillar

Show the people who make Cheese To Share possible and the dynamic that exists inside the team.
