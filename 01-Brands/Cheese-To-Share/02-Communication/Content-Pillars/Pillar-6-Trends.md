## What this pillar is

Content pillar based on **adapting viral internet trends to the CTS universe**.

Goal: leverage formats, audios, dynamics, or ideas already circulating on social media and reinterpret them inside the restaurant context.

This pillar doesn't try to explain or tell a complex story. Its function is to **connect with current internet culture** and participate in conversations already happening online.

## How it works

Structure per piece:

```
Trend hook → Restaurant adaptation → Key moment → Punchline
```

Keep the essence of the original trend but reinterpret it inside the CTS universe.

## Key of the format

Strength is in **speed + cultural relevance**.

Content can include:
- viral audios
- popular TikTok / Reels formats
- challenges
- humor structures circulating online
- editing / narrative trends

Key isn't to copy the trend exactly — it's to **adapt it to the restaurant context**.

## Each piece defined by

- **Trend hook** — the viral element that makes content recognizable
- **Trend format** — the structure being replicated / adapted
- **Restaurant adaptation** — how the trend connects with kitchen, team, or customers
- **Key moment** — the scene where the trend is clearly recognized
- **Style** — light, humorous, fast, unexpected
- **Reaction / twist** — interpretation of the trend in the restaurant universe
- **Close** — visual or humorous punchline

## Why this works

The pillar lets CTS **actively participate in social media culture**. Trends already have built audience attention. Adapted correctly, they help:

- increase reach
- generate interaction
- connect with new audiences
- keep content fresh and current

## Flexibility

Can adapt to many trend types without losing brand coherence:
- humor trends
- editing trends
- viral audios
- reaction dynamics
- fast storytelling formats

And involve different universe elements:
- the team
- the kitchen
- customers
- products

## Idea set

### 1) POV trend
- Hook: "POV: you work at Cheese To Share."
- Style: observational humor.
- Scale: quick work moments.

### 2) Reaction trend
- Hook: a viral reaction audio.
- Idea: use the audio to represent an everyday restaurant situation.

### 3) Editing trend
- Hook: signature visual opening.
- Idea: apply the format to something at the restaurant.
- Scale: before → process → result.

### 4) Comparison trend
- Hook: "Expectation vs Reality."
- Idea: compare expectation vs. what really happens at the restaurant.

### 5) Viral audio trend
- Hook: popular TikTok audio.
- Idea: scene that fits the audio meaning.

### 6) Exaggerated situation trend
- Hook: "When this happens at the restaurant…"
- Idea: turn a real dynamic into viral humor.

## Principle of the pillar

Take viral internet trends and reinterpret them inside the Cheese To Share universe.
