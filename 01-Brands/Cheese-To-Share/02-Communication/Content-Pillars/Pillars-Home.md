## Purpose

The 6-pillar content system for Cheese To Share. Every piece of content should map to one of these pillars. Use this hub to plan, brief, and audit content.

## The 6 pillars at a glance

| # | Pillar name | Focus | Format core |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Table** | Product / menu | Visual product, texture, hunger trigger |
| 2 | **The Event** | Catering / experiences | Event flow, live dessert, setup montage |
| 3 | **The Kitchen** (Kitch1n) | Storytelling | Dramatized skits, day-in-restaurant |
| 4 | **The Share** | Couple / friends / family | Moments around the table |
| 5 | **The Team** | Culture / staff | Behind the scenes, dynamics, trends adapted |
| 6 | **Trends** | Viral / cultural | Adaptations of viral formats to CTS universe |

## Shared structure across pillars

Every piece follows the same skeleton, even when the tone changes:

```
Hook → Situation base → Development → Escalation → Close
```

The structure stays consistent. The **tone** and **role of the protagonist** changes per pillar.

| Pillar | Protagonist | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Table | Food | Indulgent, sensorial |
| Event | Experience | Aspirational, celebratory |
| Kitchen | Drama / humor | Dramatized, observational |
| Share | People at table | Warm, emotional |
| Team | Staff dynamics | Natural, humorous |
| Trends | Format itself | Light, trend-relevant |

## When to use which pillar

- **Lead with The Event** when driving catering inquiries (paid + organic).
- **Lead with The Table** when driving walk-in / pickup demand.
- **Use The Kitchen + The Team** to build relatability + community feel.
- **Use The Share** when reinforcing the brand promise "We love to share."
- **Use Trends** for reach + algorithmic distribution.

## What ties them all together

The CTS brand promise — **"We Love To Share"** — is the universal anchor. Every pillar should be able to reconnect back to it without forcing.
