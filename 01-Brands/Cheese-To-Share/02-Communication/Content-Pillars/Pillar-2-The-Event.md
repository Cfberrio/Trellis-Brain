## What this pillar is

A content pillar that shows **how CTS becomes part of the important moments people host** through events and catering.

The protagonist here is not just food. It is the **complete experience of the event** — planning, montage, service, guest reaction.

Goal: don't just show plates. Show how the brand participates in **real celebrations**.

## How it works

Structure per piece:

```
Hook → Event context → Preparation → Event moment → Close
```

The piece shows **the story behind the moment**, not just the catering.

## Each piece is defined by

- **Hook** — how the event / situation is introduced
- **Event type** — birthday, private gathering, family celebration, corporate, etc.
- **Preparation moment** — the visible behind-the-scenes
- **Main moment** — when food arrives or the experience peaks
- **Style** — elegant, close, celebratory, BTS
- **Reaction / ambient** — how the moment feels
- **Close** — image or line that reinforces the value of the service

## Why this works

The pillar transforms catering into an **aspirational experience**. Not just food delivered — a birthday made special, a gathering everyone shares, a celebration that becomes memorable.

Viewer doesn't see food. **They see a moment they could live.**

## Flexibility

Adapts to:
- real events
- catering for celebrations
- prep processes
- event montage
- guest reactions
- pre-event planning
- post-event moments

## Idea set (template starters)

### 1) A Full Event Experience (canonical ad family)
- Hook: "Así es como elevas un evento sin complicarte."
- Situation: CTS handles catering + setup of an event.
- Idea: educate the client on how catering works.
- Style: educational / professional / aspirational.
- Scale: prep → transport → setup → event moment.
- Close: clear CTA to reserve date.

### 2) Before the event
- Hook: "El evento todavía no empezó… pero aquí ya empezó todo."
- Style: behind the scenes.
- Scale: prep → packing → organization.
- Close: pickup leaving for the event.

### 3) The moment catering arrives
- Hook: food arriving at the event.
- Scale: arrival → presentation → reaction.
- Close: guests tasting.

### 4) What actually happens at a real event
- Hook: "This is what really happens at an event."
- Style: documentary / observational.

### 5) The dessert moment at an event
- Hook: cheesecake or dessert table arrival.
- Style: celebration / sweet moment.
- Scale: presentation → cut → reaction.

### 6) The event as seen from the kitchen
- Hook: "While they celebrate… this happens here."
- Style: BTS dynamic.
