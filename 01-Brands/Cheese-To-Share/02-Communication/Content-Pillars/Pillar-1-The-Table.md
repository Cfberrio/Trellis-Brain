## What this pillar is

A visual content pillar where **food is the protagonist**. Each piece converts a CTS product into an attractive visual that invites the viewer to imagine the experience of eating it.

This is not a menu listing. It is **sensory product content**.

## How it works

Structure per piece:

```
Hook → Presentation → Detail → Moment of desire → Close
```

The piece is short, fast, highly visual. Food is not over-explained — it is **shown so it provokes hunger**.

## Each piece is defined by

- **Hook** — how the piece opens to capture attention
- **Protagonist product** — which menu item / dish is centered
- **Product moment** — what action occurs (cut, serve, taste, plate)
- **Style** — elegant / indulgent / casual / cozy
- **Key detail** — the texture or element that makes it special
- **Close** — last image or line that leaves desire

## Why this works

The product becomes a **visual experience**, not just food. Examples:
- the moment a cheesecake is cut
- the sound of the knife entering
- cream moving
- a plate hitting the table
- someone taking the first bite

Goal: viewer **imagines the flavor before tasting it**.

## Flexibility

Adapts to:
- new product launches
- menu items
- combos
- seasonal items
- plating montages
- ingredient textures
- a customer tasting something

## Idea set (template starters)

### 1) New launch
- Hook: "Something new just landed at Cheese To Share."
- Style: clean, elegant, mystery → reveal → texture → cut.
- Close: direct invitation to try.

### 2) The first cut
- Hook: knife entering the cheesecake.
- Style: ASMR / close-ups.
- Scale: cut → cream → plated → first bite.

### 3) What can you order here?
- Hook: "POV: you walk into Cheese To Share."
- Style: dynamic visual menu.
- Scale: plate after plate.

### 4) Staff favorite
- Hook: "The team's favorite."
- Style: natural / close.
- Close: "If you worked here, you'd know why."

### 5) The perfect texture
- Hook: exaggerated close-up of texture.
- Style: slow motion / sensorial.

### 6) The order everyone makes
- Hook: "The most-ordered dish."
- Style: social proof.
- Close: "Now you understand why."

## Principle of the pillar

Take a product from the CTS menu and present it visually so that it becomes the protagonist of the piece.
