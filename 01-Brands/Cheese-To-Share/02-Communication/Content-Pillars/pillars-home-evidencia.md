# Pillars Home — Evidencia

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: Cheese To Share content is organized into four pillars: 'The Kitchen' (dramatized storytelling/skits of restaurant situations), 'The Table' (product-focused content highlighting textures and cravings), 'The Event' (catering and event experience/setup), and 'The Share' (shared-experience storytelling reflecting the brand principle 'We Love To Share').
- **rule**: A 'strong idea' for Cheese To Share content must be tellable as a story, have a powerful hook, serve a strategic purpose (educate, trust, positioning), lead to a natural CTA, be easy to understand, and use language that makes the experience feel tangible and shareable.
