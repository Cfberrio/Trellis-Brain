## What this pillar is

Content pillar that shows how CTS is lived **better when shared**.

The protagonist is not the food. It's **the people who share it**: couples, friends, and families who turn a visit into a meaningful moment.

This pillar comes directly from the central brand principle:

> **"We Love To Share."**

## How it works

Structure per piece:

```
Hook → Moment context → Interaction → Sharing moment → Close
```

The content shows not just the product but **the dynamic between people**.

## Key of the format

The strength is in showing **the human experience around food**:
- couples sharing dessert
- friends ordering multiple plates to try together
- families celebrating something special
- spontaneous table conversations
- reactions to trying something
- small gestures that show connection

Goal: viewer **sees themselves reflected** in these situations.

## Each piece defined by

- **Hook** — how the shared moment is introduced
- **Relationship type** — couple, friends, or family
- **Base situation** — what kind of encounter is happening
- **Sharing moment** — the instant food becomes collective
- **Style** — warm, close, natural, emotional
- **Reaction / interaction** — gestures, conversations, reactions that reflect connection
- **Close** — a moment or line that reinforces the sharing idea

## Why this works

People don't go to restaurants just to eat. They go for:
- a date with their partner
- a friend reunion
- a family outing
- a conversation that runs long

Food is the starting point. **The real experience is the shared moment.**

## Idea set

### 1) First date
- Hook: "POV: first date at Cheese To Share."
- Style: romantic / light / natural.
- Scale: conversation → share a plate → moment gets more comfortable.
- Close: laughter or complicity.

### 2) The friend who always wants to try everything
- Hook: "The friend who always asks 'let me try.'"
- Style: social humor / observational.
- Close: "Fine… let's order another one."

### 3) The family table
- Hook: a table full of plates.
- Style: warm / everyday.
- Close: toast or family laughter.

### 4) Nobody wanted dessert… until it arrived
- Hook: "Everyone says they're full."
- Idea: cheesecake arrives, everyone ends up tasting.
- Close: the empty plate.

### 5) The first bite moment
- Hook: "That moment when everyone wants the first piece."
- Style: observational / close.
- Close: reactions to the flavor.

### 6) A conversation that runs long
- Hook: "Came for dessert… stayed for hours."
- Style: relaxed / emotional.
- Close: final shot of an empty table.
