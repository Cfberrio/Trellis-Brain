# CTS Marketing Narratives and Content Inventory

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: The slogan 'We Love to Share' originated from observing customers naturally sharing single plates of cheesecake with multiple spoons (the 'Eight Spoons' phenomenon).
- **preference**: CTS follows a 'Simple to Special' philosophy: using simple ingredients (bread, cheese, fruit) but elevating them through intention, layering, and presentation standards.
- **rule**: The 'No-Guilt' standard for desserts prioritizes clean ingredients, balanced sweetness, and intentional texture so customers feel good after eating.
- **rule**: Guidance for the 'perfect cheese bite' at events: start with the cheese base, add one sweet contrast (jam/fruit), and finish with texture (cracker/nut).
- **decision**: New product development at CTS is 'Customer-Built,' utilizing real-time feedback and tastings to adjust flavors and textures before finalizing items.
- **fact**: The brand values leadership and adaptation in catering, exemplified by the 'Wedding Chaos' event where the team successfully improvised a setup after a highway accident.
