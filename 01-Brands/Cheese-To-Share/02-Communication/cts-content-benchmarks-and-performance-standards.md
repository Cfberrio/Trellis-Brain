# CTS Content Benchmarks and Performance Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: CTS weekly content output benchmark is 2–3 pieces, ensuring 100% alignment with brand aesthetics (appetizing, clear, commercial) with a focus on visual storytelling and clear CTAs.
- **fact**: Weekly growth benchmarks for CTS: Followers 0% to +1%, Likes +1% to +3%, Reach/Views +3% to +6%, Saves 1–3, and Shares 2–4.
- **rule**: Operational priority: CTS shares an editor with LT. During high-load weeks (website, menus, commercial campaigns), content volume can stay at the minimum (2 pieces) to maintain commercial consistency.
- **rule**: Content strategy for CTS should prioritize narrative Reels, process videos, and content linked to the Avalon Park/local community, as these formats showed better performance in April 2026.
