# CTS Content Strategy and Scripting Rules

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Content for Cheese To Share is organized into four pillars: The Table (product-focused), The Event (catering/celebrations), The Kitchen (skits/behind-the-scenes), and The Share (social/sharing experiences).
- **rule**: Scripts must be written in English, feeling natural and appetizing, with humor that is dry, familiar, and slightly absurd for 'The Kitchen' skits.
- **rule**: Avoid vague marketing phrases like 'This is not just food' or 'It became a memory'; instead, use concrete sensory details (texture, crunch, creamy) and specific social proof (people's favorite, top choice).
- **process**: The content creation workflow follows a 4-step process: Process the Q&A for insights, Classify by Pillar, Define the Format (Reel, Carousel, Skit), and Develop the Story structure.
