## 1. Why is Avalon Park a good place for the Cheese To Share concept?

Luis Eduardo says Avalon Park is a calm, safe, relaxed, and very pleasant community. He highlights its green areas, the lake, the friendliness of people, and the fact that it's close to the airport. He also mentions it's well connected to other nearby communities and that this gives a lot of value to the location.

## 2. What detail of Avalon best reflects the concept of sharing?

He says Avalon is characterized by hosting many events and generating strong synergy with the community. He talks about farmer markets, street events, celebrations like Christmas, 4th of July, Latin Night, and other activities that bring people together — including people coming in from other zones.

## 3. Do you participate actively in those events?

Yes. Luis Eduardo confirms they participate actively and that this community dynamic is exactly part of what makes Avalon different.

## 4. What is your favorite dish in all of Cheese To Share? Which one do you enjoy most and could eat over and over?

He mentions two main ones:
- The **Focaccia Toscana**, because he enjoys making it for the amount of ingredients and fusions it carries.
- The **cheese boards**, because there creativity flies and they never come out exactly the same.

He also mentions he enjoys plating the cheesecakes.

## 5. Which dish would you recommend to someone visiting for the first time?

The **cheesecake**. He explains it's not a normal cheesecake — it's a fused Basque cake, created by them with Erika, with different cheese combinations and toppings following that same logic. For him, that difference is what makes the product special.

## 6. What kind of experience would you like someone to remember after visiting Cheese To Share?

He says he'd like people to remember **that peculiar fusion of flavor** — something that stays in the palate and in memory. He also wants them to remember having a good time at the table, with wine or a drink, and that the experience becomes associated with **sharing something unforgettable**.

## 7. Where is the real heart of your location?

He places it in **Downtown Avalon**, because that's where commerce, community, parks, restaurants, and markets converge. For him, that central point makes Cheese To Share part of a **real flow of community life**.
