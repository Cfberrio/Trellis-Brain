# Social Media Management and Automation

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Social media management for CTS involves daily/weekly content publication and monitoring, monthly calendar organization, and metric analysis via Canva templates to identify high-performing content.
- **rule**: Content scheduling is centralized in GoHighLevel, utilizing recurring templates for repetitive content like promotions and reminders to optimize workflow.
- **preference**: CTS social media CTAs are categorized by intent: Direct Action (Order now), Engagement (Which flavor wins?), Sharing (Tag your dessert partner), and Conversion via DM (Message us for menu).
- **rule**: Specific keywords trigger automated responses in GoHighLevel: 'MENU' sends the catering menu PDF, 'CATERING' sends packages, and 'PRICES' sends the price list.
