## Purpose
The three content pillars that every TF piece must map to. Each pillar is structural — not optional. If a piece doesn't fit a pillar, it's not ready for production.

## Pillar 1 — Cultura del Equipo
**What it is:** How the team works, behind-the-scenes, processes cotidianos.

**Sub-themes:**
- Nuestros valores como equipo
- Procesos cotidianos (planning, editing, review sessions)
- Personalizaciones / personajes de nuestra área
- Historias del equipo: creación, integración de cada miembro, historias de marcas que manejamos
- Behind the scenes de decisiones reales

**Series concept:** Personajes (Duolingo-style) — each team member brings their own personality to brand content while staying within the unified vibe. Reference: 4curious YouTube channel (Cristian's prior work — different characters per content type).

## Pillar 2 — Educativo / Informativo
**What it is:** Teach what works, what doesn't, why brands struggle. Position TF as experts who understand the problem before selling the solution.

**Sub-themes:**
- Conceptos que conocemos / técnicas que usamos / uso de IA / automatizaciones
- Tips
- Comunidad Trellis
- Diagnóstico de contenido para marcas
- Framework "Problema → Diagnóstico → Solución → Ejecución"

**Standard video structure:** problem → diagnosis → solution → execution.

## Pillar 3 — Storytelling
**What it is:** Stories with brands, case studies, dramatizations.

**Sub-themes:**
- Historias con marcas
- Casos de éxito
- Dramatizaciones
- Cosas que nos hayan pasado

**Rule:** Every pillar should contain storytelling, but Pillar 3 is *only* storytelling — narrative, characters, arc, payoff.

## Execution Style (TF Visual + Narrative Identity)

- **Motion graphics / Vox style** for educational pieces
- **AI integration** for specific visual elements (not as crutch)
- **Storytelling cinematográfico** — narrative arc, not just info dump
- **A-roll** with strong intent (not filler talking head)
- **Subtítulos animados** as part of the design language
- **Lenguaje cercano** — direct, not corporate, not "agency-speak"
- **Crear personalidad** — distinct character voices per team area
- **Final con CTA simple** ("DM me" / "Escríbenos")

## Series Concepts (recurring content frameworks)

### Series 1 — Problemas de Contenido
Identifies a specific brand content problem, diagnoses why it happens, shows how Trellis solves it.

### Series 2 — El Error
- El error de contenido que veo en marcas
- El error que mata el engagement
- El error que hace que tu contenido parezca publicidad

### Series 3 — No Es El Algoritmo
- No es el algoritmo, es tu contenido
- No es el algoritmo, es tu storytelling
- No es el algoritmo, es tu estrategia

## What TF Avoids In Content
- Generic agency self-promotion ("we're Trellis, we do marketing")
- "Bla bla bla" content that doesn't show actual work or method
- Mismatch between agency content style and the work shown to clients (kills credibility)

## Decision Rule
Before producing any piece:
1. Which pillar?
2. Which soil? (see [[../06-DNA/Soils-Framework|Soils]])
3. Which idea from the bank?
4. Which hook? (see [[../02-Communication/Hook-Library-TF|Hook Library]])
5. Which value will the viewer leave with?

If any answer is unclear → not ready for production.
