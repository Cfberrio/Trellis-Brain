## Identity
Trellis Fields is the **internal agency hub and authority brand** for Trellis — a strategy, content, reporting, automation, and conversion operations company that turns messy business context into usable execution across multiple brands.

TF is founder-led, systems-driven, AI-native — without sounding AI-hype.

## One-line model
**For brands:** Trellis Fields turns messy business context into clean execution — content, conversion, reporting, automation — without making it depend on the founder.

**For the community (Skool, future):** Trellis Fields teaches operators the same systems we use internally, with frameworks they can implement (not just theory).

**For the business:** Authority brand + agency revenue + community/membership revenue, all reinforcing each other.

## What TF Is Really Selling
Not content. Not "marketing". **Systems that hold weight under real volume + multiple brands**, with a storytelling spine that survives without the founder grinding every piece.

## Strategic Center Of Gravity
TF wins when these four hold simultaneously:
1. The team can ship multiple brands of content without the founder being the bottleneck
2. Every piece of content maps to a pillar + a soil + a buildup + a value + a CTA (no improvisation)
3. The lead-magnet engine captures intent rather than asks for purchase
4. The community proves the system works by replicating it in members' own businesses

## Canon Frameworks (locked)
- **Soils** (1–6) — see [[../06-DNA/Soils-Framework|Soils Framework]]. Every piece of content lives in exactly one soil.
- **3 Storytelling Pillars** — Hook + Build Up + Value. CTA is its own discipline. See [[../06-DNA/Storytelling-Pillars|Storytelling Pillars]].
- **Lead Magnet Theory (Hormozi-aligned)** — small problem solved + bigger problem revealed + desire for main offer increased. Never satisfy fully.
- **Framework "Problema → Diagnóstico → Solución → Ejecución"** — TF's signature framework for any content or service piece.

## What TF Does Not Do
- Inspiration-driven content (depends on mood)
- Templates without strategy
- "Just post more"
- Generic agency vocabulary
- Selling tools where the answer is a system

## Operational Implications
- The **brief in 5 minutes** is the contract between idea and production. No brief = no production.
- Revision rule: **2 rounds max** (Round 1 = structure / Round 2 = polish).
- Every piece has a **Definition of Done** before it enters production.
- Feedback is structured, not emotional.

## Open Decisions
- Whether TF community (Skool) becomes a primary monetization driver or stays as authority engine
- Whether TF eventually has its own commercial offer separate from agency work
- Whether the "characters" content concept (Duolingo-style multi-character brand identity) becomes part of TF brand or stays internal experiment
