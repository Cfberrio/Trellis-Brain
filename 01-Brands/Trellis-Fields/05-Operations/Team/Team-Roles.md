## Purpose
Canonical role definition for each Trellis team member. Used for: weekly recap evaluation, hiring/expanding the team, onboarding new contributors, and resolving ambiguity about who owns what.

> Roles are not job titles. They are **areas of responsibility**. Each numbered category is non-negotiable for that role.

---

## Strategist Director — María José González Hernández

1. **Strategic Growth & Conversion Leadership**
   - Architect and execute strategies that bridge the gap between audience interest and customer conversion, focusing on lead magnets, mini-offers, and nurturing sequences
   - Design and optimize the full customer journey — ensure every campaign, CTA, and piece of content is engineered to capture intent, qualify leads, and drive measurable growth in conversions and lifetime value

2. **Content Architecture, Planning & Brand Storytelling**
   - Lead the creation of content pillars and frameworks that ensure every piece is intentional, on-brand, and mapped to a strategic objective (authority, social proof, curiosity, identity)
   - Oversee the weekly and monthly content planning process — balance of storytelling, education, and conversion-focused assets per brand
   - Guide the team in adapting content to platforms, audiences, and campaign goals — focus on hooks, narrative structure, and emotional resonance

3. **Team Alignment, Coaching & Creative Feedback**
   - Set clear expectations, OKRs, and standards for the creative team
   - Provide high-level, actionable feedback that elevates both creative output and strategic thinking of editors, designers, and collaborators
   - Mentor team members on translating references and feedback into strategic, brand-aligned execution

4. **Process Optimization, Automation & Analytics**
   - Drive the adoption of automation in management, analytics, and content distribution — minimize manual work
   - Implement and refine workflows for content inventory, B-roll organization, and campaign management
   - Analyze campaign and content performance — use data to iterate strategy, optimize conversion funnels, inform future planning

5. **Brand-Building, Innovation & Market Positioning**
   - Launch and oversee new campaigns, content series, and internal initiatives that build brand equity and create emotional connection
   - Ensure every brand touchpoint reinforces the company's values, standards, and unique market position
   - Stay ahead of industry trends, competitor moves, and audience shifts — adapt strategy and creative direction proactively

## How To Use This Note

### Weekly Recap (Trellis Fruits)
Each person's section maps to the columns of the [[../Rituals/Trellis-Fruits-Weekly-Format|Trellis Fruits Weekly Format]]: Communication / Delays-Progress / Highlights-Wins / Content Produced / Meetings-Notes.

### Hiring
When growing the team, decide whether to:
- Expand an existing role (e.g., add a second Junior Editor)
- Create a new role that doesn't currently exist (e.g., dedicated Copywriter, dedicated VA)

Either way, the new person's responsibilities must be documented at this level of detail before the role goes live.

### Conflict Resolution
If two people argue about who owns something, this note is the source of truth. If neither owns it explicitly → it's a gap, and María José assigns it.
