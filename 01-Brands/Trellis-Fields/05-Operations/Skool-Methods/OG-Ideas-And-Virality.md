## Purpose
Method for generating **original content ideas** (not copying competitor ideas). Foundation principle for why content goes viral.

## The Core Equation
> **Virality = Value**

The more valuable your video, the more viral it goes.

## How To Create Value (3 steps)
> **Value is created when pain is alleviated. Pain is alleviated when problems are solved.**

People are selfish. They have to know what they're getting out of the video. If you yap about your personal life with no value → no one cares.

You must give your audience **something**.

## The Original Idea Method

### Step 1 — Talk to your audience
Literally get on a call with someone in your audience. Ask:
- "What are you struggling with right now?"
- "What content can I make that would help you out?"

They'll say things like: "I don't know how to play this riff" or "I don't know how to get crispy audio." Turn that exact problem into a video.

### Step 2 — Solve their specific problem
Once you have the problem, the video title = "How to [solve their problem]".

**Example:**
- Brady said: "How do I get crispy audio like yours?"
- → Video idea: "How to get crispy audio"
- → Already validated because Brady asked it
- → Solves a real pain point

### Step 3 — Validate on YouTube
Search "[problem]" on YouTube + check view counts.
- High view counts on similar videos = validated demand
- Use existing videos as **structure inspiration**, not copy

### Step 4 — Use AI to script (e.g., Poppy or ChatGPT)
> "Rewrite this video '[topic]' in my style and tone of voice. Follow the principles of how a YouTube video should be scripted."

Get a draft → edit for your brand voice → record.

## Audience Width Determines Reach

- **Solve niche problem** ("how to play this pentatonic scale in D minor") → niche audience → low views
- **Solve broad problem** ("how to play guitar for beginners") → millions of beginners → potential millions of views

> Beginners' guides do well because most people are beginners.

## The Edutainment Continuum

Every video lands somewhere on this spectrum:

```
[Pure Education] ───────────── [Pure Entertainment]

     ↑                                      ↑
Chemistry course                       Mr. Beast
"Intro to Chemistry"                   "Minecraft but everything is random"
```

### Where each platform performs best
| Platform | Sweet spot |
|---|---|
| **YouTube** | Education + edutainment (deeper, longer attention) |
| **TikTok** | Entertainment-heavy |
| **Instagram** | Entertainment-leaning |

### Trellis style
Aim **slightly left of center** — mostly education with entertainment elements. Storytelling + a bit of personality keeps the video engaging while still teaching.

## Two Sub-Types Of Value

### Information value
Audience problem: "I don't know how to do X."
Video solves it by teaching.

### Entertainment value
Audience problem: "I'm bored."
Video solves it by being entertaining.

> Sometimes both at once — that's the edutainment sweet spot.

## The Trellis Original-Ideas Workflow

1. **Talk to your audience** (DM, call, comment review)
2. **Capture the problem in their own words**
3. **Convert to video title:** "How to [their specific problem]"
4. **Validate on YouTube** (check view counts for similar videos)
5. **Use AI to draft script** in your voice/tone
6. **Edit + record + publish**

## Anti-Patterns
- ❌ Yapping about yourself with no value
- ❌ Solving a niche problem when you want broad reach
- ❌ Copying another creator's idea without adapting to your audience
- ❌ Pure entertainment with no underlying value (doesn't scale)
- ❌ Pure education without entertainment hooks (people leave)
