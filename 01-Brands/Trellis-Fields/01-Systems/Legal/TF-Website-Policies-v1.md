Six documents for Trellis Fields. TF is B2B, so the middle document is **Client Services Terms**, not a consumer booking policy — and the highest-value page is the **AI & Data Handling Statement**, because that is where TF's actual governance discipline is a selling point rather than a disclosure obligation.

**Not legal advice.** Counsel review required, particularly the Client Services Terms and the cross-border data section.

> All commercial terms below are `[A6 — recommended defaults]`. Nothing in the vault defines TF's billing cycle, term, or termination notice. These are drafted to be reasonable for a retainer agency and must be confirmed before use.

## 3.1 Structure

These Client Services Terms apply to all services we deliver. Each engagement is defined by a **Statement of Work (SOW)** or proposal naming the scope, deliverables, fees, and dates. Where an SOW conflicts with these Terms, the SOW controls for that engagement.

## 3.2 What we do

Strategy, content, reporting, automation, and conversion operations — turning business context into execution. Common deliverables: next actions and task structures, editor-ready briefs, reporting summaries with recommended decisions, SOP drafts, automation scopes, scripts and CTAs, and funnel or offer recommendations.

## 3.3 What is out of scope unless the SOW says otherwise

Media spend, third-party software licenses and subscriptions, stock or licensed assets, paid tooling, talent fees, and any platform costs. Those are billed at cost or paid directly by you.

We do not deliver inspiration-driven content, templates without strategy, or tool recommendations where the real answer is a system.

## 3.4 What we need from you

Work moves at the speed of your inputs. You agree to provide:
- A named decision-maker with authority to approve
- Access to the systems in scope, at the access level agreed
- Source material, context, and brand assets
- Feedback within the agreed review window

**The brief is the contract between idea and production. No brief, no production.** Where an approved brief or required input is outstanding, timelines shift accordingly.

## 3.5 Revisions and approval

- **Two rounds of revision per deliverable.** Round 1 is structure. Round 2 is polish.
- Every deliverable has a **Definition of Done** agreed before production starts.
- Feedback is consolidated and specific. Additional rounds, or changes that alter the approved brief, are new scope.
- A deliverable is accepted when approved, or `[5]` business days after delivery if no feedback is received.

## 3.6 Fees and payment `[A6]`

- Retainers are invoiced `[monthly, in advance]` and due `[on receipt / net 15]`.
- Project and sprint work is invoiced `[50% to start, 50% on delivery]`.
- Late payments accrue `[1.5% per month]` and we may pause work on accounts more than `[15]` days overdue.
- Fees are exclusive of taxes and third-party costs under §3.3.
- Fees may be adjusted with `[30]` days' written notice, effective at the next renewal.

## 3.7 Term and termination `[A6]`

- Retainers run month to month after any initial minimum term stated in the SOW.
- Either party may terminate with `[30]` days' written notice.
- Either party may terminate immediately for material breach not cured within `[10]` days of notice.
- On termination: you pay for work performed through the termination date; we deliver completed and in-progress work product for which you have paid; we return or delete your materials on request per §3.9; and access credentials are handed back and our access revoked.

## 3.8 Ownership of work `[A6 — position must be chosen]`

**Recommended position:** on full payment for an engagement, you own the deliverables produced specifically for you — copy, briefs, reports, SOPs, and creative assets.

We retain ownership of our **background IP**: our frameworks, methods, internal templates, automation patterns, prompt libraries, and the systems we use to produce the work. Where a deliverable contains background IP, you receive a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use it in your business.

**Portfolio and case studies:** we may describe the work and results in our portfolio, case studies, and marketing **only with your prior written approval of the specific materials**. You may withdraw approval for future use at any time.

## 3.9 Confidentiality and your data

- We treat your business information, metrics, customer data, and strategy as confidential and use it only to deliver your services.
- **Credentials are never stored in our knowledge base.** Where credentials are required, they are held in a dedicated secrets store and excluded from all documentation and AI processing.
- **Brand isolation:** your context, voice, and data are not mixed into work for other clients.
- **Access is least-privilege and read-only by default.** Where we analyse advertising accounts, we request read-only permissions and do not modify campaigns, budgets, creative, or settings without explicit written authorization.
- On termination, we return or delete your materials on written request, except copies we must retain for legal, accounting, or backup purposes.

We process your business context through AI tools to synthesize, structure, and draft. This is core to how we work and we do not hide it. Our commitments, controls, and your opt-out are in the [AI & Data Handling Statement](/ai-and-data), which forms part of these Terms.

# PAGE 6 — AI & DATA HANDLING STATEMENT
**Publish at:** `/ai-and-data`

> This is the page worth building properly. TF is AI-native and processes client business context through AI tools. Most agencies doing this say nothing. Saying it clearly, with the specific controls TF already runs, converts a disclosure obligation into a trust asset — and answers the question every serious B2B buyer is now asking in procurement.

**How Trellis Fields uses AI with your data**
Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]

## 6.1 We use AI. Here is exactly how.

AI is our intelligence layer. We use it to synthesize messy business context — founder notes, meeting transcripts, KPI snapshots, funnel feedback, performance data — into structured recommendations, briefs, reports, and action plans.

**AI is not our system of record.** It does not hold your data as its source of truth, and it does not make decisions about your business.

## 6.2 What we process

- Context you give us: notes, transcripts, goals, constraints, brand materials
- Performance data we extract from systems you authorize: advertising accounts, CRM, project tools
- Our own frameworks and internal documentation

## 6.3 What we do not process

- **Credentials.** API keys, passwords, and tokens are never entered into our knowledge base, documentation, or AI tools. They are held in a dedicated secrets store.
- **Compensation and restricted internal records**, which are excluded from our knowledge base by policy.
- Any dataset you designate as excluded.

## 6.4 Controls we actually run

| Control | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Read-only by default** | When we analyse advertising accounts, we request read-only permissions. We do not modify campaigns, budgets, conversions, creative, or settings without explicit written authorization. |
| **Per-document AI eligibility** | Every document in our internal knowledge base carries an explicit flag marking whether it may be used by AI systems. Documents marked otherwise are excluded. |
| **Brand isolation** | Client context is compartmentalized. Your data, voice, and strategy are not mixed into another client's work. |
| **Sensitivity classification** | Documents are classified by sensitivity, and restricted material is excluded from extraction and AI processing entirely. |
| **Human decision authority** | Automation informs direction. It does not replace human judgment. Every deliverable is reviewed by a person before it reaches you. |
| **Grounded output** | Where information is missing, we make the smallest defensible assumption and label it. We do not invent metrics or process states. |

## 6.5 Meeting recordings

Internal and client meetings may be recorded and transcribed to produce notes and action items. We tell participants before recording and stop on request. Clients may request deletion of transcripts related to their account.

## 6.6 AI providers

We use third-party AI providers to process this information. `[C — name the providers, and confirm with each whether business-tier terms exclude your data from model training. State the answer here plainly. This is the single question B2B buyers ask.]`

## 6.7 Your choices

You may ask us to:
- Exclude specific systems, datasets, or documents from AI processing
- Stop recording any call
- Delete transcripts and derived notes associated with your account
- Receive a summary of which systems we access and at what permission level

Contact `[A4 — EMAIL]`.

## 6.8 Limits

AI-assisted output can be wrong. We review before delivery, but you remain responsible for decisions you make based on our recommendations, and for verifying anything that carries legal, financial, or regulatory consequence. Our work is not legal, financial, or tax advice.
