## Core Principle
> **No enviamos tráfico para vender. Enviamos tráfico para capturar intención.**

Most brands:
- Send traffic
- Ask "Buy" / "Quote" / "Schedule"
- 90% aren't ready
- They leave and never come back

The Director's role:
> Design the bridge between **interest** and **purchase**.

## Mental Framework

### Rule #1
**We convert traffic into relationship before sale.**

If we don't have a warm audience:
- Don't ask for direct purchase
- Offer a **Mini Oferta** first

### Rule #2 — A Lead Magnet must do 3 things
1. Resolve a small problem
2. Create awareness of a bigger problem
3. Increase desire for the main offer

Never:
- Resolve the central problem completely
- Satisfy the need totally

> If you satisfy totally, there's no reason to buy.

## 3 Lead Magnet Types

### Type 1 — Reveal a Problem (most powerful)
**Objective:** Crear DEPRIVACIÓN.

| Brand | Example |
|---|---|
| DR | "3 errores que están frenando el progreso de tu hijo" |
| CTS | "¿Tu evento está perdiendo impacto por este detalle?" |
| Reliable Venues | "¿Cuánto dinero pierdes por no automatizar reservas?" |

### Type 2 — Free Trial / Taste Test
**Objective:** Dar valor → Retirarlo → Crear dependencia.

| Brand | Example |
|---|---|
| DR | Primera sesión gratuita estructurada |
| CTS | Degustación exclusiva con registro |
| Reliable Venues | Demo limitada del dashboard |

### Type 3 — One Step of Many
**Objective:** Resolver paso 1 → Revelar que faltan 5 más.

| Brand | Example |
|---|---|
| DR | Evaluación técnica individual |
| CTS | Checklist para planear evento perfecto |
| Reliable Venues | Auditoría inicial de flujo de reservas |

## Delivery Formats
Four possible formats:
1. **Software / Tool**
2. **Información**
3. **Servicio gratuito**
4. **Producto físico**

**Director's question:** ¿Cuál formato genera más percepción de valor con menor costo operativo?

## Cost vs Acquisition Principle
If:
- Cost to deliver = $25
- Close rate = 1 of 4
- CAC = $100

And customer is worth $2,000+ → it's absurd not to do it.

> Pensar en Lifetime Value, no en costo inmediato.

## Naming Is Strategy
The lead magnet name matters more than its content initially.

> No vendemos el vehículo. Vendemos el resultado.

| ❌ Bad | ✅ Good |
|---|---|
| "6-week squat training" | "Big Booty Bootcamp" |
| "Guía PDF para eventos" | "Checklist que evita que tu evento fracase" |

## Errors The Director Must Not Permit
1. Lanzar tráfico sin capturar lead
2. Crear lead magnet sin CTA
3. Dar demasiado valor y eliminar necesidad futura
4. No calificar leads antes de dar servicio gratis
5. No probar nombres / headlines
6. Asumir que "los freebie seekers no compran"

## Director's Weekly Ritual (every Monday)
1. ¿Estamos enviando tráfico directo a venta?
2. ¿Tenemos lead magnet activo?
3. ¿Está bien empaquetado?
4. ¿Tiene CTA claro?
5. ¿Está creando deseo por la oferta principal?
6. ¿Estamos midiendo conversión post-consumo?

## Real Impact
This doesn't increase traffic. It increases **conversion**. And conversion:
- Impacts margin
- Impacts cashflow
- Impacts reinvestment capacity
- Impacts growth speed
