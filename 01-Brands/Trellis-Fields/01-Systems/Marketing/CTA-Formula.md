## The Formula
Every CTA must have **3 ingredients**:
1. **Claro** — what action exactly
2. **Acción específica** — single action (not a list of options)
3. **Razón para hacerlo ahora** — why this moment

## Examples

### ✅ Correct
> "Descarga la guía ahora antes de que cerremos cupos esta semana."

Has: clear ("descarga la guía"), specific ("ahora"), reason ("antes de que cerremos cupos").

### ❌ Incorrect
> "Si quieres saber más, contáctanos."

Missing: specific action (contact how?), reason now (why this week?), and is conditional ("si quieres") which loses momentum.

## More Examples By Soil

| Soil | Good CTA | Bad CTA |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Desconocido | "Sigue para más como esto, soltamos uno cada lunes" | "Síguenos" |
| 2 — Descubrimiento | "Guarda esto, lo vas a necesitar cuando lances tu próxima campaña" | "Comparte con tus amigos" |
| 3 — Consideración | "Mira el case study completo en el link, te muestra el antes/después en 3 minutos" | "Más info aquí" |
| 4 — Primera compra | "Reserva tu primera sesión esta semana, solo abrimos 4 cupos" | "Contáctanos si te interesa" |
| 5 — Recurrente | "Únete a la próxima sesión, jueves 7pm, te mando el link" | "Te esperamos" |
| 6 — Comunidad | "Tag a alguien de la casa que necesita ver esto" | "Comparte si te gustó" |

## CTA Anti-Patterns To Kill
- **Conditional CTAs:** "Si quieres…" / "Por si te interesa…" — they signal you don't believe in the value
- **Compound CTAs:** "Suscríbete, comparte, y comenta" — choose ONE per piece
- **Vague action verbs:** "Conoce más", "Descubre", "Explora" — say what they'll actually get
- **No urgency, no scarcity:** without a reason now, the CTA is a wish
- **No what-happens-next:** the viewer must know exactly what comes after they act

## "What Happens Next" Detail
Per [[../../../Discipline-Rift/06-DNA/Conversion|DR Conversion DNA]] (Hormozi-aligned): every CTA should be followed (implicitly or explicitly) by "what to do next / when / what they get / what happens next".

This converts CTA from "ask" to "promise".

## Decision Rule
Before publishing any piece, the CTA must pass:
- [ ] Clear action verb?
- [ ] Single specific action (not a menu)?
- [ ] Reason now (scarcity / urgency / timing)?
- [ ] What happens next implied?
- [ ] Matches the soil of the piece?

If any check fails → not ready.
