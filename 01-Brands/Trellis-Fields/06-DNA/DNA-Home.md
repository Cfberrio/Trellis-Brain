## Purpose
The strategic skeleton of Trellis Fields' content + brand operating system. Distilled from the "Nuestros Frutos" workshop (Dec 2024) where Luis + María José + Charlize + Julián defined the methodology.

## Two Core Frameworks

### 1. Soils (1–6)
Every piece of content lives in exactly one soil. The soil dictates emotion, expectation, depth, and CTA shape.

See [[Soils-Framework]] for definitions + content-type per soil.

### 2. Storytelling Pillars (Hook + Build Up + Value)
Three structural pillars every piece of content must contain. CTA is separate discipline.

See [[Storytelling-Pillars]] for definitions + decision rules per pillar.

## Operating Principle
> "Los frutos no aparecen por accidente. Son el resultado directo de las semillas que se plantan con intención." — Luis

Content does not bear fruit by accident. It's the direct result of seeds planted with intention. The Soils framework + Pillars exist to make that intention conscious and operational.
