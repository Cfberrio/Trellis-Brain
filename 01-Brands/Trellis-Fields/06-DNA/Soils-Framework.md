# Soils Framework (1–6)

## Parent

## Purpose
The Soils framework classifies every piece of content by the **state of the audience's relationship** with the brand. Soil determines emotion, hook style, depth, value type, and CTA shape.

> Every piece of content lives in **exactly one soil**. Mixed soils = mixed signal = lost retention.

## Soils Definition

### Soil 1 — Desconocido (Unknown)
**Audience state:** Has never heard of the brand.

**Hook type:** Curiosity + clarity
- "Esto es lo que hacemos (sin que te des cuenta)"
- "Si eres nuevo en X, esto te va a ahorrar tiempo"

**Objective:** Exist in radar without asking for commitment.

**Value type:** Clarity + curiosity. Brief, simple, no overload.

**CTA shape:** Soft — "follow", "see more", "DM if curious".

### Soil 2 — Descubrimiento (Discovery)
**Audience state:** Aware brand exists but hasn't engaged deeply.

**Hook type:** Simple problem + mini revelation
- "La mayoría piensa esto… y por eso falla"
- "La forma más fácil de entender X es esta"

**Objective:** Hook interest + generate familiarity.

**Value type:** Clarity + curiosity. Slightly deeper than Soil 1.

**CTA shape:** Soft engagement — "save this", "share with someone who…", "open DM".

### Soil 3 — Consideración (Consideration)
**Audience state:** Evaluating brand vs alternatives.

**Hook type:** Objection + proof
- "Si estás entre A y B, mira esto"
- "Lo que nadie te muestra antes de decidir…"

**Objective:** Build trust.

**Value type:** Explanation + comparison + structure.

**CTA shape:** Mid-commitment — "see case study", "book a call", "apply for…".

### Soil 4 — Primera Compra (First Purchase)
**Audience state:** Has decided to try, needs reassurance.

**Hook type:** Accompaniment + safety
- "Si es tu primera vez, haz esto primero"
- "Evita este error en tu primera experiencia"

**Objective:** Reduce friction + validate decision.

**Value type:** Validation + risk reduction.

**CTA shape:** Direct action — "register", "buy", "schedule".

### Soil 5 — Recurrente (Recurring)
**Audience state:** Already a customer/member. Needs reason to stay engaged.

**Hook type:** Novelty + insider
- "Lo nuevo que estamos probando esta semana…"
- "Esto cambió cómo hacemos X"

**Objective:** Maintain interest and continuity.

**Value type:** Depth + novelty + recognition.

**CTA shape:** Continuity — "next session", "try this", "share in community".

### Soil 6 — Comunidad (Community)
**Audience state:** Identifies as part of the brand's inner circle.

**Hook type:** Belonging + participation
- "Solo los de la casa entienden esto…"
- "¿Quién más vivió esto?"

**Objective:** Activate community.

**Value type:** Belonging + participation + identity.

**CTA shape:** Co-creation — "tag someone", "vote", "share your version", "hot seat".

## Decision Rule
Before producing any piece, the brief must answer: **which soil**?

If the piece can address multiple soils, split it into separate pieces — one per soil. Do not try to address mixed audiences in one asset.

## Operating Note
Value misplaced by soil = value lost. Soil mismatch is the #1 reason a piece "feels off" without the team knowing why.
