## Representative examples (inline)

### School requests details → 10-point logistics answer (highest-leverage)
**School (Ms. Alvarez, Potter's House):** Could you provide details — facility rental, insurance, screening, ratios, cost, grades, space, dismissal, references, agreement?
**DR:** Good morning Ms. Alvarez! Sure! Happy to provide more details. 1. Facility Use... 2. Insurance (COI, Additional Insured)... 3. Level 2 background screening (OCPS), badges... 4. Ratio ~1:10... 5. Family cost, 6-week season, equipment included... 6. K–5, min 8 / max 20... 7. Space needed... 8. Supervision/Dismissal... 9. References... 10. Agreement for your review.

### Soft NO → gracious, leaves door open
**School (Ms. Permenter, Luminary):** At this time I have not agreed to offer this program... I plan to survey our community first.
**DR:** Hi Ms. Permenter! Sorry for the misunderstanding. Please let me know if your families would be interested. Although Lake Nona has the most youth volleyball clubs, we focus on the developmental phase, bringing the program to schools like Sun Blaze, Village, Laureate and Moss Park. We'd love to work together next school year. Will stay in touch.

### Facility conflict → flexible
**School (Ms. Megan, Orangewood):** Internal struggle with the gym... backup plan is to rent the pavilion next door... either way you're set for Wednesdays.
**DR:** Hi Ms. Megan! Anytime! Sounds good. Please keep me updated so I can update info on our website as you plan it.

## How to use these
Faculty register is more formal + logistics-focused ([03](03-faculty-communication.md)). Answer every question asked, lead with trust items (insurance/screening/ratios) when a school is vetting, stay flexible, never pressure on a "no", confirm the next concrete action. Contracts/pricing/legal → draft + flag ([06](06-escalation-rules.md)).
