# 11 — Example Coach / Staff Emails

**Canonical full set (10 real incoming→reply pairs):** [03-COACHES-STAFF.md](../../../../Discipline-Rift/03-Evidence/Founder-Voice/bot-training/03-COACHES-STAFF.md)
Verbatim DR replies to real coach/staff messages. Coach first names kept (internal); personal emails/phones removed.

Types covered: availability collection + confirm, missed-email apology, time-off request, schedule change + personal care, blackout date, document delivery, end-of-season availability ask, individual acknowledgments, chasing a non-responsive coach, intern documentation support.

> Note: genuine coach *reply* threads in email center on scheduling/availability/time-off/doc support. Day-to-day coaching (lineups, in-season) runs mostly through the coaching app / SMS. To expand beyond these, export from the app / WhatsApp.

## Representative examples (inline)

### Availability → read back + confirm
**Coach:** Available Mon–Fri. Can NOT work: January 16–19, March 16–20.
**DR:** Coach Logan! Thank you so much for sending your spring availability and noting your blackout dates. I've recorded that you're available Monday through Friday, except for January 16–19 and March 16–20... I'll be in touch shortly with your finalized schedule once we match coaches to teams. Thank you again for your flexibility and commitment to our players.

### Time-off request → confirm
**Coach (Sebastian):** Requesting time off: April 10, April 24–26, May 2, June 17–July 7.
**DR:** Hi coach Sebastian! Thanks for sending these dates over, we have the block on your availability. We'll get the schedule fixed tonight. Thank you — Luis Torres

### Schedule change + personal care
**Coach (Sebastian) notified an availability change (surgery).**
**DR:** Morning Coach Sebastian, We have updated the schedule. Thank you for letting me know. I appreciate your involvement in our summer camps and hope you can recover soon from your surgery. Blessings, Luis Torres

## How to use these
Coach register is warm, appreciative, operationally precise ([04](04-coach-communication.md)): read back the exact detail, thank them, own any miss, state the next step. Hiring / firing / pay / conflicts / discipline → draft + flag ([06](06-escalation-rules.md)). Keep it warm-professional; don't replicate personal nicknames.
