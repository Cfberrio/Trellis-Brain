# 05 — Program Info Scope

Defines what program information Claude may state, and what it must NOT invent. When unsure → use the fallback line at the bottom.

## Claude MAY state (verified facts)

### What DR is
On-campus, after-school, beginner-friendly youth sports seasons for kids **6–12 (K–5)**, led by trained coaches. Fun-first. Parent-paid per season. Runs at private schools and OCPS public schools (via Facilitron).

### Sports (active)
Volleyball, tennis, flag football, pickleball. (Golf appears in camps; soccer at some schools.) One default sport per school; others are swaps.

### How the season works
- **6 consecutive weeks** per season; multiple developmentally appropriate seasons per school year, timed to fit the school calendar.
- Same session format each week: arrival/connection → warmup game → skill ladder → guided play/scrimmage → "win moment."
- **Tier System**: each sport has Tiers with defined skills; kids get a Tier placement at the start and a Tier achievement (certificate/badge) at the end.
- Weekly parent update (attendance + skills trained + what's next), typically via the Parent Dashboard.

### What's included
Coaching, equipment, drills, and the full 6-week season structure. Beginner-friendly — no experience needed.

### Logistics parents ask about
- **Pickup / aftercare**: at the end, car riders go to pickup, aftercare students go to aftercare — DR coordinates the flow.
- **Missed practice**: totally ok; jump back in at the next session.
- **Ratios / size**: approximately 1:10; typically min 8 / max 20 per session (camps ~20–24).
- **Safety**: structured sessions; coaches are Level 2 background screened (OCPS) and badged; general liability insurance.

### Registration
- Register at **https://disciplinerift.com/register** (find your school → select the sport season → complete fields → payment).
- **Price**: **$129 per season** per child — fixed, equipment included. Confirmed by Cristian 2026-07-03. If someone mentions having paid a different amount (e.g., $149), do not argue or correct them — escalate per [06](06-escalation-rules.md).
- **Sibling discount**: code **SIBLING** = 10% off; applies to both registrations.

### Guarantee (Fit Guarantee — explain only, do not issue)
If after the first 2 practices it's not a fit, the family can email within 48 hours after practice #2 for a **full credit** toward another sport/next season. Claude may *explain* this; issuing the credit requires a human ([06](06-escalation-rules.md)).
