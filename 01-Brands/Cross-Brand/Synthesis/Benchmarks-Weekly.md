### System-wide benchmarks

| Metric | Weekly benchmark |
|---|---|
| On-time delivery | 90% of pieces delivered on date (per realistic editor benchmark) |
| Strong corrections | Max 1 strong round per piece |
| Repeated errors | Goal: 0 critical repeated errors / week |
| Avg editing time | Reduce gradually. Initial goal: 15% less in 6–8 weeks |
| High-performing pieces | Min 1 winning piece per editor per week, or per brand every 2 weeks |
| Applied learning | Each week leave 1 clear adjustment to repeat or correct |
| Benchmark alignment | Every assigned task must answer: "Does this bring us closer to the brand's benchmark?" |
| Real team capacity | Editor 1: Trellis + DR. Editor 2: CTS + LT. Benchmark not measured as if each brand has dedicated editor. |

## How to read these numbers

Not every post grows that %. Compare general weekly performance vs previous week — aspire to that improvement range **without losing sight of real team capacity**.

Benchmark not just for volume — for deciding if assigned tasks build strategic progress.

**Examples:**
- Trellis 2 pieces / week → must fulfill clear function: authority, criterion, positioning, distribution.
- DR 1 piece / week → cannot be generic. Must bring brand closer to real program proof (volleyball, coaching, parents, enrollment).
- CTS 2 pieces / week → at least one with clear commercial intention (product, catering, order, experience, CTA).

## Recommendation by stage

### Level 1 — Minimum acceptable
- Trellis: 1 strategic piece published
- DR: 1 piece with program proof connection
- CTS: 2 pieces, min 1 with commercial intention
- LT: may skip publishing if CTS load is high

### Level 2 — Good performance
- Trellis: 2 pieces published
- DR: 1–2 pieces
- CTS: 2–3 pieces
- LT: 1 simple piece if capacity exists
- Min 1 applied learning per editor

### Level 3 — Strong week
- Trellis: 2 pieces with good positioning
- DR: 2 pieces, including program proof
- CTS: 3 pieces, including narrative/commercial Reel
- LT: 1 piece without affecting CTS
- Min 1 winning format detected or repeatable

## Operational load adjustments

### Editor 1 heavy on Trellis weeks
- Trellis maintains priority
- DR can operate at range minimum
- DR doesn't disappear — can drop to 1 piece / week
- That piece must be strategic, not filler

### CTS heavy weeks (website / flyers / menus / design / commercial campaigns / urgent pieces)
- LT drops to secondary priority (0–1 piece / week)
- CTS maintains 2 pieces if high operational load
- CTS commercial consistency must not break

## Key Trellis note (April 2026)

Trellis wasn't evaluated with same weight in April — newly active on socials that month. April report focused on CTS + DR with clear reach / publication / followers / interaction metrics.

**But Trellis is NOT secondary. Trellis is main strategic priority.**

Difference is in **how it's measured:**
- Priority on consistency
- Priority on authority
- Priority on criterion
- Priority on positioning
- Priority on repeatable formats
- Priority on reach + initial distribution
- Don't yet demand too many saves/shares as main metric

Trellis must have fixed weekly planning space even when sharing editor with DR. Goal: build a brand that communicates leadership, process, criterion.

## April 2026 key signal

April produced a clear signal: **the problem wasn't just producing content.**

- CTS had more reach (8,787 people, +119.7% vs March) but lost 60 net followers.
- DR also grew reach (1,809 people, +64.5% vs March) but lost 2 followers and didn't build enough volleyball proof, a strategic priority.

New benchmark must be **more realistic + more strategic.**

Not "more content for the sake of more content." Each piece must respond to clear intention:

```
benchmark → task → piece → CTA → learning → repetition
```
