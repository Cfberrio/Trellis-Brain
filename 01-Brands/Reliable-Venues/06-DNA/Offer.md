## Product name

**Trellis Venue Revenue Operator** — BookingOS + DemandOS, performance-aligned.

## Promise (measurable)

> "In 30 days, we install the AI-driven inquiry→deposit booking system and make every inquiry trackable in one pipeline; in 90 days, we increase booked revenue above your baseline — without hiring more booking staff or adding tool sprawl."

Mechanism = **speed + reduced effort** (DFY), which increases perceived value.

## Mechanism (vehicle)

**Trellis BookingOS + DemandOS** — a done-for-you revenue layer that converts **inquiry → instant response → follow-up → deposit → booked calendar** (unified CRM / workflows), plus a demand layer that creates consistent inquiries.

## Offer construction — obstacles → solutions → delivery → "done"

### Obstacle 1 — "Inquiries everywhere" (no single tracking)
- **Solution:** Centralize inquiry capture + pipeline in one system.
- **Delivered as:** DFY CRM / pipeline setup + intake consolidation.
- **Done looks like:** Every inquiry becomes a contactable lead, sitting in one tracked pipeline.

### Obstacle 2 — Response lag (leads cool off)
- **Solution:** 24/7 AI first response + standardized next steps.
- **Delivered as:** DFY AI booking / inquiry agent + response scripts.
- **Done looks like:** New inquiries receive immediate response + routed to next action.

### Obstacle 3 — No follow-up → ghosting
- **Solution:** Automated follow-up engine (nudges, no-show recovery, reactivation).
- **Delivered as:** DFY follow-up workflows + templates.
- **Done looks like:** Follow-up happens automatically until deposit / tour booked.

### Obstacle 4 — Deposits inconsistent → calendar stays soft
- **Solution:** Deposit automation + booking workflow standardization.
- **Delivered as:** DFY payment / deposit flows + booking SOP handoffs.
- **Done looks like:** Deposits collected consistently and tracked per inquiry / date.

### Obstacle 5 — Dead dates not actively filled
- **Solution:** Calendar optimization + "dead day" push logic.
- **Delivered as:** DFY offer / availability pushes through DemandOS (see tiers).
- **Done looks like:** Dead days actively marketed with trackable campaigns + follow-up.

### Obstacle 6 — Demand isn't reliable (marketing optional = outcomes unguaranteeable)
- **Solution:** **Minimum required DemandOS** (Foundation), expand by tier.
- **Delivered as:** Demand tier selection + DFY execution.
- **Done looks like:** At least one reliable acquisition path live + being improved before adding more.

## Trim rule

Remove anything that doesn't remove an obstacle or increase success rate. Offer wins by being **outcome-driven + hard to compare**, not by adding nice-to-haves.

## Stack (one path, not a buffet)

| Phase | Window | What |
|---|---|---|
| Step 1 — Fastest win | Days 1–30 | Baseline + BookingOS install (pipeline, AI response, follow-up, deposit tracking) |
| Step 2 — Core build | Days 31–60 | Stabilize conversion + handoffs (deposit consistency, workflows, SOPs) |
| Step 3 — Scale / maintenance | Days 61–90+ | DemandOS execution + optimization (add channels only after first is reliable) |

## Proof + guarantee assets (likelihood engine)

### Proof inventory
- Before / after pipeline screenshots
- Baseline vs post-install booked revenue dashboards
- Call recordings / walkthrough demos
- Objection library + categorized rebuttals

### Guarantee — Trial With Penalty
**Type:** Trial + performance-based extension (not vibes-based free month).

- **Month 1:** "Install Month" is **$0** *if* they complete required actions (centralize revenue path, complete onboarding, provide baseline numbers).
- **If not met:** Install Month converts to standard fee, or first month becomes paid.

Free isn't unconditional — it's conditional on behaviors that create success. Prevents heavy DFY work for venues that won't let us measure or operate the system.

## Price + packaging

### Required: Operator Fee (by venue stage)

Charged as **% of incremental booked revenue above baseline**:

| Stage | Monthly booked revenue | Operator % |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ≈$15k/mo | 15% |
| 2 | ≈$25k/mo | 13% |
| 3 | ≈$50k/mo | 10% |
| 4 | ≈$100k/mo | 8% |
| 5 | ≈$250k/mo | 5% |

### Required minimum: DemandOS tier

To be the umbrella solution and protect the guarantee, **Foundation DemandOS is mandatory** unless they can prove a reliable inquiry source AND still allow full tracking + centralized pipeline.

| Tier | What's included | Added % on incremental |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundation (Required)** | One reliable acquisition path: SEO + Google Ads | +5% |
| **Growth** | Foundation + Meta | +8% |
| **Dominate** | Brand Design + Strategic Growth + Business Modeling | +10% |

**Expansion rule:** only add tiers after the first path is reliable ("choose ONE reliable acquisition path first, then max it out").

### What this pricing screens for

- **Attracts:** owners willing to centralize the revenue path + run a measurable, outcome-based relationship.
- **Excludes:** venues who want to pick pieces, compare vendors, and negotiate line items (commoditized purchasing behavior).

### Exclusivity (optional add-on)

Every venue needs this service. Default = serve as many as possible. Optional **exclusivity program** locks out peer venues — premium fee.

## Why "required Foundation" makes us the umbrella

- Commoditized providers get price-compared + swapped on undercut.
- Strong brand + bundled outcome increases pricing power + prevents "10% undercut" churn.
- A Grand Slam / category-of-one offer does differentiation work — we don't defend line items.

## Internal policy (one sentence)

> **"We only work with venues who want more booked revenue and will let us run the revenue path end-to-end; Foundation DemandOS is the minimum required to guarantee outcomes — otherwise we're not a fit."**
