## Master metric

Everything is measured against one master metric:

> **Incremental booked revenue above baseline.**

Sales, ops, reporting, and billing all align to this metric. If a number doesn't connect back to it, it doesn't make the scoreboard.

## Three measurement layers

### Layer 1 — Sales pipeline (weekly cadence)

**Leading indicators (team-owned):**
- Warm reachouts sent
- Cold reachouts sent
- New leads created
- Minutes producing outreach assets (hooks / bodies / proof)
- Roadmap calls booked

**Lagging indicators (results):**
- Engaged leads
- Qualified opportunities
- Installs started (Install Month accepted)
- Month-2 conversions (revshare activated)
- Incremental revenue captured (billing basis)

**Conversion rates tracked weekly:**
- Attempt → engaged
- Engaged → booked call
- Booked → showed
- Showed → qualified
- Qualified → Install Month started
- Install Month completed → Month-2 conversion
- Month-2 retention (90 / 180-day)

### Layer 2 — Fulfillment quality (account-level)

Per client, every month:
- **First response time** (target: minutes, not hours)
- **Follow-up coverage** (attempts per inquiry)
- **Deposit rate** (deposits collected / inquiries qualified)
- **Booked revenue vs baseline** (the master metric)
- **Utilization** (dead dates filled)

### Layer 3 — Business health (portfolio-level)

- New accounts started per month
- Average revshare % per account (mix tracking)
- Average incremental uplift per account
- Churn rate (monthly + 90-day cohort)
- LTV by stage tier
- Top-tier expansion rate (Foundation → Growth → Dominate)

## Billing-aligned metric definitions

To prevent disputes:

- **Baseline:** trailing 60–90-day average monthly booked revenue, pre-go-live, verified via deposits / bookings records.
- **Incremental booked revenue:** current month booked revenue − baseline.
- **Total take rate:** Operator % (by stage from [[01-Brands/Reliable-Venues/06-DNA/Offer|Offer]]) + Demand tier % (Foundation required).
- **Billing window:** monthly in arrears default; quarterly optional.

## Reporting cadence

| Cadence | Output | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | Sales scoreboard (leading + lagging) | Sales team + leadership |
| Monthly | Per-account scorecard (baseline vs current) | Each venue client + ops |
| Monthly | Portfolio health (new accounts, churn, ARR) | Leadership |
| Quarterly | QBR per client + portfolio strategy review | Operator lead + client decision-makers |

## What we do NOT measure

- Vanity reach numbers disconnected from incremental booked revenue.
- Tool counts / "we did 50 things this month" busy metrics.
- Activity hours without conversion linkage.
- Anything not traceable to a venue's baseline + uplift.
