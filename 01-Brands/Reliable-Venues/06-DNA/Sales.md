## 0. Sales doctrine (non-negotiables)

- We are not a "marketing vendor" or "software" — we're the operator that installs + runs the revenue layer and gets paid on revenue unlocked.
- Only promise outcomes we can control. If demand is required, **Foundation DemandOS is required** (unless they prove a reliable inquiry source AND allow full tracking + centralized pipeline).
- "Free month" is not a giveaway — it's a **Trial With Penalty** tied to compliance actions that make success possible.
- Everything runs off the same metric: **incremental booked revenue above baseline**. Sales, ops, reporting, billing follow this.

## 1. Operating definitions

- **Lead:** unique contactable decision-maker record in CRM (name + phone / email + venue).
- **Reachout / Attempt:** one attempt (dial, VM, email, text).
- **New lead created:** new unique contactable record.
- **Engaged lead:** does at least ONE — replies with buying signal, books roadmap call, submits qualifiers.
- **Qualified opportunity:** engaged + confirms open dates / underutilized inventory + inquiry-to-deposit leakage + decision power + will centralize pipeline + will provide access / data.

## 2. Stage + baseline rules

- **Stage:** classified by current monthly booked revenue (per [[01-Brands/Reliable-Venues/06-DNA/Offer|Offer]] tiers).
- **Baseline:** trailing 60–90-day avg monthly booked revenue pre-go-live (verified by deposits / bookings records).
- **Incremental booked revenue:** current month booked revenue − baseline.
- **Total take rate:** Operator % (by stage) + DemandOS tier % (Foundation required).

## 3. Scoreboard — weekly operating cadence

### Leading indicators (owned by team)
- Warm reachouts sent
- Cold reachouts sent
- New leads created
- Minutes producing outreach assets (hooks / bodies / proof)
- Roadmap calls booked

### Lagging indicators (results)
- Engaged leads
- Qualified opportunities
- Installs started (Install Month accepted)
- Month-2 conversions (revshare activated)
- Incremental revenue captured (billing basis)

### Conversion rates tracked weekly
- Attempt → engaged
- Engaged → booked call
- Booked → showed
- Showed → qualified
- Qualified → Install Month started
- Install Month completed → Month-2 conversion
- Month-2 retention (90 / 180-day)

## 4. ICP + disqualifiers

### Ideal Customer Profile (priority)
- Event venues (weddings / corporate / buyouts / milestones)
- Inventory exists (open dates)
- Demand exists OR accepts required Foundation DemandOS
- Current process scattered / slow (clear leakage)
- Decision-maker attends call + will centralize the revenue path

### Disqualifiers (fast "no")
- Stage 0: cannot provide baseline numbers (no actual venue)
- Won't centralize inquiries
- Doesn't want any operational change

## 5. Core Four acquisition plan

### Warm 1:1 (partners + intros)
- **Weekly target:** 500 warm attempts
- **Targets:** planners, DJs, caterers, photographers, rental companies, venue vendors, prior clients
- **Ask:** "Who has open dates and leaks inquiries?"
- **KPI:** intros generated + engaged replies

### Warm 1:Many (daily teardown email)
- **Daily:** "Venue Booking Leak Fix" (1 short audit)
- **KPI:** replies matching engaged-lead criteria + referrals

### Cold 1:1 (primary engine)
- **Weekly target:** 500 cold attempts
- **Mix:** dials + emails + text follow-ups
- **KPI:** engaged leads + booked roadmap calls

### Cold 1:Many (segmented campaigns)
- **Weekly target:** new angles + new segments shipped
- **KPI:** cost per engaged lead (list + labor + tooling) — not vanity metrics

## 6. Messaging DNA — what we say repeatedly

### Core problem statement (friction chain)
> "Inquiries are scattered → response lag → no follow-up → deposits inconsistent → owner bottleneck → dead dates."

### Core outcome statement
> "We install and run the inquiry-to-deposit revenue layer so bookings happen without you."

### Core mechanism statement (category-of-one)
> "BookingOS (pipeline + AI response + follow-up + deposit automation) + required Foundation DemandOS (reliable inquiries), paid on uplift above baseline."

### Five message angles (rotate)
- **Speed closes:** "First response time is your biggest booking lever."
- **Deposits solve uncertainty:** "Deposits are the difference between 'interest' and 'booked.'"
- **Systems beat people:** "Humans create delay, inconsistency, and salary drag."
- **Dead dates are inventory:** "You're sitting on perishable inventory."
- **Single source of truth:** "One pipeline or you can't fix leaks."

## 7. Outreach assembly line

### Weekly assets produced
- Hook bank (questions / labels / commands / statements / lists / stories)
- 3–5 "meat" bodies (mini-audit, proof, mechanism, teardown, offer stack)
- 1–2 CTAs (book roadmap, reply keyword for teardown)

### Operating rules
- Keep hook tracker (date, hook, segment, reply rate, booked rate).
- **70/20/10 mix:** winners / new concepts / experiments.
- Every objection becomes new proof, new line, or new hook.

## 8. Cold call SOP (15–45 sec to earn next step)

**Goal:** qualify in under 2 minutes + book roadmap call.

### Opener (choose one)
- "Quick question — are you currently trying to fill open dates, and is your inquiry-to-deposit process tracked in one place?"
- "Do you know your average first response time to new inquiries?"

### If "yes / kinda"
- "We install and run the inquiry-to-deposit system: instant response, automated follow-up, and deposit collection in one pipeline. We get paid on the booked revenue we unlock above your baseline."

### Micro-qualifiers (pick 3)
- "How many inquiries do you get in a typical month?"
- "What happens after someone inquires — how do they get from inquiry to deposit?"
- "Do you ever lose people because you respond late or don't follow up?"
- "Do you have open dates you want filled in the next 30–60 days?"
- "Who besides you is involved in booking decisions?"

### CTA
- "If I show you exactly where deposits leak and what your baseline is, would you be open to a 15-minute Venue Scaling Roadmap call?"

### If "not interested" — downsell CTA
- "Totally fair — want me to send a 2-minute teardown checklist you can compare to your current process?"

### Voicemail (10 sec)
- "This is [Name]. I noticed most venues lose bookings in the response / follow-up gap. If you have open dates, I can show a quick leak map. Text me 'PIPELINE' at [#]."

## 9. Cold email SOP

### Email #1 — Pattern interrupt + 1 question
- **Subject:** "Holding dates without deposits?"
- **Body:** "Quick one — do you have open dates you're trying to fill, and is your inquiry → deposit process tracked in one place?"

### Email #2 — Mechanism + outcome
> "Most venues lose bookings in first response + follow-up. We install the inquiry-to-deposit system (instant response, follow-up, deposits) and get paid on uplift above baseline. Worth a 15-min roadmap?"

### Email #3 — Mini-audit teaser
> "I looked at how venues typically handle inquiries — 3 common deposit leaks. Want me to send a 2-minute leak map?"

### Email #4 — Proof prompt
> "Seeing venues cut ghosting and stabilize deposits when the pipeline is centralized. Want the walkthrough?"

### Email #5 — Breakup + downsell
> "Should I close your file, or are you the right person for bookings? If you want, reply 'PIPELINE' and I'll send the teardown checklist."

### Rules
- One CTA per email.
- Every email answerable with a 1-word reply.

## 10. Roadmap call SOP (Entry → Core close)

**Call objective:** move them from "curious" → "commit to Install Month."

### Structure
1. Confirm basics (stage, decision-maker, timeline, inventory).
2. Quantify the leak (response time, follow-up coverage, deposit rate, tool sprawl, owner time).
3. Show the path (3 steps): install BookingOS + baseline + deposit workflow → operate + optimize follow-up + fill dead dates → scale demand (Foundation required; upgrades optional).
4. Present the deal: Install Month = Trial with Penalty (free if compliant); Month 2+ = % of incremental + required Foundation %.
5. Close: "If we can install this in 30 days with you doing only the compliance checklist, are you ready to start the Install Month?"

### Compliance checklist (read aloud)
- Centralize inquiries into one pipeline.
- Provide access to calendar / inboxes / phone-SMS / payment.
- Use the booking flow (no shadow process).
- Provide baseline data for verification.

## 11. Closing system — objections → terms / downsells, not discounts

### Buckets (tag every time)
- Money / Time-bandwidth / Fit / Permission

### Standard handling
- **Money:** re-anchor to performance alignment ("paid on uplift"), then offer terms / structure (not discounts).
- **Time:** "DFY operator model — your job is compliance, not execution."
- **Fit:** re-qualify stage + constraints; disqualify fast if needed.
- **Permission:** "Let's bring them onto the roadmap recap; I'll present the leak map and the trial terms."

### Pre-written downsells
- Systems Operator Plan (fixed minimum, no demand work) if they refuse revshare but will centralize ops.
- Maintenance Lite if seasonal / cash crunch.
- Pilot with Penalty if they fear commitment.

## 12. Post-close handoff — sales → ops

### Immediately after close (same day)
- Signed agreement
- Baseline verification request
- Access checklist sent
- Kickoff booked
- Pipeline created + tags / stages standardized

### Install Month "done" checklist (sales promises, ops delivers)
- One pipeline live
- AI first response live
- Follow-up engine live
- Deposit workflow live
- Dashboard live (baseline vs current)

### Day 30 review (upsell moment)
- Inventory + wants faster growth → present DemandOS tier upgrade.
- Bookings stable + utilization high → present Optimization layer.

## 13. Marketing Machine loop — sales improves weekly

### Weekly ritual (non-negotiable)
- Record + tag calls (wins + losses).
- Build objection library (money / time / fit / permission).
- Clip proof: before / after dashboards, "why they bought," "what changed."
- Update hooks (subject lines / openers), scripts (rebuttals + qualifiers), sequences (best-performing angles).

## 14. Policy — avoid price chopping (umbrella positioning)

- **Minimum required bundle:** BookingOS + Foundation DemandOS (unless explicitly proven demand exists and they still allow full tracking + centralization).
- **Don't sell components.** Sell the outcome system.
- If asked for line-item pricing: "We don't price tools. We price outcomes and operation. The bundle is required because the pieces only work as a system."

## 15. Daily execution checklist

- 100 cold attempts / day (mix calls + emails)
- 100 warm attempts / day (partner intros + follow-ups)
- 1 teardown email shipped / day
- 1 new hook added / day
- All objections tagged same day
- All booked calls get proof nudges (post-booking + pre-show)
