## Best customer (tight)

**Owner / operator (or GM) of a private event venue** who:

- Has **unused inventory** (open dates) and/or inconsistent inquiry → deposit → booked-date conversion.
- Willing to **centralize the revenue path** (inquiries, follow-up, deposits, calendar) into one system.
- Has **decision-making power on the call** (no "need permission" loop).
- Wants a measurable, outcome-based relationship (performance alignment).

Use **Yes-Questions / If-Then** qualifiers in outreach to filter to this avatar.

## Day-to-day pain (sequence of friction)

The offer gets more valuable when each "next problem" is solved as it shows up:

- Inquiries arrive across channels → **no single place to track**
- Response lag → **leads cool off**
- No consistent follow-up → **ghosting / no-shows**
- Deposit collection inconsistent → **dates held without money**
- Calendar + messy ops → **owner becomes the bottleneck**

## Dream outcome (in their words)

Structured as **Dream Outcome + higher likelihood + less time delay + less effort** (the 4 value levers):

- "I want deposits hitting automatically and dates locking in without me chasing people."
- "I want one pipeline where I can see every inquiry and exactly what's happening."
- "I want bookings to happen without adding staff or babysitting the process."
- "I want predictable revenue from my calendar."

## Top obstacles (ordered → becomes deliverable list)

1. **No single capture + tracking system** for inquiries
2. **Slow / inconsistent first response** (leads decay)
3. **No clear next step** (tour / call / deposit path is fuzzy)
4. **Follow-up isn't relentless or standardized**
5. **Deposit collection is manual / late / inconsistent**
6. **Pipeline visibility is poor** (can't tell where revenue is stuck)
7. **Calendar utilization logic is absent** (no operating cadence for filling gaps)
8. **Operational handoffs are inconsistent** (post-booking workflows depend on people)

## Top objections (ordered)

Fall into predictable buckets — time, money, fit, other people / permission, decision avoidance.

1. **Money:** "This is expensive / I can't afford it."
2. **Time / bandwidth:** "I'm too busy to implement something new right now."
3. **Fit:** "This won't work for my venue / our clients are different."
4. **Other people / permission:** "I need my partner / manager to sign off."
5. **Decision avoidance:** "Send me info / I need to think about it."
