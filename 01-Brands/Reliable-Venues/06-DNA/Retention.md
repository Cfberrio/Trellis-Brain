## Why they stay

The retention thesis: a venue stays paying monthly because **the system keeps producing measurable lift** they can't replicate internally.

### Retention anchors (recurring proof points)
- Monthly **baseline vs current scorecard** delivered without them asking.
- **Deposit consistency** + **response time** staying low without their daily input.
- Owner is **not the bottleneck** — system runs without them.
- Continuous "**found money**" — ghosts recovered, dead dates filled, leaks patched.

If any anchor weakens, churn risk goes up. Track each one monthly.

## Why they leave (top churn risks)

| Risk | Signal | Counter-move |
|---|---|---|
| Seasonal cash crunch | Failed payment, cancel intent | Maintenance Lite downsell — keeps system live, retains data |
| "We can do this in-house" | Reduced ops engagement, fewer questions | Re-anchor to revenue uplift dollars they'd lose |
| Demand layer not converting | Inquiry volume up, booking flat | Recommend Optimization layer (margin lever) |
| Owner stops paying attention | Missed Day-30 review | Auto-schedule next review + show monthly uplift report |
| Fulfillment quality drift | Response time creep, missed follow-ups | Re-tune AI + sequences before client notices |

## Expansion ladder (LTV growth)

Retention compounds when accounts move up tiers. Expansion follows three predictable jumps:

### Jump 1 — Foundation → Growth DemandOS
- **Trigger:** Foundation channel reliable + inventory still has capacity.
- **Acceptance moment:** Day 30 review.
- **Economics:** +3% on top of Foundation.

### Jump 2 — Growth → Dominate DemandOS
- **Trigger:** Aggressive growth desire + dead-date filling priority.
- **Acceptance moment:** Capacity-constraint moment or first quarterly review.
- **Economics:** +5% on top of Foundation.

### Jump 3 — Operator → Revenue Optimization (Stage 3+)
- **Trigger:** Booking volume high, constraint is now margin per date.
- **Acceptance moment:** Quarterly business review.
- **Economics:** +2–4% on incremental OR fixed monthly + performance kicker.

### Jump 4 — Single venue → Portfolio Operator
- **Trigger:** Owner has multiple venues or acquisition plans.
- **Acceptance moment:** Strategy call after proof is established.
- **Economics:** Custom minimum + lower revshare band + portfolio reporting.

## Retention rituals (operational)

These cadences keep retention anchors alive:

| Ritual | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly baseline vs current scorecard | Monthly | Ops + Reporting |
| Day-30 install review | Once per account | Sales + Ops |
| Quarterly business review (QBR) | Quarterly | Operator lead |
| Cancellation save automation | Triggered (failed payment / cancel intent) | Ops |
| Win-back / dead-date push | Monthly per venue | DemandOS team |
