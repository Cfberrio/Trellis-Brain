# Post-Booking Communication Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: OEV post-booking communication follows a standardized sequence of 9 touchpoints (I01, I02, E01, S01, P01, P02, S02, S04, S06) across email and SMS, designed to provide only necessary information at each stage.
- **rule**: All OEV communications must follow a 'read once and understand' standard, using a warm and direct tone, numbering lists for tasks, and including a standardized reference block at the end of every email.
- **fact**: The Event Page (orlandoeventvenue.org/accesscode) is the canonical source for all guest-facing event information, including access instructions, door codes, Wifi, rules, checklists, and the Guest Report.
