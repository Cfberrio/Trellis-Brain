# Customer Communication Playbook

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: When replying to clients, open with a warm, casual greeting (e.g., 'Hi [Name]!', 'Good morning [Name]!') and use first-person 'you guys' to maintain a professionally warm tone.
- **rule**: Always answer specific logistical questions (size, hours, fees) with exact numbers and use checklists for scope (e.g., LED/AV packages) to ensure clarity and avoid ambiguity.
- **process**: For all quote requests, ask for the three mandatory booking inputs: date, start/end time, and expected headcount before confirming availability or pricing.
- **rule**: State all fees plainly (e.g., $199 cleaning fee, $199 setup/breakdown) and reframe over-capacity requests (e.g., 300 guests) by politely restating the 90-guest maximum instead of over-promising.
- **process**: Route standard bookings and tours to the website self-serve links; move complex production or catering quotes to a phone call to finalize details.
