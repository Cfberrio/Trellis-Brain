# Retention — Evidencia

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Retention for OEV is driven by predictable experiences, clear expectations, and fast rebooking processes.
- **fact**: Early churn indicators include lack of onboarding form completion, uncertainty regarding booking type/timeline, resistance to rule acknowledgement, payment friction, and unclear AV needs.
- **rule**: Expansion paths include upgrading to Daily for complex setups, adding production packages for AV/LED/streaming, and adding setup/breakdown or security services based on event risk.
- **process**: Referral mechanism: Ask for a review first, then a referral, triggered only after a successful post-event inspection. Rewards are provided as credit toward future bookings or add-ons.
