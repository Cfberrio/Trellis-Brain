## Market / Submarket
**Local event space rental (≤90 capacity) in Orlando.**

Submarkets we serve today: **corporate gatherings, workshops, celebrations.**

> Filter: prioritize submarkets where the event is time-bound (pain) + buyer has budget (purchasing power) + buyer is easy to reach in identifiable groups (easy to target) + market is growing.

## Competitor Set
- **Other local event venues** with similar capacity
- **Meeting rooms / multi-purpose spaces** that rent by the hour/day

## Decision Constraints — Non-Negotiables

### Minimum Buying Power Required
**Pricing floor (before add-ons):**
- Hourly minimum: 4 hours × $140/hr = **$560**
- Cleaning fee (required): **$199**
- True minimum to book hourly: **$759**
- Daily special (24-hour access): $899 + $199 cleaning = **$1,098**

**To block / hold the date:**
- 50% deposit required to reserve the date (based on total invoice: rental + cleaning + any add-ons)
- Remaining 50% due per reservation terms

### Minimum Urgency / Pain Required
Must have a **date-bound event** (corporate meeting/training, workshop, celebration) where the alternative is costly:
- scrambling for a compliant space
- reduced attendee experience
- reputational hit
- lost ticket/revenue (for workshops)
- event cancellation risk

The buyer must value **certainty + smooth execution** more than "cheapest possible room."

## Where Buyers Can Be Reached Reliably (channels / aggregation)
- **Corporate planners:** office admins, HR/ops coordinators, team leads (local business groups, professional communities, company admin networks)
- **Workshop hosts:** coaches/trainers/creators running in-person sessions (creator/education communities, workshop organizer lists, event-host networks)
- **Celebration planners:** people planning birthdays/anniversaries/showers (local community groups, event-planning communities, referral networks)

**Rule:** if a channel can't produce **consistent inquiries** from the 10–90 guest segment, it's not "reliable."

## Hard Disqualifiers (state upfront to prevent bad bookings)
- **Capacity cap:** 90 guests max
- **Alcohol:** beer & wine only (no liquor)
- **4-hour minimum** for hourly bookings
- **A/V usage requires a production package** (tech minimums apply)
- **Setup/breakdown expectations** must be accepted (overtime/restore fees apply if not)

## "We Win Here Because…"
We win in corporate/workshop events because we **reduce planning friction** (furnished room + prep kitchen + clear capacity) and **increase likelihood of a smooth event** via optional staffed production (AV/LED/streaming), while keeping the format flexible (hourly or 24-hour access).

> Value Equation translated into venue language: increase dream outcome + likelihood; decrease effort/time.
