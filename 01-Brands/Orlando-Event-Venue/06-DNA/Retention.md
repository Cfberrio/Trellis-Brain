## Retention Driver — what ongoing value keeps them
For a venue, "retention" = **repeat bookings + referrals**.

Retention drivers:
- **Predictable experience** (no surprises)
- **Clear expectations** (rules + costs + timelines)
- **Fast rebooking** (make it easy to repeat)

## Churn Triggers — what predicts cancellation early
Use churn inversion: ask "what would make them leave?" then do the opposite.

Venue translation (early cancellation predictors):
- **They haven't replied** to the onboarding form (missing details = low commitment)
- **They haven't chosen Hourly/Daily or confirmed timeline** (uncertainty)
- **They resist rule acknowledgement** (future fee conflict)
- **Final 50% not planned** (payment friction)
- **AV needs unclear**

## Expansion Paths

### Upsell Rules (increase spend)
| Trigger | Move |
|---|---|
| Setup/teardown heavy OR they want long access OR multiple event segments | **Upgrade to Daily** ($899 + cleaning) |
| AV/LED/streaming required | **Production package** (reduce failure risk) |
| Client wants convenience OR tight timeline | **Setup/Breakdown service** ($100 flat) |
| Event type or headcount = higher risk | **Security service** (when needed) |

### Cross-Sell Rules (sell additional thing)
- Tablecloths ($5 each + $25 cleaning)
- Paid setup
- Add-on tech hours
- Extra cleaning services

> Only when it supports their stated outcome.

### Downsell Rules (save "no's")
- **Personal events promo** (e.g., baby showers) only under predefined conditions
- The downsell exists to turn "nothing" into "something" (downsell quantity/quality)

## Referral Mechanism (when/how)
- **Trigger:** Post-Event inspection passes (or event success confirmed)
- **Ask:** review first, then referral second (same workflow)
- **Reward:** credit toward future booking or add-on credit

> Full structure → see [[Referral-System|Referral System]].

## LTV Levers (Hormozi's Crazy 8 — actively use, not just measure)

| # | Lever | OEV application |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Increase price | Periodic price tests (planned, not random) |
| 2 | Decrease cost | Staffing efficiency + standardized checklists |
| 3 | Cross-sell | Add-ons (setup / tablecloths / security) |
| 4 | Buy again | Corporate recurring "meeting block" offer after success |
| 5 | Buy more at same time | Push Daily when it fits |
| 6 | Buy a better version | Production packages (Basic → LED → Workshop) |
| 7 | Downsell quantity | Smaller personal-event options to avoid losing the deal |
| 8 | Downsell quality | Budget version to salvage price objections |
