- 50% payment to lock the date. Luis explicitly avoids the word "deposit" — frames it as community trust, not a credit-card hold.

### Non-Profit Discount
- $199 discount = effectively waives the cleaning fee.
- Offered on weekend dates to mission-aligned non-profits.
- Note: Communication Manual previously documented a separate "50% off base rental Mon–Fri" non-profit discount. These appear to be two separate offers (weekday rental discount vs. weekend cleaning-fee waiver). **Needs reconciliation with Luis.**

### Catering
- Zero restrictions. Customer can bring any caterer.

### Alcohol
- Non-profit identity is the canonical reason. Phrase: "we have to stand by our principles and our structure."
- Customers may bring water, sodas, non-alcoholic drinks. Beer/wine only through approved vendor/bar package.

### Production
- Customer-facing reason: "we have to pay the actual producer" — not framed as upsell.
- Customer selects production package on website; producer handles event day.

### Outdoor Patio
- Under construction. Permitted. Not photographed yet.
- Capacity: 20–30 people comfortably.
- Mention as a near-future amenity, do not promise availability for current bookings.

### Calendar
- Booked through August 2026. Real scarcity — use as urgency lever (with date stamp; refresh monthly).

### Connect-Call Play
- For higher-stakes inquiries (multi-day, mission-aligned, corporate), Luis offers to bring the founder/decision-maker live onto the call for credibility.

## Communication Patterns Observed (codified in Call-Communication-Approach)
1. Host posture, not salesperson posture.
2. Live-quote on the website instead of explaining pricing from memory.
3. Identity framing first ("we operate for non-profits, so we are non-profit").
4. Use customer's title/identity respectfully ("doctor," honoring the prophet/ministry roles).
5. Reframe "deposit" away — community trust, not credit-card hold.
6. Soft-sell production as transparent cost pass-through.
7. Mirror customer values (religious customer → "amen, amen"; community customer → community language).
8. Story-based credibility ("I did this yesterday with another person").
9. Give micro-permissions ("feel free," "you guys can").
10. Say no with structure, not apology ("we have to stand by our principles").
11. Real urgency from real scarcity ("we're booked through August").
12. Close warm ("I love the heart you have… I really love your spirit").
