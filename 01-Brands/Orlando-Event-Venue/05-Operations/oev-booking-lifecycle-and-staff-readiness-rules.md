# OEV Booking Lifecycle and Staff Readiness Rules

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: A booking only transitions to 'Pre-Event Ready' status when three conditions are met: the checklist is complete, at least one staff member is assigned, and every assigned staff member has explicitly approved their attendance for that specific event.
- **rule**: Automatic rejection of staff invitations due to non-response is disabled. Invitations with no response remain in a pending state.
