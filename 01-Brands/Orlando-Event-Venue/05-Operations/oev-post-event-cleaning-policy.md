# OEV Post-Event Cleaning Policy

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: A cleaning fee is charged separately from the base rent for every booking.
- **rule**: Clients must vacate the venue within 1 hour of the event's conclusion.
- **process**: Staff must inspect the venue and update the booking status to 'Post-Event' once cleaning is complete.
- **rule**: Any damages identified during inspection are documented in the booking report and deducted from the security deposit.
