# OEV Venue Layouts

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: Standard Table Layout: 6-foot rectangular tables placed separately, seating up to 8 guests per table; best for dining with space between groups.
- **definition**: Banquet-Style Layout: Tables connected in long rows for an elegant, cohesive look; ideal for showers and family-style events.
- **definition**: Classroom Style Layout: Tables and chairs in rows facing the stage/screen; best for workshops and presentations.
- **definition**: Theater Style Layout: Chairs only in rows facing the stage/screen; best for conferences and ceremonies.
- **definition**: Mixed Layout: Combination of seating and open space for mingling, activities, or dancing.
