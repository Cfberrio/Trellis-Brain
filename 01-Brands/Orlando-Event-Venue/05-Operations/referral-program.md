# Referral Program

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: OEV operates a referral program for partners with specific qualification criteria (new lead, fits venue constraints, 50% deposit paid, event completed), reward tiers (Corporate/Workshop vs. Personal), and capacity throttles (monthly/program caps, green inventory rules).
- **rule**: Referral rewards are earned upon deposit payment but paid 7 days after the event closes and passes inspection. No payout is issued for cancelled events or chargebacks.
- **rule**: Referral rewards are subject to stacking rules: they cannot be combined with other discounts (e.g., Baby Shower Content Credit) unless approved.
