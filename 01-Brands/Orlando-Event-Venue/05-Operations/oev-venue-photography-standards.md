# OEV Venue Photography Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Venue photography for 3D tours requires wide horizontal panoramas (3:2 to 16:9 ratio) shot from the center of each room at eye level (~1.5m) with all lights on and HDR enabled.
- **rule**: Photography technical specs: Minimum 3000px width (4000-8000px ideal), JPEG max quality or RAW/HEIC, leveled horizon, and no ultra-wide 360-degree iPhone sweeps (limit to 100-120 degrees).
- **process**: Standard venue shot list: Exterior (with address 3847 E Colonial Dr), Welcome Area, Main Entrance, Presentation Setup (projectors on), Event Space, Prep Kitchen, Storage, and Restrooms.
- **rule**: Photo delivery must be via original files on Google Drive; WhatsApp is prohibited due to quality compression.
