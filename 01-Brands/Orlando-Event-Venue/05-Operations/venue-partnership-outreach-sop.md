# Venue Partnership Outreach SOP

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: OEV conducts outreach to larger venues (150-200+ capacity) to establish partnerships where OEV refers over-capacity leads in exchange for a kickback.
- **rule**: When pitching partnerships, staff must lead with the partnership opportunity rather than software, emphasizing that OEV has paid leads they cannot accommodate due to size constraints.
- **process**: The outreach workflow involves qualifying the venue's capacity, handling objections about lead sharing, and booking an in-person meeting (preferred) or remote meeting on the Reliable Venues schedule in GHL.
- **fact**: OEV receives approximately 15-20 leads daily, which serves as the primary justification for seeking partners to handle overflow.
