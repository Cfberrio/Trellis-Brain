# Venue Partnership Qualification and Outreach

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: When OEV receives a lead too large for its capacity (sub-90 guests), the lead is referred to a partner venue to maintain the relationship and open doors for future collaboration.
- **rule**: Target partner venues must be located in or near Orlando, have a capacity above 100 guests (ideally 150-200+), and be legitimate event spaces like ballrooms, historic venues, or hotels.
- **rule**: Outreach should skip production companies, catering-only companies, and event planners that do not own/operate their own event space.
- **process**: The qualification workflow involves searching the venue on Google to verify capacity and location before moving them to a 'Qualified' status in the CRM/GoHighLevel pipeline.
