## Booking & Availability

**Q: How do I book Orlando Event Venue?**
A: Request a booking through our website. After submitting your request and paying the 50% deposit, our team reviews the details and confirms the booking.

**Q: Is my date guaranteed as soon as I inquire?**
A: Not yet. Dates are secured after the deposit is received and our team confirms your booking.

**Q: How long does confirmation take after I pay the deposit?**
A: Typically within ~24 hours. We verify timing, capacity, and venue readiness so your confirmation is accurate.

**Q: Can I tour the venue before booking?**
A: Yes — if tours are available. Tell us your preferred days/times and we'll share options.

**Q: Can I hold a date without paying the deposit?**
A: No. A deposit is required to secure a date.

**Q: Do you accept last-minute bookings?**
A: Yes, depending on availability. If your event is soon, we may send your balance payment link immediately after the deposit.

**Q: What information do you need to confirm my booking?**
A: Event date, start/end time, estimated guest count, event type, and any special needs (vendors, setup help, add-ons).

**Q: Do you host kids' events?**
A: Yes — just ensure the guest count stays within capacity and rules are followed.

**Q: Do you host adult birthdays/celebrations?**
A: Yes — subject to house rules and capacity limits.

**Q: Can I book multiple dates?**
A: Yes. We can help you schedule multiple reservations based on availability.

## Payments (Deposit + Balance)

**Q: How much is the deposit?**
A: The deposit is 50% of your total.

**Q: When is the remaining balance due?**
A: The remaining 50% is due 15 days before your event date.

**Q: How do I pay the remaining balance?**
A: We send a secure Stripe payment link by email when your balance is due.

**Q: Will you remind me about the balance payment?**
A: Yes. If payment isn't completed, reminders may be sent with a new secure link.

**Q: Does the payment link expire?**
A: Yes — Stripe links typically expire for security. If it expires, you'll receive a new one.

**Q: I paid — how do I know it went through?**
A: Your booking updates automatically. If you want confirmation, reply to the email and we'll verify.

**Q: Can I split payments?**
A: Generally it's deposit (50%) + remaining balance (50%). If you need a special arrangement, ask us.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Stripe checkout supports major credit/debit cards and other methods depending on your device/bank.

## Venue Basics (What's Included)

**Q: What's included with my booking?**
A: Capacity up to 90 guests, 10 tables, 90 chairs, a prep kitchen, and 2 full bathrooms.

**Q: What is the maximum capacity?**
A: Maximum occupancy is 90 guests.

**Q: Do you provide tablecloths, decor, or setup services?**
A: Add-ons may be available. Ask what's currently offered and we'll share options/pricing.

**Q: Do you have a kitchen?**
A: We have a prep kitchen (great for staging food). Cooking on-site may require approval.

**Q: Do you have Wi-Fi?**
A: Yes. Wi-Fi details are provided in your access instructions.

**Q: Is the venue wheelchair accessible?**
A: Ask us and we'll confirm the best entry route and any accommodations.

**Q: Is parking available?**
A: Yes, parking is available nearby. If you expect many cars, tell us and we'll advise.

## Time, Setup & Breakdown

**Q: Can I arrive early to set up?**
A: Setup and breakdown must stay within your booked time unless you book additional time.

**Q: What happens if we go overtime?**
A: Overtime/late cleanup fees may apply. We recommend planning setup and breakdown carefully.

**Q: How much time should I plan for setup/breakdown?**
A: Plan enough time within your booking window so you're not rushed. If you need extra time, ask us to extend your booking.

**Q: Can I change my event time after booking?**
A: Possibly — subject to availability. Reply to your booking email with the change request.

## Rules & Restrictions (Most Asked)

**Q: Is alcohol allowed?**
A: Alcohol is not allowed unless you have written approval. Violations can trigger fees and enforcement.

**Q: Is smoking allowed?**
A: No. Smoking is prohibited.

**Q: Are drugs allowed?**
A: No. Drugs are strictly prohibited.

**Q: Are pets allowed?**
A: No pets. Service animals may be allowed with prior authorization.

**Q: Is glitter/confetti/rice allowed?**
A: No. These items are prohibited because they cause heavy cleanup and can trigger fees.

**Q: Can we use candles/open flame?**
A: Open flames are not allowed unless specifically approved.

**Q: Can we use nails/staples/tape on walls?**
A: No nails or staples. Avoid residue-heavy tape. If you have a décor plan, ask us first.

**Q: Can we play loud music / use a DJ?**
A: Amplified music is allowed within local noise limits. Keep volume respectful.

**Q: Do events have a hard end time?**
A: Yes — events must end by the agreed booking time (and before any local quiet hours requirements).

## Vendors, Catering & Food

**Q: Can I bring my own food?**
A: Yes, commercially prepared food is typically fine.

**Q: Can I cook on-site?**
A: On-site cooking is not allowed unless pre-approved.

**Q: Can I hire a caterer?**
A: Yes. Professional caterers may be required to provide liability insurance depending on setup.

**Q: Can I bring a bartender?**
A: Alcohol policies still apply. Ask us before planning anything involving alcohol service.

## Access & Day-Of Info

**Q: How do I get access to the venue on event day?**
A: We send access instructions about 72 hours before your event so you have the most accurate entry details.

**Q: Will you send a door code / lockbox instructions?**
A: Access instructions are provided closer to the event for security.

**Q: What should I do when I arrive?**
A: Arrive a few minutes early, review access instructions, and plan setup inside your booking window.

**Q: Who do I contact if I'm locked out or need help?**
A: Reply to your email thread or contact support using the support details provided in your emails.

## Changes, Cancellations, Rescheduling

**Q: Can I reschedule my booking?**
A: Possibly — subject to availability. Reply with your preferred new date/time.

**Q: What is your cancellation policy?**
A: Policies can vary. Reply to your booking email and we'll provide the policy that applies to your reservation.

**Q: Can I transfer my booking to someone else?**
A: Ask us — this may be possible with proper verification.

## Host Report (After the Booking)

**Q: What is the Host Report?**
A: A quick 1–2 minute form completed after your booking ends to document the venue condition and report any issues.

**Q: When do I complete the Host Report?**
A: After your booking ends (not at the beginning).

**Q: Why is the Host Report required?**
A: It helps close out your reservation, confirm the venue condition, and resolve issues quickly if anything happened.

**Q: How do I access the Host Report link?**
A: We send it by email as your event approaches and again near event day.

## Cleaning & Damage

**Q: Do we need to clean before we leave?**
A: You're expected to follow basic cleanup expectations and return items to the proper layout within your booked time.

**Q: What happens if something is damaged?**
A: The guest is responsible for damages. Minimum charges may apply depending on severity.

**Q: What if we need extra cleaning help?**
A: Ask us ahead of time — additional services may be available.

## Support & Communication

**Q: Can I reply to the emails and reach a real person?**
A: Yes — reply to the email thread and our team will help.

**Q: I didn't get an email — what should I do?**
A: Check spam/junk folders first. Then reply or contact support and we'll resend.

**Q: What should I do if my details changed (guest count, timing, add-ons)?**
A: Reply to your most recent email. We'll update your booking and confirm back.

## Quick Objection Handling (good for Voice Agent)

**Q: Why can't I send invites right after I pay the deposit?**
A: Because we still need to confirm details (timing/capacity/readiness). Waiting prevents confusion if anything needs adjusting.

**Q: What makes Orlando Event Venue a good choice?**
A: It's designed for smooth hosting — tables/chairs included, strong capacity for celebrations, clear rules, and guided communication so you're not guessing what's next.

**Q: I'm worried about hidden fees.**
A: We keep rules and penalties clear upfront (no glitter/confetti, no smoking, no drugs, capacity limits). If you're unsure, ask before the event — our goal is preventing problems, not charging fees.
