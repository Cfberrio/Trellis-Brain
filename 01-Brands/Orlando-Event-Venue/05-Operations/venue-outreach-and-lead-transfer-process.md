# Venue Outreach and Lead Transfer Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: When OEV receives a lead that exceeds venue capacity (typically >90-100 guests), the lead should be transferred to a partner venue to maintain the relationship and open doors for future collaboration.
- **rule**: Target partner venues must be located in Orlando or nearby, have a capacity of 150–200+ guests, and be legitimate event spaces (ballrooms, hotels, historic venues).
- **rule**: Outreach should skip non-venue entities such as production companies, catering-only businesses, or event planners without dedicated space.
- **process**: The outreach workflow involves searching venues via Google/Excel, verifying capacity and location, qualifying the contact in the CRM, and booking a discovery meeting.
