# OEV Trash-Bag Signage — Poster Production Spec

## Parent
- [[../OEV-Staff-Operations-Console|OEV Staff Operations Console]]

## Related
- [[../OEV-Admin-Command-Center|OEV Admin Command Center]]

## Purpose
Print-ready instructions to create **three small posters** that run the trash-bag loop at the venue. Hand this doc to whoever builds the signs (Canva, a print shop, or a label maker). Each poster below gives you: where it goes, the exact words to print, and the layout/print notes.

> **Assumption (labeled):** Posters are **bilingual English + Spanish** because the cleaning/event crew is mixed-language. If everyone reads English only, drop the Spanish lines — the layouts still work. — *grounded assumption, confirm with Luis*

## The Loop (why there are 3 signs)
The three signs teach one cycle so nobody has to be told twice:

1. **At the trash can** → take the full bag out, put a new bag in.
2. **At the patio door** → drop the full bag here.
3. **At the kitchen drawer** → grab a new bag here.

Trash can → patio door → drawer → back to trash can. Print all three or the loop breaks.

---

## Poster 1 — Trash Can ("empty it, re-line it")

**Where it goes:** On or directly above each trash can — eye level, where someone changing the bag will see it.
**Quantity:** One per trash can.

**Exact copy to print:**

> ### THROW THE TRASH AWAY — PUT A NEW BAG IN
> *Bote la basura — ponga una bolsa nueva*
>
> 1. Tie the full bag and take it out
> 2. Drop it at the patio door
> 3. Put a fresh bag in the can
>
> *Bolsas nuevas: cajón de la cocina*
> **New bags: kitchen drawer**

**Layout / design notes:**
- Orientation: **portrait**, size **8.5 × 11 in (Letter)**.
- Big bold headline (top third), the 3 numbered steps in the middle, the "new bags location" line at the bottom.
- Optional icon row: 🗑️ → 🚪 → 🗄️ (full bag out → door → drawer) to mirror the loop.
- High contrast: black text on white, or white text on the OEV accent color.

---

## Poster 2 — Patio Door ("full bags go here")

**Where it goes:** On the door to the **other patio** — the drop-off point for dirty/full bags. Stick it at eye level on the door itself.
**Quantity:** One.

**Exact copy to print:**

> ### FULL TRASH BAGS GO HERE
> *Las bolsas de basura llenas van aquí*
>
> Leave tied bags on this side.
> Do not leave bags anywhere else.
>
> *Deje las bolsas amarradas de este lado.*

**Layout / design notes:**
- Orientation: **portrait**, **8.5 × 11 in**.
- Headline does the work — make it the largest element. A down-arrow ⬇️ or a tied-bag icon under the headline reads instantly.
- Consider a colored border (red or OEV accent) so it's obvious from across the room that this door = the drop spot.

---

## Poster 3 — Kitchen Drawer ("new bags live here")

**Where it goes:** On the front of the **specific drawer** in the kitchen that holds the new/clean trash bags.
**Quantity:** One.

**Exact copy to print:**

> ### NEW TRASH BAGS ARE IN THIS DRAWER
> *Las bolsas de basura nuevas están en este cajón*
>
> Take one, re-line the can, close the drawer.
>
> *Tome una, forre el bote, cierre el cajón.*

**Layout / design notes:**
- Orientation: **landscape**, sized to the drawer face — **half-Letter (8.5 × 5.5 in)** works for most drawers; size up/down to fit.
- Headline + one action line is enough. A small drawer/bag icon helps.
- If the drawer is small, drop the action line and keep only the headline in both languages.

---

## How To Produce

1. **Build:** Canva is fastest — one document, three pages (Poster 1 & 2 = Letter portrait, Poster 3 = half-Letter landscape). Reuse the same font, color, and icon set across all three so they read as a set.
2. **Brand it:** OEV logo small in a corner. Keep it clean and operational — these are crew signs, not customer-facing.
3. **Print:** Color, 8.5 × 11 cardstock for 1 & 2; cut Poster 3 to drawer size.
4. **Protect:** **Laminate** all three (or slide into a clear sleeve). They live near trash, water, and hands — bare paper won't last.
5. **Mount:** Command strips or clear packing tape on all edges so they don't peel.
