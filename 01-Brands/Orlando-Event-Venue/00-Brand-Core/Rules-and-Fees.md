## Purpose
Canonical venue rules and the fees charged for non-compliance. Source of truth for sales calls, contracts, post-booking emails, and dispute resolution.

## Rules + Fees

| # | Rule | Fee for Non-Compliance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | All alcohol runs through our in-house bar service. No outside alcohol, no outside bartenders, no BYOB. Guests must be 21+ to consume. | $500. Event may be terminated without refund if severe. |
| 2 | No drugs on the property. | $500 + immediate termination. Law enforcement may be notified. |
| 3 | No smoking or vaping indoors or in the immediate outdoor surroundings. | $500 cleaning/deodorizing. |
| 4 | No pets. Certified service animals welcome with documentation. | $250 cleaning. |
| 5 | Outside caterers OK with proof of liability insurance + health/safety compliance. Pre-packaged or chain-restaurant food OK. **No on-site cooking** — prep kitchen is staging + re-heating only. | $500 for unauthorized on-site cooking or unapproved caterers. |
| 6 | No glitter, confetti, rice, or sparklers. | $500 cleaning. |
| 7 | Setup + breakdown = 50% of booked time, combined. Event runs in the remaining 50%. | Overtime: $350/hour. Cleaning fee if not restored: $300. |
| 8 | Music + noise must stay within local ordinances. Doors closed after 9 PM. | $350. Event may be terminated without refund if severe. |
| 9 | Maximum occupancy: 90 guests. | $500. Local authorities may shut down event. |
| 10 | No nails, staples, or residue-leaving tape. No open flames or candles unless pre-approved. Use of stage, screens, lighting, or AV requires the matching production add-on. | $400 per violation. |
| 11 | Guests are liable for damage to venue, furniture, or equipment. | Repair/replacement at cost. $400 minimum for minor damage. |
| 12 | Chairs and tables: guests arrange and restore to original layout within setup/breakdown time. | $400 if not restored. |

## Enforcement
- Interior + entry cameras monitor the event.
- Post-event camera review may trigger penalties.
- Noise sensors track sound levels.
- Severe violations (drugs, repeated alcohol breach, occupancy overage, severe noise) may terminate the event without refund.
