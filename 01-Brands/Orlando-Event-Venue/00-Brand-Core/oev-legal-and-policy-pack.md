# OEV Legal and Policy Pack

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: A reservation is held only after the required initial payment is received and OEV sends written confirmation; quotes or unpaid checkouts do not hold dates.
- **rule**: Standard payment schedule: 50% due at booking, remaining 50% due 15 days before the event. If booked within 15 days, full balance is due at booking.
- **rule**: Alcohol may only be served through a licensed and insured bartender approved by OEV; no self-service or BYOB is permitted.
- **rule**: Unapproved overtime is charged at $350 per hour, rounded up to the next hour.
- **rule**: Venue monitoring includes video surveillance and noise sensors; sensors measure sound levels but do not record conversations.
