# OEV Constraints

## Parent
- [[01-Brands/Orlando-Event-Venue/00-Brand-Core/Brand-Home|Orlando Event Venue - Brand Home]]

## Related

## Hard Constraints
- Maximum capacity: 90 guests.
- Hourly bookings require a 4-hour minimum.
- Bar service is available as a paid add-on for all private events. OEV coordinates the service; the client selects a package and pays as part of the booking. No outside alcohol or outside bartenders permitted.
- Technical equipment usage requires the relevant production package.
- Setup and breakdown expectations must be accepted.
- The buyer must fit the minimum economics.

## Commercial Constraints
- Hourly floor starts at $759 including required cleaning.
- Daily floor starts at $1,098 including required cleaning.
- Optional add-ons increase total spend and should be framed early when relevant.

## Strategic Constraint
OEV should not chase leads that only want the cheapest empty room. The business is built around certainty, clarity, and reduced execution risk.

#### Multi-day Bookings (new constraint)

Multi-day events are entered as **separate reservations**, one per day. The system does not support a single combined multi-day reservation.

**Operational impact:**
- Each day has its own confirmation, payment, access instructions
- Each day has its own 50% hold
- Multi-day discounts (25% workshop) are applied to each base rental line individually
- Staff/cleaning is scheduled per reservation

**Sales rep rule:**
- When quoting multi-day, break down the cost per day in writing.
- When booking, walk the customer through each day's reservation separately, or book one and send the link for the others.

#### Calendar Capacity (date-stamped)

As of 2026-05-20, OEV is booked through August 2026. Use this as real-scarcity-driven urgency on calls, but **refresh the date stamp monthly** — stale urgency lines lose credibility.

**Rep rule:** before saying "we're booked through [month]," check the actual calendar state. Don't recite a stale fact.

#### Non-Profit Identity (operational constraint)

OEV operates as a non-profit and references this identity in customer-facing communication. This is not just a marketing positioning — it underpins:
- The alcohol policy (non-profit principles)
- The community framing (trust over deposits)
- The discount structure (non-profit discounts exist)

Reps must understand this identity is real and consistently apply it. Do not contradict the non-profit framing in customer communication.
