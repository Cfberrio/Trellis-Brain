Paid acquisition, the website as a conversion surface, and the lead magnet. This hub was previously listed as a deferred system in [[01-Brands/Orlando-Event-Venue/00-Brand-Core/Brand-Home|OEV Brand Home]]; it is now active because real campaign and website work happened between June and August 2026.

## Current state (2026-08-20)
- **Paid:** Google Ads Performance Max ran in June 2026 and failed for measurable, documented reasons. No new spend until the blockers in the post-mortem are cleared.
- **Site:** rebuilt as a one-page in July 2026, styled on the DR structure with OEV branding, with legacy routes redirected to anchors.
- **Lead magnet:** Event Planning Kit with the PLAN50 offer, replacing HOST100 / SAVE100 / SAVE50, live since 2026-08-04.
- **Instrumentation:** GA4 purchase conversion now fires on booking confirmation. Before July 2026, no conversion event existed at all.

## Standing rule
Never buy traffic into a funnel whose capture path has not been tested in production that week. The June campaign paid for visitors while the popup silently rejected every submission for six weeks.
