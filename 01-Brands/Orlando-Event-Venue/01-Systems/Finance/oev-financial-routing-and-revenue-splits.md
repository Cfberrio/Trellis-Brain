# OEV Financial Routing and Revenue Splits

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Payments are processed via Stripe Connect with automated splits: Base Rent incurs a 20% software/management fee to RV, with the remainder to D’Space.

- **rule**: Food & Beverage (F&B) payments incur an 8% fee, with funds routed to D’Loft instead of the D’Space account.
