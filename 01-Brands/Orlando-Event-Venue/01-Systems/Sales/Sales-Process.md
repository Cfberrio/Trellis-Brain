# OEV Sales Process

## Parent
- [[01-Brands/Orlando-Event-Venue/01-Systems/Sales/Sales-Home|OEV Sales Home]]

## Related
- [[01-Brands/Orlando-Event-Venue/01-Systems/Finance/Payment-Rules|OEV Payment Rules]]

## Core Sequence
1. Capture contact and event details.
2. Confirm fit: date, guest count, event type, rules.
3. Confirm availability or closest alternatives.
4. Send booking / quote path.
5. Ask for 50% deposit when qualified.
6. Confirm booking.
7. Select add-ons.
8. Hand off to pre-event ops.
9. Capture proof after event.

## Standard Principle
Do not let the lead remain in vague interest. Move them to a binary next step.

### 2026-05-20 — Sales technique additions from Luis × Non-Profit Inquiry call

Source: [[01-Brands/Orlando-Event-Venue/04-Training/Source-Calls/2026-05-20_Luis_NonProfit_Inquiry|Source Call 2026-05-20]]

Full play-by-play in [[01-Brands/Orlando-Event-Venue/04-Training/Call-Communication-Approach|Call Communication Approach]] — this is the operational summary.

#### Live-Quote-on-Website Technique (canonical sales move)

Instead of explaining pricing from memory, walk the customer through the live website in real time, build a placeholder booking with their specs, and read the total off the screen.

**Workflow:**
1. Say: "What I'll do is — I'm going to our website right now and pick a random date close to yours."
2. Open the OEV website.
3. Pick a placeholder date.
4. Build the booking — daily or hourly, plus their add-ons.
5. Walk them to the total on screen.
6. Read: "That number is what you'd pay for [the daily / X hours] with [add-ons]."
7. Send the email recap with the breakdown after the call.

**Why:** removes pricing doubt, makes the website the source of truth, gives the customer a real number not a quote.

#### Multi-Day Booking Pattern (new operational rule)

Multi-day events = separate reservations, one per day.

**Rep workflow:**
1. Confirm which days the customer wants.
2. Explain the separate-reservation structure: "Each day goes in as its own booking. You'd have access for the full day on each one."
3. Quote each day separately, then sum.
4. If non-profit, apply the matching discount per day (weekday vs. weekend).
5. After the call, email a clean breakdown with each day's reservation listed.

**Customer-facing line (from Luis):**
> "If you want Thursday, Friday, and Saturday — those go in as three separate bookings. You'd do them individually. You'd have access for the full day on each one if you do the daily rate."

#### Connect-Call Play (new sales technique)

For high-stakes inquiries, the rep offers to bring the founder onto the call live for credibility and final approval.

**When to deploy:**
- Multi-day booking
- Inquiry over ~$2,000 total
- Customer asks for the decision-maker
- Mission-aligned or partnership-tone conversation
- Non-profit asking for a custom discount

**When NOT to deploy:**
- Standard single-day birthday party
- Tour scheduling
- Routine pricing question

**Customer-facing line (from Luis):**
> "You can call him and connect the call. I actually did that yesterday with another customer who was trying to figure out a multi-day."

#### Soft-Sell Production as Cost Pass-Through (new framing rule)

When a customer needs AV, frame the production package as the cost of paying the producer — not as an upsell.

**Customer-facing line (from Luis):**
> "Not because I want to charge you guys — we have to pay the actual producer."

Reps should never use marketing language for production. Just the cost-transparency frame.

#### Identity-First Framing (new opening pattern)

For mission-aligned, non-profit, religious, community, or partnership-tone customers, lead with OEV's identity before pricing.

**Customer-facing line (from Luis):**
> "We operate for non-profits — we are a non-profit. We do a lot of community work."

Pricing comes AFTER the identity is established. Identity-first changes the entire conversation tone.

#### Updated Sales Flow (post-call)

1. **Open warm.** First name, calm tone.
2. **Get the basics.** Event type, day(s), time, guest count.
3. **Identity-frame** if customer is mission-aligned or non-profit.
4. **Check availability live.**
5. **Use the website as the visual.** Ask: "Have you been to our website?"
6. **Live-quote on the site.** Build the booking in front of them.
7. **Stack add-ons** based on their needs (production, bar, setup, tablecloths).
8. **Apply the right discount** if it fits (non-profit, multi-day workshop, $100 closer).
9. **Soft-sell production** as cost pass-through if AV is needed.
10. **Address alcohol** with the non-profit identity frame if it comes up.
11. **Surface real urgency** (calendar truth).
12. **Connect-call** if high-stakes.
13. **Close warm.** Match their energy.
14. **Send post-call text within 5 min** with booking link.
15. **Send post-call email within 1 hour** with full quote breakdown.
16. **Tag customer in GHL** for commission tracking.
