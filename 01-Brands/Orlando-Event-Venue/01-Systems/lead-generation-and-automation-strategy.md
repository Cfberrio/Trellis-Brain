# Lead Generation and Automation Strategy

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The OEV lead magnet strategy uses two primary types: 'Reveal Problems' (diagnosis) and 'One Step of a Multi-Step Process' (availability/quote).
- **rule**: Scarcity and urgency (Floor 6) should only be used when specific conditions are met: hold expiring within 24h/6h, competing inquiry exists, tech resource is limited, or peak-date inventory is tight. Never use 'just because' urgency.
- **process**: The inbound call objective is to capture four fields (date/time, guest count, event type, A/V needs) and deliver the 'Instant Availability + Quote Pack' via SMS during the call.
