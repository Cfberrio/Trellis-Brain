# D Space Orlando — Partnership Pitch (2026-05-29)

## Parent
- [[../Partnerships-Home|Venue Partnerships — Home]]

## Context
D Space Orlando is a competing all-inclusive venue. Mutual interest established with G. Gonzalez. OEV is venue-only; D Space remains independent (their own venue, other venues, services elsewhere). This partnership runs on a single referral code and a services-at-OEV provision.

Internal truth (not for client-facing copy): OEV does not want to operate services. D Space does. When a D Space-referred client (or any OEV client) needs services, D Space is the preferred provider — provided they keep the service areas maintained.

## Deal Structure — Single Mechanism

### Code: **DSPACE**

When a customer books OEV with code DSPACE:
- **Customer:** 10% off base rental
- **D Space:** 10% commission on base rental (paid by OEV)
- Both percentages calculated on the same gross base rental amount (pre-discount).

### Math examples

| Booking | Base rental | Customer pays (–10%) | D Space commission (10%) | OEV net |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 hours | $560 | $504 | $56 | $448 |
| 6 hours | $840 | $756 | $84 | $672 |
| 1 day (24h) | $899 | $809.10 | $89.90 | $719.20 |
| 2-day workshop (25% off) | $1,348.50 | $1,213.65 | $134.85 | $1,078.80 |
| 3-day program (25% off) | $2,022.75 | $1,820.48 | $202.28 | $1,618.20 |

Commission paid within 7 business days of final balance + event closeout.

## Services at OEV

D Space may bring their services (catering, décor, planning, coordination, AV, beverage) to OEV's venue for any client — referred by them or not.

**Operational rule:** D Space is responsible for maintaining the service areas during and after the event. Any space used for service prep, plating, beverage setup, etc. must be returned in the same condition. Standard OEV venue rules apply (occupancy 90, no glitter/confetti, no open flames unless pre-approved, setup/breakdown within booking window).

D Space is not exclusive to OEV. They continue operating their own venue and any others. The partnership is non-exclusive on both sides.

## Tracking + Payout

- Customer uses code **DSPACE** at booking → attribution is automatic.
- OEV confirms attribution at deposit.
- Commission released within 7 business days after final balance is collected and event closes out.
- Monthly statement to D Space: referred bookings, status, payout amount, payout date.

## Boundaries

- Code applies to new OEV clients only (not previously contacted/in our system).
- One discount per booking — DSPACE does not stack with other promos.
- Discount and commission both apply to base rental only. Cleaning fee, processing fee, AV, beverage service, add-ons excluded.
- D Space's service areas at OEV must be maintained — non-negotiable.
