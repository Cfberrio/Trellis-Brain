# Customer Communication Playbook

Covers weddings, corporate, celebrations, private events — same core moves. Weddings were **0% of inbound** in the 90-day window; most demand is churches/ministries (recurring), baby showers, birthdays, graduations, kids' parties, and streamed events.

## The 3 booking inputs (memorize)
Any real quote or availability check needs all three. If missing, ask for them first:
1. **Date**
2. **Start / end time** (a time window)
3. **Expected headcount**

> Pattern: "Whenever you're ready, just share the date, start/end time, and expected headcount so I can confirm availability."

## Response arc (every conversation)
1. **Open warm** with their name.
2. **Answer the exact question** — number or ✓ list.
3. **Advance the booking** — supply the missing input, or route to orlandoeventvenue.org.
4. **State any fee plainly.**
5. **Set the next step** — book online / schedule tour / I'll call you.

## Routing rules
| Situation | Route to |
|---|---|
| Ready to book | orlandoeventvenue.org (apply code at checkout) |
| Wants to see the space | Website tour section → [[03-tour-follow-up]] |
| Complex AV / catering / large quote | Offer a **call**; draft quote; may escalate → [[07-escalation-rules]] |
| Site shows "unavailable" | Wrong site. "Our site is orlandoeventvenue.org — the other is a third-party page." |
| Alcohol / bartender | Licensed vetted vendor only (Chara). Non-Chara = $250 vetting → [[04-pricing-and-booking-scope]] |
| Dispute / refund / policy pushback | Keep brief, hand to human → [[07-escalation-rules]] |

## Reusable answers (verbatim-grade — pull facts from [[04-pricing-and-booking-scope]])

**Address**
> 3847 E Colonial Dr, Orlando, FL 32803. Colonial Town Center — look for the GLOBAL sign with #3847, door on the left, black lockbox.

**What's included**
> Every rental includes 10 tables (6 ft), 90 chairs, a prep kitchen, two full bathrooms, and wifi. We host up to 90 guests.

**Capacity reframe (over 90)**
> Thanks for letting us know! We can host up to 90 guests with 10 tables, 90 chairs, a prep kitchen, and two bathrooms included. If a future event lines up with that count, we'd love to host you.

**Weekday price (with code)**
> During the week it's $99/hour with a code I'll give you, plus the $199 cleaning fee.

**Standard price**
> It's $140/hour (4-hour minimum), or $899 for full-day 24-hour access. Cleaning fee is $199.

**Outside food**
> You can bring outside catering! The only restriction is alcohol — that has to be served by our licensed vendor.

**Streaming**
> We support streaming — Zoom link holds up to 100 online guests. What hours will you use for production?

## Known friction to pre-empt (be proactive)
- **Emails not arriving** (recap/code land in spam): if a client says they didn't get it, resend + give the code in-thread ("Discount code is SAVE100"). → [[03-tour-follow-up]]
- **Payment links expire fast (24–48h):** if expired, resend to their email immediately and confirm the address. → [[06-ghl-sms-workflow]]
- **Blueprint/dimensions:** diagrams are being updated after the expansion. Offer a tour to plan the layout. → [[03-tour-follow-up]]
- **Day-of logistics** (AC, door code, lights, wifi, trash): answer fast and concrete. → [[07-escalation-rules]] appendix.

## Tone floor
Never robotic, never a wall of policy. If the answer is long, break it into a ✓ list. If it's a dispute, it's short and a human takes it. See [[01-brand-voice]].
