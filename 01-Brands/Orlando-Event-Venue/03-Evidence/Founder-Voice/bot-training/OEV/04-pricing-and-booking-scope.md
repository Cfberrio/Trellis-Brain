# Pricing & Booking Scope — Source of Truth

## Reconciliation notes (read first)
- **Rate:** Standard **$140/hour (4-hour minimum)**. **$99/hour is a weekday promo unlocked by a code.** Bot quotes $140/hr unless a valid weekday code applies, then $99/hr.
- **Size:** **~1,830 sq ft** (space was recently expanded; diagrams/blueprints are being updated). Older "2,000 sq ft" figure is superseded.
- **Capacity:** **90 guests** (both sources agree).
- **Daily:** **$899** full-day 24-hour access (both sources agree).

**Worked example:** 9 pm–2 am = 5-hour block → 5 × $140 = $700 + $199 cleaning = **$899 total**.

## Deposit, balance, changes
- **Deposit secures the date.** No date is held without a reservation.
- **Remaining 50%** due ~2 weeks before the event.
- **Payment links expire in 24–48h** — resend on request (confirm the email first). → [[06-ghl-sms-workflow]]
- **Headcount changes** allowed up to **3 days before**. After that, **no refund** — inventory is already purchased.
- Credit not applied at checkout can be **refunded** after the fact (escalate the refund → [[07-escalation-rules]]).

## Alcohol & vendor policy (non-profit rule — non-negotiable)
- Alcohol **must be served by a licensed, vetted vendor**. Default vendor: **Chara Mobile Bar**.
- Want a different bartender? **$250 admin vetting fee** (because the venue is non-profit).
- **Outside food / catering is allowed.** Alcohol is the *only* restriction.
- Liquor must be **picked up by the vendor**, not dropped directly at the venue.

## AV / tech & media
- **Basic package:** microphones, speakers, projector, tech assistance.
- **LED package:** everything in Basic + **stage LED wall screen**. 6-hour block; team sets up, runs, and breaks down. Technician effectively acts as **DJ** from the client's playlist. Included: ✓ AV system ✓ mics ✓ speakers ✓ projectors ✓ tech assistant ✓ stage LED wall.
  - LED at $99/hr = **$594 for 6 hours**.
- **All AV equipment requires a hired producer/tech to operate** (min 3 hours).
- **Photo/Video:** videographer/photographer **$200/hour**, ~4h minimum (flexible to 2h).
- **Setup/teardown labor** (corporate/full-service): **$199 setup + $199 breakdown**.
- Missing on-site: **wireless mics** — can be brought from the main auditorium at no extra cost if coordinated in advance.

## Recurring / weekly bookings
- Supported (churches/ministries book weekly).
- Requests seen: ~$180/week ($720/mo) for Sunday-morning service blocks.
- **No standard weekly rate is published yet** → treat as a custom quote, escalate → [[07-escalation-rules]].
