# Verbatim Email Examples

Use these as the tone target for [[05-email-draft-workflow]]. Note where a reply **reframes**, **states a fee plainly**, or **routes to a call**.

## 1 — Full venue FAQ, over-capacity reframe (nonprofit dinner, 300 ppl)
**Client (Bianca):** [8-part FAQ — availability for 300 @ May 7 6–10pm, rental fees, nonprofit discount, F&B minimums, catering, AV, parking/ADA, deposit/cancellation.]
**OEV:**
> Hi Bianca, Thanks for letting us know! Totally understood. If a future event lines up with a smaller guest count, we can host up to 90 guests with 10 tables, 90 chairs, a prep kitchen, and two bathrooms included. Whenever you're ready, just share the date, start/end time, and expected headcount so I can confirm availability. Pricing is $140/hour (4-hour minimum) or $899 for full-day 24-hour access. You can also start a reservation anytime at https://orlandoeventvenue.org/book. Hope we get to host you soon. — Orlando Event Venue Team

→ *Reframes 300 → 90 max, gives the 3 inputs, quotes plainly, routes to /book.*

## 2 — Capacity/size/availability/floor protection (45 ppl, paint event)
**Client (Michelle):** multiple entertainment stations, 45 ppl, 24-hr hold for 9/8–9/10. Capacity & availability?
**OEV:** > Hi Michelle, All of your requested dates are available, and we can put the venue on a 24-hour hold for you. I would suggest you book the daily special online. Here's the website https://orlandoeventvenue.org
**Client:** How large is the space?
**OEV:** > Hi Michelle! 2,000 sq feet. Since you mentioned 45 people, we definitely have room for you guys. *(Note: current bot answer is ~1,830 sq ft — see [[04-pricing-and-booking-scope]].)*
**Client:** Date change — 9/15,16,17,29,30, 10/1 available?
**OEV:** > All of the dates that you requested are available. We do have some internal meetings taking place, but they can be moved depending on what you guys need. Happy to help
**Client:** Visqueen on the floors OK? (paint may be involved)
**OEV:** > The Visqueen itself typically should not damage the floor, but can you share what it would be used for? … No big deal on our end, that's fine. Although we are about to renovate the floors and walls this week — would be a bummer if anything gets messed up, but I'm sure it'll be fine as long as you guys are responsible for a solution. We can also invoice you guys if needed.

→ *Answers, asks a clarifying Q before OK'ing risk, protects the venue without killing the deal.*

## 3 — Corporate AV spec + F&B quote (TaxDome, 100 ppl)
**Client (Olha):** full AV spec + F&B for 100, Sept 4. Asks for a cost estimate.
**OEV:** > Please find the estimate attached including everything below per your request. The only thing missing is the food and beverage. If our venue is still an option, I'll send pricing for F&B.
**OEV (gaps + fee, honest):** > The only thing we don't currently have is the wireless microphones, which — if you approve the quote — I'll request from our main auditorium at no additional cost. We just need to coordinate in advance. Full labor for setup and teardown also needs to be added: $199 setup + $199 breakdown.
**OEV (availability):** > Here is our calendar for September. A lot of availability as we are booked two months in advance.

→ *Itemized estimate, honest gap + free fix, fees plain, offers a call.* Escalation candidate → [[07-escalation-rules]].

## 4 — "What does the LED Package include?" (audio/DJ)

The technician attending the event will essentially be the DJ — he can move songs along from the playlist you provide, or take guest recommendations if you're open to that. Let me know if you have other questions.

→ *✓ checklist, resolves the DJ question concretely.*

## 5 — Pricing + hours / time cutoff (late-night birthday)
**Client (Tnaija):** 25th birthday, 7/10. Hours? Is there a cutoff (9pm–2am)?
**OEV:** > The 7/10 is currently open. You can book the hourly rate, $140/hour (minimum 4 hours), or the daily rate, $899 for the whole day.
**OEV:** > Yes, 9 pm to 2 am is fine. That's a 5-hour block. Total would be $700 ($140/hr × 5) + $199 cleaning fee. Let me know if you want to lock that in.

→ *Confirms the block, does the math, states the fee, asks for the close.*

## 8 — Lead responsiveness (grandmother's 75th, 75 ppl)
**Client (Michael):** 75 ppl, 1 bar, outside food, dance floor, possible DJ + presentation.
**OEV:** > Hi Michael! Perfect, will reach out today
