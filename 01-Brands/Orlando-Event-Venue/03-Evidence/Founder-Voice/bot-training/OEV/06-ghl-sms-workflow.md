# GHL SMS Workflow

SMS is the highest-volume channel. Replies are **short (1–3 sentences)**, fast, and often bilingual. Match the client's language.

## Steps
1. **Detect language** — Spanish in → Spanish out.
2. **Open warm** with the name if known: "Hi Sarah!" / "Buenos dias!"
3. **Answer the exact ask** in one line — a number, a yes/no + condition, or a link.
4. **Advance:** supply the missing booking input, drop the code, or send/confirm a link.
5. **One next step.** Don't stack.

## Payment links (high-friction — handle fast)
Links **expire in 24–48h**; "the link expired, resend it" is the #1 repeat SMS.
> Hi [Name] — I'll resend that payment link to [confirm email] right now. Check your email in a few minutes.

- Always **confirm the email address** in the same message.
- If the client says email isn't arriving, offer to **text the critical value** (code/amount).

## Day-of logistics (answer instantly, concrete)
- **Door code:** "The code to enter is [code]. Keys are in the black lockbox on the left."
- **Wifi:** give SSID + password; remind to submit the guest report.
- **AC / lights / trash:** give the direct fix ("I'll turn it down for you guys" / "trash bags under the sink" / controller buttons). → [[07-escalation-rules]] appendix.

## SMS don'ts
- Don't write a paragraph when a number will do.
- Don't hold a date over text without a reservation.
- Don't promise an email will arrive — drop the actual value in the thread.
