# Tours & Follow-Up

## How tours work at OEV
- **Self-access tours** are booked by the client on the website tour section + a live video call.
- After a tour, OEV sends a **recap email with a discount code** (SAVE100 = $100 credit).
- Tour invites are sometimes **auto-sent** when a lead submits interest — clients confuse this with a reservation. Clear that up (see script).

## Scheduling a tour

**Client wants to book a tour**
> Hey [Name], hope you're doing well! Are you able to go to the tour section of the website and pick a date that works for you? If none of those work, let me know.

**Client asks for a specific/soonest slot**
> Absolutely — you're all set for [time] on [day]. Pull up to the entrance and call 407-974-5979 when you arrive; we'll meet you inside. 3847 E Colonial Dr, Orlando, FL 32803.

**Same-day / "can I come today?"**
> Sure! [Confirm a specific time.] Call 407-974-5979 when you arrive.

**Complex event wants to see the space** (conference + gala, streaming, AV)
> When would you guys like to come tour the venue? Your requested dates are currently available. I'll give you a call tomorrow, or if you arrive today let me know. → likely escalate to a call, [[07-escalation-rules]].

## Clearing the tour-vs-reservation confusion
Client thinks a tour booking = a venue reservation:
> My apologies — the tour invite was sent automatically when you submitted interest. To actually hold your date you reserve at orlandoeventvenue.org. For your event on [date], we [have / don't have] availability from [window]; we could host [alternate window]. Want me to shift your reservation?

## Post-tour follow-up (the conversion moment)
After the tour, the recap + discount code go out by email. **Deliverability is the known failure point** — recaps and codes land in spam. Handle proactively:

**Client: "I didn't get the email / recap / code"**
> On the discount code — it's an automated email, I'll check what happened to it. The code is **SAVE100** (applies at checkout). I'll resend the recap to [confirm their email] now.

**Rules**
- Always **give the code in-thread** (SAVE100) rather than only promising an email.
- **Confirm the email address** out loud before resending.
- If a personalized code errors at checkout, it's usually a name typo in the system — fix and confirm: "Fixed! Your name had a typo on our system, should be good now."

## Tour-day logistics (quick answers)
- **Address:** 3847 E Colonial Dr — GLOBAL sign #3847, door on left, black lockbox.
- **On arrival:** call 407-974-5979.
- **Door / lockbox code:** issued per event (e.g. "code to enter is 06062026; keys in the black lockbox on the left").

## Next step after a good tour
> Very happy to hear! The code to apply during checkout is SAVE100. You can reserve anytime at orlandoeventvenue.org — the date isn't held until the reservation is in.

## Escalate when
- Client wants internal meetings moved to fit their tour/date → human (calendar override).
- Complex multi-part event (day conference + evening gala) → offer a call.
