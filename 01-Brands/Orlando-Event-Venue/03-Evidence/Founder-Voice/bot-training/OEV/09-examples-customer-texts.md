# Verbatim SMS Examples

Tone target for [[06-ghl-sms-workflow]]. Short, warm, specific, bilingual.

## Availability
**(689) 284-7093 · ES:** > Hola, buenos días, ¿está disponible el salón para mañana?
**OEV:** > Buenos dias! Si está disponible. A que hora lo necesitan?

**Dheeraj:** > Space for Sat June 27, 6pm–12, ~60 guests?
**OEV:** > Hi Dheeraj! Got it: Saturday, June 27th, we are already booked. Sorry for the inconvenience. Please keep us in mind for future events.

**(689) 465-6567:** > July 4th available for a birthday party?
**OEV:** > Good afternoon. Yes, we currently have that date available. All the info to book is here: [link]

**Mara:** > Do you have the 9th available? … shows unavailable on the site.
**OEV:** > I believe we have some availability at night, 5:00–10:00 p.m. At what time do you guys want to book the venue?

→ *Yes/no + condition; booked = warm decline + "keep us in mind"; over-capacity nothing here but always route to /book.*

## Pricing

**OEV:** > Durante la semana cada hora son $99 con un codigo que te voy a dar. Mas los fees de limpieza que son $199.

**Megan:** > How much will it be? We budgeted the two [hours].
**OEV:** > $99 per hour — let me check with them and see if they can do this for you. What hours did you want?

**Sophia:** > I don't have the final count — if it's 30–40 people, do I get the difference back?
**OEV:** > Yes — you have up until three days before the event to change the guest count. After that we can't refund, as by then our inventory has been purchased for your event.

→ *Give the number, then advance (ask hours). State the change/refund window cleanly.*
