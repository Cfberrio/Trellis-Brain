## Current Systems (What Exists Today)
### 1\. Microsoft Forms
*   Primary registration tool for OCIA candidates, retreats and faith formation sign-ins
*   Allows custom questions (unlike Camino)
*   Does NOT require church registration (important for OCIA inquirers)
*   Used for attendance tracking via QR codes
### 2\. Camino (Diocese-Mandated)
*   Ordered by the diocese approximately 2 months before discovery
*   Wendy is the only one who enters data
*   Good for children's classes, NOT flexible for adult formation/OCIA
*   **Adults are not entered in Camino** (they are not enrolled in classes); only children are. Parent and adult attendance lives only in the new system.
*   Cannot edit signup forms; requires church registration (deal-breaker for OCIA inquirers)
### 3\. Gravity Forms (Legacy)
*   Old HTML-based form system, being phased out
### 4\. Gift Central (Payment Gateway)
*   Current payment processor; no integration with forms, only hyperlinks to the payment page
*   Wendy creates weekly payment reports every Tuesday; manual reconciliation between forms and payments
### 5\. Excel Spreadsheets (Master Lists)
*   OCIA master list with candidate classification, color-coded (red = needs personal follow-up, e.g., marriage issues)
*   Attendance sign-in sheets, catechist sheet, shared files on SharePoint/OneDrive
### 6\. Paper Documents
*   Certificates (baptism, first communion, confirmation), sponsor forms
*   **A physical file is kept on every candidate**

## Programs & Structure
### Sacraments (OCIA)
Staff menu and page title say "Sacraments" (decided in the Oct 2 review call); one person is an "adult receiving sacraments"; public pages keep "OCIA".

**Three groups (Tree meeting order, same as the build):**
1. **Group 1: Catholics completing sacraments** (baptized Catholic; Confirmation + First Holy Communion). Receive sacraments at the **Confirmation Mass with the bishop on Feb 8, 2027** at St. Joseph (rehearsal the Friday before). Then move into regular adult faith formation.
2. **Group 2: Baptism** (never baptized). Baptism + First Holy Communion + Confirmation at the **Easter Vigil, Mar 27, 2027**. Rite of Election at the diocesan level.
3. **Group 3: Profession of Faith** (validly baptized in another Christian church). Profession of Faith + First Holy Communion + Confirmation at the Easter Vigil.

**Current scale (Oct 1 import of Johanna's master list):** 145 people: 49 in Group 1, 65 in Group 2, 25 in Group 3, and 6 still to confirm baptism (1 doubtful, 5 unknown). 140 came from the master list and 5 were attending without being registered.

**Documents per group:**
*   Catholics: copy of the baptismal certificate.
*   Baptized in another denomination: proof with the Trinitarian formula (certificate, affidavit, or notarized affidavit).
*   Married Catholics, and anyone already convalidated: the **Catholic marriage certificate** from the church (not the civil license).

**Marriage categories:** needs convalidation · needs annulment · annulment already granted (verify with the decree) · single · married in the Church (prove with certificate) · natural marriage of two non-Catholics (becomes sacramental when both are baptized; no action, just teach it) · divorced and not cohabiting (no annulment needed to receive, only to remarry).
*   Timelines: convalidation about 6 months; annulment 1 to 2 years; some annulments are denied. Annulments go through the diocesan tribunal via the parish marriage coordinator, not formation.
*   Goal: nobody keeps coming for months thinking they'll receive and then finds out they can't. Tell them early and keep inviting them to class.
*   Separate marriage class for about 12 to 14 people (Johanna's idea, Mary to decide). This year all groups share one class; next year each group gets its own path.

**Sponsors:** required for Baptism and Confirmation; sponsors attend the rites and the rite rehearsals.

**Rites booklets (Orianna):** based on the ritual book updated in 2024. Rites: Sending, Election, Scrutinies, Reception, Easter Vigil. Father changes options each year, sometimes the day before.

**Attendance rules:** max 3 unexcused absences per semester; candidates attend Sunday plus one weekday session (Mon, Wed or Thu), and the weekday class counts as that week's class; summer classes never count as absences.

**Call campaign for people registered but not attending:** clean duplicates, split the list (about 10 per catechist), call every other day, up to 3 attempts, warm script first (Luis writes it), then gather marriage, sponsor and obstacles.

### Adult Faith Formation
*   Open to ALL adults; website says "Adults do not need to register". Everyone signs in at the session.
*   Runs parallel to children's sessions so parents stay
*   **Parents:** this year all parents are required and invited; parents of a child in sacrament prep are the hard requirement (high priority). Any of 4 nights counts: Mon, Wed, Thu, or Spanish Tuesday. Sacrament status comes from the uploaded class list, never guessed from grade.
*   Topics designed yearly; catechists choose sessions from a shared schedule
*   Goal: change culture from "checkbox" mentality to genuine faith engagement (Amazing Parish)
### Children's Faith Formation
*   Handled by Mary (separate). Children and youth OCIA register via Camino.

## Weekly Workflow (Before the System)
**Every Tuesday:** Gift Central report → manual reconciliation of payments with form submissions → new registrations added to the master Excel → OCIA candidates classified → QR-form attendance reviewed.
**Ongoing:** certificate follow-up, OCIA interviews (especially red-flagged cases), new parishioner data to Wendy for Camino, catechists self-schedule in a shared sheet.

* * *

## Requirements for the New System
### Must Have
*   Custom registration forms; no church registration requirement for OCIA inquiry
*   Payment integration (Stripe) and automated reconciliation
*   Attendance with a **rotating check-in code** that changes every few minutes (no phone location used or stored)
*   Absence tracking with configurable limits (3 per semester)
*   Master list management with classification/color-coding
*   Certificate/document tracking (what's received, what's missing, physical file)
*   Catechist scheduling; weekly reporting
### Should Have
*   Camino pathway (feed Wendy without requiring Camino day to day)
*   Parent attendance linked to children's sacrament requirements
*   Communication/follow-up workflows; case management for complex OCIA situations
### Nice to Have
*   Self-service portal for candidates; certificate reminders; English/Spanish

* * *

## Branding Requirements
*   Source: [stjosephorlando.org](https://stjosephorlando.org/); parish logo (white and black versions); website palette
*   Should feel native to the parish, not a third-party tool

## Strategic Vision (Sept 30 proposal)
*   **Phase 1:** Events & Retreats
*   **Phase 2:** Adult Formation & OCIA
*   **Phase 3:** Weekly Schedule & Check-In
*   **Future:** children's formation, rooms and facilities, catechist scheduling, sacramental records and certificates, ministries and volunteers. (Rooms calendar and catechist scheduling are already built and can be shown early.)
*   The system is owned by the parish and provided at no cost; the parish's data stays the parish's. Only Stripe fees apply; Adult Formation and OCIA stay free, only events and retreats are paid.
*   Camino remains for diocese compliance; the system handles day-to-day operations.
