## Resumen
Módulo de Fase 2 para los adultos que reciben sacramentos (OCIA): grupos según el orden de la reunión Tree (G1 católicos que completan sacramentos, G2 Bautismo, G3 Profesión de Fe; 49/65/25 + 6 = 145), camino sacramental por persona, validez del bautismo, casos de matrimonio, documentos por grupo, padrinos, campaña de llamadas, tablero de preparación, exportación a Excel y folletos de ritos. Todo eso quedó construido y verificado contra el código el 2 de octubre; en el menú de staff se llama "Sacraments" y en las páginas públicas "OCIA". Quedaron abiertos: confirmar el flujo de certificados con Yolanda y Wendy, que Mary decida sobre una clase de matrimonio separada (~12–14 personas) y la revisión de staff de las reglas Pass / Fail / ? y de ubicación en grupos.

## Descripción
Phase 2. Staff menu and page title say "Sacraments" (decided in the Oct 2 call); public pages keep "OCIA". Verified against the code Oct 2.

- [x] Groups (Tree meeting order): **Group 1** Catholics completing sacraments (Feb 8, 2027 Confirmation Mass) · **Group 2** Baptism (Easter Vigil, Mar 27, 2027) · **Group 3** Profession of Faith, baptized in another church (Easter Vigil). 49 / 65 / 25 + 6 still to confirm baptism = 145.
- [x] Sacrament path per person; one shared class this year, a separate path per subgroup ready for next year
- [x] Baptism validity: Trinitarian formula, affidavit, doubtful/invalid cases go to Group 2
- [x] Marriage cases: needs convalidation, needs annulment, annulment in process, annulment granted (decree), natural marriage of two non-Catholics, divorced and single, cohabiting. Red/yellow flags and restricted case notes.
- [x] Documents per group: baptismal certificate (Catholics), proof with the Trinitarian formula (other Christians), Catholic marriage certificate (married / convalidated), annulment decree; drag-and-drop uploads; **physical file** checkmark
- [x] Sponsors (required for Baptism and Confirmation; attend rites and rehearsals)
- [x] Interview / contact status and notes editable from the list
- [x] Call campaign: caller per person, 3 attempt checkboxes, outcome, "Split calls" among catechists (\~10 each)
- [x] Readiness dashboard for Feb 8 and the Easter Vigil; Excel export with one tab per group
- [x] Rites booklets per year (Sending, Election, Scrutinies, Reception, Easter Vigil) and who receives what
- [ ] Confirm the certificate flow with Yolanda and Wendy
- [ ] Separate marriage class (\~12–14 people): Mary to decide
- [ ] Staff review of the Pass / Fail / ? rules and group placement rules (in the open questions)
