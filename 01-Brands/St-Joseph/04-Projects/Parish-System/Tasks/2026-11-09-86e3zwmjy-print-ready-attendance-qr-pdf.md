## To Keep in Mind
*   Generate the PDF from the selected session's actual data and existing attendance QR/check-in URL. The printed QR must open the same check-in destination as Show QR for that exact session, not a generic program page or another date.
*   Reference dates November 2, 3 and 4 illustrate the design only. Do not hard-code these dates, program names, times, rooms or topics. Support both English and Spanish sessions, preserving accents and localized session details.
*   Include the topic when present and keep the layout clean when it is absent. Long program names and topics must not overlap the QR or spill onto another page.
*   Deliver an actual downloadable PDF with a meaningful filename based on the session date and program. Keep it to one portrait page with printer-safe margins, crisp text and a large, high-resolution or vector QR with sufficient white space for reliable scanning.
*   Preserve the existing Show QR and attendance flows. Downloading a PDF must not create a check-in or change attendance.
*   Test downloads for multiple dates and both languages. Verify displayed session details and QR destination, then scan the exported PDF on screen and a printed copy to confirm both open the correct session check-in.

### 2026-11-10 08:42 · Cristian Berrío
 the check-in sheet PDF is built. It goes live when Cristian publishes.

Where: Schedule > Sessions > Show QR. There is a new Download PDF button right under the code. The same button appears in the class roster's Show QR. It hides while the code is in full screen, so the room never sees it.

What you get: one Letter portrait page laid out like your three sheets. It has the seal, St. Joseph Catholic Church, Orlando, Florida USA, SCAN TO CHECK IN · ESCANEE PARA REGISTRARSE, the program, weekday/date · time · room, the topic if there is one, a large QR code, the English and Spanish instructions, and a footer with the sheet name and "Page 1 of 1".
Everything comes from that session's own data. Nothing is fixed to Nov 2–4.
Spanish sessions print in Spanish, for example "Martes, 3 de noviembre · 7:00 PM – 8:30 PM · Salón Social".
File names follow your pattern: "11.02 AFF English.pdf", "11.03 Formacion de Fe Espanol.pdf".
Long program names and long topics shrink and wrap, and the code gets a bit smaller if needed. The sheet always stays on one page, and text never overlaps the code.
Downloading changes nothing: no check-in is made and attendance stays as it is.

Checked: I read the code of every sheet with QR-reading software. Each one opens the same check-in link as Show QR for that session, even from a low-resolution image. I tested English and Spanish sessions on several dates, plus a very long name and topic.

Attached: samples made from the real sessions of Nov 2 (EN), Nov 3 (ES) and Nov 9 (EN, long topic). Their codes are the real check-in links.

One thing to check: please scan one on screen and one printed copy with a phone. I could not do that part.

Note: rooms are only stored in English, so the Spanish room names come from a short list: Church → Iglesia, Social Hall → Salón Social, Room/Hall X → Salón X, Conference Room → Sala de Conferencias, Annex → Anexo. Tell me if you want different wording.
