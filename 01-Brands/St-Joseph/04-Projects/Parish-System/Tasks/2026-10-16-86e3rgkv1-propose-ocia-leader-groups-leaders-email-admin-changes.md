### 1\. Grouping proposal (from the meeting logic)
**What the meeting decided**
*   Groups of \~10 per leader, **same people over time**, matched by **season of life** so trust builds faster.
*   Groups live **outside OCIA Sundays** (hangouts, coffee, ice cream, rosary, one-off Bible study, testimonies).
*   **No assigned seating on Sundays.** Mary, Bill and Matt said no. Leaders just invite their people to come 10 to 15 min early for breakfast and sit together. Never announce fixed groups.
*   Luis groups people using the data already in the database: date of birth, marital status, college/student, etc.

**Proposed grouping rules**
1. Start from the 69 attending only. Non-attenders come in a second pass after the text outreach.
2. Sort by season of life using: age (DOB), marital status (single / engaged / married / needs convalidation), student or not, kids in the household.
3. Hard cap: **10 per group**. If a bucket is bigger, split it into two groups of the same season; don't mix seasons to fill seats.
4. Couples and engaged pairs stay in the same group and count as 2.
5. Exclude minors (the 14 and 16 year olds flagged for Youth Faith Formation).
6. Match leader to group by season of life and, where it matters, by gender/age (meeting point: respect lands better with someone in a similar stage).

**Proposed buckets (final sizes depend on the real data)**

| \# | Season of life | Who | Leader (tentative) |
| ---| ---| ---| --- |
| 1 | College / students | \~18 to 22, students | To confirm |
| 2 | Young adults, single | \~23 to 30, single | To confirm |
| 3 | Engaged and newly married | Couples, incl. convalidation cases | Christo Edmond (married, offered to walk with married candidates) |
| 4 | Married with kids | Parents | To confirm |
| 5 | Adults 30 to 45 | Single or married without the above | To confirm |
| 6 | Adults 45+ | Older adults | To confirm (one leader said they'd prefer older adults) |
| 7 | Overflow of the biggest bucket | Same season as its source | Needs leader |
| 8 | Overflow of the next biggest | Same season as its source | Needs leader |

Luis holds groups 7 and 8 until 2 new leaders are found. Kat Velez already sits with an OCIA small group every week and is open to sponsoring, so she's a natural fit for whichever group has candidates still looking for a sponsor.

### 3\. Admin dashboard proposal
**A. Admin home: three windows at the bottom**

Under the current home stats, add three summary windows: **Leaders**, **Catechists**, **Staff**. Each shows the key numbers and opens its section on click.

**B. New left-nav section: Leaders**

Its own section, not inside People.
*   **Groups board**: one card per group with season of life, leader, count (x/10), and a Needs leader badge when empty.
*   **Suggest groups** button: builds a first draft from DOB, marital status, student and kids, max 10 per group, couples kept together. Admin reviews and moves people between groups with **drag-and-drop**.
*   **Group page**: members with phone/email, last 3 attendances, "needs" chips (convalidation, baptism certificate, sponsor, documents) without case notes, hangout log (date, who came), prayer list, leader notes.
*   **Copy / export contacts** per group, so Luis can send each leader their list while leaders have no logins.
*   **Leaders roster**: each leader, their group, last check-in, and their pending items as a checklist (same items as the email).
*   Counters on top: 69 attending, 8 groups, 6 leaders, 2 open spots.

**C. People = access only**
*   Tabs in this order: **Admin · Staff · Catechist · Leader**. Drop the "St. Joseph Adults" category here.
*   **Admin**: Kori and Father Ben only.
*   Separate internal admin group for us, not visible to the parish.
*   From People you give, review and change access and roles. That's all it does.
*   Clicking any person opens their profile everywhere in the app (today some rows open nothing). Admin profiles too, even if they're mostly empty.

**D. Staff section**
*   Nav item just called **Staff** (not "Staff space").
*   Holidays becomes one tab inside it.
*   Build the skeleton (directory + empty tabs) for functions we'll add later.
*   Load everyone from the staff Excel list into the Staff category.

Load the 25 people from [08-Catechist-Directory-and-Checkpoints.xlsx](https://t9017418223.p.clickup-attachments.com/t9017418223/b9a20efc-76b1-4296-a682-18cf5337ecae/08-Catechist-Directory-and-Checkpoints.xlsx) into the Catechist category with program, language, phone, email and notes.
