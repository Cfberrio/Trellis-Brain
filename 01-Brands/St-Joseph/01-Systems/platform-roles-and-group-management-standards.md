# Platform Roles and Group Management Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: The 'People' section in the platform is specifically for access management and the assignment or review of roles.
- **rule**: Users are permitted to hold multiple roles simultaneously (e.g., both leader and catechist).
- **rule**: Leaders are granted access to the platform only after receiving formal approval.
- **process**: Attendees (e.g., from OCI) should be organized into groups of 10 or fewer and assigned to existing leaders.
- **decision**: The administrator dashboard will include a specific section for leaders located in the left sidebar.
