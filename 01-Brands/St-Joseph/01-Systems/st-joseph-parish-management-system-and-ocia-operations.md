# St. Joseph Parish Management System and OCIA Operations

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: The St. Joseph parish management system is a custom cloud solution (TanStack Start, Supabase, Postgres) owned by the parish to replace legacy tools like Microsoft Forms and Excel.
- **process**: The system rollout follows a three-phase plan: P1 Events & Retreats (Stripe integration), P2 Adult Formation & OCIA (sacramental paths and document tracking), and P3 Weekly Schedule & Check-In.
- **rule**: OCIA attendance policy: Maximum of 3 unexcused absences per semester; attending a weekday Adult Faith Formation class counts as the week's OCIA class.
- **rule**: Room booking and facility security: Choirs use Hall BC; interior hall doors are deadbolted to separate adults from children's areas; room conflicts are managed via Nth-Tuesday rules.
- **fact**: OCIA groups are categorized into: Group 1 (Catholics completing sacraments - Feb 8), Group 2 (Baptism - Easter Vigil), and Group 3 (Profession of Faith - Easter Vigil).
