# Sacraments Platform Rules and Logic

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: OCIA attendance is tracked by week, not by individual class; a participant is marked 'attended' for the week if they check in to at least one of the available weekday options (Mon, Wed, or Thu).
- **rule**: OCIA participants are allowed a maximum of 3 unexcused absences per semester; summer sessions do not count toward this limit.
- **rule**: Marriage subgroups are independent of baptism status: 'Married outside the Catholic Church' requires no documents; 'Divorced/previously married' requires an annulment decree; 'Cohabiting' requires a pastoral flag.
- **process**: For shared email addresses in the system, the email is retained for one person and cleared for others; staff must call to obtain unique emails for OTP onboarding.
- **rule**: All adults receiving sacraments are required to submit a Parish Registration form as part of their documentation.
