# Parish Systems Discovery and OCIA Operations

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: OCIA candidates are categorized into three groups: Group 1 (Catholics completing sacraments), Group 2 (Unbaptized/Baptism), and Group 3 (Profession of Faith for those baptized in other denominations).
- **rule**: OCIA attendance rules allow a maximum of 3 unexcused absences per semester; candidates must attend Sunday plus one weekday session (Mon, Wed, or Thu).
- **fact**: The parish uses Camino for diocese-mandated data entry (primarily children), but it is not flexible for adult formation or OCIA inquirers as it requires church registration.
- **decision**: The new system rollout follows a three-phase strategy: Phase 1 for Events & Retreats, Phase 2 for Adult Formation & OCIA, and Phase 3 for Weekly Schedule & Check-In.
- **rule**: Marriage documentation requirements vary by status: Catholics need a Catholic marriage certificate; those baptized in other denominations need proof with the Trinitarian formula.
