# SOP: OCIA Attendance Reconciliation

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: OCIA attendance reconciliation involves verifying claims against email correspondence and small-group leader reports, ensuring manual sign-ins are captured, and distinguishing between attendance and explained absences. Attendance is not automatically inferred from group membership or registration.
- **rule**: OCIA attendance does not determine sacramental readiness; commitment and discernment are primary. Missed sessions may be addressed via weekday formation, but weekday formation is encouraged rather than mandatory for those attending Sunday sessions.
