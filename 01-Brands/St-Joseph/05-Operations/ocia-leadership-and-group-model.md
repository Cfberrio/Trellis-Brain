# OCIA Leadership and Group Model

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: OCIA leaders accompany groups of approximately 10 people organized by life stage (college, young adult single, married, older adults).
- **rule**: Groups are consistent over time and engagement occurs primarily outside of OCIA Sundays; there is no assigned seating or public announcement of fixed groups in the room.
- **process**: Leaders are responsible for starting group chats, hosting at least one hangout between sessions, praying for members by name, and identifying administrative needs (convalidation, documents, sponsors) to pass to staff.
- **decision**: OCIA Sundays include a 15-20 minute 'Traditions' block, breakfast, and a 'mini-retreat' atmosphere.
