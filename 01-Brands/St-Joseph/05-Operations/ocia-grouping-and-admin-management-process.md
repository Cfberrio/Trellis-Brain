# OCIA Grouping and Admin Management Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: OCIA registrants are split into groups of 10 or fewer, matched by season of life (age, marital status, student status, and presence of kids) to build trust.
- **rule**: OCIA groups are informal and live outside Sunday sessions; leaders invite members to sit together for breakfast 10-15 minutes early rather than using assigned seating.
- **decision**: The St. Joseph Admin dashboard will include a dedicated 'Leaders' section for group management, separate from the 'People' directory, with privacy controls preventing leaders from seeing sacramental case notes.
- **rule**: Couples and engaged pairs in OCIA are kept in the same group and count as two individuals toward the 10-person cap.
