# OCIA and Sacraments System Rules

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Staff menu and page titles use 'Sacraments', while public-facing pages retain 'OCIA'.
- **rule**: OCIA Groups are categorized into: Group 1 (Catholics completing sacraments), Group 2 (Baptism), and Group 3 (Profession of Faith for those baptized in other churches).
- **rule**: Baptism validity requires the Trinitarian formula and an affidavit; doubtful or invalid cases are assigned to Group 2.
- **process**: Required documentation includes baptismal certificates, proof of Trinitarian formula for non-Catholics, Catholic marriage certificates, or annulment decrees depending on the group.
- **rule**: Sponsors are mandatory for Baptism and Confirmation and must attend rites and rehearsals.
