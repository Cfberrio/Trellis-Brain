# OCIA and Adult Sacraments Classification Rules

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: OCIA (Order of Christian Initiation of Adults) only applies to people who have not been baptized Catholic and are entering the faith (unbaptized, baptized in another Christian church, or doubtful/unknown baptism).
- **rule**: Baptized Catholics completing First Communion and/or Confirmation must be classified under 'Adult Sacraments' (Sacramentos para Adultos) and are strictly excluded from OCIA counts, reports, and rites.
- **process**: Individuals with 'doubtful or unknown' baptism status remain in the OCIA group until verification; if verified as a Catholic baptism, they must be automatically moved to the Adult Sacraments group.
- **decision**: For the current cycle, Adult Sacraments students may share the same class time and QR attendance tracking as OCIA, but they must remain logically distinct in the database and dashboards.
