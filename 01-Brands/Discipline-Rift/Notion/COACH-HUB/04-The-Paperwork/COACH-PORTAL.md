# COACH PORTAL
The Coach Portal is DR's field app. Your dashboard link arrives in your assignment message: it has the roster, the weekly plans, and session logging.
## WHAT YOU DO IN IT
- **Log in**: email code, no password. You see only your assigned teams.
- **Attendance**: pick your team, pick the session, mark each student present or absent. Each tap saves immediately. The stats panel shows enrolled, present, absent and unmarked.
- **Messages**: individual parent threads and team-wide broadcasts, with attachments up to 25MB. Check the bell icon for unread messages.
- **Weekly reports**: attendance summaries go out by email automatically, including top absences. Your logging feeds them.
## THE TWO THAT ARE NOT OPTIONAL
1. Attendance every session. "Track Class Attendance: Keep Records" is coach responsibility 7.
2. Session logging by 8 PM ET. If a session is not logged by then, you get the reminder message automatically.
