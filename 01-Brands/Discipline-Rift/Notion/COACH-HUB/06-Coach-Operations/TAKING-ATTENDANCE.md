# TAKING ATTENDANCE
## THE SEVEN STEPS
1. Log in at [disciplinerift.com/coach](http://disciplinerift.com/coach). The page greets you: "Welcome, [your name] • N assigned teams".
2. In **My Teams**, tap your team. It fills in when selected. Many teams? Use the **Search in my teams...** box. The first team is picked for you.
3. The card **Select Session – [team]** appears. Tap today's practice, for example *Monday, July 20, 2026 — 15:00 - Orangewood*.
4. In the **Control** card, tap **Start Check-in**. That button stays grey until you pick a session.
5. **Attendance List – [team]** opens with your roster, sorted by last name, each player with their grade.
6. Tap **Present** or **Absent** for every player. The badge changes immediately.
7. Tap **Finish**. That only closes the list: there is nothing else to submit.
## READING THE NUMBERS
- **Total**: players on the roster. **Present** in green, **Absent** in red.
- While checking in you also get **Marked: X / Y** and **Pending: Z**.
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	**Pending is your to-do list. Finish every practice at Pending: 0.** A player you never touched is "Not marked", which is not the same as absent.
</callout>

	You can only set **Present** or **Absent**. If a player with a blue **Excused** badge shows up anyway, tap **Present**: your tap always wins.
</callout>
## SAVING
- Every tap saves by itself, instantly. The blue box under the list says "Session Summary: X of Y students present" and "Changes are saved automatically in the system".
- **Made a mistake? Tap the other button.** The last tap wins, as many times as you need.
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	Weak signal in the gym: if a badge does not change colour after you tap, the save did not go through and the app will not warn you. Move, tap again, and check **Pending: 0** before you leave.
</callout>
## THE TIMING RULE
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	**You cannot take attendance for a practice that already happened.** The list only shows today and future dates. Yesterday's practice is gone by the morning and only the office can fix it. If you forget, message the office the same day.
</callout>
- You can open a future practice and mark it early. Be careful not to mark the wrong day.
- The small date badge on the Attendance List card shows **today's date**, not the date you selected. Trust the date in the **Select Session** list.
## TAKE ACTION
- [ ] Take attendance every practice, on the day itself
- [ ] Mark every player: finish at **Pending: 0**
- [ ] Mark at the start of practice, not from memory that night
- [ ] Leave blue **Excused** badges alone unless the child shows up
- [ ] Forgot a day? Tell the office the same day
