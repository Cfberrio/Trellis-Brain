# WHEN SOMETHING BREAKS
## LOGGING IN
**The code email never arrives** — Search your inbox for "Discipline Rift login code", then check Spam and Promotions. Wait a minute and request one more. Mark it Not spam so future codes come through. Nothing after two tries: message the office, we may have another email on file.
**"Error sending code..."** — That email is not registered as a coach. Check the typo first. If it is correct, your account is not set up yet: message the office.
**"Invalid code..."** — Wrong digits or an expired code. Always use the newest email; older codes stop working. Retype carefully, or Change Email and request a fresh one.
**The code works but I am sent back to the login screen** — Your login exists but is not linked to your coach profile. You cannot fix this. Message the office with these words: "my code works but the dashboard sends me back to login".
**It asks me for a password** — Wrong page. [disciplinerift.com/login](http://disciplinerift.com/login) says DR Admin and is for the office. Go to [disciplinerift.com/coach](http://disciplinerift.com/coach).
## TEAMS AND SESSIONS
**My team is missing from My Teams** — The office has to move the team to **ongoing**, usually by its first practice. If today is that first practice, message the office now.
**"No scheduled sessions for this team"** — The season ended, or the schedule has not been loaded. Message the office with the team name.
**Today's practice is not in the list** — It was canceled. That is how cancellations appear: the date is simply gone. If it is a surprise, confirm with the office before you drive out.
**Start Check-in is grey** — You have not picked a session. Tap a date in Select Session first.
## ATTENDANCE
**I forgot to take attendance yesterday** — You cannot fix it: past dates disappear from your list. Message the office the same day with the team, the date, and who was present.
**A badge does not change when I tap** — The save did not go through, almost always weak signal. Move and tap again. Confirm Pending: 0 before you leave.
**A player shows a blue Excused badge** — Their parent told us in advance they would miss that day. Leave it, unless the child shows up: then tap Present.
**I marked the wrong player** — Tap the other button. The last tap always wins.
**A player on my court is not on the list** — They are not enrolled on that team, or they are on another one. Let them practice, then message the office with the child's name and the team.
## MESSAGES
**"Please select an image or video file"** — Only photos and video can be sent. PDFs and documents are blocked: send a photo of the page instead.
**"File size must be less than 25MB"** — The video is too long. Record a shorter clip or send a photo.
**"No parents found for this team"** — No students are actively enrolled on that team yet. Message the office.
**I got no email when a parent wrote** — Expected: there are no email or text alerts. The bell only shows while the dashboard is open. Check Messages before and after each practice.
## ODDS AND ENDS
**Some words are in Spanish** — A few screens still show Spanish text: "Verificando sesión...", "Cargando lista de estudiantes...". Nothing is broken, translation is in progress.
**Why 15:00 and not 3:00 PM?** — The schedule uses the 24-hour clock. After noon, subtract 12: 13:00 is 1 PM, 15:00 is 3 PM, 16:30 is 4:30 PM.
**Can I change my name, email or phone?** — Not from the dashboard. Message the office.
**Does it work on my phone?** — Yes: phone, tablet or computer. On a phone the top bar shrinks to icons. House is Dashboard, speech bubble is Messages, bell is notifications, arrow is Sign Out.
**Can I print the roster?** — Not from the coach dashboard. Ask the office for a roster PDF.
