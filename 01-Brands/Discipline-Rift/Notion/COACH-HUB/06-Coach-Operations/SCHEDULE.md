# SCHEDULE
<callout icon="🗓️" color="gray_bg">
	**THE 26–27 SCHEDULE**
	[Open the 26–27 schedule folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1eRJhyKS8jx-UI_xaUOd8Egb-i5KzZTOG?usp=drive_link)
	*Every practice date for the entire school year. Check it before you commit to anything else.*
	**Times are shown as arrival / start–end** — for example 2:45 / 3:15–4:15. The arrival time is when you are on campus, not when practice starts.
</callout>
## WHY THIS MATTERS
Players expect their coach to be there. That is the whole job. We are here for them, and a season only works if the person in front of them is the same person every week.
## REQUESTING A DAY OFF
<callout icon="⏰" color="gray_bg">
	**TWO WEEKS MINIMUM**
	Request any day off at least **two weeks before** that practice. That is what it takes to find and brief your cover.
	**Email** [info@disciplinerift.com](mailto:info@disciplinerift.com) with the exact dates you cannot coach, and which teams they affect.
</callout>
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	Less than two weeks notice is not a request, it is a problem to solve. Send it anyway and send it immediately — but expect that we may not be able to cover you.
</callout>
