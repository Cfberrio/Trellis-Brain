# MESSAGING PARENTS
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**Select Message All to send the same message to every parent/guardian on the team. Each family receives it individually. It is not a group chat.** Parents never see each other or each other's replies.
</callout>
Open **Messages** in the top blue bar. Two tabs: **Individual Messages** and **Team Messages**.
## ONE PARENT
1. **Messages**, then the **Individual Messages** tab.
2. Under **Select Team & Parent**, choose the team. Your first team is chosen for you.
3. Choose the parent. Each line reads **Parent Name — Child1 Child2 (2)**: the names are their kids on that team, the number is unread messages from them.
4. The chat opens: **Chat with \[Parent Name\]**. Type at the bottom, press Enter or the blue button.
- Parent replies arrive **live**, no refresh needed.
- Opening a conversation marks it read.
- Your messages sit on the right in blue, the parent's on the left in white.
- Only the **last 50 messages** load. There is no "load older".
## ATTACHMENTS
Tap the paperclip, **Attach File**.
- **Photos and video only.** No PDFs, no documents.
- **25 MB maximum.** A long video is rejected: record a short clip.
- The file previews before you send it; the red X removes it.
- Photos and video play inside the chat for the parent.
## MESSAGE ALL (THE WHOLE TEAM)
This is your after-practice update: one positive, the focus, progress or next step, a note if needed.
1. **Messages**, then the **Team Messages** tab, then pick the team.
2. **Send Message to Entire Team** tells you *"This message will be sent to N parents"*.
3. Write it, attach a photo or video if you want, tap **Send to N parents**.
4. Green confirmation: *"Message sent successfully to N parents"*.
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	Message All is **not a group chat**. Each parent gets it as a private message from you. Answers come back to you in the Individual Messages tab.
</callout>
Everything you have sent is listed under **Broadcast History** with the date and how many parents received it.
## THE BELL
- The bell in the top bar shows a red badge with your unread count, **9+** above nine.
- Tap it: parent name, team, unread count. Tap a row to jump into that conversation.
- The badge hides while you are on the Messages page.
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	There are **no email or text alerts** for parent messages. The bell only shows while you have the dashboard open. Check Messages before and after each practice.
</callout>
## TAKE ACTION
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	Message All after every practice, before you leave campus. Reply within 24 hours during the season. Message All for what everyone needs: today's update, bring water, gym change, showcase day. Individual message for anything about one child.
</callout>
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	Never handle payments, refunds or registration in chat: send those to the DR office. Cancellations and schedule changes are announced by the office, not by you, unless the office asks you to.
</callout>
