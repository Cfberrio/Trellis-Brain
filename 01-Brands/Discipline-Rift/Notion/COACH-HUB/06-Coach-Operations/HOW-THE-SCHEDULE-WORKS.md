# HOW THE SCHEDULE WORKS
## WHERE IT COMES FROM
The office does not load your practices one by one. Each team is set up with a pattern:
- a **day of the week**, for example every Monday
- a **start and end time**
- a **season start date and end date**
- a **location**, the school
- a list of **canceled dates**: holidays, closures, weather
Your dashboard turns that pattern into individual practice dates. One line per practice.
## READING A LINE
> **Monday, July 20, 2026**
> 15:00 - Orangewood
- Line one is the date of that practice.
- Line two is the **start time** and the **location**.
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	Times use the 24-hour clock. 15:00 is 3:00 PM, 16:30 is 4:30 PM. The **end time is not shown**: check your assignment email or ask the office how long your session runs.
</callout>
Every line also carries a small badge reading **scheduled**. It is only a label; it means nothing special.
## WHAT THE LIST SHOWS
- **Today and forward only.** Past dates drop off by themselves. An empty list means no upcoming practices on that team.
- **Earliest first.**
- **A canceled practice simply disappears.** There is no strikethrough and no Canceled label.
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	This is the habit that matters: **check the Select Session list before every practice.** If today is missing from the list, that practice is canceled. That absence is the notification.
</callout>

## WHEN SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
**My team is not in My Teams** — A team only shows once the office moves it to **ongoing**, normally by its first practice. If your first practice is today and the team is missing, message the office now: it is one click on their side. Teams still open for registration are hidden on purpose until the season starts.
**The team is there but has no sessions** — You see "No scheduled sessions for this team". Either the season ended, or the schedule has not been loaded. Message the office with the team name.
**A date I expected is missing** — Almost always it was canceled on purpose: holiday, school closure. If you believe it should be there, message the office **before** you drive out.
**Two of my teams practice at the same time** — Do not solve it yourself. Message the office: conflicts are fixed by reassigning a coach or moving a session, both office-side.
