# LOGGING IN
## WHAT YOU NEED
- The link: [disciplinerift.com/coach](http://disciplinerift.com/coach) — bookmark it. It does not come up in a Google search.
- The email address DR has on file for you. There is **no password**: you get a fresh 6-digit code every time.
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	Do not use [disciplinerift.com/login](http://disciplinerift.com/login). That screen says DR Admin and asks for a password: it is for the office. Coaches log in only at **/coach**.
</callout>
## THE SIX STEPS
1. Open [disciplinerift.com/coach](http://disciplinerift.com/coach). You see a card titled **Dashboard Coach**.
2. Type your email under "Enter your email to receive a code". Capital letters do not matter.
3. Tap **Send Code**. A blue box confirms: "OTP code sent to your@email.com. Check your inbox."
4. Open the email from Discipline Rift, subject **"Your Discipline Rift login code"**. It has a **6-digit code**.
5. Type the 6 digits and tap **Verify Code**.
6. Green check, "Login successful!", and the page loads your dashboard on its own after about 2 seconds.
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	On a phone, when the code email lands your keyboard usually offers the code right above it. Tap it and it fills in by itself. Copy and paste also works: extra spaces are cleaned for you.
</callout>
## WHAT TO KNOW
- **Wrong email typed?** Tap **Change Email** and start again.
- **There is no Resend button.** To get a new code: Change Email, re-enter your email, Send Code.
- **Codes expire.** Always use the newest email. If a code stops working, ask for another.
- **You stay logged in.** Most days you open /coach and land straight on the dashboard.
- **Signing out:** **Sign Out** in the top blue bar, top right. Do it on a borrowed or shared device.
