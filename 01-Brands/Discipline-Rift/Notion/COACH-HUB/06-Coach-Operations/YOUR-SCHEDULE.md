	**Time:** 2:45 / 3:15–4:15

	- 2:45 = you are on campus by this time. Not leaving home, not in the car line, not looking for parking. On campus.

<callout icon="🚗" color="gray_bg">
	**SCHOOL DISMISSAL TRAFFIC**
	Schools run car lines at the time you arrive. Plan for it. When safe and permitted, make your way to parking rather than sitting in the car line as if it were your destination. Follow school traffic direction. The goal is parked, checked in and ready by your arrival time.
</callout>
<callout icon="🔄" color="gray_bg">
	**SCHEDULE CHANGES**
	- Check the current schedule before every practice.
	- Schedules can move around registrations and season setup.
	- Important current changes come through DR and WhatsApp.
	- If an old screenshot or email differs from the live schedule, the live schedule wins.
	- The Coach Dashboard session list is for taking attendance. It is not your schedule. A session missing from the Dashboard is not a cancellation: follow this schedule and call DR at (407) 614-7454.
</callout>
## REQUESTING A DAY OFF
<callout icon="⏰" color="gray_bg">
	**TWO WEEKS MINIMUM**
	Request any day off at least **two weeks before** that practice. That is what it takes to find and brief your cover.
	**Email** [info@disciplinerift.com](mailto:info@disciplinerift.com) with the exact dates you cannot coach, and which teams they affect.
</callout>
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	Less than two weeks notice is not a request, it is a problem to solve. Send it anyway and send it immediately, but expect that we may not be able to cover you.
</callout>
<callout icon="🚨" color="gray_bg">
	**RUNNING LATE OR CANNOT ATTEND TODAY**
	Call DR immediately at **(407) 614-7454**. The line rings DR leadership so an available leader can respond. Do not wait for the start time. Do not arrange cover with another coach on your own unless DR confirms it.
</callout>
