# DASHBOARD PROBLEMS
<callout icon="📞" color="gray_bg">
	**CALL DR AT (407) 614-7454** when the Dashboard shows `offline: true`, when you cannot complete attendance, when today's session is missing, or when a player is not on your roster. The line rings DR leadership so an available leader can respond. Calling never means practice is canceled.
</callout>
<callout icon="📶" color="gray_bg">
	**Offline on screen, or attendance will not complete.** Load the Dashboard before entering the school. If it shows `offline: true`, or you cannot reliably finish check-in, **call DR at (407) 614-7454**. Do not treat it as a schedule change. Keep coaching; DR resolves the record.
</callout>
## LOGGING IN
<details>
<summary>**The code email never arrives**</summary>
	Search your inbox for "Discipline Rift login code", then check Spam and Promotions. Wait a minute and request one more. Mark it Not spam so future codes come through. Nothing after two tries: message the office, we may have another email on file.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**"Error sending code..."**</summary>
	That email is not registered as a coach. Check the typo first. If it is correct, your account is not set up yet: message the office.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**"Invalid code..."**</summary>
	Wrong digits or an expired code. Always use the newest email; older codes stop working. Retype carefully, or Change Email and request a fresh one.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**The code works but I am sent back to the login screen**</summary>
	Your login exists but is not linked to your coach profile. You cannot fix this. Message the office with these words: "my code works but the dashboard sends me back to login".
</details>
<details>
<summary>**It asks me for a password**</summary>
	Wrong page. [disciplinerift.com/login](http://disciplinerift.com/login) says DR Admin and is for the office. Go to [**disciplinerift.com/coach**](http://disciplinerift.com/coach).
</details>
## TEAMS AND SESSIONS
<details>
<summary>**My team is missing from My Teams**</summary>
	The office has to move the team to **ongoing**, usually by its first practice. If today is that first practice, message the office now.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**"No scheduled sessions for this team"**</summary>
	The season ended, or the schedule has not been loaded. Message the office with the team name.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**Today's practice is not in the list**</summary>
	That is a Dashboard issue, not a cancellation. Check the schedule linked in Notion; if the practice is there, it is on. **Call DR at (407) 614-7454** to fix the missing session. Only DR communicates cancellations, never a missing row.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**Start Check-in is grey**</summary>
	You have not picked a session. Tap a date in Select Session first.
</details>
## ATTENDANCE
<details>
<summary>**I forgot to take attendance yesterday**</summary>
	You cannot fix it: past dates disappear from your list. Message the office the same day with the team, the date, and who was present.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**A badge does not change when I tap**</summary>
	The save did not go through. Check for `offline: true`. If you cannot reliably complete check-in, **call DR at (407) 614-7454**. Confirm Pending: 0 before you leave campus.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**A player shows a blue Excused badge**</summary>
	Their parent told us in advance they would miss that day. Leave it, unless the child shows up: then tap Present.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**I marked the wrong player**</summary>
	Tap the other button. The last tap always wins.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**A player on my court is not on the list**</summary>
	Do not resolve it yourself. **Call DR at (407) 614-7454** with the player's name and team so enrollment can be confirmed, and follow DR's direction. The player's word is not confirmation; an older list does not override the Dashboard.
</details>
## MESSAGES
<details>
<summary>**"Please select an image or video file"**</summary>
	Only photos and video can be sent. PDFs and documents are blocked: send a photo of the page instead.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**"File size must be less than 25MB"**</summary>
	The video is too long. Record a shorter clip or send a photo.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**"No parents found for this team"**</summary>
	No students are actively enrolled on that team yet. Message the office.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**I got no email when a parent wrote**</summary>
	Expected: there are no email or text alerts. The bell only shows while the dashboard is open. Check Messages before and after each practice.
</details>
## ODDS AND ENDS
<details>
<summary>**Some words are in Spanish**</summary>
	A few screens still show Spanish text: "Verificando sesión...", "Cargando lista de estudiantes...". Nothing is broken, translation is in progress.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**Why 15:00 and not 3:00 PM?**</summary>
	The Dashboard session list uses the 24-hour clock: 15:00 is 3 PM. Your actual schedule (arrival / start–end) is the one linked in Your Schedule, not this list.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**Can I change my name, email or phone?**</summary>
	Not from the dashboard. Message the office.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**Does it work on my phone?**</summary>
	Yes: phone, tablet or computer. On a phone the top bar shrinks to icons. House is Dashboard, speech bubble is Messages, bell is notifications, arrow is Sign Out.
</details>
<details>
<summary>**Can I print the roster?**</summary>
	Not from the coach dashboard. Ask the office for a roster PDF.
</details>

</callout>
