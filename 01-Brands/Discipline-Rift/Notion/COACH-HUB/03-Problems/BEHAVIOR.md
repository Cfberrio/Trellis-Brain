# BEHAVIOR
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**Correct the action, not the kid.** "Feet behind the line" is a correction. "You never listen" is a verdict. Say what to do next, not what they are.
</callout>
<callout icon="1️⃣" color="gray_bg">
	**PREVENT IT FIRST**
	Most behaviour problems are a practice-design problem. Long lines, long explanations and dead time make kids invent their own game. Protect the hour: multiple lines, high reps, short cues, next setup ready. Clear boundaries named out loud before the first ball.
	[[COACH RULES]]
</callout>
<callout icon="2️⃣" color="gray_bg">
	**THE 3 CALL-OUT RULE** (current DR rule)
	1. **First call-out:** you speak to the player. Name the action, name what you want instead, move on.
	2. **Second call-out:** the player sits out briefly, in sight, then rejoins. Keep it short; the goal is the rep, not the punishment.
	3. **Third call-out:** parent talk. This routes through DR, not you alone. Tell the parent at pickup what happened, specifically and without a verdict, and **call DR at (407) 614-7454** the same day.
</callout>
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	**SAFETY OVERRIDES THE LADDER.** Hitting, throwing equipment at someone, leaving the group, refusing to stay in the boundary: stop practice, keep everyone supervised, and call DR at (407) 614-7454 now. Do not wait for a third call-out.
</callout>
<callout icon="👪" color="gray_bg">
	**PARENTS ARE THE PARTNERSHIP.** A parent who already hears good things from you will hear a hard thing from you too. DR steps in on serious or repeated cases, and can move a player off a team. That only works if the parent has already heard from you, in the Dashboard and in person. Never let removal be the first thing a family hears.
	[[PARTNERING WITH PARENTS]]
</callout>
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	Fix the drill before you blame the kid. Then: speak, sit out, parent + DR. Safety skips the ladder.
</callout>
