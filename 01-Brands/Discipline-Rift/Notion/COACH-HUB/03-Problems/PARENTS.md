# PARENTS
## THE ONE RULE THAT ALREADY EXISTS
DR notifies the parents, not the coach. A coach fielding logistics is a coach not preparing a practice, and it breaks the promise made to schools that DR handles parent communication. When a parent asks about schedules, payments, or registration: take their name, tell them DR will contact them, and pass it to the program director.
