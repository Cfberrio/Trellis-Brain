# EMERGENCIES
## WHAT DR HAS ALREADY COMMITTED
"In an emergency, our coaches follow the host school's on-site emergency procedures, contact emergency services as appropriate, notify you, and notify the school's designated contact." That is the promise made to parents. DR holds no copy of any school's procedures today: collecting them per campus is the blocking task.
## WHAT THIS PAGE WILL HOLD
- A weather and lightning rule you can apply standing on a field
- Who may stop or evacuate a session on site
- The missing child procedure: steps, time threshold, second contact
- When you call 911 yourself, and who else gets called in what order
- What you do when you cannot make a practice
## ESCALATION
