# EMERGENCIES
<callout icon="🚨" color="gray_bg">
	**THE SPINE**
	1. **Follow the host school's on-site emergency procedure.** Every campus has one; school staff run it. In an evacuation, lockdown or site emergency, you do exactly what school staff direct.
	2. **Call 911** yourself when someone's safety needs it now. Do not wait for permission.
	3. **Keep supervision and your head count.** The group moves as one, you count when you land, nobody leaves your sight.
	4. **Notify DR: (407) 614-7454.** The line rings DR leadership. DR notifies parents and the school's designated contact.
	5. **Stay until released** by school staff or DR, with every child accounted for.
</callout>
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**WHAT DR HAS PROMISED PARENTS.** "In an emergency, our coaches follow the host school's on-site emergency procedures, contact emergency services as appropriate, notify you, and notify the school's designated contact." This page is how you keep that promise.
</callout>
