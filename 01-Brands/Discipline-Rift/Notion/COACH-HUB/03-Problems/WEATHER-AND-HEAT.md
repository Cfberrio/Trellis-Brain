# WEATHER & HEAT
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	**WEATHER ADAPTATION AND CANCELLATION ARE DIFFERENT.** Rain does not mean you cancel practice. You find cover and keep going. DR communicates cancellations, through WhatsApp and the schedule. You do not send cancellation messages.
</callout>
<callout icon="🌧️" color="gray_bg">
	**RAIN**
	Find a covered space when one is available: pavilion, covered walkway, cafeteria, gym. Continue or adapt the practice to the space. Head count before you move and when you land. Coordinate with the front office when you move into a school space.
</callout>
<callout icon="⚡" color="gray_bg">
	**THUNDER OR LIGHTNING**
	Move indoors when possible. Ask the front office for an indoor space and continue or adapt there. Keep the group together and counted. Do not stay under a pavilion or tree waiting it out if an indoor option exists.
</callout>
<callout icon="☀️" color="gray_bg">
	**HEAT**
	- Prefer pavilion, covered or shaded areas when available.
	- Water breaks every 20 minutes, and on request always.
	- Bring your own water.
	- Ice containers have been useful for cooling players down and work as a small positive tool. Not a medical treatment.
	- A child who looks unwell in the heat: shade, water, sit them down, get school staff. If it is more than that, it is an injury.
	[[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/Notion/COACH-HUB/03-Problems/INJURY-INCIDENT|INJURY / INCIDENT]]
	*Stop, supervise, school procedure, 911 if needed, call DR.*
</callout>
<callout icon="📞" color="gray_bg">
	**NOT SURE?** Move to cover, keep coaching, and call DR at (407) 614-7454. The line rings DR leadership. Calling is not cancelling.
</callout>
