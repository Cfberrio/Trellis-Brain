# NEED HELP
<callout icon="📞" color="gray_bg">
	**NEED IMMEDIATE DR HELP? CALL (407) 614-7454.** This line rings DR leadership so an available leader can assist. Calling never means practice is canceled.
</callout>
<callout icon="🚑" color="gray_bg">
	**SOMEONE IS HURT OR THE SCHOOL IS IN AN EMERGENCY**
	[[INJURY / INCIDENT]]
	*Stop, supervise, school procedure, 911 if needed, call DR, file the report.*
	[[EMERGENCIES]]
	*Evacuation, lockdown, site emergency. Follow school staff.*
	[[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/Notion/COACH-HUB/04-The-Paperwork/ACCIDENT-REPORT|ACCIDENT REPORT]]
	*The form. Same day.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🔍" color="gray_bg">
	**A PLAYER PROBLEM**
	[[MISSING PLAYER]]
	*A rostered player is not with the team. Ask, send one or two responsible players, supervise, call DR.*
	**Player says they are registered but is not on your Dashboard roster:** call DR at (407) 614-7454 with name and team. Do not resolve it yourself.
	[[BEHAVIOR]]
	*A kid will not stop. Correct the action, 3 call-out rule, safety skips the ladder.*
	[[PARTNERING WITH PARENTS]]
	*An upset parent, or a hard conversation.*
</callout>
<callout icon="⛈️" color="gray_bg">
	**WEATHER OR CANCELLATION**
	[[WEATHER & HEAT]]
	*Rain: cover. Lightning: indoors. Heat: shade and water. You adapt; DR cancels.*
	[[PRACTICE CANCELLATION]]
	*DR communicates cancellations. A missing Dashboard session is not one.*
</callout>
<callout icon="⏰" color="gray_bg">
	**YOU ARE LATE OR CANNOT ATTEND**
	Call DR now at (407) 614-7454. Do not wait for the start time. Do not arrange cover with another coach on your own unless DR confirms it. Planned days off: two weeks notice by email.
	[[YOUR SCHEDULE]]
	*Day-off request rule and the running-late procedure.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🛠️" color="gray_bg">
	**THE DASHBOARD IS WRONG**
	[[DASHBOARD PROBLEMS]]
	*Offline, missing session, player not on roster, login trouble. Most fixes take 30 seconds; the rest say call DR.*
	[[LOGGING IN]]
	*Email + 6-digit code. If the code does not arrive, this page says why.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	When in doubt: keep the group supervised, keep your count, call DR. Nobody gets in trouble for asking early.
</callout>
