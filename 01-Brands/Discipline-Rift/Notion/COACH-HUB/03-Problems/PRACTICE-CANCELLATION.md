# PRACTICE CANCELLATION
<callout icon="⛈️" color="gray_bg">
	**WHAT DR ALREADY DOES**
	Parents get an email and a text. The school gets a phone call. The missed practice is made up by extending the season.
</callout>
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**YOU DO NOT SEND THOSE MESSAGES.** DR admin notifies the parents. Do not text families yourself — two versions of the same news is how a cancellation turns into a complaint.
</callout>
<callout icon="💬" color="gray_bg">
	**HOW A CANCELLATION REACHES YOU**
	DR communicates it: WhatsApp (main group or your day chat), direct contact, and the schedule. Before every practice, check the current schedule and your chats.
	**A session missing from the Coach Dashboard is not a cancellation.** It is a Dashboard issue. Follow the schedule and call DR at (407) 614-7454 to fix it. Only DR's word cancels a practice.
</callout>
<callout icon="⛈️" color="gray_bg">
	**WEATHER IS NOT CANCELLATION.** Rain: covered space, keep going. Thunder or lightning: indoors, keep going. You adapt; DR cancels.
	[[WEATHER & HEAT]]
</callout>
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	**ALREADY ON CAMPUS WITH PLAYERS WHEN THE CALL COMES?** Players stay supervised and counted. Follow the school's instruction and DR's. Dismissal rules apply in full: every child handed off through the school's process, nobody unaccounted for. Call DR at (407) 614-7454 if anything is unclear.
</callout>
