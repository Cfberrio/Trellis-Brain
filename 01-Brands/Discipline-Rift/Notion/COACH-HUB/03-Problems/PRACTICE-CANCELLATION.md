# PRACTICE CANCELLATION
## WHAT DR ALREADY DOES
When a practice is cancelled inside 24 hours (weather, facility, coach emergency), the DR office runs a set workflow: parents get an email and a text, the school gets a phone call, and the missed practice is made up by extending the season. You do not send those messages. DR notifies the parents, not the coach.
## WHAT THIS PAGE WILL HOLD
- How a coach is told a practice is cancelled, and how to confirm before traveling
- What to do if you are already at the school with kids in front of you and the message never reached you
- What to do when you cannot make a practice: "coach emergency" is a named cancellation trigger and no document says what the coach does
