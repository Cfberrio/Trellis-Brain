# PRACTICE CANCELLATION
<callout icon="⛈️" color="gray_bg">
	**WHAT DR ALREADY DOES**
	Parents get an email and a text. The school gets a phone call. The missed practice is made up by extending the season.
</callout>
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**YOU DO NOT SEND THOSE MESSAGES.** DR admin notifies the parents. Do not text families yourself — two versions of the same news is how a cancellation turns into a complaint.
</callout>
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	**NOT WRITTEN YET** — who makes the call and by when, how you are told, and what you do if you are already on campus with players in front of you. Until that is here: keep the players safe, follow the school's instruction, and contact DR admin immediately.
</callout>
