# PARTNERING WITH PARENTS
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**THE ONE RULE.** Establish a point of connection with every parent: in person at pickup when you can, and through the Coach Dashboard after every practice. Before there is a problem, not because of one.
</callout>
<callout icon="🤝" color="gray_bg">
	**HOW WE TREAT PARENTS**
	- Parents are partners. They sent you their kid; they are on your side until you give them a reason not to be.
	- Build the connection early. Greet them at pickup, learn names, say something real.
	- Give them visibility. A parent who knows what their child is learning this week is a parent who reinforces it at home.
	- Be specific with positives. "Great energy" is fine; "she moved her feet before every contact today" is a parent who tells three friends.
	- Raise concerns early and professionally. Describe what happened, not who the kid is. Never diagnose a child.
	- Serious or repeated situations go to DR. DR steps in only after the parent has already heard from you.
</callout>
<callout icon="✉️" color="gray_bg">
	**THE AFTER-PRACTICE MESSAGE**
	Every practice, before you leave campus, short and personable. Four parts:
	1. **A positive.** One thing the group did well today.
	2. **The focus.** What you worked on.
	3. **Progress or next step.** How the team moved, what comes next week.
	4. **A note, if needed.** Anything parents need to know.
	*Example, not a script:* "Great practice today. We worked on serving control and the team improved in height and direction. We will keep building consistency next week. Great energy from the group today."
	Send it with **Message All** in the Coach Dashboard. Every family gets it individually. It is not a group chat.

	**WHEN A PARENT IS UPSET.** Listen first. Do not argue at the car line and do not promise outcomes you do not control. Say what you saw, say what you will do next practice, and tell them DR will follow up if it needs to. Then **call DR at (407) 614-7454** the same day so DR hears it from you, not from the parent.
</callout>
