	Coach! A kid is hurt or something happened that has to be reported. What you do in the moment, in order. Every **→** below is a door: tap it.
</callout>
# INJURY / INCIDENT
<callout icon="🚑" color="gray_bg">
	**IN THE MOMENT**
	1. **Stop the activity** as needed. The hurt child is your priority; the drill is not.
	2. **Keep the group supervised.** Two coaches: one with the child, one with the team. Alone: bring the team in close, sit them down, keep them in sight.
	3. **Follow the host school's emergency procedure.** Front office and school staff know their building, their nurse, their AED. Use them.
	4. **Call 911** when it is serious: unconscious, not breathing normally, heavy bleeding, suspected broken bone, head knock with confusion or vomiting, allergic reaction. When in doubt, call.
	5. **Notify DR: (407) 614-7454.** The line rings DR leadership. DR contacts the parent when the situation requires it; you do not leave the child to make calls.
	6. **Document it** the same day.
	[[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/Notion/COACH-HUB/04-The-Paperwork/ACCIDENT-REPORT|ACCIDENT REPORT]]
	*File this for any injury or incident, even a small one.*
</callout>
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	**WHAT YOU DO NOT DO.** You are not a medic. Do not give medication, do not diagnose, do not move a child who may have a neck or back injury, do not decide a head knock is fine. School staff and emergency services make medical calls. You keep the child safe and calm, keep the team supervised, and get the right people there.
</callout>
<callout icon="👪" color="gray_bg">
	**PARENTS.** DR handles the parent call for anything serious. For a minor scrape you handled on the spot, tell the parent at pickup and mention it in your after-practice message. The parent should never hear about an incident from their child first.
</callout>
