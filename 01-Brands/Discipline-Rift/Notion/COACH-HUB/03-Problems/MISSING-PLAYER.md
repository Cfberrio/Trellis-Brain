# MISSING PLAYER
<callout icon="🔍" color="gray_bg">
	**THE PROCEDURE**
	1. **Identify who is missing.** Attendance against the Dashboard roster tells you the name. Do not assume absence without checking.
	2. **Ask.** Aftercare staff, the front office, nearby school staff: where might this player be? They usually know: sick today, picked up early, in aftercare.
	3. **Send a responsible player when appropriate.** One responsible player, or two responsible players together, to check the likely spot and come straight back. Tell them exactly where to go and to return directly.
	4. **Keep supervising the rest of the team.** The group stays together, in one place, in your sight, the whole time. You do not leave the group to search.
	5. **Still unconfirmed? Call DR at (407) 614-7454.** The line rings DR leadership so an available leader can respond. Do not wait until the end of practice. Do not let the parent be the one who tells us.
</callout>
<callout icon="⏱️" color="gray_bg">
	**MID-PRACTICE, THE COUNT COMES UP SHORT**
	Stop the drill. Recount. Scan the space, bathroom, water fountain. Ask the players; they usually know. At 60 seconds still short: run the procedure above.
