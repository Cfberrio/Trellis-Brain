# ARRIVAL
<callout icon="⏰" color="gray_bg">
	**1 · BE ON CAMPUS BY THE ARRIVAL TIME ON YOUR SCHEDULE**
	The schedule shows **arrival / start–end**, for example 2:45 / 3:15–4:15. Arrival means already on campus. Everything below fits in that window. It does not fit in five minutes.
	[[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/Notion/COACH-HUB/06-Coach-Operations/YOUR-SCHEDULE|YOUR SCHEDULE]]
	*Where and when. Open the schedule folder from there.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🚗" color="gray_bg">
	**2 · PARK AND CHECK IN**
	Schools run car lines when you arrive. Plan for it, follow school traffic direction, get to parking when safe and permitted. Bring your ID, wear your badge, check in at the front office. Introduce yourself if the person at the desk is new to you: they are the first people you will need if anything goes wrong later.
</callout>
<callout icon="📍" color="gray_bg">
	**3 · FIND WHERE YOUR PLAYERS ARE BEING GATHERED TODAY**
	If the Coach Dashboard shows a meeting spot for your team, go there. Otherwise ask the front office or school staff where your players are being gathered today, and set the team meeting point there. Schools move things; the front desk knows.
</callout>
<callout icon="👋" color="gray_bg">
	**4 · GATHER PLAYERS**
	Greet every player by name, every week. Bring the group together in one place. Start accounting for who is here.
</callout>
<callout icon="🥤" color="gray_bg">
	**5 · BATHROOM, WATER, SNACK. HERE, BEFORE YOU MOVE**
	They just came from a full school day. Handle it at the meeting point, before you walk anywhere.
	**Water bottles filled.** Everyone, not just the ones who ask.
	**Snack eaten here, sitting down.** Not carried to the court, not eaten while walking.
	**Bathroom now.** Send them in pairs, know who went, hold the group until they are back.
	**Hands washed, trash in the bin.** You leave the spot the way you found it. That is what keeps you welcome at the school.
	*Why: one hour is already limited. A child who is thirsty, hungry or needs the bathroom is not going to learn, and a child who asks mid-drill costs you the whole group. Ten minutes here buys you a clean hour.*
</callout>
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	**Food is never shared between players.** You will not know who has an allergy. A child eats what their own adult sent, and nothing else.
</callout>
<callout icon="✅" color="gray_bg">
	**6 · TAKE ATTENDANCE AND SET YOUR NUMBER**
	In the Coach Dashboard, per player, before the first drill. Load the Dashboard and screenshot the roster email before you enter the school: there is no signal inside. If it shows `offline: true`, mark attendance on the screenshot and enter it in the Dashboard once you have signal. Cannot get it in? Call DR at (407) 614-7454.
	Attendance gives you today's number. Say it to the team: "We have fourteen today." Write it down. That number governs every count for the rest of the session.
	[[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/Notion/COACH-HUB/06-Coach-Operations/TAKING-ATTENDANCE|TAKING ATTENDANCE]]
	*The eight taps, and what to do with no signal.*
	[[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/Notion/COACH-HUB/02-Every-Session/HEAD-COUNTS|HEAD COUNTS]]
	*Count at every transition.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🔍" color="gray_bg">
	**7 · RESOLVE ANYONE MISSING BEFORE YOU START**
	Do not start practice with an unexplained absence. Ask the front office and aftercare. Follow the Missing Player procedure.
	[[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/Notion/COACH-HUB/02-Every-Session/ROSTER-AND-PLAYER-ACCOUNTABILITY|ROSTER & PLAYER ACCOUNTABILITY]]
	*Late arrivals, missing players, a player not on the list.*
	[[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/Notion/COACH-HUB/03-Problems/MISSING-PLAYER|MISSING PLAYER]]
	*Ask, send, supervise, call.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🚶" color="gray_bg">
	**8 · WALK TO THE PRACTICE SPACE AS ONE GROUP**
	Count before you leave the meeting point. Count again the moment you land. Two coaches: one leads, one takes the back. Alone: you take the back, where you can see all of them.
</callout>
<callout icon="🧱" color="gray_bg">
	**9 · THE SAFETY CHECK BEFORE THE FIRST BALL**
	Ninety seconds, before you teach anything.
	- **The space.** Walk it. Wet patches, loose equipment, open doors, anything on the floor that should not be there.
	- **The boundaries.** Cones, chalk or existing lines. Show the players where practice stops. Name the edges out loud.
	- **Water.** Everyone knows where the fountain is before they need it. Break every 20 minutes, and on request always.
	- **Your exit.** Know where you would take the group if you had to leave the space quickly.
	- **Your phone.** On you, charged, not in a bag across the gym.
	- **Equipment.** Set for the first activity. Next setup in your head before this one ends.
</callout>
## WHAT DR HAS COMMITTED
These are live promises to schools and parents. Your arrival routine keeps them.
- Coaches are on campus by the arrival time on the schedule.
- Coaches wear badges and are Level 2 background screened through OCPS.
- Check-in and attendance are recorded at every single session.
