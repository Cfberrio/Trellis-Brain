# ARRIVAL
<callout icon="⏰" color="gray_bg">
	**BE ON CAMPUS AT THE ARRIVAL TIME ON YOUR SCHEDULE.**
	Your roster shows it as **arrival / start–end** — for example 2:45 / 3:15–4:15. The arrival time is when you are on campus, not when practice starts. Everything below fits in that window. It does not fit in five minutes.
	[[YOUR ROSTER]]
</callout>
## THE SEQUENCE
<callout icon="🚪" color="gray_bg">
	**1 · CHECK IN AT THE FRONT DESK**
	Bring your ID. Wear your badge. Introduce yourself if the person at the desk is new to you — they are the first people you will need if anything goes wrong later.
	**Confirm your meeting spot while you are there.** Your roster names it; the front desk knows if the school moved it today.
</callout>
<callout icon="🚪" color="gray_bg">
	**2 · GO TO THE MEETING SPOT AND GATHER YOUR PLAYERS**
	The meeting spot is named on your roster — cafeteria, gym lobby, media center. It differs by school. Greet every player by name, every week. This is where belonging starts and it costs you nothing.
</callout>
<callout icon="🚪" color="gray_bg">
	**3 · TAKE ATTENDANCE IN THE COACH DASHBOARD**
	Per student, saved immediately. Not on paper, not from memory afterwards.
	**No signal?** Mark up a screenshot of the roster PDF and enter it as soon as you have service.
	[[TAKING ATTENDANCE]]
</callout>
<callout icon="🚪" color="gray_bg">
	**4 · CHASE ANYONE MISSING BEFORE YOU START**
	Ask the front desk first. If the child is at school but not with you, check aftercare. Do not start practice with an unexplained absence.
</callout>
<callout icon="🥤" color="gray_bg">
	**5 · WATER, SNACK AND BATHROOM — ALL OF IT HAPPENS HERE**
	They just came from a full school day. Handle it at the meeting spot, before you walk anywhere.
	**Water bottles filled.** Everyone, not just the ones who ask.
	**Snack eaten here, sitting down, before you move.** Not carried to the court and not eaten while walking.
	**Bathroom now.** Send them in pairs, know who went, and hold the group until they are back.
	**Then hands washed and trash in the bin.** You leave the meeting spot the way you found it — that is what keeps you welcome at the school.
</callout>
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	**Food is never shared between players.** You will not know who has an allergy. A child eats what their own adult sent, and nothing else.
</callout>
<callout icon="🔢" color="gray_bg">
	**6 · SET YOUR NUMBER AND SAY IT OUT LOUD**
	Attendance gives you today's number. Say it to the team: "We have fourteen today." Write it down. That number governs every count for the rest of the session.
	[[HEAD COUNTS]]
	*When you count, how you count, and what to do when the number is wrong.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🔢" color="gray_bg">
	**7 · WALK TO THE PRACTICE SPACE AS ONE GROUP — AND COUNT ON ARRIVAL**
	Count before you leave the meeting spot. Count again the moment you land. Two coaches: one leads, one takes the back. Alone: you take the back, where you can see all of them.
</callout>
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**Why the needs come first.** A child who is thirsty, hungry or needs the bathroom is not going to learn a platform, and a child who asks mid-drill costs you the whole group's attention. Ten minutes at the meeting spot buys you a clean hour on the court.
</callout>
## THE SAFETY CHECK BEFORE THE FIRST BALL
Ninety seconds, before you teach anything.
- **The space.** Walk it. Wet patches, loose equipment, open doors, anything on the floor that should not be there.
- **The boundaries.** Show the players where practice stops. Name the edges out loud so they hear them.
- **Water.** Everyone knows where the fountain is before they need it. Break every 20 minutes, and on request always.
- **Your exit.** Know where you would take the group if you had to leave the space quickly.
- **Your phone.** On you, charged, not in a bag across the gym.
## WHAT DR HAS COMMITTED
These are live promises to schools and parents. Your arrival routine keeps them.
- Coaches are on campus by the arrival time on the schedule.
- Coaches wear badges and are Level 2 background screened through OCPS.
- Check-in and attendance are recorded at every single session.
