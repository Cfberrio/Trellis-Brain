	Coach! Your team is never one level. This is how one Practice Design serves beginners and advanced players in the same hour, and how two coaches share it. Every **→** below is a door: tap it.
</callout>
# COACHING DIFFERENT LEVELS / CO-COACHING
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**THE RULE.** Same Practice Design. Same learning objective. Different rung or constraint when needed. You never run two practices; you run one practice at two difficulties.
</callout>
<callout icon="🎚️" color="gray_bg">
	**THE DIALS YOU TURN**
	Same skill, same challenge, one dial moves:
	- shorter vs longer distance
	- larger vs smaller target
	- stationary vs moving entry
	- slower vs faster feed
	- no pressure vs pressure (defender, clock, score)
	- one decision vs multiple decisions
	- more time vs less time
	A beginner serves from 4 metres to a big zone. An advanced player serves from the line to a cone. Same drill, same objective, same coaching.
</callout>
<callout icon="🪜" color="gray_bg">
	**MIXED-LEVEL GROUPS**
	- Start each player, or each line, where success is possible today.
	- Advanced players move faster up the same ladder. Let them.
	- Beginners may stay longer on an early rung. That is fine.
	- Do not force every player to finish the same rung.
	- Do not split into two unrelated curricula. Split lines and constraints, not the objective.
	[[PROGRESSION LADDER]]
	*The sample ladder. Same rungs, different dial per line.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🤝" color="gray_bg">
	**TWO COACHES: BEFORE PRACTICE**
	A two-minute call or message. Agree:
	- the learning objective for the day;
	- the first setup;
	- who brings which equipment;
	- who leads which part, where each coach stands;
	- who owns the head count and the back of the group when moving.
</callout>
<callout icon="🤝" color="gray_bg">
	**TWO COACHES: DURING PRACTICE**
	- Two coaches means more reps, not more lecture. Split lines or run two stations; do not both stand and explain.
	- Same method, same goal, same cues. Players should hear one language.
	- Say it out loud when a player moves lines or rungs, so both coaches know.
	- Count changes are announced to each other immediately.
</callout>
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	Plan the objective and two starting rungs before you arrive. Turn one dial per line. Two coaches: agree the plan before the car door closes.
</callout>
