# LIFE LESSON
**Objective**: Connect life skills and values to the practice and the sport, helping players understand and apply these lessons both on and off the court.
1. **Introduce the Word of the Week**: Introduce a key life lesson or value as the "Word of the Week." Choose something relevant to sport and personal development: "Integrity," "Resilience," "Responsibility."
2. **Explain the Meaning**: Give a brief explanation of what the word means and how it applies to both sports and life. Example: **Word**: Integrity. **Explanation**: "Integrity means doing the right thing, even when no one is watching. In sports, it's about being honest, playing by the rules, and respecting your opponents. In life, integrity is about being true to your values and making good choices, even when it's hard."
3. **Discuss the Life Lesson**: Ask: "Why is integrity important in sports? Can anyone give an example of a situation where they had to show integrity?" Share a personal or famous example that demonstrates it.
4. **Assign a Task**: Ask players to look for the word in their own lives during the week and come back ready to share their understanding and an example of someone demonstrating it.
5. **Wrap-Up**: Encourage players to practice the lesson throughout the week, in sport and personal life. These lessons make them better athletes and better people.
