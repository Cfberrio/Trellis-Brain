# YOUR ROSTER
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	**Site details are per school, not global.** Nothing in this hub can tell you your meeting spot or your arrival time. Only your roster can. If you did not get one, ask before you drive out — do not guess and do not copy another coach's site.
</callout>

## THE MEETING SPOT
This is where your session actually begins.
**It is named on your roster.** Cafeteria, gym lobby, media center — it differs by school.
**Double-check it with the front desk on day one.** Schools move it and do not always tell us.
**It is where the needs get handled** — water, snack and bathroom, before you walk anywhere. See [[ARRIVAL]].
**It is where your first count happens**, and the number everything else is measured against.
## ATTENDANCE AND THE DASHBOARD
**Check players in** at the meeting spot, per student, saved immediately.
**Submit final attendance before you leave campus.** Not that night, not the next morning.
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	**No signal at your school?** Mark up a screenshot of the roster PDF on your phone and enter it in the dashboard as soon as you have service. The paper version is a bridge, never the record.
</callout>
## HOW EACH CHILD GOES HOME
Your roster tells you which of these each child is. Learn it in week 1.
<table header-row="true">
<tr>
<td>Pickup method</td>
<td>What you do</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Car rider</td>
<td>Release one by one at the loop. Visually confirm the ride every time.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Aftercare</td>
<td>Walk them directly to aftercare and hand them over to a staff member.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Walker or biker</td>
<td>Follow the pickup method written on the roster for that child. If it is not written, they do not leave on their own — ask the front desk and call DR admin.</td>
