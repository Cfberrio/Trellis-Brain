# ZONES GAME
**Field Awareness, Scoring Structure & Situational Intelligence**
---
# 1. Field Overview (Complete Zone Structure)
Walk players through the field from an overhead perspective before beginning.
Use consistent numbering every practice.
---
## Zone 1 – End Zones
**Purpose:** Touchdowns
- Ball must cross into this zone to score.
- Both ends of the field are Zone 1.
---
## Zone 2 – Goal-Line No-Run Zones
**Purpose:** Must Pass
- Located just outside each end zone.
- If the ball is spotted in this zone:
	- You cannot run.
	- You must pass.
These zones are always active when the ball is placed there.
---
## Zone 3 – One-Point Try Line
**Purpose:** 1-Point Conversion
- Ball is placed on the far edge of the goal-line no-run zone.
- Must pass.
- Cannot run.
---
## Zone 4 – Two-Point Try Line
**Purpose:** 2-Point Conversion
- Located 5 yards behind the 1-point line.
- You may run or pass.
---
### Field Order Facing the End Zone:
Two-Point Line → One-Point Line → No-Run Zone → End Zone
That logic stays consistent.
---
# Zone 5 – Middle No-Run Zone (Grades 4–5 Only)
![](https://dt5602vnjxv0c.cloudfront.net/portals/83227/images/field.png)
**Applies primarily to 4th & 5th grade.**
2nd & 3rd graders can still learn it conceptually.
### Location:
- 10 yards wide total.
- Extends 5 yards on either side of midfield (the 50-yard line).
### Purpose:
1. Functions as a no-run zone **only if the ball is spotted inside it.**
2. Contains the first down line (midfield).
3. You must cross midfield to earn a first down.
### Clarification:
The middle no-run zone works exactly like the goal-line no-run zones **when the ball is placed inside it.**
- If the ball starts inside Zone 5 → must pass.
- You cannot run the ball out of that zone.
- If you receive a handoff outside and run through it → it does not activate.
### The only difference from other no-run zones:
It includes the first down marker.
That is the only functional difference.
---
# Additional Field Terms (Coach Reference)
These are not numbered zones but are important for vocabulary clarity:
### Open Field Area
- Space between no-run zones.
- Normal run or pass allowed.
### First Down Line
- Located at midfield.
- Must cross to reset downs.
This ensures coaches using the curriculum understand full field language.
---
# 2. Lesson Objective
Players will:
1. Identify each zone by number and function.
2. Move quickly to the correct zone when called.
3. Explain what is allowed in that zone.
4. Connect field position to scoring and first down decisions.
---
# 3. How to Run the Game
## Phase 1 – Recognition Speed
Coach calls:
> “ZONE 5!”
Players:
- Sprint.
- Touch correct line within 5 seconds.
Out if:
- Wrong zone.
- Too slow.
Cue:
> “Sprint first.”
---
## Phase 2 – Add Rule Understanding
After players arrive, ask:
> “What happens here?”
Expected answers:
Zone 1 → “Touchdown.”
Zone 2 → “Must pass.”
Zone 3 → “1-point try — must pass.”
Zone 4 → “2-point try — run or pass.”
Zone 5 → “Middle no-run zone. Must pass if ball is spotted here. Also first down line.”
If correct:
- Earn point or bring back one teammate.
If incorrect:
- Out.

# 1. Field Overview (Corrected Zone Logic)
Before starting the game, walk players through the field from an eagle’s-eye view.
We are defining zones as follows:
---
## Zone 1 – End Zones
**Purpose:** Touchdowns
- This is where we score.
- Ball must cross into this zone to count.
- Both ends of the field are Zone 1.
---
## Zone 2 – No-Run Zones
**Purpose:** Must Pass
- Located just outside each end zone.
- If the ball is spotted in this zone:
	- You cannot run.
	- You must throw a pass.
This zone forces decision-making under constraint.
---
## Zone 3 – One-Point Try Line
**Purpose:** 1-Point Conversion
- This is set on the far edge of the no-run zone.
- You must pass from here.
- Ball is placed on the edge of the no-run zone for the 1-point try.
Clarification:
- The 1-point try is from the farthest back no-run zone line.
- It is always a pass.
---
## Zone 4 – Two-Point Try Line
**Purpose:** 2-Point Conversion
- Located 5 yards behind the 1-point line.
- From this line:
	- You may run or pass.
Field Order Facing the End Zone:
> Two-Point Line → One-Point Line → No-Run Zone → End Zone
That is the correct sequence.
---
## (Optional for Older Groups)
If using midfield first-down marker, that can be labeled separately, but your warm-up Lens Game structure focuses on the scoring-side zones listed above.

# 2. Lesson Objective
Players will:
1. Identify each zone by number and purpose.
2. Move quickly to the correct zone when called.
3. Explain what is allowed in that zone.
4. Connect scoring rules to field position.
This builds field intelligence — not chaos.
---
# 3. Why This Game Works
Players develop:
- Field orientation
- Rule clarity under time pressure
- Conversion awareness
- Situational scoring understanding
- Competitive urgency
Game transfer:
- Fewer illegal runs in no-run zones
- Faster extra point alignment
- Smarter conversion decisions
- Clearer understanding of scoring structure
---
# 4. How to Run the Game
---
## Phase 1 – Recognition Speed
Coach calls a zone:
> “ZONE 3!”
Players must:
- Sprint immediately.
- Touch the correct line within 5 seconds.
Coach counts:
> “5–4–3–2–1 STOP!”
Out if:
- Wrong zone
- Too slow
- Not touching the line
Coaching cue:
> “Sprint first. Think while moving.”
---
## Phase 2 – Add Rule Understanding
After players arrive, ask:
> “What happens here?”
Expected answers:
- Zone 1 → “Touchdown.”
- Zone 2 → “Must pass.”
- Zone 3 → “One-point try — must pass.”
- Zone 4 → “Two-point try — run or pass.”
If correct:
- Earn 1 point
	OR
- Bring back one teammate (team version)
If incorrect:
- Player eliminated.
---
# 5. Team Jailbreak Structure
Split into:
- Red Team
- Blue Team
Out if:
- Wrong zone
- Slow
- Incorrect explanation
Bring back rule:
- Correct explanation brings back ONE teammate only.
Do not allow full resets.
Game ends when one team is eliminated.

# 8. Structured Rounds (To Prevent Chaos)
Round 1: Speed only
Round 2: Add questions
Round 3: Harder questions
Final Round: Championship
Sideline players:
- Quick feet
- Ball security holds
No standing still.
