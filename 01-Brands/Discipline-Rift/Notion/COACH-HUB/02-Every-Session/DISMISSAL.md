# DISMISSAL
"Dismissal is the part that goes wrong when you're rushed." That line is from your own onboarding message, and it is the reason this page exists.
## WHAT DR HAS ALREADY PROMISED
These commitments are live in parent and school communication today. This page has to make them keepable.
- "After dismissal, we gather the kids, run practice at the school facility, and at the end we direct car riders to pickup and aftercare kids to aftercare."
- "Check-in and check-out are recorded. Participants are released only to authorized parties or to coordinated aftercare."
- Rule: DR notifies the parents, not the coach. A coach fielding logistics is a coach not preparing a practice.
## WHAT THIS PAGE WILL HOLD
- The holding pattern: where the group waits and how car riders and aftercare kids are separated
- How you verify an authorized adult, and what you say to one you do not recognize
- The late parent threshold: how many minutes, then who you call, in what order
- The last child rule: you do not leave until every child is collected, and what to do if the office is closed
