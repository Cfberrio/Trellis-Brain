# ROSTER & PLAYER ACCOUNTABILITY
<callout icon="📲" color="gray_bg">
	**YOUR CURRENT ROSTER**
	**Use the Coach Dashboard as your primary roster and attendance tool during practice.** [https://disciplinerift.com/coach/](https://disciplinerift.com/coach/)
	A PDF roster may also contain the live roster. It is secondary: during practice you operate from the Dashboard.
	Load the Dashboard before you enter the school.
	If the Dashboard shows a **meeting spot** or another team-specific detail, follow what it shows.
	The schedule, not the roster, owns your address and arrival / start–end times.
	[[COACH DASHBOARD]]
	[[YOUR SCHEDULE]]
</callout>
<callout icon="✅" color="gray_bg">
	**ATTENDANCE IS ACCOUNTABILITY, NOT ADMIN**
	- Every listed player gets accounted for. Present, or you know why not.
	- An unexplained absence gets actively checked, not marked and forgotten.
	- Today's number is the basis for every head count until dismissal.
	- Attendance is recorded per player in the Dashboard before the first drill, and complete before you leave campus.
	[[TAKING ATTENDANCE]]
	*The button-by-button steps.*
	[[HEAD COUNTS]]
	*Counting at every transition.*
</callout>
<callout icon="⏰" color="gray_bg">
	**LATE ARRIVAL**
	A player joins after you started: update attendance in the Dashboard, update your number out loud immediately, tell your co-coach if you have one.
</callout>
<callout icon="🔍" color="gray_bg">
	**MISSING PLAYER**
	A rostered player is not with the team: you find out why. Do not just mark absent and move on.
	1. Identify who is missing.
	2. Ask aftercare, the front office or nearby school staff where the player may be.
	3. When appropriate, send one responsible player, or two responsible players together, to check and come straight back.
	4. Keep supervising the rest of the team the whole time.
	5. Status still unconfirmed: **call DR at (407) 614-7454**.
</callout>
<callout icon="📋" color="gray_bg">
	**PLAYER SAYS THEY ARE REGISTERED BUT IS NOT ON THE DASHBOARD**
	Do not resolve it yourself. **Call DR at (407) 614-7454** with the player's name and team so enrollment can be confirmed. Follow DR's direction from there. The player's word alone is not confirmation, and an older list does not override the Dashboard during practice unless DR says so.
</callout>
<callout icon="📶" color="gray_bg">
	**DASHBOARD CONNECTIVITY**
	Load the Dashboard before entering the school. If it shows **`offline: true`**, or you cannot reliably complete attendance, **call DR at (407) 614-7454**. That line rings DR leadership so an available leader can respond. Calling does not mean practice is canceled.
	[[DASHBOARD PROBLEMS]]
</callout>
