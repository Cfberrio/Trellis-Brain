## BASE GAME
Demonstrate the game. Keep the base rules simple enough that the constraint, not the rules explanation, creates the learning.
## USE IT TO CHECK
Skill or decision · skill or decision · connection of skills.
## CONSTRAINT MENU
Space · target · direction · pressure · scoring · role · starting position · touches or reps · communication.
## OBSERVABLE EVIDENCE
What must the coach see to say "they learned it"?
## IF IT BREAKS
**FREEZE → CUE → REPLAY**
Use the shortest correction that lets players immediately try the same situation again. Do not turn CFU into a lecture.
## PROGRESS THE CHECK
If players succeed, what one condition changes next?
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	Change one condition, not three. If everything changes at once you learn nothing about what they know.
</callout>
