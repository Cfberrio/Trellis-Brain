# PROGRESSION LADDER
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	The Progression Ladder organizes how a skill grows during one practice and across the six-week season. **Every rung: Skill + Problem + Gamified Challenge.** All three belong together.
</callout>

## HOW THE LADDER WORKS
![How to read one step — skill on top, problem underneath, gamified challenge as the solution](https://prod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/6f6cfb08-0636-44bc-a088-3627f4d72bee/8784fdf1-8384-439d-aa5f-5a844db9d3e0/ladder-anatomy.png) <!-- notion-hosted, URL expires -->
<callout icon="🪜" color="gray_bg">
	**STEP 1**
	**Skill:** name it
	**Problem:** what can players not do yet?
	**Challenge:** what game or scored task gives them repeated chances to solve it?
</callout>
<callout icon="🪜" color="gray_bg">
	**STEP 2**
	**Skill:** name it
	**New problem:** what became visible once Step 1 improved?
	**Challenge:** keep the task playful, measurable, and connected to the problem.
</callout>
<callout icon="🪜" color="gray_bg">
	**STEP 3+**
	**Skill(s):** two skills, connected
	**Problem:** a later rung can revisit an old skill under a new condition, or connect two skills players already know.
	**Challenge:** increase realism without jumping ahead of understanding.
</callout>
## INSIDE EVERY RUNG
**NAME THE PROBLEM → SHOW IT → CHALLENGE → COACH REPS → VARIATION → CHECK**
1. **Name the problem positively.** Turn "we can't do this" into the next challenge.
2. **Demonstrate.** Show the movement and narrate the few things players should notice.
3. **Run a Gamified Challenge.** Give the problem a target, score, role, or win condition.
4. **Coach every rep.** Specific praise plus one actionable correction.
5. **Add variation after success.** Change angle, distance, direction, target, entry, role, space, pressure, or sequence.
6. **Check.** Look for observable evidence before deciding the next rung.
## A NEW RUNG DOES NOT ALWAYS MEAN A NEW SKILL
<callout icon="🔁" color="gray_bg">
	**SAME SKILL, NEW PROBLEM**
	Pass contact → move feet → reach ball → target pass.
	*The skill stays. The developmental problem changes.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🔁" color="gray_bg">
	**OLD SKILL, NEW CONDITION**
	Pass a coach toss → later receive a serve.
	*The player retrieves the same skill under a more representative entry.*
</callout>
Introduce a new technical skill when the success of the previous rung creates a reason for it. Teach **one new skill at a time**, then let skills accumulate.

## SERIAL PRACTICE
The season should feel like connected sport logic, not isolated topics. A conceptual sequence can look like:
**Skill A → Skill A under a new condition → Skill B → A + B → Skill C → A returns under C**
The order must make sense to the sport. The goal is repeated retrieval and eventual transfer.
## GOLDEN TICKET / SPECIAL PLAYER
Use this as an optional role-upgrade mechanic, not a random prize.
<callout icon="🎫" color="gray_bg">
	**EXAMPLE**
	3 successful reps to target = Golden Ticket → the player becomes a Special Player → harder entry, new target role, setter / QB / server role, or another constraint.
	*The reward creates the next learning opportunity.*
</callout>
