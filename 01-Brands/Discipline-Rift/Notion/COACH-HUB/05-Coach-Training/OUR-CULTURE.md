# OUR CULTURE
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**CULTURE: THE WAY WE DO THINGS, HOW WE CONNECT AS PEOPLE**
	> `WE ARE COACHABLE!`
	**“We are coaches with attitude willing to discover our best version.”**
	That is the one line. Everything below is what it looks like on a Tuesday at 3:15.
</callout>
<callout icon="🧢" color="gray_bg">
	**AROUND KIDS**
	- **We are the coach, they are the passengers.** Our job is to take them where they want to go, where we know they can go, and where they cannot get on their own. Being a coach is teaching love for the sport.
	- **We start where they can succeed.** The ladder is a sample. A kid who wins the first rung stays; a kid who fails it three times leaves the sport. Most of the 15 out of 20 who quit never had a coach who met them where they were.
	- **We are Friendly first.** Greet every player by name, with a smile, before the first rep. A kid who feels seen tries harder.
	- **We are Upfront.** Clear expectations, honest feedback, short cues. No sarcasm, no shouting, no silent treatment.
	- **We are Enthusiastic.** Our energy sets the tone. If we are flat, they are flat.
	- **We build an irresistible environment.** Fun is not the reward for learning. Fun is how they learn.
</callout>
<callout icon="👪" color="gray_bg">
	**AROUND PARENTS**
	- **Parents hear from us before they hear from their kid.** One short update after every practice: one positive, what we worked on, what comes next.
	- **We never diagnose a child.** We describe what we saw and what we are doing about it. We do not label a kid as behind, difficult or gifted.
	- **We are Upfront with parents too.** A minor scrape gets mentioned at pickup. Anything serious goes through DR, not through us alone.
	- **We protect the relationship.** A parent who trusts the coach re-enrolls. A parent who is surprised does not.
</callout>
<callout icon="🤝" color="gray_bg">
	**AROUND EACH OTHER**
	- **We are Humble.** There is always more to learn, from a fellow coach and from the players themselves. We share what works. We ask when we do not know.
	- **We are Hungry.** We show up wanting this practice to be better than the last one. For the players and for ourselves.
	- **We are Smart.** Emotional intelligence in every interaction: aware of our own state, aware of theirs, resolving conflict in a way that strengthens the relationship.
	- **We are Efficient and Driven.** We respect each other's hour. On time, prepared, in the group chats, responsive. A coach who is late makes a co-coach solve two problems.
	- **We ask for help early.** A problem at 3:20 is easier than a problem at 4:10. Calling DR is not weakness, it is the culture.
	- **We are coachable.** We take feedback the way we ask players to take it: hear it, try it, report back.
</callout>
