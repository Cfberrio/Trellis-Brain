# READING SHELF
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**HOW TO USE THIS SHELF.** One book at a time. Read a chapter, pick one idea, try it at your next practice. If it works, it goes in your 1% Solutions. If it does not, you learned something about your players. Start with the first one: it is where most of the DR coaching language comes from.
</callout>
## THE SHELF
<callout icon="/icons/gradebook_green.svg" color="gray_bg">
	**START HERE**
	**The Coach's Guide to Teaching** — Doug Lemov
	*The source behind most of the DR teaching language: cues, check for understanding, retrieval, practice design. The PDF is at the bottom of this page.*
</callout>
<callout icon="/icons/brain_green.svg" color="gray_bg">
	**HOW PLAYERS LEARN**
	**Practice Perfect: 42 Rules for Getting Better at Getting Better** — Doug Lemov, Erica Woolway, Katie Yezzi
	*How to design reps so the skill actually sticks. The book behind "high reps, short cues, fast transitions".*
	**The Talent Code** — Daniel Coyle
	*Why deep practice at the edge of ability beats talent. Explains why the ladder starts where players can succeed, not where you wish they were.*
	**Mindset** — Carol Dweck
	*Growth mindset versus fixed mindset. The science behind "We are coachable" and behind praising effort over outcome.*
</callout>
<callout icon="⛰️" color="gray_bg">
	**HOW TEAMS AND CULTURE WORK**
	**The Culture Code** — Daniel Coyle
	*What makes a group safe, connected and willing to try. Read this before you decide a kid "has a bad attitude".*
	**Legacy: What the All Blacks Can Teach Us About the Business of Life** — James Kerr
	*Humility, sweeping the sheds, leaving the jersey better than you found it. Closest thing in print to the DR core values.*
	**Wooden on Leadership** — John Wooden with Steve Jamison
	*The Pyramid of Success and a coach who defined winning as effort, not score. The DR Formula owes him a lot.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	Pick one. Read one chapter this week. Bring one idea to Thursday's practice.
</callout>
