## WHAT REPEATS EVERY YEAR
Five things have been taught in more than one conference. They are the spine of how DR coaches, and they are not up for reinvention.
<callout icon="🚌" color="gray_bg">
	**THE ORIGIN OF “COACH”**: Hungarian *kocsi*, a wagon, then a bus. Something that takes you where you cannot get alone. Taught in 2023 and again in 2025.
</callout>
<callout icon="⛽" color="gray_bg">
	**FUELED**: named in 2023, defined in 2024, repeated in 2025. The behavioural standard.
</callout>
<callout icon="🏷️" color="gray_bg">
	**HUMBLE · HUNGRY · SMART**: the same three core values in a second vocabulary. Humility, perseverance, adaptability. 2024 and 2025.
</callout>
<callout icon="❤️" color="gray_bg">
	**TEACHING LOVE**: 2023's retention statistic and 2024's Chicago study land in the same place. Passion retains players, technique alone does not.
</callout>
<callout icon="📈" color="gray_bg">
	**SMALL IMPROVEMENT, COMPOUNDED**: 2024's M+/M−/M+ deep practice becomes 2025's one hundred 1% solutions. Same idea, sharper name.
</callout>
