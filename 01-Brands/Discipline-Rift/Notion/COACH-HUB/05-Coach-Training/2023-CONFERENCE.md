Diego's session, never written into a topic page. What the slides carried:

- **Impact** — group dynamics can make or break a class. What do we do about it?
- **Tactics** — small groups · small group goals · learning plus fun

### TAKE ACTION!
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	Only **38%** of kids ages 5–11 stick with the sport they currently play. In a class of 20, roughly **6** will stay with it.
</callout>
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
