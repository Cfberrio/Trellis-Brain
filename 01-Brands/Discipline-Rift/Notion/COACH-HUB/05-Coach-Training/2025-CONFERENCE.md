<callout icon="3️⃣" color="gray_bg">
	**SESSION 3 — PRACTICE WITH PURPOSE, DEVELOPMENTAL BY DESIGN** · Michaela Franklin
</callout>
**What makes a great practice**
1. **Intentional and structured** — goal-oriented, with a clear plan for progression
2. **Diverse styles** — retrieval, spaced, blocked, serial and randomized practice
3. **Feedback-driven** — continuous and constructive throughout
4. **Planned and organized** — 1–2 hours of planning for a 3-hour practice
**The five practice styles**
- **Retrieval** — actively recalling learned material. *Players explain a rule mid-drill, or answer “what does proper passing posture look like?”*
- **Spaced** — distributing sessions over time. *Setting on Mon/Wed/Fri instead of one intense block.*
- **Blocked** — one skill for an extended period, ideal for initial acquisition. *Shuffling to pass in an unbroken sequence.*
- **Serial** — interleaving distinct activities to build adaptability. *Alternating passing and hitting drills.*
- **Randomized** — unpredictable timing and patterns to promote real-game decisions. *Drill #10 Scramble.*
**The daily practice structure** — the same flow every session, to reduce anxiety and foster development
1. **Pre-practice brief** — huddle, walk the full plan drill by drill, invite clarifying questions *(credit: communication coach Betsy Butterick)*
2. **Dynamic warm-up** — sport-specific, ball-incorporated, early contacts, fun
3. **Skill training** — focused positional work in groups
4. **Combination training** — integrating positions for collaborative skills and transitions
5. **Game play** — structured drills targeting specific team needs, rarely just “games to 25”
6. **Cool down** — player-led where possible; even little tikes can lead with guidance
7. **Post-practice debrief** — 3 things learned, 2 that went well, 1 to work on. Loud enough for parents to hear. Be intentional even in the goodbye: *“Bye Tommy, way to nail your shuffling today!”*
**Pace of drill** — high-intensity, high-repetition, minimum downtime. Continuous ball contact, multiple balls in play, feeders distributing fast.
