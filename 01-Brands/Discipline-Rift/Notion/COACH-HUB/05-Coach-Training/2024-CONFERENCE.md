<callout icon="2️⃣" color="gray_bg">
	**TALK 2 — COACHES TRAINING**
	The shape: **analogy → connect the dots → actual content.**
</callout>
**The analogies**
- **The Golf Ball** — in 15 seconds, write three experiences that have impacted your life on the golf ball. Early golf balls were smooth and travelled poorly; players noticed that dents made the ball fly further, and manufacturers eventually added dimples on purpose. A modern ball has 300–500. Your experiences are your dimples. What propelled you is what you reflect on.
- **Row the Boat** — row with direction, power and speed. The oar is your source of energy: purpose, mission, vision. We are all in the same boat, each with a unique oar. Every day you coach you decide: row or don't. Not rowing costs the whole boat.
**The content**
- **You have two goals** — teach life lessons by recognising and being intentional about a teachable moment, and develop talent by communicating complex ideas specifically: perform the move, then break it into its actions.
- **Talent = Skill + Passion** — skill grows through deep practice; passion ignites the unconscious energy for that growth. Teach the skill with exactitude, inspire with passion through information.
- **The M+ / M− / M+ pattern** — demonstrate the correct way, explain the how and why, show the incorrect way, remodel the correct way, then repeat and reinforce. Seeking small improvement, one day at a time.
- **Creating an irresistible environment** — make initial learning pleasant and rewarding, include more playful activity, give positive reinforcement, adjust performance, set high standards.
- **The DR Coach Approach** — perceptiveness (trained eyes, curious about mistakes) · GPS reflex (short, specific, high-definition bursts, never "what if / maybe") · motivation man ("got another one in you!") · probing strategy (if A doesn't work, change approach).
**The research** — a 1980s University of Chicago study of world-class tennis players, pianists, mathematicians, neurologists and sculptors found most started with average instructors. What drove them was ignited and sustained passion. Teaching love is what keeps a learner hooked.

### REFERENCE READING — CONSCIOUS COACHING
<details>
<summary>Chapter notes from the 2024 FORMAT doc. Chapter 5 is missing from the source.</summary>
	### CH 1 — THE VALUE OF CONSCIOUS COACHING
	- Address the psychological and emotional needs of the individual. People have unique personalities and different experiences, so coaches must adapt. The coach–athlete relationship develops through mutual respect, trust and effective communication. Athletes must feel they belong and have a purpose — and so must coaches.
	### CH 2 — MOTIVATION
	- Tailor motivation to the individual. Intrinsic motivation drives passion and long-term sustainability; extrinsic drives short-term goals and milestones. Intrinsic motivation is nurtured through the coach's attitude and behaviour toward the class. Clear achievable goals give players a horizon. Consistent constructive feedback is necessary. Encourage players to motivate each other.
	### CH 3 — ADAPTABILITY
	- All athletes are unique. Coaches must adapt to different groups. Reflect on classes and adapt continuously.
	### CH 4 — TRUST
	- Without trust, athletes will not immerse themselves in the lesson or the coach. Be on time, be specific, be prepared. Athletes expect a reliable source of knowledge. Progress builds trust, for parents and players. Show empathy — it proves you understand them.
	### CH 6 — VALUES & STANDARDS
	- Instill values; the main purpose is to teach fundamental values for life. Responsibility, perseverance, discipline. Shared mission and vision. Everyone should understand their role and what is required. Hold everyone to a high standard. Use assessments, feedback and reviews. Failures are opportunities, not endpoints.
</details>
