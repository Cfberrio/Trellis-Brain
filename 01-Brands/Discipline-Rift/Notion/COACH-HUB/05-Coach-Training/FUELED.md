# FUELED
> <span underline="true">**6-Letter Acronym for: how we behave! How we approach coaching others.**</span>
	## **1. Friendly**
	- **Be Approachable**: Always greet your players with a smile and take an interest in their well-being. A friendly coach creates a positive atmosphere.
	## **2. Upfront**
	- **Communicate Clearly**: Be honest and transparent with your team. Let them know what’s expected and give straightforward feedback.
	## **3. Enthusiastic**
	- **Inspire with Energy**: Bring excitement and energy to every practice. Your passion will motivate your players to push themselves and enjoy the sport.
	## **4. Leader**
	- **Guide and Motivate**: Set a strong example and lead with purpose. Help your players work towards shared goals and celebrate their progress.
	## **5. Efficient**
	- **Use Time Wisely**: Keep practices organized and focused. Ensure every minute counts by sticking to your plan and minimizing downtime.
	## **6. Driven**
	- **Chase Progress**: Bring a relentless drive to improve, both your players' skills and your own. Show up hungry to make this practice better than the last one.
