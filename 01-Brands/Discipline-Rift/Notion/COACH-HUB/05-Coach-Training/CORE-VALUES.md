# CORE VALUES

<callout icon="🏷️" color="gray_bg">
	Our core values answer the following questions:
	- Why do we exist?
	- What do we do?
	- When do we win?
</callout>
<empty-block/>
---
### HUMILITY - “HUMBLE”
We acknowledge that there is always more to learn, even from the players themselves. Our approach coaching with an open mind, sharing knowledge with fellow coaches and taking initiative towards collaboration
### PERSEVERANCE - “HUNGRY”
We consistently encourage players to push through challenges, whether it’s a tough practice or personal matters with relentless drive to improve both our coaching skills and those of our players. 
### ADAPTABILITY - “SMART”
We apply emotional intelligence in all interactions. This means being aware of your own emotions, resolving conflicts in a way that strengthens relationships and perspectives of players emotional states, understanding their motivations.
