# 1% SOLUTIONS

> There is no 100% solution. There are **ONE HUNDRED 1% solutions.** {color="brown"}
### **CONCEPT**
<details>
<summary>Great coaching is not one perfect approach. It is many small corrections, applied consistently, that compound.</summary>
	### PURPOSE
	- Give every coach a toolbox instead of a rulebook.
	### CRAFT
	- Coaching is a socio-behavioral craft. It is built, not born.
</details>
---
<callout icon="📐" color="gray_bg">
	**THE MATH**
</callout>
- **1% better every session** compounds to roughly **37x** by mid-season.
- **The point**: you are never one big fix away. You are one small fix away, repeated.
- **The ask**: leave this training with one 1% you will run next practice.
---
### WHERE THE 1% LIVES
- Three surfaces. Every improvement you make lands on one of them.
	<callout icon="🧠" color="gray_bg">
		**KNOWLEDGE** — what you know about the sport and how kids learn it
	</callout>
	<callout icon="🗣️" color="gray_bg">
		**COMMUNICATION** — how clearly and how fast you can transfer it
	</callout>
	<callout icon="🎖️" color="gray_bg">
		**LEADERSHIP** — whether the group follows you into it
	</callout>
- Pick the weakest surface. That is where your 1% is cheapest to earn.
---
<callout icon="⚙️" color="gray_bg">
	**RELATIONSHIPS THROUGH EXCELLENCE**
</callout>
> “To build a long-term relationship, you have to help people get better. You have to teach first, teach better, and use that as the tool to connect.”
- **Most coaches** put the relationship before the technique.
- **We invert it**: we build the relationship *through* the technical teaching.
- **Why it is hard**: teaching better is technical, and the details are mundane. That is exactly why it is our edge.
---
### THE COACH'S POV
1. **SEE** — eyes in the right place, know your cues
2. **DECIDE** — quickly, and accept it will be imperfect
3. **ACT** — clarify, simplify, repeat, push, smile, re-explain
<callout icon="👁️" color="gray_bg">
	The reality is messy groups and short attention spans. Coaching is perception before it is instruction.
</callout>
---
### WHO YOU ARE COACHING
<callout icon="😄" color="gray_bg">
	**FUNNY JOE (K–2)** — learns through playful challenge and positive reinforcement. Motivated by the joy of discovery and by being seen.
</callout>
<callout icon="😎" color="gray_bg">
	**COOL JESS (3rd–5th)** — learns through goals and visible skill gains. Motivated by cool friendships and growing competence.
</callout>
- Same drill, two different sales pitches. Read the age before you open your mouth.
---
### THE TOOLBOX MINDSET
- **No best method.** There is a toolbox. We teach diverse groups, so the method has to vary.
- **1% improvement** = picking the right tool for the right job, not buying a new tool.
- **Inside-the-box thinking**: the creativity we want is an unexpected use of a common idea, not a brand new invention. Add value to what already exists.
---
### TAKE ACTION!
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	What if every coach made their players 1% better after every single session?
</callout>
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	What if you were 1% more attentive to how you managed time, gave feedback, built relationships, and checked what was actually learned?
</callout>
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	Pick ONE. Run it next practice. Then pick the next one.
	= PLAN your game PLAN, and gamefy all of it `coaching is a craft, not a talent`
</callout>
