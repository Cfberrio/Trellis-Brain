# COACH MESSAGES
You will get these emails through the season. Two matter every week: C6 is your weekly plan, C7 fires when you have not logged a session.
## BEFORE THE SEASON
<table>
<tr>
<td>**C0**</td>
<td>Application received</td>
<td>Right after you apply</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>**C1**</td>
<td>Book the interview</td>
<td>Application approved</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>**C3**</td>
<td>Offer + onboarding checklist</td>
<td>Offer accepted</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>**C4**</td>
<td>Your season assignment: school, sport, roster, director contact, dashboard link</td>
<td>Matched to a team</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>**C5**</td>
<td>Week-before prep</td>
<td>7 days before first practice</td>
</tr>
</table>
## IN SEASON
<table>
<tr>
<td>**C6**</td>
<td>Weekly session plan, one per week</td>
<td>Start of each season week. This is your plan: read it before practice</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>**C7**</td>
<td>Session record reminder</td>
<td>Fires automatically if today's session is not logged by 8 PM ET</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>**C8**</td>
<td>Schedule change or cancellation</td>
<td>A session changed or was cancelled</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>**C9**</td>
<td>Mid-season check-in. Replies are read by a person: answer it</td>
<td>Start of week 3</td>
</tr>
</table>
## SEASON CLOSE
<table>
<tr>
<td>**C10**</td>
<td>Season close + next season</td>
<td>After the last session</td>
</tr>
</table>
Your assignment message (C4) carries the contact that matters all season: the program director's name and phone. Save it when it arrives.
