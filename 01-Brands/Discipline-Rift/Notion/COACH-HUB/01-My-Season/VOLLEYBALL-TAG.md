# VOLLEYBALL TAG
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP AND BOUNDARIES**
	Ten players per coach in a marked clear area. Choose two taggers.
	No balls in hands during chasing.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Demonstrate a gentle tag on the upper arm or shoulder, the boundaries, and controlled movement.
2. A tagged player steps into the marked reset area, shows a ready stance and two side steps, then re-enters immediately.
3. No pushing, no blocking routes, no chasing across adjacent courts.
4. Change taggers after a short round of about 45 seconds.
## How a round ends
End each timed round together. Celebrate everyone getting back into play quickly. No last-player-out elimination.
## Variations
Begin walking for younger players. Add a direction change on a signal later. Do not ask them to hold a locked platform while running.

	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Personal space, response to the stop signal, and balance. This one leads naturally into **Beat the Ball**.
</callout>
