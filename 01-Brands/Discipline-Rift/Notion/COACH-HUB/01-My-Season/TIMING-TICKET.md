# TIMING TICKET
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	One supervised hitting lane with a clear landing area. One tosser, one attacker, one collector. Everyone else practises shadow rhythm in a separate area.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Demonstrate the approach slowly and mirror it for handedness. Right-handed three-step: **left–right–left**. Left-handed: **right–left–right**.
2. Begin the approach after the high toss leaves the feeder's hand.
3. First rehearse the approach and a balanced finish with no strike, then add a controlled contact.
4. Reset before the next feed. Rotate after two or three attempts.
## How to progress and win
One ticket mark for matching the ball's timing and finishing in balance. A hard hit with an uncontrolled landing does **not** earn the mark.
**Golden Ticket:** three controlled timed reps unlock receiving a teammate set.

	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Cue: **"See it, then go."** Watch rhythm and space. Do not insist everyone jumps equally high.
	**If it breaks:** timing falls apart → go back to the higher predictable toss.
</callout>

</callout>
