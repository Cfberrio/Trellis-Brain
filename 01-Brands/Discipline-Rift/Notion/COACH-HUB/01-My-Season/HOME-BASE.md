# HOME BASE
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Trios: tosser, receiver, target. Mark a starting spot and a separate support spot in a clear lane.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Receiver moves to catch or pass a toss toward the target.
2. After contact, the receiver moves to the support spot **while watching the ball**.
3. Target catches and returns to the tosser. Receiver resets to the starting spot.
4. Rotate after three balls. Later remove the fixed support spot and ask players to choose useful space.
## How to progress and win
One point for a playable contact **plus** being ready for the next ball. The recovery only counts while their eyes stay connected to play.
**Golden Ticket:** three useful recoveries unlock the return-ball version.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Cue: **"Your touch is not the end."** Do not teach a rigid return to the same cone regardless of where the ball is.
	**If it breaks:** player runs away looking at the floor → shorten the route and demonstrate turning so the ball stays visible.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
The player supports the next contact even when the target moves to a different side.
