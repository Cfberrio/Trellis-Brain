# POLICE & ROBBERS
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP AND BOUNDARIES**
	Two taggers and eight movers per coach.
	Cones mark a central "treasure" collection area and separate home markers.
	Use beanbags or flat tokens, never rolling balls.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Movers collect one token at a time and walk or jog it to their home marker.
2. Taggers use gentle shoulder or upper-arm tags outside the collection area.
3. If tagged, return the token to the central area, visit a reset marker, and try again immediately.
4. No guarding the collection entry. Switch taggers after about 45 seconds.
## How a round ends
Count the group's delivered tokens at the end of the round. Try a new route next round. Taggers are part of the group, not a punishment role.
## Variations
Start with walking and one tagger. Later, widen the starting positions rather than making the tagging more aggressive.
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Scanning before moving. Keep this an Intro game. Serving under pressure belongs in a challenge, not here.
</callout>
