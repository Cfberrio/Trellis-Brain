# TENNIS GAMIFIED CHALLENGES
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**RACKET & BALL CONTROL**
	**→** [[Bungee Jumps]]
	*Problem: can't balance or bounce the ball with racket control yet — the literal first-contact activity.*
	**→** [[Jacks]]
	*Problem: taps the ball once, loses control — named progression, Onesies through Fivesies. Added 2026-09-21.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**FOREHAND**
	**→** [[Sending the Mail]]
	*Problem: forehand has no direction. Rally inside the tramlines only.*
	**→** [[Instant Rally — Forehand]]
	*Problem: no turn, arm-only swing. Isolates the swing path.*
	**→** [[Bungee Jumps]]
	*Problem: no repeatable contact point — earlier stage than the other two.*
	**→** [[Target Tennis]]
	*Problem: contact happens but no directed accuracy.*
	**→** [[Lobster Trap]]
	*Problem: feeder varies the toss — forces footwork-to-contact under a moving entry.*
	**→** [[Direction Call]]
	*Problem: shots have no direction under pressure — crosscourt/down-the-line called live, not just mentioned. Added 2026-09-21.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**BACKHAND**
	**→** [[Elephant Play]]
	*Problem: backhand and forehand don't connect. Live two-touch rally, alternating sides.*
	**→** [[Bungee Spots — Backhand]]
	*Problem: flat, downward swings. Isolates the low-to-high path.*
	**→** [[Card Shark]]
	*Problem: mixed-level group, no differentiation — card sets stroke and rep count per pair.*
	**→** [[Rally Pyramid]]
	*Problem: rally dies, no visible progress — builds a ball pyramid per success.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**VOLLEY**
	**→** [[Balloon Hoop]]
	*Problem: no reps without a partner. Solo repetition against a wall or fence.*
	**→** [[Volley Goalkeeper Challenge]]
	*Problem: can't sustain control under live pressure. Partner volley rally, count consecutive touches.*
	**→** [[Zigzag Volley]]
	*Problem: no reps without a large group — line volley relay for 4-6 players.*
	**→** [[Djokovic Ball]]
	*Problem: plateaued past basic control — high-ceiling extension. Advanced dial, not a required rung.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**SERVE / RETURN**
	**→** [[Target Hoops]]
	*Problem: serves land anywhere. Target-zone serving game once the motion is solid.*
	**→** [[Color Call]]
	*Problem: return is a guess, not a plan. Builds a return menu players can choose from.*
	**→** [[Serve Pyramid]]
	*Problem: target serving too hard, no visible progress — gentler on-ramp before 18-Option Serving.*
	**→** [[Around the Clock]]
	*Problem: serves land anywhere, no zone awareness — earlier stage than 18-Option Serving.*
	**→** [[Horse Serving Game]]
	*Problem: needs replay value past 18-Option Serving. Advanced dial, not a required rung.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**OVERHEAD / LOB**
	**→** [[Lob the Giant]]
	*Problem: lob is too flat, easily smashed. Builds height and depth.*
	**→** [[Overhead Smash Drill]]
	*Problem: overhead swings across the body instead of up. Connects the serve motion to a moving ball.*
	**→** [[Backboard to Practice Overheads]]
	*Problem: no independent repetition option, players avoid the overhead entirely.*
	**→** [[Reject That Lob]]
	*Problem: net player takes bad overheads instead of controlling the point. Added 2026-09-21.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**MOVEMENT**
	**→** [[Red Light/Green Light Runs]]
	*Problem: slow, drifting first step. Builds explosive first-step reaction.*
	**→** [[Shuffle & Catch]]
	*Problem: no split step, flat-footed reactions. Times the split to an opponent's contact.*
	**→** [[Capture the Clothespins]]
	*Problem: recovery not tied to a scoring stake — serve/return points with a visible team score.*
	**→** [[Team Singles]]
	*Problem: recovery breaks down under a live rally, not just a cone drill.*
</callout>

<callout icon="🏆" color="gray_bg">
	**GOLDEN TICKET BRANCHES — cross-skill, run concurrently with a week's main rung**
	*Not standalone rungs. A player who clears the entry condition on the week's main game gets pulled forward into the next skill, same court, same practice, per [[PROGRESSION LADDER]]'s Golden Ticket pattern and [[PRACTICE DESIGN]].*
	**→** [[Close It At The Net]]
	*Forehand/Backhand → Volley. Rally 3, close the 4th ball at net. Used in Weeks 1–2.*
	**→** [[Smash The Lift]]
	*Volley → Overhead. Volley-rally 5, finish a floated ball with a smash. Used in Week 3.*
	**→** [[Call It, Play It]]
	*Serve → Rallying & Scoring. Serve consistency unlocks self-officiated short games. Used in Week 4.*
</callout>
