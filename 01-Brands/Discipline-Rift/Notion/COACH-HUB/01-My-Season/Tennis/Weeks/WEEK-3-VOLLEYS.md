## WHERE ARE WE?
Forehand and backhand exist but are inconsistent. Retrieve both from the baseline, then introduce the volley — the biggest technical break from a groundstroke swing (punch, don't swing).
## OPEN
Retrieve: forehand/backhand rally check from the baseline. Warm-up from the bank.

## PROGRESSION LADDER
| Step | Skill | Problem | Gamified Challenge | Variation | Observable Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forehand + Backhand (retrieve) | Inconsistent under any movement | [[Elephant Play]] | Live, no calling | Sustains rally, reads and reacts |
| 2 | Volley | Full swing at the net | [[Balloon Hoop]] | Alternate sides | Short punch motion, racket up between reps |
