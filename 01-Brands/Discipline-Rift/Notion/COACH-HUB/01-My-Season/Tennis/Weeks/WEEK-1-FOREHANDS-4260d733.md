## WHERE ARE WE?
First week. No retrieval yet — show players the sport, then teach the first stroke and the movement that supports it. USTA QuickStart: scaled court, low-compression ball, lower net.
## OPEN
Welcome. Show a brief Serve-Rally-Point sequence so players see where the season goes. Optional opener: Shoe-Work Tag from the warm-up bank.

## PROGRESSION LADDER
| Step | Skill | Problem | Gamified Challenge | Variation | Observable Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (optional — true beginners only) | Racket Control (pre-stroke) | Player has never held a racket / can't control it before any swing | [[Jacks]] or [[Bungee Jumps]] (toss/bounce/catch off the strings, no swing yet) | Alternate palm-up/knuckles-up face once controlled | 3+ consecutive controlled catches off the strings |
| 1 | Movement (Ready Position) | No ready position, flat-footed | Shadow Strokes (warm-up bank) | Add a partner call | Recognizable ready position on cue |
| 2 | Forehand | Arm-only swing, no turn | [[Instant Rally — Forehand]] | Add a bounce | Visible turn before contact |
| 3 | Forehand | Swinging down, netted balls | [[Sending the Mail]] | Widen to half-court | Low-to-high swing path, ball clears net |
| 4 | Forehand + Movement | Reaches instead of stepping | [[Red Light/Green Light Runs]], then re-run [[Sending the Mail]] | Add lateral movement before the feed | Steps before swinging, contact out front |
Start at the earliest unresolved problem. Advance from visible success; protect time for CFU and Close.
<callout icon="🏆" color="gray_bg">
	**GOLDEN TICKET BRANCH — a pair that clears 3 clean forehand exchanges in Step 2/3 gets waved forward, same court, same practice, to** [[Close It At The Net]]**.** They keep rallying, then close the 4th ball with a punch volley — the first live tie between this week's skill and next week's. Not required for the whole group; a dial, not a rung. See [[COACHING DIFFERENT LEVELS / CO-COACHING]].
</callout>

## CLOSE
**What did we learn?** Why do we turn before we swing? Show your ready position.
**Recognition:** Who tried the next rep after a miss?
**What's next?** We'll retrieve the forehand and ready position, then learn the backhand.
