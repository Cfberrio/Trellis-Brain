# LOB + PROBLEM: TOO FLAT, GETS SMASHED → LOB THE GIANT
**Setup:** Coach ("the giant") stands at the net with a racket raised overhead. Players lob from the baseline, trying to clear the raised racket.
**Show It:** Demonstrate the long, high finish that clears a raised racket with margin.
**Challenge:** 3 lobs in a row that clear the giant's racket and land in the court.
**Progress/Win:** 3 in a row = giant raises the racket higher (or steps closer).
