# CLOSE IT AT THE NET
**Entry Condition:** 3 clean, controlled groundstroke exchanges in a row on the week's main rung (Instant Rally — Forehand in Week 1, or a live rally in Week 2's Two-Touch Rally). This is the gate.
**Setup:** Once a pair clears the gate, the coach waves the attacking player forward toward the net for that lane only. Feeder keeps rallying, then floats one ball short and soft instead of deep.
**Show It:** Demonstrate the full pattern once, live: rally 3 shots, short ball, attacker moves in and closes it with a punch volley, using [[TENNIS VOLLEY]]'s cues ("racket up," "punch, don't swing," "catch it out front"). Name it: "Three to rally, one to close."
**Challenge:** String together 3 groundstrokes, then finish the 4th (fed short) ball at the net with a controlled volley into the open court.
**Progress/Win:** 3 successful closes = fully unlocked — this pair runs the pattern as their standard version for the rest of the practice. Next stretch: add a target zone at the net for the volley finish.

**Coach Watches For:** Racket up before moving in, a split step on the transition to net, punch (not swing) at contact.
