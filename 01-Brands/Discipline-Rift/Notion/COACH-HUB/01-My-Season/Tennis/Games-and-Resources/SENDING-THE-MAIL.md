# FOREHAND + PROBLEM: NO DIRECTION → SENDING THE MAIL
**Setup:** Pairs, one racket + ball each, standing on throw-down lines/cones a short distance apart, target hoop or zone at the partner's feet.
**Show It:** Demonstrate a forehand push/drive that lands inside the target, low-to-high swing, contact out front.
**Challenge:** Partner underhand-feeds; hitter drop-hits a forehand aiming for the hoop. Partner catches and feeds again.
**Progress/Win:** 5 landed in the hoop = win the round. Then rally it — partner returns instead of just catching.

**Coach Watches For:** Turn before the swing, low-to-high path, contact out in front.
