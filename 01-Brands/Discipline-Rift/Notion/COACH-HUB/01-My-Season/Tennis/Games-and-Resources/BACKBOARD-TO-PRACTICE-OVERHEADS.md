# BACKBOARD TO PRACTICE OVERHEADS
## SETUP
- Players: 1+ per backboard/wall section, or work in pairs taking turns
- Equipment: a wall or backboard, rackets, a few balls each
- Space: any flat wall with clearance, players stand roughly 2-3 tramline-widths back
## SHOW IT
Start with a self-drop feed or a soft toss into the wall. As the ball comes off the wall, set up sideways, racket back and up, and hit a controlled overhead into the wall so it comes back playable.
## CHALLENGE
See how many controlled overheads a player can chain in a row against the wall without the ball getting away from them or the swing breaking down into a flat slap.
## PROGRESS / WIN
Start with 3 in a row as the target. Once a player reaches 5+, they've earned the right to try it live off a fed lob instead of the wall.

## COACH WATCHES FOR
Swinging across the body instead of up, no sideways turn before contact, ball getting behind the player.
