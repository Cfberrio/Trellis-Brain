# SERVE IT UP
**Setup:** Doubles set-up, teams of two to four players on each side of the net.
**Base Game:** One team member serves the ball over the net (drop-hit or served out of the air); the opposing team tries to catch it with a racket. A successful serve over the net earns 2 points, a successful racket-catch earns 1 point. Alternate serving and receiving after each serve.
**Use It To Check:** Whether serve motion holds up under a scored, team format rather than only in an isolated feed.
**Constraint Menu:** Conduct on one side of the net only for beginners, removing the "serve over the net" requirement. Straight-across serves only vs. diagonal for advanced players.
**Observable Evidence:** Serves clear the net consistently and land in bounds more often than not; players can articulate why a serve is harder to catch — placement into empty space, more pace.
**If It Breaks:** If nobody can serve over the net, move the whole game to one side and drop the "clears the net" requirement until motion is more consistent.
**Progress The Check:** Require the return to be a full groundstroke instead of a catch once serve consistency is established.
