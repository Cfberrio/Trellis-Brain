# FOREHAND + PROBLEM: SHOTS HAVE NO DIRECTION UNDER PRESSURE → DIRECTION CALL
**Setup:** Cones or targets set crosscourt and down-the-line on the opposite side of the net. Players feed from the baseline.
**Show It:** Demonstrate the same stroke hit two ways — crosscourt (across the body) and down-the-line (same side as the swing) — from the same feed.
**Challenge:** Coach or partner calls "crosscourt" or "down the line" right before the feed arrives. Player must direct the shot to the called target, not just get it in.
**Progress/Win:** 3 correct-direction shots in a row advances the round. Widen targets for easier, narrow for harder.

**Coach Watches For:** Contact point and follow-through actually change between the two directions — not just the same swing landing in different spots by chance.
