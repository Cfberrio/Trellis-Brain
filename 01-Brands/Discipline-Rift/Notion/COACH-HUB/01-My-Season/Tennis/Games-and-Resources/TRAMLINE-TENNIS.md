# TRAMLINE TENNIS (SCORED TO 21)
**Setup:** Two players rally inside the tramline (doubles alley) only, standard scoring to 21, win by 2.
**Base Game:** Live rally, points won on errors or balls landing outside the alley.
**Use It To Check:** Whether forehand/backhand control and direction hold up when it counts, not just in a cooperative rally.
**Constraint Menu:** Allow one bounce for beginners; no-bounce touch volleys for advanced; backhand-only games to isolate that side.
**Observable Evidence:** Consistent low-to-high swing path under scoring pressure, shots stay inside the narrow target.
**If It Breaks:** Nobody can keep it in the alley — widen to half-court temporarily, then narrow back.
**Progress The Check:** Shrink the alley width or require alternating sides once 21 comes easily.
