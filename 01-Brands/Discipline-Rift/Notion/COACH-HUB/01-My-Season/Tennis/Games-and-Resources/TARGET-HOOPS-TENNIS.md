# SERVE + PROBLEM: LANDS ANYWHERE → TARGET HOOPS
**Setup:** Pairs, standing either side of a net/barrier tape, each with a hoop on the floor in front of them behind their own line.
**Show It:** Demonstrate the push/underhand-to-overhand serve motion aiming to land the ball inside the partner's hoop, scoring a point for each successful landing.
**Challenge:** Server serves, aiming for the partner's hoop. Partner then serves back aiming for the first server's hoop.
**Progress/Win:** Count points over a set number of serves. "Can you beat your score?" — repeat and track improvement.
