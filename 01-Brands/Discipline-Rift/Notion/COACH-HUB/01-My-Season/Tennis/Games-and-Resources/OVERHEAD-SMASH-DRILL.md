# OVERHEAD + PROBLEM: SWINGS ACROSS THE BODY → OVERHEAD SMASH DRILL
**Setup:** Coach feeds high, soft lobs from close range. Player starts in ready position at the net.
**Show It:** Demonstrate tracking with the off-hand, trophy pose, full extension, down-and-out contact.
**Challenge:** 5 overheads in a row that land in the court with a visible trophy pose.
**Progress/Win:** 5 in a row = coach feeds from further back, faster lob.
