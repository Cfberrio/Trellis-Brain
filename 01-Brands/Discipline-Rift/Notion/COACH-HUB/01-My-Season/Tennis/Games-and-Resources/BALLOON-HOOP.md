# VOLLEY + PROBLEM: NO REPS WITHOUT A PARTNER → BALLOON HOOP
**Setup:** Pairs facing across a low net/barrier tape. One balloon (or light foam ball once volleys are consistent), a hoop on the floor as target on the sender's side.
**Show It:** Demonstrate a short, firm downward push — not a swing — that taps the balloon toward the hoop. Fingertips/racket face pointed upward at contact, short abbreviated motion.
**Challenge:** Partner throws the balloon up; volleyer taps it down aiming for the hoop.
**Progress/Win:** 5 hits into the hoop before switching roles = win. Then swap the balloon for a light foam ball once control is consistent.
