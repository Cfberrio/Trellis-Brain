# MOVEMENT + PROBLEM: SLOW FIRST STEP → RED LIGHT/GREEN LIGHT RUNS
**Setup:** A ~60-foot lane, players start behind a line.
**Show It:** Demonstrate moving on "green," freezing into ready position on "red" — explosive stop and start, not a jog.
**Challenge:** Move fast on green, freeze in balanced ready position on red. Add movement variety each round: crazy arms/slow legs, fast arms/slow legs, fast legs/slow arms, fast arms and legs together.
**Progress/Win:** Last player still moving when "red" is called restarts from the back; game continues with the whole group.
