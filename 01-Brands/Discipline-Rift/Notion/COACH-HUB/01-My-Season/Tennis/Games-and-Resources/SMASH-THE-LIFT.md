# SMASH THE LIFT
**Entry Condition:** 5 consecutive controlled touches in [[Volley Goalkeeper Challenge]] (does not need to reach 10, just needs the streak).
**Setup:** Once a pair clears the gate, on the next exchange the feeder floats one ball high and short instead of driving it — the ball an unprepared volleyer would mishit into the net.
**Show It:** Demonstrate reading the high, short ball and switching from a volley punch to [[OVERHEAD SMASH & LOB]]'s trophy-pose finish — same trophy position as the serve, "point and track" the ball the whole flight.
**Challenge:** Volley-rally 5 touches, then correctly identify and finish a floated ball with an overhead instead of a volley.
**Progress/Win:** 3 correct finishes = fully unlocked — this pair keeps volleying with the smash live in the mix, not as a one-off rep. Next stretch: feeder disguises the float so the attacker must decide late.
