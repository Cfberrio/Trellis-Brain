## BASE GAME
Players rally from the baseline using the skill of the day (forehand, backhand, volley, or serve). Miss the shot into the net or out → cross to "jail" (the marked zone behind the opposite baseline). A player in jail escapes by catching a fed ball off one bounce, then rejoins their own team's line.
## USE IT TO CHECK
Whether a stroke holds up under repeated live attempts with a real consequence for missing — not just in a fed, cooperative rep.
## CONSTRAINT MENU
Cap jail attempts at 3 feeds before automatic re-entry, so no one sits out long. Score by **team points earned back** (one point per successful escape) instead of by who survives longest. Scale the skill required to whatever the week is teaching.
## OBSERVABLE EVIDENCE
Form holds up on the shot that matters (game-point pressure), not just on cooperative reps earlier in the rep line.
## IF IT BREAKS
**FREEZE → CUE → REPLAY.** If a whole line is stuck in jail, shorten the catch requirement (two bounces instead of one) for that round only.
## PROGRESS THE CHECK
Once a group is escaping easily, remove the bounce and require a clean catch, or add a second constraint (must return crosscourt to escape).
