# VOLLEY + PROBLEM: CAN'T SUSTAIN CONTROL LIVE → VOLLEY GOALKEEPER CHALLENGE
**Setup:** One player at the net in ready position — racket up like a goalkeeper. Coach or partner feeds soft balls from close range.
**Show It:** Demonstrate the goalkeeper framing: racket stays up and ready between every feed, short punch-volley back, immediate reset to ready.
**Challenge:** Punch-volley every feed back over the net without a backswing, resetting to ready position after each one.
**Progress/Win:** Count clean volleys in 60 seconds. Beat your own score next round — this is the individual check before pairing up for a live rally.
