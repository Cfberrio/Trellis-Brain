# MOVEMENT + PROBLEM: RECOVERY NOT TIED TO A SCORING STAKE → CAPTURE THE CLOTHESPINS
**Setup:** Two teams, five clothespins each, lined up on their own side near the net. Play from the service line.
**Show It:** Demonstrate a point played out with a serve — server wins the point if the return isn't made, returner wins if it is.
**Challenge:** Winner of each point runs to the opponent's side and claims one clothespin. Alternate serving teams after each point.
**Progress/Win:** Team with the most clothespins when time is called wins, or first team to claim all of the opponent's pins.
