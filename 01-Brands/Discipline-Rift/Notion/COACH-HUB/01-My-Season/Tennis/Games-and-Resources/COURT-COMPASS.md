**Court orientation.** New group or new space, no rackets needed first. Coach names a line — baseline, service line, net, singles sideline, doubles sideline — and calls it out during a round of movement.
**Setup:** Group spread out along the baseline. Coach or a player leader stands at the net.
**How it runs:** Coach calls a line name, players sprint to touch it, then hold ready position until the next call. Run 4-6 calls per round.
**DR standard — no last-player-out elimination.** Instead of eliminating the last arrival, the last player(s) to reach the line does a quick reset task (3 shadow-swings, or name the line back to the coach) in a marked reset area, then rejoins the next call immediately. Everyone keeps moving; nobody sits out.
**Progress:** Once names are automatic, call two lines in sequence ("baseline, then net") to build in decision speed.
