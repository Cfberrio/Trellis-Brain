# RACKET & BALL CONTROL + PROBLEM: TAPS THE BALL ONCE, LOSES CONTROL → JACKS
**Setup:** One racket + ball per player, spread out, palm-up racket face.
**Show It:** Toss, let bounce, tap up once with the racket (palm up), catch. Call it "Onesies."
**Challenge:** Repeat, adding one tap each round — Onesies, Twosies, Threesies, and so on.
**Progress/Win:** Reach "Fivesies" (5 consecutive taps) = move to continuous tap-ups with no counting pause.

**Coach Watches For:** Racket face staying flat and open, wrist staying stiff (not slapping at the ball).
