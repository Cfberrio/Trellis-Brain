# BACKHAND + PROBLEM: MIXED-LEVEL GROUP, NO DIFFERENTIATION → CARD SHARK
**Setup:** Deck of cards. Assign a color to each stroke (e.g. red = forehand, black = backhand). Players work in pairs at the service line.
**Show It:** One player draws a card, runs back to their line, and the pair rallies using the stroke and rep count the card sets — number = rally length, face cards = choose your own, joker = wild.
**Challenge:** Complete the rally length shown, then draw again. Track how many cards a pair clears in the session.
**Progress/Win:** Move players farther from the net as they clear more cards. The joker forces an immediate stroke swap mid-rally for advanced pairs.
