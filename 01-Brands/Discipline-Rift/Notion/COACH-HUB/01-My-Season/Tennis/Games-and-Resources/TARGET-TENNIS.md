# FOREHAND + PROBLEM: CONTACT WITHOUT DIRECTION (EARLIER STAGE THAN TRAMLINE TENNIS) → TARGET TENNIS
**Setup:** Partners face each other a few steps apart. One partner spreads their feet into a wide "goal."
**Show It:** Demonstrate striking a stationary ball on the ground through the partner's feet using a forehand motion.
**Challenge:** Ball starts on the floor. Players take turns trying to strike it through their partner's legs, switching roles after each attempt.
**Progress/Win:** 3 goals in a row wins the round. Widen the goal for easier, narrow it for harder.
