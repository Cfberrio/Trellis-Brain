# BACKHAND + PROBLEM: DOESN'T CONNECT TO FOREHAND → ELEPHANT PLAY
**Setup:** Pairs. Roller has the ball; hitter has a racket and two spots in front of them — one to the left, one to the right.
**Show It:** Demonstrate rolling to the right spot → hitter plays forehand; rolling to the left spot → hitter plays backhand (or reverse for a left-handed hitter). Same push-return both sides.
**Challenge:** Roller sends to either spot at random. Hitter must read which spot and use the correct side, pushing the ball back.
**Progress/Win:** 3 correct-side returns in a row from each spot = win. Then remove the visual spot cue and roll to open space — hitter reads the ball's line instead.

**Coach Watches For:** Clean grip/turn transition between sides, no rushed swings, correct side chosen without a verbal reminder.
