# CALL IT, PLAY IT
**Entry Condition:** 2 of 3 serves land in the box in [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/Notion/COACH-HUB/01-My-Season/TARGET-HOOPS-TENNIS|Target Hoops]].
**Setup:** Once a pair clears the gate, they get their own mini-court and play out full points — serve starts it, rally continues with any stroke already learned, first to call the outcome wins the exchange.
**Show It:** Demonstrate: serve in, rally, ball lands out — the receiver calls "out" immediately, out loud, before either player argues about it. Demonstrate saying the score before the next serve.
**Challenge:** Play a short game to 5 points. Every point requires the server to say the score first and the players to call in/out themselves — no coach adjudication.
**Progress/Win:** First pair to 5, calling every point correctly and out loud = fully unlocked — this pair plays real games for the rest of the practice instead of returning to serve reps.

**Coach Watches For:** Calls made immediately and out loud (not after a pause to see who complains), score stated before the next serve, reset to ready after every point regardless of who won it.
