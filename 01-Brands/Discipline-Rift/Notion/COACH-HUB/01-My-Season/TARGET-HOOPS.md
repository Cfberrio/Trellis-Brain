# TARGET HOOPS
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Three short lanes per coach where possible. A tosser and a target or catcher per lane. Flat markers or hoops placed clear of foot traffic.
	**Twenty players:** each coach owns ten, groups of 3 + 3 + 4. Every role rotates.
</callout>
<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
	**DECIDE THE PURPOSE BEFORE THE ROUND** — teammate pass on the same side, **or** a final send across the net. They are two different jobs and two different scores. Do not mix them.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Name the purpose for this round.
2. In teammate mode, pass toward a catcher standing near a marked area. The catcher stays out of the hoop.
3. In send mode, aim over a lowered net toward an empty floor target.
4. Three feeds, then rotate roles. Retrieve only when the lane is clear.
## How to progress and win
**Teammate mode:** one point for a playable catch near the target.
**Send mode:** one for over and in, two for the target.
Do not mix the score meanings.
**Golden Ticket:** three useful passes unlock a different target angle. Three controlled sends unlock a smaller target.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Cue: **"Show where it should go."** Judge the first pass by the next contact, not by whether it crosses the net.
	**If it breaks:** player always sends over → demonstrate first contact to a teammate and final contact over the net as two separate jobs.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
The player can direct toward either of two destinations when told the purpose before the feed.
