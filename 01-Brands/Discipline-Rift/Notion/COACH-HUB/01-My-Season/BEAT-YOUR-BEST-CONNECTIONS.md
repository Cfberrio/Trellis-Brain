# BEAT YOUR BEST CONNECTIONS
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Groups of four or five, one ball, familiar Pass → Set → Catch roles. A fifth player rotates into the feeder role every sequence.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Choose one known sequence and show a successful rep.
2. For a short round of about 90 seconds, accumulate completed connections.
3. Rotate roles every ball. **Do not restart the score after a miss.**
4. Take a brief cue break and try another round with the same conditions.
## How to progress and win
The score is total completed connections. Only compare rounds when entry, distance, and catch allowance are the same.
**Golden Ticket:** a team improvement unlocks one new constraint, chosen by the players from two suitable options.
## Adjust the challenge
<table header-row="true">
<tr>
<td>Make it easier</td>
<td>Make it harder</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Use Pass → Catch, or Catch → Set → Catch.</td>
<td>Remove one catch support, or change one ball-entry angle after a successful round.</td>
</tr>
</table>
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Praise the adjustment that helped: **"Your higher first ball gave the setter time."** Do not let speed replace contact quality.
	**If it breaks:** one player dominates → enforce role rotation. Haste ruins play → remove the timer and use a fixed number of starts.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
The group repeats the connection after a pause. Then test a new condition rather than treating a high total as mastery.
