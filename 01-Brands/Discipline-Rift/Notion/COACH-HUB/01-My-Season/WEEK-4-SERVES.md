## WHERE ARE WE?
Forehand, backhand, and volley are in place at some level. Retrieve them briefly, then spend most of the session on the serve — underhand first, building toward overhand — plus a first pass at return of serve.
## OPEN
Retrieve: short groundstroke + volley rally check. Warm-up from the bank, toss-focused if possible (Throw Up and Catch).
