# HALF-COURT DOUBLES
**Setup:** Doubles set-up using only the front half of the court (service boxes). Play begins with a drop-hit or self-feed instead of a serve.
**Base Game:** Volley-only doubles — no groundstrokes allowed. Each player must stay on their own side of the court. Use real scoring, announcing the score before each point.
**Use It To Check:** Whether volley control holds up in live, competitive doubles play, not just a fed volley rally.
**Constraint Menu:** Play cooperatively — count how long the point lasts — for beginners. Require 3 shared shots before a winning attempt is allowed for advanced pairs, to prevent immediate put-aways.
**Observable Evidence:** Players punch-volley rather than swing, communicate on their side of the court, and can explain the advantage of net position.
**If It Breaks:** If points end in one shot every time, add the 3-shot-minimum constraint before a winner can be attempted.
**Progress The Check:** Play on the full court, incorporating serves and groundstrokes into the same live doubles format.
