# CLAIM THE SQUARES
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Mark two large receiving zones at first, later four, later nine.
	Three short serving lanes share the targets only when retrieval is paused. Use cones or flat tokens to record claimed zones off court.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Server chooses and **names** a target before serving.
2. A serve landing in the selected zone claims it for the team.
3. Rotate server after each attempt. Collect balls during a clear reset.
4. Begin with "claim both areas." At nine zones, play three in a row.
## How to progress and win
One claim for landing in the chosen area. Over and in somewhere else is a successful serve but not a claim.
**Golden Ticket:** three intentional hits unlock a smaller area, or the responsibility of choosing the target.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Cue: **"Choose, face, finish."** Ask where the player intended it to go **before** you praise the accuracy.
	**If it breaks:** nobody claims a zone → enlarge the areas. Do not introduce nine targets before broad directional success.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
The player names two different destinations and reaches both across a short sample.
