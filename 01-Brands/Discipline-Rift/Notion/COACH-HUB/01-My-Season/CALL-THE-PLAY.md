# CALL THE PLAY
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Pairs or trios, half-court. Work off a coach feed or a partner toss. No net needed to start.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Demonstrate the three calls out loud: **"I"** before you touch the ball, **"Here"** from whoever is receiving it, **"Lurking"** from both players once it's back over, as they reset into ready position.
2. Feed the ball. Every rally needs all three calls, every time.
3. If a call gets skipped, stop the rep and start over. Don't finish a silent rep.
4. Rotate roles every few feeds so everyone calls and everyone receives.
## How to progress and win
A rally where every call happened out loud earns a point, win or lose. Winning the rally on top of that earns a bonus point.
**Golden Ticket:** first team to 5 clean-call points gets to pick the next challenge.

	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Calls coming after contact instead of before. Cue: **"Call it before you touch it, not after."**
</callout>

They're calling on their own mid-rally, without you prompting them.
