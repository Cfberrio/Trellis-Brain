## WHERE ARE WE?
All four strokes (forehand, backhand, volley, serve/return) have been introduced. This week adds the overhead and lob — the serve motion applied to a moving ball — and interleaves everything so far instead of teaching in isolation.
## OPEN

## RALLY PATTERNS
Once all strokes are interleaving, name a pattern instead of just rallying: deep-deep-short (two deep balls to set up a short, attackable ball) or crosscourt-crosscourt-down the line (build the point, then change direction to win it). Coach calls the pattern before a King/Queen point; players execute it live.
## CLOSE
**What did we learn?** Why does the overhead start in the same trophy position as the serve? Can you name the rally pattern you just ran?
**Recognition:** Who picked the right shot for the situation, live?
**What's next?** Week 6 is assessment — full game day, then End of Season Celebration.
