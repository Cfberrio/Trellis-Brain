# JAILBREAK
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Two small groups, one lane each. Group lines up behind the attack line on one side. Coach feeds from the same side. The other side of the net is the jail: a marked catch zone.
	**Twenty players:** two lanes per coach, five per lane.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. First player steps up. Coach tosses. Player sends it over the net with the contact you named today.
2. Ball over and in: player goes to the back of the line. Ball short, out, or in the net: player crosses around the net end into jail.
3. Players in jail stand in the catch zone. Catch a teammate's ball in the air and you walk back to the line. Someone is always coming back.
4. **Cap:** nobody stays in jail longer than three feeds. After three, they walk back regardless.
## How to progress and win
One team point per ball over and in. Bonus point per catch from jail. Round ends after every player has had three feeds. Team beats its own score next round.
**Golden Ticket:** the group clears jail twice in a row → move the feed line back one step.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	The same contact under a little pressure. Cue: **"Same swing, same height."** Jail is a catch job, not a punishment; keep jail players active and cheering.
	**If it breaks:** half the group in jail → shorten the distance, do not shorten the cap. Nobody ever misses → the feed is too easy; add the angle.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
Success rate is the same with and without jail. Then use [[DELIVER THE PACKAGE]] or [[BUILD THE BRIDGE]] when the goal is the first ball to a teammate, not over the net.
