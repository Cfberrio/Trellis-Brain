# SERVE + PROBLEM: NEEDS REPLAY VALUE PAST 18-OPTION SERVING → HORSE SERVING GAME
**Setup:** Service box divided into T / Body / Wide zones with cones or lines.
**Show It:** First server calls their target zone out loud, then serves.
**Challenge:** Each following player must match the same zone or take a letter toward H-O-R-S-E. First to spell HORSE is out; last player standing wins.
**Progress/Win:** Winners move to serving from behind the baseline instead of the service line.
<table header-row="true">
<tr>
<td>Variation</td>
<td>Change</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Easier</td>
<td>Fewer letters needed (e.g. "OUT" instead of "HORSE"), larger zones</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Harder</td>
<td>Full HORSE, serve from the baseline</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Doubles version</td>
<td>Teams take turns matching the call</td>
</tr>
</table>
**Coach Watches For:** Players calling the zone honestly before they know if they'll make it — this is a target-commitment game, not just accuracy.

<tr>
<td>Coach Language</td>
<td>Use When</td>

<td>"Call it first"</td>
<td>Calling the zone after seeing where the ball landed</td>

<td>Aiming down the middle to avoid missing</td>
</tr>
