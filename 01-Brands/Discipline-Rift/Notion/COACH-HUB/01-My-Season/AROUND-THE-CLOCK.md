# SERVE + PROBLEM: SERVES LAND ANYWHERE, NO ZONE AWARENESS (EARLIER STAGE THAN 18-OPTION SERVING) → AROUND THE CLOCK
**Setup:** Mark four target zones in the service box — back-left, back-right, front-left, front-right. Partners alternate serving.
**Show It:** Demonstrate aiming at each zone in turn, cooperating to hit all four as a pair.
**Challenge:** Pairs alternate serves, trying to hit all four zones in as few total serves as possible.
**Progress/Win:** Beat the pair's own best serve count. Progress to a competitive version — first pair to hit all four zones wins.

**Coach Watches For:** Players calling out which zone they're aiming for before they serve — this builds intention, not just repetition.
