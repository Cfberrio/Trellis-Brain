# WEEK 5 - RUNNING LANES

**Theme:** *Play Fast, Stay Organized*
**DR Focus:** one cue, high reps, fast resets, no sitting (JOB ZONE)
## Cue of the Week (RB cue — 1 cue only)
**“INSIDE ELBOW UP — let QB place it in your pocket.”**
(We keep QB coaching minimal: “place it firm.”)
**NFL FLAG skill cues (handoff + receive) support this week:**
- QB handoff: turn/face sideline, extend both arms, place ball firmly in RB stomach
- RB receive: step diagonal toward the lane, **inside elbow up/outside elbow down**, let QB place ball in pocket, then tuck and run
---
## Equipment / Setup
- Cones or spots to mark **running lanes (holes)** and a line of scrimmage
- Flags on everyone
- 4–8 footballs (or foam)
- Optional: hula hoops for scoring targets (Team Touchdown game)
---
# DR PRACTICE SPINE (60 minutes)
## Block 1 (3 min) — Belonging + Routine
**Script (tight):**
“Team in. Sport is the vehicle. Today we’re leveling up **handoffs + running lanes**.
Our ONE cue: **INSIDE ELBOW UP — let QB place it in your pocket.**
If you’re out, you’re on a job—quick job, back in fast.”
**Micro-CFU:** “Show me ‘inside elbow up’… freeze.”
---
## Block 2 (8 min) — Retrieval Warm-up (last week’s cue + movement)
**Use: Team Flag Tag (curriculum-aligned)**
- Everyone wears flags, two teams, everyone is “it.”
- When you pull a flag, sprint it to your “home base” (cone/hoop) and drop it, then rejoin. (No standing.)
**Retrieval cue (from Week 4):** “TUCK + LOCK IT AWAY” on any ball carry (quick reminder only).
**JOB ZONE (10s) when out:**
- Instant Coach: show a teammate **inside elbow up** (even during warmup, we keep the week’s language alive)
Why this works: small-sided, constant reads/movement, no lines—exactly what game-based learning recommends.
---
## Block 3 (7 min) — Skill Clarity (new cue)
### 20–30s demo: “Pocket Receive”
**RB body picture:**
1. Step **diagonal toward the lane (hole)**
2. **Inside elbow UP**, outside elbow down
3. **Do NOT reach**—QB places it in the pocket
4. Tuck + run
**Coach only says (every rep):**
👉 “Inside elbow UP… pocket… go!”
**CFU (5 seconds):**
“Freeze—show me inside elbow up.”
---
## Block 4 (12 min) — Fluency Reps (high reps, low talk)
### Drill A: “Handoff Fit” Reps (pairs)
**Setup:** 1 QB + 1 RB at cones (LOS), lane cone ahead.
**Reps:** 8–10 clean exchanges each, then rotate.
**Constraints to boost success:**
- Start at walking speed
- RB *must* show elbow-up pocket before QB can move the ball
**Coach feedback (1 cue only):**
- If RB reaches: “Elbow up—pocket still.”
- If exchange is sloppy: shorten distance + slow down (rebuild success)
(These directly reinforce the curriculum “receiving a hand-off” cues.)
### Drill B (optional 2-min add-on): “Cadence Freeze”
QB says “Set… Hut!” → RB freezes pocket shape before the handoff happens.
---
## Block 5 (13 min) — Decision Reps (add read/time/defender)
### Drill: Running Lanes (curriculum anchor)
**Setup:** lanes/holes labeled (use the lane diagram concept).
**How it runs:**
- QB calls the lane: “**1 left!**” (or 2/3/4/5/6)
- On “Set-Hut,” RB takes **diagonal first step** toward that lane and receives handoff (elbow-up pocket), then runs through the lane.
**Add pressure (light, one variable):**
- Put a **walk-speed defender** at the lane entrance. Defender can only pull flags *after* the handoff (no contact).
- RB decision: hit the lane fast OR bounce one step to the next lane if blocked (advanced group only).
**Coach keeps it to the SAME cue:**
👉 “Inside elbow UP.”
---
## Block 6 (14 min) — Application / Constraint Game
You have two great curriculum-based game options—pick one depending on your space.
### Option 1 (most on-theme): **Box Drill** (curriculum Lesson 5)
**Setup:** “Box” / split field with two sides. Players are divided offense/defense.
**Rules (simple):**
- Offense tries to carry into opponent territory.
- Defense can pull flags once offense enters.
- If RB flag is pulled: RB gives ball to the other team and returns to their side to rejoin. Then roles switch after a round.
**Scoring that rewards the cue:**
- 1 point = clean handoff (coach sees elbow-up pocket)
- 1 point = runner hits the called lane
- Defense still earns 1 point for a clean flag pull
### Option 2 (more “scoreboard fun”): **Team Touchdown (Small-Sided Game)**
**Setup:** hoops placed left/center/right. End zone hoops worth 6, sideline hoops worth 3 (per curriculum).
**Rules:**
- 2–3 on offense vs 2–3 on defense.
- Offense runs the ball and **places it into a hoop** to score.
- Defense pulls flags only in opponent territory (as written).
**Constraint bonus:** scoring only counts if the possession started with a **clean elbow-up pocket** handoff.
**JOB ZONE stays active** (no sitting):
This aligns with the “small-sided, constraints, everyone perceiving/reacting” guidance.
---
## Block 7 (3 min) — Wrap + Exit Ticket
**1 question (fast):**
“On a handoff, do we **reach** for the ball or let the QB place it?”
Expected: “Let QB place it.”
**1 show-me:**
“Freeze pocket: inside elbow up.”
**1 shout-out tied to cue:**
“I saw ___ keep inside elbow up and get a clean pocket—that’s why the run was smooth.”
---
# Week 5 Gamefication: “Playcaller Level-Up”
- Every time a **QB calls the correct lane** and the **RB executes elbow-up pocket receive**, that pair earns a **Playcaller token**.
	(Use a simple tally on a clipboard.)

If you want a quick defense-themed “runner vs sharks” style game as a 2-minute finisher some weeks, the “sharks and minnows” concept reinforces staying in bounds + dodging while defenders pull flags.
