# MINI VOLLEYBALL
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	2v2 or 3v3 on a smaller court when staffing and space allow.
	With ten per coach, two adjacent 2v2 games use eight players while two rotate in every two rallies as starters or observers.
	If you cannot see both games clearly, run 5v5 **Catch → Connect → Send** instead.
</callout>
## Base play
1. Begin with a gentle toss, or a serve from a short distance the server can actually make.
2. Up to three contacts. At entry, allow one agreed catch per side and say exactly which contact it is.
3. Rally point when the other side cannot return in bounds.
4. Play to five, or three minutes. Rotate everyone on a schedule, regardless of who is winning.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH RESPONSE**
	Watch several rallies without calling destinations.
	If every point ends on the start, simplify the entry.
	If one child dominates, change starter and partner roles.
	If the technique only appears after a prompt, record "with cue" and replay it independently.
	Close by asking one player what made the next contact easier, then change one condition and see whether that choice survives.
</callout>
