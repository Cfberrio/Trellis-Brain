# MINI-TENNIS / QUICK-START PLAY
**Setup:** Scaled-down court (service boxes only, or short court to the service line), low-compression ball, lower net if available. Matches USTA QuickStart scaling for the group's age/level.
**Base Game:** Live rally scoring — points to 7 or 11, win by 2. Underhand or scaled serve to start each point. Standard in/out rules on the scaled court.
**Use It To Check:** Whether the whole skill set (movement, groundstrokes, volleys if the group has them, serve) holds together in real play, not isolated reps — this is the closest the curriculum gets to "just play tennis."
**Constraint Menu:** Cooperative rally-count mode for the youngest/newest players before switching to competitive scoring. Add a volley-only zone near the net for groups working that skill. Doubles pairs for bigger groups needing more reps per court.
**Observable Evidence:** Players choose the right shot for the situation (not just swinging the same way every time), recover to ready position between shots, and track score correctly.
**If It Breaks:** If rallies die immediately, scale the court down further or slow the ball more. If players are bored waiting for a turn, split into more mini-courts with more players active at once.
**Progress The Check:** Move the boundary lines out toward a full court as consistency holds, one line at a time.
