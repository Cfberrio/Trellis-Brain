# RING OF FIRE
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Two teams, one lane each, lined up on the same side of the net. On the other side, one target per lane: a hoop on the floor, or a hoop hung from the net antenna or a stand if your site has one. **Never a player holding the hoop.**
	Coach at the net between the lanes to feed and count.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Name the contact for this round: serve, pass from a coach toss, or set from a coach toss.
2. First player in each lane sends the ball over the net at the ring. Through the hung hoop, or landing inside the floor hoop, is a hit.
3. Player collects their own ball around the net end and joins the back of the line. Next player goes as soon as the lane is clear.
4. Three rounds, one contact each. Change the contact, keep the target.
## How to progress and win
One point over the net, three points through or in the ring. Team beats its own score from the last round.
**Golden Ticket:** five ring hits in a round unlock a smaller hoop or a farther start line.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	The player looking at the ring before the contact. Cue: **"See it, then send it."** Ask where they aimed before you say anything about where it went.
	**If it breaks:** no ring hits at all → bring the ring to the attack line. Players sending hard to reach it → raise the hoop, do not move it back.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
Ring hits come from a chosen spot, not luck, across a short sample. Then check the same choice inside a rally in [[FIND THE SPACE]].
