# MIRROR & FREEZE
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP AND BOUNDARIES**
	Pairs face each other with clear space. One leader, one mirror.
	No ball needed to start.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Leader makes one step left or right. The mirror follows and stays balanced.
2. On "freeze," both show a ready posture with hands free.
3. Switch leader every 20 to 30 seconds.
4. Next round: the leader self-tosses and catches while the mirror moves, then balances as the catch happens.
## How a round ends
One pair point for matching movement and a balanced freeze. Five points ends a round.
## Variations
One step only at first, then add a short shuffle. Avoid exaggerated crouching and do not require hands joined throughout the movement.
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Balanced arrival and eyes tracking the leader or the ball. Leads into **Move–Stop–Set Squares** or **Beat the Ball**.
</callout>
