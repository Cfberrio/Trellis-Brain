# 2+2 BUILD UP
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	SETUP
	Two players per side of a real net: one near the net, one deeper.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Back player either passes the ball or catches it and throws it up to the net player.
2. Net player sets it back for the deep player to send over. Catching is only allowed on that middle touch, to keep the rally going while the pattern sets in.
3. Reset and repeat. Rotate the near/deep roles regularly.
## How to progress and win
Count consecutive crossings as a team score, not points against the other side.
**Golden Ticket:** 10 clean crossings in a row unlocks [[3v3 KEEP IT ALIVE]].
## Adjust the challenge
<table header-row="true">

<td>Make it easier</td>
<td>Make it harder</td>
</tr>

<td>Allow a catch on any touch, not just the middle one, while the pattern is brand new.</td>
<td>Layer in the Water Ditch constraint (a dead strip along the net) once contact is reliable.</td>

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	The pattern collapsing back into one-touch-and-over under pressure. Cue: **"Three before you send it."**
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
The team runs the full pattern on its own, without a coach reminder, even as the pressure ramps up.
