# FOUR SQUARE
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	A 2x2 grid of squares, about 2 m each, chalked or taped. Four players, one per square. One is the King (starts the ball). The rest of the group waits in a short line at the entry square.
	**Twenty players:** two grids per coach, eight active, two entrants each. Only run two grids if you can see both.
	![Four square grid layout](https://prod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/6f6cfb08-0636-44bc-a088-3627f4d72bee/ef035613-ef09-4c26-b448-a748202ac2bb/four-square-31.png) <!-- notion-hosted, URL expires -->
	*2023 DR layout. King square bottom left, entrants queue at the bottom right.*
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. King starts the ball with a toss to any square.
2. The player in that square sends it to another square with one contact: pass or set, whatever you named today. Stay inside your square.
3. Ball hits the floor in your square, or you send it out: you leave, walk to the back of the line, the next entrant takes your square. King moves to the empty square, or stays King if it was not their error.
4. Rounds run two minutes. Then swap who starts as King.
## How to progress and win
One point for every controlled send that another player can play. Count the grid's best streak, not who lasted longest. Entrants keep the line moving; nobody waits more than three rallies.
**Golden Ticket:** a five-contact streak unlocks a rule change for that grid: no catches, or a required set.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Eyes up before the ball arrives. Cue: **"Where next?"** Look for a player who chooses a square, not one who just gets rid of the ball.
	**If it breaks:** the same player is out every rally → allow one bounce for that player only. Ball never stays in → make the squares bigger.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
Players direct to a chosen square from more than one entry. This game checks contact and scanning, not team play: test the connection in [[BUILD THE BRIDGE]] or a CFU game.
