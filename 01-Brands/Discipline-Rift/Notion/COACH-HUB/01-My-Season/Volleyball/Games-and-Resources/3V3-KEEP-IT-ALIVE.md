# 3v3 KEEP IT ALIVE
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Three players per side of a real net.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. No more catching — it has to be a real pass-set-attack sequence.
2. One catch is still allowed per team, as a safety valve, if the rally is about to break down.
3. Reset and repeat. Rotate positions so everyone passes, sets, and sends.
## How to progress and win
Count consecutive crossings as a team score, same as 2+2.
**Golden Ticket:** mastery at this rung (the team is stringing sequences together without needing the safety-valve catch) unlocks [[4v4 RALLY]].

## Ready for the next problem when
The team runs the full pattern on its own, without a coach reminder, even as the pressure ramps up.
