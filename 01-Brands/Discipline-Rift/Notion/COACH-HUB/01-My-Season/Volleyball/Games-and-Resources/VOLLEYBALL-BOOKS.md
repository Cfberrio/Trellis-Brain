# VOLLEYBALL BOOKS
Three source books back this round of curriculum updates. Attached below are two reference PDFs on file. Here's what each source is good for and where its limits are.
## CEV School Project Teachers Handbook (Confédération Européenne de Volleyball)
Ages 8–12, lesson-by-lesson. Best for communication sequences (the "I / Here / Lurking" call chain and its "High, High, Flat" extension, both now live in [[CALL THE PLAY]]) and small constraint tweaks like the Water Ditch, now added to [[WHOSE BALL?]] and the 2+2/3v3/4v4 ladder.
## Volleyball Australia — Spikezone Coaching Resource Guide
Best for game-based skill combinations. Source for the 2+2 → 3v3 → 4v4 small-sided ladder and the alternate "superhero" cue chain for attacking (now on the [[ATTACKING]] page and [[HIGH HAND TARGET]]).
## Volleyball Canada — Long Term Development 3.0
Framework-heavy, thin on drill content. One portable, concrete rule pulled from it: **net height should roughly match the shortest player's reach at the wrist.** Use that as your standing setup note before you tape up a court.
LTD 3.0 also references **Smashball**, a 5-skill-level progression game. The level-by-level rules are not in this document — they live in Volleyball Canada's separate Smashball Trainer App, which DR does not have on file. Don't run Smashball off a guess. If a coach wants it, get the Trainer App first.
