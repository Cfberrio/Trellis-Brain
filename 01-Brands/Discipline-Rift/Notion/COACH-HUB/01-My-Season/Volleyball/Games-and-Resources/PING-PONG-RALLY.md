# PING PONG RALLY
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	A narrow lane across the net, one team per side, two or three players per team. One ball. The rest of each team lines up behind its own baseline, outside the lane.
	**Twenty players:** two lanes per coach, five per side per lane.
	![Ping pong rally layout](https://prod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/6f6cfb08-0636-44bc-a088-3627f4d72bee/f0601f62-aea7-4a34-a58a-54c70fc24dfb/ping-pong-54.png) <!-- notion-hosted, URL expires -->
	*2023 DR layout. Enter from the baseline, exit along the outside of the lane.*
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Coach tosses to Team 1. The front player returns it over the net with one contact, then **exits along the outside of the lane** and joins the back of the line.
2. The next player is already stepping in as the ball crosses. Team 2 does the same.
3. The ball may bounce once at entry level. Say so out loud.
4. Ball down twice, or out of the lane: the coach restarts with a toss to the other side. Nobody sits.
## How to progress and win
Count team returns across the net. Ten returns in a row is a team win. Points against the other team only after the group holds ten.
**Golden Ticket:** ten in a row unlocks no bounce, or a required pass instead of any contact.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	The exit and the entry, not the contact. Cue: **"Play, then go."** The next player must be in the lane before the ball comes back.
	**If it breaks:** players crossing the ball path on the way out → stop, walk the exit route once, restart. Rally never reaches three → coach tosses every ball for one round.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
The team keeps the rally with no coach reminder about exits. Check real recovery afterwards in [[HOME BASE]] or [[SPACE & SUPPORT]].
