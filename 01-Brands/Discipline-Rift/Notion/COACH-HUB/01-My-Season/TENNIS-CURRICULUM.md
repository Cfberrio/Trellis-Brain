# TENNIS CURRICULUM
CURRICULUM CULTURE: how we do things, how we connect as coaches. Built on USTA QuickStart principles — scaled courts, low-compression balls, and lower nets buy beginners the time every stroke on this page needs.
<callout icon="📐" color="gray_bg">
	**STANDING RULES — NON-NEGOTIABLE**
	**3 lines of players per coach.** Structure every drill and game so each coach runs 3 lines, not fewer. Fewer lines means fewer reps and more players standing around.
	**Teach 2+ skills per practice.** A practice must cover two or more skills, not one. Narrowing to a single skill leaves players undertrained on the rest of the ladder.
</callout>
<callout icon="🎾" color="gray_bg">
	**THE SEASON**
	*All six weeks built spiral-style: retrieve what's known, introduce one new thing, interleave, assess. Each week names the Skill and the Problem; you choose the game from the banks below.*
	[[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/Notion/COACH-HUB/01-My-Season/WEEK-1-FOREHANDS-4260d733|WEEK 1 - FOREHANDS]]
	*Forehand + Movement/Ready Position. Show them the sport, then teach the first stroke.*
	[[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/Notion/COACH-HUB/01-My-Season/WEEK-2-BACKHANDS-10804528|WEEK 2 - BACKHANDS]]
	*Retrieve forehand, introduce Backhand.*
	[[WEEK 3 - VOLLEYS]]
	*Retrieve both groundstrokes, introduce Volley.*
	[[WEEK 4 - SERVES]]
	*Retrieve groundstrokes + volley, introduce Serve/Return.*
	[[WEEK 5 - ALL SKILLS]]
	*Introduce Overhead/Lob, interleave everything learned.*
	[[WEEK 6 - ASSESSMENT]]
	*Full-game assessment day, tiered rubric, End of Season Celebration.*
</callout>
