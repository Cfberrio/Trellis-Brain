# TENNIS WARM-UP GAMES
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**SHOE-WORK TAG**
	*New group or new space. Throw-down spots scattered around the space, no rackets, no balls. Players move freely; on "stop," everyone lands on the nearest spot using a called footwork pattern (hop on one shoe, land two-to-one, etc.) and holds ready position. Add a partner "watch and copy" round for peer feedback.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**SHADOW STROKES**
	*Energy is flat. Coach calls a stroke name (forehand, backhand, volley, overhead) — players shadow-swing without a ball. Fast, no equipment, resets focus.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**SHADOWS**
	*Footwork before contact. Partners standing opposite each other across a line, both with a ball. One partner moves along the line and rolls/bounces/catches the ball freely using either hand; the other mirrors the movement exactly. Switch roles.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**HUNT AND GATHER**
	*Energy is flat, bigger space. Scatter balls across the court, players sprint to collect as many as possible into their own basket in 60 seconds. Pure movement, no technique.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**TOSS AND CROSS**
	*New group. Hoops spread around the space, one per player. Players toss the ball up above head height, aiming to land it inside the hoop, building the exact toss control the serve needs later. Coach can call right or left hand. Progress to catching above head height with the opposite hand.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**LADDERS**
	*Bigger space, older group. Throw-down spots (2 colors) and lines placed in channels across the space. Players move up the "ladder" in teams: jump 2-footed onto a line, hop onto the right spot with the right foot, left spot with the left foot. Builds the lateral movement every groundstroke depends on with a real footwork sequence, not just a shuffle.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**FIRE ANTS**
	*Brand-new group, zero equipment. Players stomp imaginary "fire ants" in a stationary ready position on the coach's call; progress to skip-and-stomp or shuffle-and-stomp.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**RACQUET QUICKNESS**
	*Energy reset, alternative to Shadow Strokes. Partners face each other, rackets balanced on the frame; on the count of three they swap places and catch each other's racket before it falls.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**SHAKE-HANDS TOSS**
	*New group, builds toss timing before Throw Up and Catch. Partners shake hands and hold the grip so their arms form a "net"; with the free hand, toss a ball over it, let it bounce, catch with one hand.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**BALL DROPS**
	*Doubles as serve-toss prep, complements Throw Up and Catch. Hold two balls at shoulder height in a "T" position, palms down; drop one, partner catches with one hand after the bounce, alternating sides.*
</callout>
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**COURT COMPASS**
	*New group or new space. Court-vocabulary warm-up — coach calls a line (baseline, service line, net, sideline), players sprint to touch it, reset and go again. No last-player-out elimination; the slowest arrivals reset and rejoin the next call.*

</callout>
