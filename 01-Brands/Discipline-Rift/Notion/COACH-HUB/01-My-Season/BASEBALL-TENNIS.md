# BASEBALL TENNIS
**Setup:** Mark the court into zones like a baseball diamond — short zone near the net = single, mid-court = double, deep near the baseline = triple, and a small target in the back corner = home run. Two teams, batting order.
**Base Game:** "Batter" gets a fed or self-fed ball and must land it in a zone to advance a "runner" (imaginary or a cone moved base to base). Three outs (three shots that land out of bounds or in the net) ends the inning. Innings and runs scored like baseball.
**Use It To Check:** Whether players can control depth on purpose, not just get the ball over the net.
**Constraint Menu:** Restrict to forehand-only or backhand-only innings to check a specific side. Widen zones for beginners; narrow them and add a "foul" zone (too short) for advanced players.
**Observable Evidence:** Ball placement matches the intended zone more often than not — depth control, not just direction.
**If It Breaks:** If nobody can reach the deep zone, move the deep line in and rescale. If every ball is a "single," the near zone is too big — shrink it.
**Progress The Check:** Add a defense — the other team stations players in the zones and can "catch" a ball for an out, turning it into live small-sided play.
