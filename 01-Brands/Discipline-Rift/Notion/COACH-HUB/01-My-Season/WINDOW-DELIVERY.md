# WINDOW DELIVERY
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Trios: tosser, setter, catcher. One ball. The catcher stands a comfortable short distance from the setter. Leave space away from the net.
	**Twenty players:** each coach owns ten, groups of 3 + 3 + 4. Every role rotates.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Tosser delivers a high, gentle ball slightly in front of the setter.
2. Setter moves under it, balances, brings hands into a comfortable window, and sets upward to the catcher.
3. Catcher receives at a comfortable height without chasing.
4. Rotate all roles after three attempts. Demonstrate the whole action before you use numbered pauses.
## How to progress and win
One point for a set the catcher can receive within one step. Collect three. Do not score minimal spin as the only measure of success.
**Golden Ticket:** three controlled deliveries unlock a moving entry, or the setter role in **Build the Bridge**.
## Adjust the challenge

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Cue: **"Under, then up to your teammate."** Watch comfortable finger-pad contact and coordinated extension, not just leg movement.
	**If it breaks:** child avoids the ball → softer, lighter ball and slower feed. Set drifts → move the feed into a reachable position before adding another cue.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
Several playable sets from a stable start, then from one small side movement.
