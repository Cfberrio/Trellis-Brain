# MOVEMENT + PROBLEM: RECOVERY BREAKS DOWN UNDER A LIVE RALLY, NOT JUST A CONE DRILL → TEAM SINGLES
**Setup:** Teams of two, one ball, one team behind the other at the center of the baseline.
**Show It:** First player on one side drop-hits the ball over the net; the returner keeps the rally going as long as possible.
**Challenge:** When the rally ends, both players rotate out and the next pair steps in. Goal is controlling the ball down the center of the court.
**Progress/Win:** Track the team's longest rally across rotations; beat the team's own record.
