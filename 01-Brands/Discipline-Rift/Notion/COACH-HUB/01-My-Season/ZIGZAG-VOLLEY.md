# VOLLEY + PROBLEM: NO REPS WITHOUT A LARGE GROUP → ZIGZAG VOLLEY
**Setup:** 4-6 players split evenly on both sides of the net, lined up down its length.
**Show It:** First player self-tosses and volleys across to the first player on the other side, who volleys to the next player in their own line, and so on up and down.
**Challenge:** Count how many volleys in a row the whole line can complete without the ball touching the ground.
**Progress/Win:** Beat the group's best count. Progress to backhand-only or alternating forehand/backhand down the line.
