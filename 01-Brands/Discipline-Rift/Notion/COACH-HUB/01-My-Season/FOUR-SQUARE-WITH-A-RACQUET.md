# CFU — FOUR SQUARE WITH A RACQUET
**Checks:** Whether a player understands and can operate inside a live, rotating scoring game — not just whether they can rally.
**Setup:** A four-square grid (tape/rope on the ground), one racket + ball per square, 4 or more players. Extra players wait "on deck" to rotate in.
**Base Game:** Ball is put in play among the four players on the grid, following a clockwise rotation toward the top square. Players hit the ball, letting it bounce once, into any other square.
**How to Progress:** If a player loses a point (misses, double-bounces, hits out), they drop to the lowest square and everyone else advances one square toward the top. A player who loses when already in the lowest square rotates off; the next "on deck" player enters there.

**Observable Evidence:** Player holds the top square by winning points — the built-in "Special Player" slot (the same Golden Ticket mechanic used elsewhere in DR: earn the top spot, then defend it under harder pressure since everyone is now aiming to dethrone you).
**Coach Watches For:** Players calling in/out honestly, keeping the ball playable rather than smashing every touch, and understanding their own square/rotation without a reminder.
