# THE LINES GAME
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP AND BOUNDARIES**
	Players spread out on a clear court.
	Walk to the end line, the sideline, the center line, and the attack line. Use flat markers if your space has different lines.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Demonstrate moving to one called line and freezing in a ready stance.
2. Give three practice calls at walking speed, then move up to a comfortable jog.
3. Call one line at a time. Everyone moves. Nobody is eliminated.
4. After four calls, let a player name the next line.
## How a round ends
The group earns one point when everyone finds the correct line and leaves personal space. Five points ends a round.
## Variations
For newcomers, point while you name the line. Later, remove the pointing, or add "ready stance" after arrival. Only use line names that actually exist on your marked court.
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Whether players know where to go without following the crowd. Ask a few individuals to point before the next round.
</callout>
