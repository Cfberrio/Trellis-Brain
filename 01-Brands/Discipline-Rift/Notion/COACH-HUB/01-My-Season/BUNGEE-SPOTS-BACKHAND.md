# BACKHAND + PROBLEM: FLAT/DOWNWARD SWING → BUNGEE SPOTS — BACKHAND
**Setup:** A zigzag line of 4 poly-spots/cones, racket + ball per player.
**Show It:** Demonstrate moving spot to spot, bungee-jumping the ball on the backhand face (knuckles up) — drop, bounce, catch on strings — at each spot before moving to the next.
**Challenge:** Move through all 4 spots, backhand-face bungee at each one, without the free hand assisting.
**Progress/Win:** Clean pass through all 4 spots = win. Then add a double-bungee (2 touches) at each spot before repeating continuously without stopping between spots.

**Coach Watches For:** Racket drop before the swing, low-to-high path, finish high, same grip held through the whole sequence.
