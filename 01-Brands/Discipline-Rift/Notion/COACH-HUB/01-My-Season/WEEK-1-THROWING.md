# WEEK 1 - THROWING

**Cue of the Week:**
STEP to your target (opposite foot)
**Support Cue:**
Eyes on hips (flag pulling)
**Theme:**
Discipline creates freedom. Scrimmage is earned.
### Purpose
- Build a repeatable throw: **side → ball to ear → STEP → throw**
- Start catching foundations: **diamond hands (mid/high) + rake hands (low)**
---
# DR Practice Structure (60 minutes)
## 1️⃣ Block (3 min) — Belonging + Boundaries + Buy-In
---
## Step 1 — Introduction (Command Presence First)
**Coach Script (confident, calm, short):**
“Welcome to DR — Disciplined Rift.
I’m Coach ____. This is Coach ____.
We’re here to help you get better — fast.
If you listen and work, you will scrimmage more.
If you waste time, scrimmage gets shorter.
I don’t control the clock. We have 60 minutes.
How we use it? That’s on us.”
*(Pause. Eye contact. No rushing.)*
---
## Step 2 — Define Discipline (20 seconds)
“Discipline means doing what you need to do — even when you don’t feel like it.
Sometimes practice is fun.
Sometimes it’s repetitive.
Both make you better.
Better players = better scrimmage.”
---
## Step 3 — Communication System (Attention Callouts)
We do not yell over chaos. We use signals.
### Callout System (Pick 1–2 and keep consistent)
**Option A: Clap Reset**
- Coach: *Clap-Clap*
- Players: *Clap*
- Coach: “Eyes!”
**Option B: Echo Call**
- Coach: “DR!”
- Players: “DISCIPLINE!”
**Option C: If You Can Hear Me**
- “If you can hear me, clap once.”
- (Layer until silent)
**Teach it once. Practice it once. Expect it always.**
**Standard:**
- Eyes on coach
- Ball still
- Mouth closed
- Body still
**Language:**
“Fast resets = more scrimmage.”
---
## Step 4 — Ground Rules (Clear + Binary)
### Our 4 Practice Non-Negotiables
1. **When coach talks → you listen**
2. **No clowning teammates**
3. **Stay in boundaries**
4. **Fast resets**
**Define Boundaries Physically**
- Show field limits
- Show sideline area
- Show Job Zone
---
## Step 5 — Scrimmage Is Earned (Make It Concrete)
“Scrimmage is your free time.
You want more scrimmage?
We move fast.
If it takes 10 seconds to get quiet → we keep our time.
If it takes 1 minute → we lose 50 seconds of scrimmage.”
Make it visible:
“We’re supposed to scrimmage 20 minutes.
If we waste time, it might be 5.”
Let silence sit.
“It’s not my choice. It’s the clock.”
---
## Step 7 — Sideline Etiquette (Prevent Chaos Early)
When off-field:
- Partner pass only
- No tackling
- No running across drills
- Stay inside cone boundary
Language:
“If you’re not engaged, you’re partner passing.
No loose cannons.”

## Micro-Check for Understanding (30 sec)
Ask:
- “What happens if we waste time?”
- “How do we get more scrimmage?”
- “What does discipline mean?”
Players answer in 1 sentence.
---
## Close Block 1 With Authority
“We don’t coach people.
We coach cues.
We don’t waste reps.
We earn scrimmage.
Let’s get better.”
---
## Block 2 (8 min) — Retrieval Warm-Up Game: Flag Tag
### Setup
- Cone rectangle boundaries
- Everyone wears flags
### How to Play
- Everyone moves, dodges, flees, chases
- Players try to pull flags
### Coaching cue (Defense)
- **Eyes on hips**
- Stay **low** and **square**
- Reach for the **hip**
### “Out” Rule (DR Job Zone)
If **both flags** are pulled → **JOB ZONE** (2 cones on sideline)
**Option A — Quick Job (10–15 sec):**
- **Dynamic burst** (choose 1): high knees / jumping jacks / mountain climbers
	**Re-entry:** clip flags back on → jump right in
**Option B — Stay-on Job (30 sec max):**
- **Reset Captain:** help one teammate re-clip / return a loose flag
	**Re-entry:** say “READY” → back in
**Micro-CFU (5 sec):**
“Where are your eyes when pulling flags?” → “Hips!”
**Safety**
- No contact, no diving, two feet down on pulls
---
## Block 3 (7 min) — Skill Clarity: Throwing (NEW cue)
### Setup
- Partners facing each other
- Start close (5–7 yards), expand only if successful
### Teach (30 seconds)
You demonstrate once. Then everyone mirrors.
**Throwing checkpoints (you say them fast):**
- [[HOW TO THROW A FOOTBALL]]
- Side to target
- Ball to ear
- **STEP to target (opposite foot)** ✅ *(today’s cue)*
- Rotate chest/hips
- Follow through
**Coach script:**
“Your **step is your aim**. Step with your **opposite foot** right at your partner.”
### CFU (show-me)
“Freeze: show me your **step foot** pointed to your target.”
---
## Block 4 (12 min) — Fluency Reps: Partner Poly Pass
### Setup
- 1 ball per pair
- Two poly spots per pair about **5 yards apart**
### How to Run (3 mini-rounds)
1. **One-knee throws** (build arm-leg opposition feel) – 4 min
2. **Stand + boxer step** (turn sideways for power) – 4 min
3. **Distance ladder** (1 step back after 2 accurate throws) – 4 min
**Rep goal:** “10 quality throws each.”
### Catching reminders (quick, not a new coaching focus)
- Mid/high: **diamond hands**
- Low: **rake hands**
- Catch → **squeeze** → tuck it
**Micro-CFU (10 sec):**
- “Why do we turn sideways?” (power/aim)
- “What does arm-leg opposition mean?” (opposite foot steps)
---
## Block 5 (13 min) — Decision Reps: “Call the Catch”
Same partners, same spots — now add a tiny decision.
### How to Play
- Thrower calls **LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH** before throwing
- Receiver must show the correct hands:
	- LOW = **rake**
	- MED/HIGH = **diamond**
- Switch roles every 3 throws
**Coach focus stays the same:** **STEP to your target** (don’t add more throw cues)
**Success criteria:**
- Thrower steps to target
- Receiver shows correct hands + squeezes
---
## Block 6 (14 min) — Application Game: Mascot Ball (Groups of 4)
### Setup
- Groups of **4**
- **QB in the middle**, 3 receivers around (triangle)
### How to Play
- QB throws to a receiver
- Receiver catches using correct hands, **squeezes**, **tucks it away**, and yells the mascot/team word
- Receiver throws back to QB
- Continue until **all 3 receivers** catch
- Rotate QB each round so everyone plays QB and receiver
### Scoring (gamefied + cue-based)
- **Touchdown = 3/3 catches** in a round
- **Bonus point** if QB shows **STEP to target** on every throw that round
### “Out” Rule (DR Job Zone — keep the game moving)
**If a pass is dropped or off-target:**
- Player goes to **JOB ZONE** for **10 seconds**
**Quick Job (primary): Instant Coach**
- Tell the next thrower: “**STEP to your target**” + quick demo step
	**Re-entry:** done → back in on next turn
**Backup Quick Job: Catch Hands Demo**
- Show **diamond** and **rake** hands once each
	**Re-entry:** done → back in
**Coach script:**
“JOB! Coach the cue. Back in fast.”
---
## Block 7 (3 min) — Wrap + Exit Ticket
### Exit Ticket (fast)
- “Freeze and show me: **STEP to your target**.”
- Partner share: “Tell 2 throw cues and 2 catch cues.”
### Shout-out
“I saw ___ step to target and ___ use diamond/rake hands the right way.”
---

**Optional personal challenge:**

---
# Differentiation
**Easier**
- Foam ball, shorter distance, slower throws
- Receivers closer to QB in Mascot Ball
- Allow a “bounce catch” on low throws at first
**Harder**
- Increase distance
- Require receiver to move 2 steps to a cone before catching
- Bonus point only if catch is made clean + immediate tuck
---
# Safety + Belonging
- No contact, no diving
- Celebrate effort + improvement
- “We coach cues, not people.”
