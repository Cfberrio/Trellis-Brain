# MOVEMENT + PROBLEM: NO SPLIT STEP → SHUFFLE & CATCH
**Setup:** Partners a short distance apart, one ball.
**Show It:** Demonstrate the partner tossing to either side; receiver reacts with a shuffle-step (no crossover) to reach it, timed off the toss like a split step is timed off contact.
**Challenge:** Partner tosses to either side; receiver must shuffle-step (not cross over) to reach and catch/return it.
**Progress/Win:** 5 clean returns without a crossover step = win. Then add a real fed ball and a split-step timed to the feeder's contact before the shuffle.

**Coach Watches For:** Hop/shuffle timed to the toss, not early or late; balanced landing; no crossover substituting for a shuffle.
