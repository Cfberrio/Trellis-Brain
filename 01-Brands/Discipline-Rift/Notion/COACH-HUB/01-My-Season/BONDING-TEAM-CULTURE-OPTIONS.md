# BONDING & TEAM CULTURE OPTIONS
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**HOW TO USE THIS PAGE**
	Pick at most one opener and one closer per session. None of these teach a volleyball skill; they build the team that makes the skill work. Skip all of them on a day the ladder needs the time instead.
</callout>
## Openers (Intro, 1–2 min)
**Pass Your Name Around.** Circle, one ball, say your name as you toss it to someone. Catch and toss only, no technique. Good for week 1 or any new group. *2023 DR curriculum, p. 11.*
**Team Building Circle.** Circle up. Each player names one teammate and one specific thing that teammate did well last time. Coach goes last. Good after a hard or scattered practice. *2023 DR curriculum, p. 11.*
**Team Handshake or Chant.** Let the group build a short handshake sequence or a one-line chant over a few sessions. Run it before the warm-up game. Keep it under 30 seconds so it never eats ladder time. *2023 DR curriculum, p. 11.*
**Through the Hoop.** Two lines facing each other, holding hands. Pass a hula hoop down the line without anyone letting go. Cooperative, not a setting drill — keep it separate from any target-hoop challenge. *2023 DR curriculum, p. 11.*
## Closers (Close, 1–2 min)
**Teammate shout-out.** Before Recognition, let one or two players name a teammate and what that teammate did to help today. Feeds directly into the coach's own Recognition line.
**Season-ending ceremony.** Week 6 only. The full script lives in the Week 6 Close block: one sentence per player by name, certificate if the site uses them, one next step for the group. Do not run a shortened version earlier in the season — it loses its weight.
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Whether the group actually wants it. A forced chant or handshake some weeks in is worse than skipping it. Let it come from the players once it starts.
</callout>
