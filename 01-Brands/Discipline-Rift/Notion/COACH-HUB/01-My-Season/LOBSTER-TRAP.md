# FOREHAND + PROBLEM: NO FOOTWORK TO A MOVING BALL → LOBSTER TRAP
**Setup:** Partners stand 6-8 feet apart. One player (the feeder) has a ball; the other (the "lobster") holds a racket in each hand.
**Show It:** Feeder tosses the ball to different spots; the lobster traps it between the two rackets after the bounce.
**Challenge:** Feeder varies the toss — left, right, short, deep — so the lobster has to move their feet to trap it. Complete 4, then switch roles.
**Progress/Win:** Progress to trapping with one racket only, then to a catch-and-send-back with a controlled forehand.
