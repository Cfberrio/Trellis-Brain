# SPACE & SUPPORT
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP AND BASE PLAY**
	Same two 5v5 courts as Catch → Connect → Send.
	Mark two broad sending areas on each side. Catch permissions follow the group's current level.
</callout>
1. Before the round, name **one** problem you will observe: finding space, or recovering into support.
2. Teams play a two or three contact rally with their usual contact rules.
3. The final sender chooses a broad area. Opponents are free to move and receive.
4. Rotate the starter every rally and the playing locations on the agreed schedule.
## One constraint per round
<table header-row="true">
<tr>
<td>Round option</td>
<td>Rule</td>
<td>Coach watches for</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Find space</td>
<td>One bonus for a controlled send toward the more open broad area.</td>
<td>Player notices defenders instead of obeying your target call.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Help next</td>
<td>One bonus when a non-receiver becomes available for the second ball.</td>
<td>Movement creates a receiving option.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Recover</td>
<td>Watch readiness as the return crosses; keep ordinary scoring.</td>
<td>Players stay connected after their own touch.</td>
</tr>
</table>
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**FREEZE, ASK, REPLAY**
	Ask "Where was the space?" or "Who could help next?" Have the player point, then replay a similar ball.
	In the next live rally, let defenders start somewhere else.
	Never give the answer while the ball is in the air.
</callout>
## Easier and harder
**Easier:** catch and toss with two large visible areas.
**Harder:** require a controlled final strike, then change the incoming angle.
Do not add a new contact skill and a new tactical rule at the same time.
