# WHOSE BALL?
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Four players: tosser, two receivers, target. Mark two starting areas and leave a shared middle seam. Soft reachable tosses only.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Toss toward one receiver first. Demonstrate saying **"mine"** before contact, and the partner preparing to help.
2. Then toss nearer the seam so the receivers have to choose.
3. The claiming player catches or passes. The other offers a target or a backup position.
4. Rotate roles after three feeds.
## How to progress and win
One point for a clear claim with one committed receiver **and** one helpful partner. Add pass quality only when passing is the focus.
**Golden Ticket:** three clear shared decisions unlock an unpredictable entry.
## Adjust the challenge
<table header-row="true">
<tr>
<td>Make it easier</td>
<td>Make it harder</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Catch and toss with a clearly favoured receiver. No points deducted for being quiet; replay the opportunity straight away.</td>
<td>Less predictable seam feeds, then serve entries. Require the helper to be available for the second ball.</td>
</tr>

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Cue: **"Mine early, help next."** Volume is not the goal. A call shouted after contact is too late to coordinate anything.
	**If it breaks:** one child takes every ball → feed wider to the other child and show how a partner creates the next play.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
Both receivers take responsibility on different balls and support when they are not first.
