# BEAT THE BALL
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Trios: tosser, mover, target. Mark a center starting spot and two reachable receiving areas. Keep neighbouring feeds in separate lanes.
	**Twenty players:** each coach owns ten, groups of 3 + 3 + 4. Every role rotates.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Begin by announcing left or right before you toss.
2. Player starts at center, moves into the flight path, and passes toward the target.
3. Target catches. The passer returns to center once the ball is secured.
4. Rotate after three feeds so everyone reads, moves, and targets.
## How to progress and win
One point for arriving balanced and producing a playable pass. In the entry version, score the movement with a catch instead of a pass.
**Golden Ticket:** three balanced arrivals unlock an unannounced direction. Not a faster ball at the same time.
## Adjust the challenge
<table header-row="true">

<td>Use a catch, a shorter movement, and a slower toss. Demonstrate one step before adding a shuffle.</td>
<td>Remove the spoken direction so the player reads the ball. Later vary short and deep within a reachable area.</td>

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	First movement while the ball is still in the air. Cue: **"Beat it to the spot."** Do not let a locked platform become the movement posture.
	**If it breaks:** player runs past the ball → shrink the travel distance. Every ball unreachable → fix the toss before you correct the child.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
The player reaches balls on both sides without you naming the direction. Recheck from a new starting position.
