# SEASON OVERVIEW

### 1. Passing & Receiving
- Throwing to a teammate with control
- Catching and securing the ball
- Passing and catching while moving
### 2. Ball Carrying
- Running with the ball under control
- Protecting the ball from defenders
- Changing speed/direction while staying balanced
### 3. Flag Pulling & Defensive Play
- Approaching the ball carrier safely
- Pulling flags consistently
- Staying between the runner and the end zone (basic positioning)
### 4. Movement & Athletic Foundations
- Starting, stopping, and changing direction
- Running in space with awareness
- Balance, coordination, and body control
### 5. Spatial Awareness
- Finding open space on offense
- Spreading out and avoiding “bunching”
- Staying in bounds and using the field effectively
### 6. Simple Game Decisions
- Choosing when to run vs. pass
- Moving to space when you don’t have the ball
- Defending the closest threat and reacting to the play
### 7. Team Communication & Sportsmanship
- Listening and responding to simple cues/signals
- Communicating with teammates (calling for the ball, helping on defense)
- Respect, effort, and positive team behavior
### 8. Rules, Safety, and Game Readiness
- Understanding basic flag football rules and flow
- Playing safely (no contact, controlled pursuit)
- Knowing how to start/stop plays and transition between offense/defense
