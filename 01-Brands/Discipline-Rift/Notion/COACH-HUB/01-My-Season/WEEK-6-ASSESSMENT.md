## ALL-SKILLS ASSESSMENT
Each player gets three tries at every stroke (forehand, backhand, volley, serve). Coaches score against the tier the player is working at.
### Observable Evidence (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced)
| Skill | Beginner | Intermediate | Advanced |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forehand | Ball over the net 2/3 tries, visible turn + low-to-high swing | Controls direction/moderate topspin, lands in a target zone | Depth, spin, pace under pressure; varies crosscourt/down the line |
| Backhand | Ball over the net 2/3 tries, correct grip + rotation | Consistent control with topspin or slice, lands in a target zone | Varies crosscourt/down the line with pace and accuracy |
| Volley | Clean contact 2/3 tries, proper grip, minimal swing | Forehand + backhand volleys to target areas, short swing | Firm volleys placed deep, minimal error, handles fast feeds |
| Serve | Ball in the box 2/3 tries, proper stance/toss/follow-through | Lands in target areas with moderate pace, consistent toss | Consistent high-velocity serves with placement or spin |
This rubric is the same one that lived on the old Week 6 page — carried over here so the tiered language isn't lost, and now also folded into each stroke's skill page under Observable Evidence.

## CLOSE — END OF SEASON CELEBRATION
**Ceremony:** Call each player's name, team claps and cheers, hand out certificates, note effort/improvement for each player.
**Pictures:** Group photo, multiple angles, share with parents via email or the usual communication platform.
**Treats:** Available for pick-up at the front desk alongside certificates.
**Flyers:** Hand out the next-season flyer with every certificate — returning players bring momentum and strengthen the team. Offer extra flyers for players to share with friends. Encourage early registration.
