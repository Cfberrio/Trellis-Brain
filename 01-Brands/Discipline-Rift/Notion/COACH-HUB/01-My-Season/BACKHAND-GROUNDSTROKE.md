# BACKHAND GROUNDSTROKE
*(One- and Two-Handed, Developmental Tennis)*
---
## Why We Use It
Most beginners have no backhand instinct — it's the first stroke where the "natural" swing is wrong. USTA QuickStart guidance favors the two-handed backhand for younger/smaller players (more control, more power from a weaker side) with a path to one-handed later for players with the strength and preference for it. Scaled courts and low-compression balls buy the extra time this stroke needs while it's new.
---
## SHOW IT — BACKHAND BY NUMBERS
### Position 1 — Ready
**Cue:** "Ready"
- Two hands on the grip (or one, for advanced one-handers)
- Split step as the feed is struck
### Position 2 — Turn
**Cue:** "Back pocket"
- Shoulders turn so the back shoulder points at the net
- Racket takes back with both hands together (two-handed) or high unit turn (one-handed)
**Why:** The turn does the work the weaker arm can't do alone.
### Position 3 — Swing
**Cue:** "Low to high, push through"
- Racket drops below the ball
- Weight steps into the shot
- Contact out in front
### Position 4 — Finish
**Cue:** "Finish high"
- Two-handed: both hands finish across the body near the opposite shoulder
- One-handed: racket arm extends and finishes above the shoulder, off-hand trails for balance
---
## Core Backhand Pattern
**1 → 2 → 3 → 4** — Ready → Turn → Swing → Finish.
---
## ADVANCED EXTENSION — APPROACH SHOT
**Cue:** "Deep, then in"
- Hit deep down the opponent's court with enough topspin/slice to stay inside the baseline
- Move forward toward the net immediately after contact
- Down the line or crosscourt depending on the opponent's position
**Why:** Same pattern as the Forehand's approach shot, mirrored to the backhand side — advanced tier only, after the core pattern is reliable under rally pressure.

## Progression Connections
Beginner: two-handed, fed backhands, short court. Intermediate: backhand off a bounce with lateral movement; introduce one-handed option for players ready for it. Advanced: backhand under rally pressure, spin and placement (crosscourt/down the line/slice).
---
## Observable Evidence (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced)
- **Beginner:** Ball over the net into the court 2 of 3 tries with correct grip and body rotation.
- **Intermediate:** Consistent control with basic topspin or slice, lands in a target zone.
- **Advanced:** Varies backhands (crosscourt, down the line) with pace and accuracy under rally pressure.
