# WEEK 1 — PASSING + SETTING
**North Star:** *Pass/Set to a target with control*
**Culture outcome (Discipline Rift):** *Commit to the decision + follow through (fast listening, fast reset, try again)*
**Daily cue:** **“Platform early.”** *(you can swap to “Angle = direction” later in the session)*
---
## Block 1 — Culture Routine + Belonging (2–3 min)
### What the coach DOES / SAYS (script)
1. **Welcome (20 sec):** “Coaches, names, welcome to DR Volleyball.”
2. **Discipline Rift definition (30 sec):**
	- “Discipline isn’t punishment. Discipline is the power to **commit to a decision and follow through**.”
3. **Team expectation (20 sec):** “When I give a cue, you respond fast. When you miss, you reset fast.”
4. **Call-and-response (30 sec):**
	- Coach: “Mine on three. 1–2–3…”
	- Team: “MINE!” *(repeat twice)*
5. **Belonging rule (20 sec):** “If you’re out in a game, you go to **Job Zone** for 10 seconds and come right back.”
### Micro-CFU (choral)
Coach: “What’s discipline?”
Team: “Commit and follow through.”
**Next progression:** Retrieval warm-up where they must listen fast + move fast.
---
### ✅ ADD-ON OPTIONS (Block 1) — Fast culture + communication boosters (30–60 sec)
Use *only if you need more energy or more buy-in* without adding time.
**Option A: “VOLLEY / ATTENTION”**
- Coach: “VOLLEY!”
- Team: “ATTENTION!”
- Coach: “Mine on three—1,2,3…”
- Team: “MINE!”
**Option B: Name Pop**
- Coach: “Say one teammate’s name.”
- **Wait** until you hear names.
- Then: “Mind on three…”

## Block 2 — Retrieval Warm-Up (6–8 min)
### A) THE LINES GAME (court awareness + listening)
This needs to be **crystal clear** for new coaches, so here’s the exact version:
### Setup
- All players start on the **center line**, spread out (no pushing).
- Coach stands where everyone can see/hear.
### Court overview (60–90 sec max)
Walk to each line and point:
- **End line / baseline**
- **Sidelines**
- **Center line**
- **Attack line (10-foot line)**
- *(Optional later: “net line” / “under the net” is not a line—don’t teach that as a line.)*
### Instructions (practice rounds first)
1. Coach says: “When I call a line, you sprint to it and **touch it with your hand**. Then freeze in athletic stance.”
2. Coach calls 3 practice rounds:
	- “Attack line!” → they run/touch/freeze
	- “Sideline!” → run/touch/freeze
	- “End line!” → run/touch/freeze
### Game version (choose ONE)
**Version 1: Elimination (original)**
- Last player to touch the line goes to **Job Zone** (not “out forever”).
- After 10 seconds, they re-enter next round.
**Version 2: Team points (faster, fewer stoppages)**
- Split into 2 teams.
- Everyone runs each call.
- **1 point** if your whole team touches the correct line and freezes in stance within 3 seconds.
- First to **7** wins.
### Add the retrieval twist (optional)
- On “Freeze!” coach asks: “Point to Zone 1!” *(scan fast)*
### Job Zone (10 seconds, no idle)
- 3 platform locks + “Platform early!”
- 2 step–shuffle reps in place
- 1 low freeze (ready position)
### Micro-CFU
Coach: “Point to the attack line.” (scan)
**Next progression:** Connect posture + footwork to volleyball stance (Crab Position).
---
### ✅ ADD-ON OPTIONS (Block 2A) — Lines Game variations (same game, more reps, more listening)
Pick **one** to keep it simple for coaches.
**Option 1: “Call + Action”**
- Coach calls a line **plus** an action:
	- “Attack line + platform freeze!”
	- “End line + hands up!”
- Purpose: adds volleyball posture retrieval without teaching new skills.
**Option 2: “Two-Line Combo”**
- Coach calls two lines in a row:
	- “Sideline—Attack line!”
- Players must touch both, then freeze.
- Purpose: faster brain + faster feet.
**Option 3: “Zone Point Check”**
- After touching a line, coach yells “ZONE!”
- Players must point to Zone 1/5/6 instantly.
- Purpose: court mapping under tempo.
---
### B) SHUFFLING CRAB GAME (posture + platform + movement)
This is your “3 P’s” game — keep it exact.
### Teach “Crab Position” with the 3 P’s (30 sec)
- **Posture:** low, knees bent, chest tall
- **Platform:** hands together, arms straight (ready to pass)
- **Position:** feet wider than shoulders, weight on toes
### Setup
- Players spread out facing the coach (enough space to shuffle without collisions).
- Coach holds a volleyball as the visual cue.
### Instructions
1. Everyone starts in Crab Position.
2. Coach moves the ball:
	- right / left / forward / backward
3. Players must **shuffle** the direction the ball moves while staying low.
### Challenge rule (keeps it fun)
- Coach may **say “right”** but move the ball left.
- Players must follow **the ball**, not the voice.
### “Out” rule (keep it light + fast)
- Out = stands up, crosses feet, or follows the wrong direction
- Out → Job Zone 10 sec → re-enter
### Micro-CFU
On whistle: “Freeze—show Crab Position + platform.”
**Next progression:** Skill install (passing), because platform is already built.
---
### ✅ ADD-ON OPTIONS (Block 2B) — Crab Game upgrades (still same posture, more volleyball transfer)
Pick **one**.
**Option 1: “Crab + Call”**
- Players must call “MINE” on every move cue.
- Out = silent OR breaks posture.
**Option 2: “Crab + Platform Late Trap”**
- Coach says: “NO PLATFORM!” while moving ball (hands relaxed).
- Coach says: “PLATFORM!” and they snap it in 1 second.
- Purpose: directly reinforces your “platform early” habit *on command*.
**Option 3: “Crab Mirror Race”**
- Split into two teams.
- Team point if everyone stays low and moves correctly for 5 consecutive cues.

## Block 3 — Teaching Block (6–8 min)
### Passing install (fast + unified language)
**Learning target:** “Pass with a strong platform to a repeatable target.”
**Cue:** **“Platform early.”** *(or “Angle = direction” if that’s your focus)*
### What the coach demos (2 reps only)
- Bad rep: platform forms late, arms swing
- Good rep: platform early, quiet arms, legs lift
### Imitation (30 sec)
- Coach calls numbers for passing sequence (your system):
	- “1 Ready” → “2 Pancakes” → “3 Low” → “4 Up”
- Everyone freezes each position quickly.
### 1-rep check (everyone sees it)
- Coach tosses to 3–5 athletes quickly (not everyone).
- Ask the group: “Was platform early?” thumbs up/down.
### Micro-CFU
Coach: “Show me platform early in 2 seconds.” (freeze)
**Next progression:** High-rep engine with targets + scoring.
---
### ✅ ADD-ON OPTIONS (Block 3) — Fast teaching “checks” (30–60 sec total)
These are quick “verifications,” not extra teaching.
**Option A: “Target Call Freeze”**
- Coach calls: “Angle to Zone 2!”
- Athletes freeze platform angle and point where the ball would go.
**Option B: “One Word Fix”**
- Coach says only one cue: “SURFBOARDS!”
- Athletes instantly correct elbows/arms without more explanation.
## Block 4 — Skill Acquisition Reps Engine (14–18 min)
Run **two main lanes** plus an optional connection lane if you have enough balls/coaches.
### Lane 1 — PASSING (pick 2 games, 5–6 min each)
### Option 1: TARGET HOOPS (your Target Drill, clarified)
**Setup**
- 2–4 hoops on the other side of the net (or cones if no hoops).
- One line of passers on your side at the attack line (easier) or deeper (harder).
- Coach feeds from the opposite side or same side.
**How it runs**
- Athlete steps up, coach tosses, athlete passes over net aiming for hoop zone.
- Athlete gets ball, returns to line fast.
**Scoring**
- Over net in bounds = 1
- In a hoop = 2
- Called “special hoop” = 3
**Coaching focus**
- Platform early
- Quiet arms
- Low → Up

### Option 2: SHUFFLE & PASS (your drill, tightened)
**Setup**
- Coach and athlete start center.
- Mark 3 spots: left/middle/right.
**How it runs**
- Coach toss → pass
- Coach + athlete shuffle to next spot together → toss → pass
- 3 passes then rotate athlete out.
**Gamify**
- “Clean 3” = athlete earns a point for their line/team.

### Lane 2 — SETTING (pick 2 games, 5–6 min each)
**Learning target:** “Move under, stop, legs up, fingertip control to target.”
**Cue:** **“Stop then push up.”** *(or “Finger tips… up!” depending what’s breaking)*
### Option 1: THROUGH THE HOOP (clarified)
**Setup**
- Basketball hoop OR held hoop target.
- Line of setters.
**How it runs**
- Coach tosses to forehead height.
- Athlete moves, stops, sets through hoop.
**Scoring**
- Through = 2
- Hits rim = 1
- Miss = 0
**Coaching focus**
- Feet stop before contact
- Legs push up
- Fingertips only
### Option 2: SETTING TO WALL (if wall space exists)
**Setup**
- Pairs at wall, one ball per pair.
**How it runs**
- Continuous sets to wall, recover, set again.
- Count consecutive clean fingertip contacts.
**Gamefy**
- “Beat your best” scoreboard (pairs record high score)

### ✅ ADD-ON OPTIONS (Lane 2 — SETTING) — More setting drills/games coaches can choose from
### Option 3: “Hershey Kiss Freeze” + Set
**Setup:** line of setters; coach tosses.

**Score:** 10 clean catches = win; drop resets.
**Focus:** controlled release, fingertip contact.
### Option 7: “No Spin Challenge”

**Score:** +1 for “quiet spin”; -1 for palm/slap.

## Block 5 — DR Teaching (Guided Decisions) 
**Learning target:** “Choose hands vs platform based on ball height/cleanliness.”
**Cue:** **“High/clean = hands. Low/fast = platform.”**
### Game: PASS-OR-SET GATE (crystal clear setup)
**Setup**
- Create two zones with cones:
	- **Hands Zone:** balls tossed higher and cleaner (above forehead)
	- **Platform Zone:** balls tossed lower/faster (below forehead)
- Athlete starts in ready stance.
**How it runs**
1. Coach tosses randomly (high or low).
2. Athlete must **say the decision first**:
	- “HANDS!” or “PLATFORM!”
3. Then execute.
**Win condition**
- 3 correct decisions in a row = “level cleared”
- Wrong decision = Job Zone 10 sec → re-enter
### Micro-CFU
Coach asks: “Why hands?”
Expected: “High and clean.”
**Next progression:** Application game under score.
---
### ✅ ADD-ON OPTIONS (Block 5) — More guided decision games (same rule, more variety)
These are still **guided** (coach controls input), not full chaos.
### Option 1: Two-Ball Reveal
- Coach holds two balls (one high toss, one low toss).
- Reveals at last second.
- Athlete must call “hands/platform” then play.
### Option 2: Color Decision
- Coach wears/holds two colored cones:
	- Red = platform
	- Blue = hands
- Coach flashes color and tosses accordingly.
- Athlete must call the decision out loud.
### Option 3: Late Call “Tight / Off”
- Coach calls “tight” (near net) or “off” (away from net) mid-flight.
- Athlete still uses the **same rule** (high/clean vs low/fast), but must move accordingly.
- Keeps footwork tied to decision.

## Block 6 — Application (Constraint Game) (12–18 min)
### GAME OF THE DAY: JAILBREAK (clarified + paced)
### Setup
- Split into Group A and Group B on opposite sides.
- Each group lines up behind the baseline.
- Coach has balls at the net or attack line for quick feeds.
### How it runs (fast cycle)
- First athlete steps to attack line.
- Coach feeds:
	- early rounds = toss
	- later rounds = faster toss / light serve
- Athlete must **pass OR set** (coach calls which, or athlete decides if you want DR).
- Ball must go over and in.
### Lives + jail
- Everyone starts with **3 lives**.
- Miss (into net/out/not over) = lose a life.
- At 0 lives → go to jail (other side).
### Breaking out (keep it safe + skill-based)
Instead of only “catch a ball,” make it skill aligned:
- Catch = allowed **only if coach calls it safe**
- OR breakout option:
	- “2 clean platform catches” (self-toss → platform pop → catch) in jail area
	- then re-enter
### Gamefy upgrades (optional)
- **Streak bonus:** team earns 1 bonus life if they hit 5 safe-to-target balls in a row
- **Decision bonus:** if athlete correctly calls “hands/platform” + executes, team gets +1 point
### Micro-CFU (between rounds, 10 sec)
Athlete: “I saw __, chose __, did __.”
**Next progression:** Debrief + retrieval.

### Option A: 2v2 Mini Volleyball (short court)

**Rule:** 3 contacts = bonus point.

**Why:** pressure + reps.
### Option D: Texas Star (ball-control version)
**Setup:** players rotate through spots after each contact.
**Rule:** point only if set hits target.
**Why:** chaotic movement + controlled contacts.
