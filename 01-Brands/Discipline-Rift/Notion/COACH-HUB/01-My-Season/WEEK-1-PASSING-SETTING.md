# WEEK 1
<callout icon="🪜" color="yellow_bg">
	**THE LADDER BELOW IS A SAMPLE, NOT A REQUIRED PATH.** Start where your players can succeed today, identify the problem you actually see, build from there. Different teams finish on different rungs. Run it inside [[PRACTICE DESIGN]].
</callout>
<callout icon="/icons/flag-pennant_green.svg" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH RULES**
	**Demonstrate everything. Don't explain.**
	**Teach one skill at a time.**
	**3 lines per coach, if possible.**
	**Coach at all times. Don't stop speaking.**
	**Give specific + actionable feedback.**
	**Add variation as soon as players begin succeeding.**
	**Check for understanding. Don't assume.**
	**Fun first. Learning always.**
</callout>
## 1. INTRO
**Welcome:** Introduce yourselves. Be fun. Be cool. "We're here to have fun. We're here to learn."
**Show Them the Sport:** Show a brief Serve - Receive/Pass - Set - Hit sequence so they see where they can go. This is a preview; teach the first actual skill separately.
**Stretch:** Stretch with the players. Keep it dynamic; coach counts 1, players 2, coach 3, players 4.

## 3. CHECK FOR UNDERSTANDING
<callout icon="🎲" color="gray_bg">
	**CFU GAME — you pick**
	[[CATCH → CONNECT → SEND]] — beginners, twenty players, two courts. Best fit for Week 1.
	[[TEAM RALLY]] — connection matters more than winning.
	[[MINI VOLLEYBALL]] — more contacts per child.
	[[SPACE & SUPPORT]] — checking decisions and covering space.
	*Whole bank: [[VOLLEYBALL CFU GAMES]].*
</callout>
Give players a chance to retrieve. If they struggle: **Freeze - Cue - Replay.**
Begin with a coach toss and a declared second-contact catch bridge if needed. Then change the entry angle. Look for a pass a teammate can play and a setter who moves under the ball. Use only learned contacts; a one-contact return remains available to a group still working on passing.
## 4. CLOSE
**What Did We Learn?** Why do we pass to a teammate? Show how you get ready for the next ball.
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**OPTIONAL CLOSING THEME** (not a required block)
	*Teamwork + Discipline.*
</callout>
**Recognition:** Who gave a teammate a playable ball or tried the next rep after a miss?
**What's Next?** We will retrieve our pass and set, then learn how a serve starts the rally.
<callout icon="🚩" color="gray_bg">
	**After practice:** record the last reliable problem solved, one CFU observation, useful scaling and the next retrieval in your practice log.
</callout>
