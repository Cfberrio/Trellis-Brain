# SAVE AND SHARE
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Trios: gentle feeder, defender, target. Small marked action area and one light ball. Begin with tosses, not hard attacks.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Defender starts ready and reads a reachable feed.
2. Move into the ball's path and direct it **up** toward the target.
3. Target catches and returns the ball.
4. Rotate after three attempts. Increase only one movement demand at a time.
## How to progress and win
One save for an upward ball a teammate can play. Three saves complete a team round.
**Golden Ticket:** three playable saves unlock a changed starting spot.
## Adjust the challenge
<table header-row="true">
<tr>
<td>Make it easier</td>
<td>Make it harder</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Slower toss, smaller area, or catch then toss so they learn the destination.</td>
<td>Add short and deep feeds, then a controlled standing send from across the net.</td>
</tr>
</table>
<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Cue: **"Up for your teammate."** Readiness before the feed and controlled redirection. No diving and no hard spikes needed here.
	**If it breaks:** ball shoots past the target → soften the feed and reduce the extra swing. Player is afraid → reduce speed and rebuild with catch and toss.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
The defender produces playable second contacts from two starting positions.
