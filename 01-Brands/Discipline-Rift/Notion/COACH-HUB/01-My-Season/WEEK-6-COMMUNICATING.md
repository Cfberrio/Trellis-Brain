*(Developmental Volleyball)*
**North Star skill:** Pass/Set to a target with control
**Culture outcome:** Discipline = commit to the decision + follow through
**Week lens:** control before power, target before speed, feet before arms
The sources strongly support three ideas that fit this week:
- passing should always be practiced **to a target**
- passing mechanics start with **feet to ball**, then platform to target
- overhead passing / setting should shape the ball at the **forehead or higher** and go square to the target

## Block 3 — Teaching Block — Passing Install
**Learning Target:** Pass with a strong platform to a partner/target
**Cue:** **Flat platform**
**Activity**
- 2 demo reps: bad vs good
- partner mirror
- toss-catch-pass for beginners if needed
**Why this belongs here**
Block 3 is where the skill gets named and clarified. For passing, the sources emphasize:
- **move feet to ball**
- **create platform**
- **contact on forearms**
- **freeze to target**
The drill-book source also frames forearm-passing cues as:
- beat the ball
- contact away from body
- platform to the target
That makes your “flat platform” cue a good one-cue teaching focus.
**Practice Method Classification:** Blocked Practice
**Micro-CFU**
- “Show me **flat platform**.”
- Observable evidence:
	- hands together
	- thumbs even
	- flat forearms
**Exit Criteria**
- player can show **hands together + thumbs even + flat forearms** on command
**Next-Block Progression**
Add repetition and target control.

## Block 5 — DR Teaching (Guided Decisions)
**Learning Target:** Choose pass vs set based on ball height
**Cue:** **Above forehead = set. Below chest = pass.**
**Activity:** **READ & REACT SHUFFLE**
**How it works**
- low toss → shuffle and **pass**
- high toss → shuffle and **set**
- vary only one thing at a time: left/right/short/deep
**Why this belongs here**
Block 5 is the first place full **See → Decide → Act** belongs. Your activity does that correctly because players must read height, choose the contact, and then send the ball to control. The source base supports the distinction:
- overhead passing/contact occurs at **face or forehead level / above shoulder height**
- forearm passing is used when players move feet to ball and create a forearm platform
So your above/below rule is a good developmental simplification for this week.
**Practice Method Classification:** Interleaving
**Micro-CFU**
Ask:
- “What did you notice that told you to set?”
	Expected:
- “It was **above my forehead**.”
Observable evidence:
- correct choice
- player points to the evidence
- ball goes to the intended target area
**Coach note**
Your **JOB ZONE** re-entry as “Instant Coach” is smart. It keeps reps flowing and reinforces cue language without idle time.
**Next-Block Progression**
Take the same decision into a competitive constraint game.

## Block 6 — Application (Constraint Game)
**Learning Target:** Under pressure, pass/set safely to stay alive
**Cue:** **Safe to target**
**Activity:** **JAILBREAK**
**Why this belongs here**
Block 6 should feel closer to volleyball than earlier blocks and test whether the learning target transfers. Your Jailbreak setup does that because players must:
- read the feed
- choose pass or set
- send a controlled ball
- stay alive under pressure
That fits the DR-UPS role of Block 6 exactly.
**Source-backed logic**
- passing quality should be tied to whether the ball reaches the setter/target with options
- setters should provide a visible target and arrive in the target area near the net
- as players improve, add movement, live reads, and variability gradually until they can perform in more game-like situations
**Practice Method Classification:** Randomized Practice
**Metrics that matter**
- team streak = number of **safe to target** reps in a row
- lose a life only for:
	1. unsafe ball that doesn’t cross
	2. wild out ball
	3. wrong pass/set choice by today’s rule
**Micro-CFU**
Between rounds:
- “See → Decide → Act in one sentence.”
	Example:
- “I saw it was high, I decided set, I set to the hoop.”
**Next-Block Progression**
Close the loop with retrieval from memory and preview the revisit.
