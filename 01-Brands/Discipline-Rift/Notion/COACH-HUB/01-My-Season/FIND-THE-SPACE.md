# FIND THE SPACE
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Four players: sender and feeder on one side, two catchers on the other. Mark two large target areas. Start with one area clearly more open.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Sender observes the court **before** receiving the feed.
2. Sender chooses a broad open area and sends by catch-and-toss, pass, or controlled standing attack, whichever matches their current skill.
3. Opponents catch and reset. Nobody tries to block.
4. Rotate roles after three sends.
## How to progress and win
One **decision** point for choosing the visibly open area. One **execution** point for reaching it. Track them separately so a good idea with a miss is still visible.
**Golden Ticket:** three sensible choices unlock a changing defender position.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Ask: **"What space did you see?"** Do not call the correct target during the attempt.
	**If it breaks:** player always chooses one side → freeze before the feed, ask them to point to the bigger space, then replay.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
The player chooses different destinations as the open space changes.
