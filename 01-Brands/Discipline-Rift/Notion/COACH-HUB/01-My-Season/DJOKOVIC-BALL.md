# VOLLEY + PROBLEM: PLATEAUED PAST BASIC CONTROL → DJOKOVIC BALL
**Setup:** Service-box points between two players.
**Show It:** Demonstrate that every shot must bounce on the hitter's own side of the net before crossing over — a volley-bounce-volley pattern, not a direct hit.
**Challenge:** Play out points under this constraint. Point is lost if the ball crosses without bouncing on the hitter's own side first, or lands out.
**Progress/Win:** First to 7 wins the game. Winners move to a harder dial — smaller box, faster pace.
