# SERVE TO UNLOCK
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Pairs with one ball. Three distance markers per serving lane. Begin at a distance where a controlled underhand contact can clear the net. Partners wait clear of the flight path.
	**Twenty players:** each coach owns ten. Every role rotates.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Demonstrate **Ready → Hold → Open Palm → Finish**. Release the supporting hand at contact. Do not pin the ball in place.
2. Three attempts toward the open opposite court.
3. Partner collects when the lane is clear, then switch server and collector.
4. Each player tracks their own successes and their own distance.
## How to progress and win
Three serves over and in across attempts unlock the next marker. Misses do not erase success. A farther marker is optional if control disappears.
**Golden Ticket:** unlocks distance **or** accuracy, never both at once. Role turns stay equal for everyone.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Cue: **"Finish toward your space."** Stable presentation, firm open contact, forward swing.
	**If it breaks:** repeated misses turning into flailing → step forward again. Never reward a forceful uncontrolled swing just because it cleared the net.
</callout>
