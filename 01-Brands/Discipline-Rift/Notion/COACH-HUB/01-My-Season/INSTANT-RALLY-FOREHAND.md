# FOREHAND + PROBLEM: ARM-ONLY SWING → INSTANT RALLY — FOREHAND
**Setup:** Player alone first, then with a partner. A line marked on the floor (or low net), ~3 feet back, racket + ball.
**Show It:** Demonstrate the 4-step ladder: (1) tap the ball up to yourself off the racket face, (2) tap it up and let it bounce off the line, (3) rally with a partner tapping over the line, (4) rally over a real net.
**Challenge:** Move through the 4 steps only after the current one is controlled — this is a real internal progression, not one drill.
**Progress/Win:** Step 1: 5 consecutive taps. Step 2: 5 consecutive over the line. Step 3: 5 consecutive with a partner. Step 4: sustain a net rally.
