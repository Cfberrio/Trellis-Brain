# 4v4 RALLY
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Four players per side of a real net.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. No catching at all. Three real contacts (pass, set, attack) every time the ball comes over.
2. Play live rallies. Score to 11.
## How to progress and win
Scored live, like a real game, to 11.
**Golden Ticket:** none above this rung, this is the top of the ladder. Winning team names the next challenge.
## Adjust the challenge

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	The pattern collapsing back into one-touch-and-over under pressure. Cue: **"Three before you send it."**
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
The team runs the full pattern on its own, without a coach reminder, even as the pressure ramps up.
