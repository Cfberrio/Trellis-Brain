# TEAM RALLY
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP AND PLAY**
	Two groups of five on opposite sides, one coach per game.
	Start with catch and release, or real contacts, matched to the players in front of you.
</callout>
1. Coach or player sends a reachable ball across the net.
2. Each side uses two or three team contacts before returning it.
3. Count one shared return every time the ball crosses legally under the rules you chose.
4. When the ball ends, restart with a different starter. Everyone stays involved.
## Shared scoring
Collect five returns across attempts before trying for a streak. Then let the group try to beat its own best. Keep the score cumulative if losing a streak kills the effort.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	A useful ball to a teammate, ready support, and recovery after sending.
	A high rally count with only catches is knowledge evidence, not technique. Record the contact version next to the score.
	If the group returns immediately on the first contact, briefly demonstrate why connecting gives another player a job.
	If three contacts create repeated dead balls, require two and build up.
</callout>
