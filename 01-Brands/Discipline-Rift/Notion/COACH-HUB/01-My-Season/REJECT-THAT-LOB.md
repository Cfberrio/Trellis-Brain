# REJECT THAT LOB
## SETUP
- Players: pairs, one at net one feeding lobs from the baseline
- Equipment: rackets, several balls
- Space: half court, net player starts a step inside the service line
## SHOW IT
Baseline player feeds a lob. Net player is allowed to reject any feed that's too tough — too deep, too high, behind them — by calling "reject" before it bounces. Only after a callable, well-placed lob do they swing.
## CHALLENGE
Net player has to make the call fast and honest, then finish the ones they accept with a real overhead, not a safe pat-back.
## PROGRESS / WIN
Once a player is rejecting appropriately and finishing accepted lobs cleanly, shrink the reject window — fewer easy calls, more decisions made on contact-quality alone.

## COACH WATCHES FOR
Taking overheads they should reject (rushed, off-balance swings), or rejecting everything out of hesitation.
