# FOREHAND + PROBLEM: NO REPEATABLE CONTACT POINT → BUNGEE JUMPS
**Setup:** Each player holds a racket, ball in the center of the strings, palm up.
**Show It:** Tilt the racket so the ball falls off the strings, let it bounce once, catch it back on the strings.
**Challenge:** Solo reps to start. Add a partner — one tilts, the other catches after the bounce, then swap.
**Progress/Win:** 5 clean catches in a row solo = move to the partner version. 5 with a partner = add movement, catching while shuffling sideways.
