# MOVE–STOP–SET SQUARES
<callout icon="🏐" color="gray_bg">
	**SETUP**
	Trios. Mark two setter starting spots around one receiving area. Tosser and catcher face the setter from clear positions.
	**Twenty players:** each coach owns ten, groups of 3 + 3 + 4. Every role rotates.
</callout>
## Demonstrate and play
1. Show the receiving area and demonstrate moving there before the ball falls.
2. Start with you naming the starting spot and giving a predictable toss.
3. Setter moves, balances, and sets to the catcher.
4. Rotate after three attempts. Later, toss first so the flight gives the movement information.
## How to progress and win
One point for arriving balanced and sending a catchable set. First team to five starts a fresh round from a new starting spot.
**Golden Ticket:** three balanced sets unlock the second arrival area.

<callout icon="💡" color="gray_bg">
	**COACH WATCHES FOR**
	Cue: **"Beat it. Balance."** Stopping is a beginner teaching aid. Do not teach that a moving player can never control a ball.
	**If it breaks:** they wait for the command → remove the pointing cue and demonstrate watching the toss.
</callout>
## Ready for the next problem when
