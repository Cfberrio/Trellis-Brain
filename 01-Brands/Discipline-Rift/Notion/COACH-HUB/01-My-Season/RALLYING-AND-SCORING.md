# RALLYING & SCORING
*(Game Literacy — Developmental Tennis)*
---
## Why We Use It
A player can own every stroke on this curriculum and still not understand tennis as a game — what "in" and "out" mean, why a rally matters, how a point is won. This is tennis's version of what Communicating is for volleyball: not a stroke, but the piece that turns isolated contacts into a game a player actually understands. Every purpose-built youth curriculum treats this as its own strand, not something players absorb by accident.
---
## SHOW IT — RALLYING & SCORING BY NUMBERS
### Position 1 — Call It
**Cue:** "Say it out loud"
- "In" or "out," called immediately, before anyone argues about it
- Call your own side; the receiver calls balls landing on their side
### Position 2 — Keep It Playable
**Cue:** "Give your partner a ball they can play"
- Send the ball where a partner can actually return it, not just anywhere as hard as possible
- Applies in cooperative rallies and in scored games alike
### Position 3 — Count It
**Cue:** "Say the score before every point"
- Know the score in the rally (consecutive count) or the game (point score)
- State it out loud before the next serve or feed
### Position 4 — Reset
**Cue:** "Win or lose, get back to ready"
- Recover to ready position after every ball, regardless of the outcome
- No sulking on a lost point, no celebrating past the next serve
---
## Core Rallying & Scoring Pattern
**1 → 2 → 3 → 4** — Call It → Keep It Playable → Count It → Reset. Runs underneath every scored game and CFU on this curriculum.

## Progression Connections
Beginner: cooperative rally counting, coach-modeled in/out calls. Intermediate: self-officiated short games, score stated before every serve. Advanced: full live-play games with no coach adjudication, disputes resolved by replay per DR's usual sportsmanship standard.
---
## Observable Evidence (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced)
- **Beginner:** Calls in/out on obvious balls when prompted; attempts to count a cooperative rally out loud.
- **Intermediate:** Calls in/out unprompted most of the time; states the score before serving without a reminder.
- **Advanced:** Self-officiates a full short game start to finish — calls, score, resets — with no coach involvement needed.
