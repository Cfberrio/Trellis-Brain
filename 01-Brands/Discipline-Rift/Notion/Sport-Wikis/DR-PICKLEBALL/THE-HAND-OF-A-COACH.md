# THE HAND OF A COACH

<empty-block/>
### **CONCEPT**
<details>
<summary>Simple yet impactful method that simplifies the journey of a coach.</summary>
	### PURPOSE
	- Functional in many areas of life, helping your personal growth.
	### LEADERSHIP
	- Aims to enhance leadership qualities in coaches.
</details>
---
<callout icon="☝🏽" color="gray_bg">
	**LOCUS OF CONTROL**
</callout>
- **Definition**: A psychological concept that explains who to blame.
- **Types**:
	1. **Internal Locus of Control**: Belief that outcomes depend on one's own actions.
	2. **External Locus of Control**: Belief that outcomes are influenced by external factors.
- **Goal**: Recognize that sometimes we are the ones to blame, and other times, not entirely.
---
<callout icon="✌🏽" color="gray_bg">
	**EMPATHETIC FEEDBACK**
</callout>
- **Goal**: Provide encouraging feedback with a strategy to motivate progress.
- **Method**: Use the "Sandwich Method" in a one-on-one approach.
- **Trust**: Build emotional trust gradually with empathy.
---
<callout icon="🤟🏽" color="gray_bg">
	**RECOGNITION**
</callout>
### RECOGNITION
- **Intentionality**: Actively recognize the contributions of others, from players to coaches.
- **Focus**: Prioritize the well-being of others over personal interests.
---
<callout icon="🤙🏽" color="gray_bg">
	**NEGOTIATION**
</callout>
- **Importance**: Effective leadership requires strong negotiation skills. 
- Being able to meet players “in the middle”
---
<callout icon="👍🏽" color="gray_bg">
	**PSYCHOLOGICAL SAFETY**
</callout>
- **Concept**: Creating a "safe environment" is essential.
- **Responsibility**: It begins with the coach, enabling players to be themselves.
- **Benefits**: Encourages players to take risks and express their opinions.
