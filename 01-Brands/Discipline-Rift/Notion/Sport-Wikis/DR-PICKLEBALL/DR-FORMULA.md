# DR FORMULA
> “How we do things”
	## Knowledge + Skill x Attitude² 
	### **1. Knowledge**
	- **Definition**: What a coach knows about the sport, including rules, strategies, and player development.
	- **Coach**: A coach uses their knowledge to teach and guide players effectively.
	### **2. Skill**
	- **Definition**: The ability to apply coaching techniques, such as running drills and making game decisions.
	- **Coach**: A coach with good skills can turn their knowledge into effective practice and game plans.
	### **3. Attitude**
	- **Definition**: The coach's mindset, including positivity, motivation, and resilience.
	- **Coach**: A positive attitude from the coach motivates players and creates a strong, supportive team environment. Attitude² means that attitude greatly boosts the impact of knowledge and skills.
