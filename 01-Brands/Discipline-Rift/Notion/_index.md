level, with content. Notion stays the source of truth; this is the readable, searchable,
linkable copy that lives in the vault.

carries its `notion_page_id` and `notion_url` in frontmatter, so any page here can be traced
back to the live page it came from.

## How to use this mirror

- **Reading**: everything is plain markdown; the sport curriculums read top to bottom in
  season order.
- **Tracing a page back**: `notion_url` in frontmatter opens the live page.
- **Editing**: edit in Notion, then re-run the mirror. Edits made here do not travel back.
- **Staleness**: `notion_last_edited` records what the page said when copied. A file whose
  `mirror_complete` is `false` was too large for one API call and carries a note saying so.

## Historical files kept on purpose

`_superseded-2026-05/` holds two hand-curated summaries from May 2026 whose Notion source
pages no longer exist. They were verified dead against a full workspace enumeration on
2026-08-12 and kept because this vault is now their only record. See the README in that folder.

## Relationship to the rest of the vault

`05-Operations/Training/By-Sport/*/Notion-Wiki/` holds older, hand-curated exports of the same
material from before the redesign. Those files carry commentary and vault cross-links; these
files do not. Where the two disagree, this mirror is the newer copy and Notion is the truth.

Working docs for the redesign itself, including the change log and the open decisions for
Luis, live in the Trellis repo at `domains/ops/notion/dr-coach-wiki/`.
