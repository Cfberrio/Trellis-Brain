# DR RSO Operational Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Background checks (Florida Level 2) and youth protection training are mandatory for all volunteers before participating in events with minors, per UCF Policy 2-005.
- **rule**: Youth protection training must be renewed every 2 years.
- **fact**: RSO banking requires a letter from the Knights of the Round Table (KoRT) office; agency accounts are not used.
- **rule**: Student volunteers are prohibited from providing transportation for children ('No transportation needed').
- **rule**: RSOs must either be added as an insured on Discipline Rift's policy or purchase their own insurance, unless department-sponsored by UCF.
