# DR Legal and Policy Pack

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Discipline Rift offers a 'Two-practice Fit Guarantee': parents may request a transfer or 100% account credit (valid for 12 months) within 48 hours after the second scheduled practice.
- **rule**: Supervision begins at check-in/transfer at the designated meeting point and ends upon release under the approved dismissal plan to authorized persons only.
- **rule**: Transactional SMS (reminders, cancellations, safety) requires affirmative opt-in; marketing SMS (progress, re-enrollment, news) requires separate consent.
- **rule**: If DR cancels a season before it begins, a full refund is issued. If cancelled after starting without makeups, a prorated refund or credit is offered.
- **rule**: Participant health and injury information is treated as sensitive; access is restricted to authorized staff, parents, emergency responders, and necessary school personnel.
