## 0) One-Line Model

### For Public
**Long:** We help parents of kids 6–12 get their child active, confident, and connected through a fun-first, on-campus after-school sports season led by trained coaches; beginner-friendly with an affordable, fixed per-season commitment.

**Short:** We help parents of kids 6–12 get their child active and confident through fun-first, on-campus after school sports seasons led by specialized coaches.

**Bio:** On-campus after-school sports for kids 6–12. Fun-first. Beginner-friendly. Coach-led.

### For School
**Standard (recommended):** We help schools increase youth sports participation for kids 6–12 with a turnkey, fun-first, on-campus after-school sports season led by trained coaches and built for safe, developmental learning funded by parent enrollment.

**Short:** We help schools boost youth sports participation with a fun-first on-campus program led by trained coaches that is parent-funded.

### For Us
**Revenue model:** Parents enroll and pay per player, per season (fixed term).

## 1) Market DNA

### Market / submarket
**Market:** Youth sports participation for kids ages 6–12.
**Submarket:** On-campus, after-school, parent-paid sports seasons where schools provide access (space + permission + distribution) and parents enroll per season (fixed term).

### Winnable wedge (where we win first)
**School type:** Elementary schools with (1) after-school traffic already in place (car riders + aftercare), (2) space availability (gym/field), (3) openness to enrichment vendors.

**Parent type:** Busy parents who want their kid active + social but won't drive to off-campus sports and don't want "club life." They value: convenience, beginner safety, predictable commitment.

**Sport spearhead:** Pick **one** default spear per school cluster (e.g., volleyball OR flag football) based on local demand + facility fit. Other sports stay as **swaps**, not headline positioning.

### Why this market now
- The biggest blocker isn't interest — it's **friction** (driving, timing, signup complexity).
- On-campus after school removes the friction by default.
- Parents are increasingly choosing options that feel **safe-to-learn** and not "elite-only."
- Schools want more participation, but only if it's **low-lift, low-risk, and operationally clean**.

### Competitor set

**Direct sports alternatives:**
- Local youth sports clubs/academies (including "elite")
- City recreation leagues / parks programs
- YMCA / community centers / church leagues
- Private coaches / small group training
- School-run clubs / intramurals

**True substitute alternatives:**
- Aftercare programs (structured supervision)
- Other enrichment vendors (STEM, tutoring, music, martial arts, dance)
- Babysitter / family pickup solutions
- Do nothing (screens, homework, unstructured after-school time)

### Internal comparison table

| Option | Logistics | Intensity | Cost / Commitment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Our offer (on-campus season)** | On-campus after school; minimal driving; clean pickup/aftercare flow | Beginner-friendly; fun-first; safe-to-learn | Fixed per-season; predictable; "everything included" |
| Club / academy | Off-campus travel; often weekends/tournaments | Higher intensity; can feel intimidating | Higher cost; longer cycles; add-ons + travel |
| Rec league / parks | Off-campus fields; weekend games; transport required | Mixed coaching; less consistent progression | Cheaper but still time-heavy; gear varies |
| Aftercare / enrichment | Convenient + supervised | Not sports-specific skill progression | Similar spend, different outcome |
| Do nothing | Easiest now | No guided progress | $0 now; "cost" shows up later (inactivity, confidence) |

### "We win here because…"
We win first at elementary schools that want low-lift enrichment because we remove the biggest blocker (logistics) with an on-campus, after-school, beginner-friendly sports season — making it easier than clubs/parks and more outcome-driven than aftercare/enrichment.

## 2) Avatar

### Primary Avatar (Parent) — payer
Parent of a child ages 6–12 at a participating school who wants their child active + confident + socially connected, and prefers a low-friction, on-campus, beginner-friendly season (fixed commitment) over off-campus leagues/club logistics.

### Parent Segmentation (behavior-based)

#### Segment 1 — "New-to-sports / anxious kid" (Fear / self-consciousness)
**Buying:** psychological safety + belonging.
**Signals:** asks "Is this for beginners?" / "What if my kid is shy/nervous?" / clicks "what to expect" / asks about embarrassment.
**Obstacles:** kid fear → trust in coaches → "will they quit?"
**Angle:** "Beginner-friendly / safe-to-learn / fun-first" / "Small wins" / "No pressure" / "Most kids start with zero experience."

#### Segment 2 — "Logistics-overwhelmed parent" (Time / friction)
**Buying:** convenience + reduced decision fatigue.
**Signals:** clicks schedule/pickup; asks "What time?" / "Where do I pick up?" / "Aftercare?"
**Obstacles:** pickup timing → calendar fit → signup friction.
**Angle:** "On-campus right after school" / "Clean dismissal" / "Fixed season = predictable routine."

#### Segment 3 — "Price-sensitive but willing if simple" (Money + risk)
**Buying:** certainty + predictable commitment (not 'cheap').
**Signals:** asks price early; clicks price/registration repeatedly; "What's included?" / "Any extra fees?"
**Obstacles:** "Is it worth it?" → hidden costs → kid quits early.
**Angle:** "Fixed per-season commitment" / "Everything included" / "Low-barrier vs club escalation."

### Segment routing rule
- Anxious/beginner questions → Segment 1
- Schedule/pickup questions → Segment 2
- Price/included/value questions → Segment 3

### Secondary Avatar (School) — gatekeeper

**School risk language (what they fear):**
1. Dismissal chaos
2. Safety incidents
3. Staff workload creep
4. Vendor unreliability
5. Equity perception (exclusive/elite vibe)

**Anti-headache promise:**
> "We run an on-campus after-school sports season that increases participation while keeping operations clean: structured supervision, defined dismissal flow, clear roles, and parent communication handled by us — not your staff."

**Trust kit (minimum):**
1. Dismissal flow one-pager
2. Safety + supervision standards
3. Coach credibility
4. Communication boundaries
5. Program structure overview
6. Equity statement

## 3) Offer DNA

### Offer name
**Discipline Rift On-Campus Sports Season** (sport swap per school: volleyball / flag / tennis / pickleball)

### Promise (measurable + auditable)
In one 6-week season, your child will:
1. **Attend X coached practices**
2. **Participate in guided gameplay** (scrimmages / game-based reps)
3. **Be assessed using sport-specific Tier System** + receive starting Tier placement + end-of-season Tier achievement
4. **Parents receive 1 weekly update** sent on fixed day (e.g., every Friday) confirming attendance + Tier skills trained + what's next

### Mechanism
A fun-first, beginner-friendly on-campus after-school season led by trained coaches using a **Tiered progression** (skills ladder) that takes kids from "new" → "competent" through consistent structure, game-based reps, and safe-to-learn coaching.

### The 3 deliverables

#### Deliverable 1 — On-campus convenience
- After school, on campus, clean dismissal/pickup handoff
- Predictable schedule + clear start/end (season container)

#### Deliverable 2 — Beginner-friendly, safe-to-learn structure
- No experience needed
- Built-in confidence ramp (teach → rep → game)
- Clear rules for psychological safety (no pressure / no embarrassment)

#### Deliverable 3 — Trained coaches + clear season structure
- Consistent coaching standards
- Same session format each week
- Tier System progression (skills ladder) + end-of-season achievement

### Tier System (measurement system)
- Each sport has Tier 1–Tier N with defined skill outcomes.
- Coaches teach toward these outcomes during the season.
- Kids receive Tier placement at start + Tier achievement at end (certificate/badge).
- Make each Tier's outcomes **non-overlapping** and written as **observable behaviors** (avoid repeats).
- Keep **4–6 outcomes per Tier** max so it's teachable and scorable.

### Guarantee — Fit Guarantee
If after the first 2 practices you don't feel it's a fit, email us within 48 hours after practice #2 and we'll issue a **full credit** toward another sport season (or the next season).

## 5) Lead DNA

### Engaged lead
At least ONE of:
- Clicks the enrollment link (or "check your school / register")
- Replies to any nurture email
- Calls (inbound questions count)

### Paid lead
Completed registration + payment.

### Weekly scoreboard
**Primary metrics:**
1. Leads created (Name+Email)
2. Engaged leads (# and %) — Engagement Rate = engaged / leads
3. Enrollments (paid) — Lead→Enroll, Engaged→Enroll Conversion

**Per-school:**
- Enrollments per school per season
- Lead→Enroll conversion by school

### Response/Follow-up metrics
1. **Time-to-first-response** (inbound calls + emails)
2. **Follow-up count per lead** before they die

### Lead sources
**Primary:** Flyer → QR → website → pop-up Parent Guide → Name + Email

**Daily:** Same-day response. Enter call-in leads into CRM immediately.
**Weekly:** Flyer distribution cadence per school. 1 proof post + 1 email shipped minimum. Review scoreboard.

## 6) Conversion DNA (Sales SOP)

### Entry point
Flyer → QR → site → pop-up → Name + Email + (recommended) phone + child age/grade + school + sport interest.

### Email welcome series
- **Email 0 (instant):** Guide + 1 clear CTA
- **Email 1:** Logistics friction (your biggest enemy)
- **Email 2:** Beginner-friendly + safe-to-learn
- **Email 3:** Trust (coach quality + safety standards)
- **Email 4:** Cost & commitment clarity
- **Email 5:** Proof drop
- **Email 6:** Decision email

### Cadence (front-load 7 days while intent is hot)
- Day 0: immediate email + call/text fast
- Days 1–2: 2 touches/day
- Days 3–7: 1 touch/day
- After Day 7: weekly through Day 30 or convert

### Qualification rule
**YES:**
- Child 6–12
- Attends participating school
- Parent can pay per season (or accepts payment option)
- Wants fun-first / on-campus convenience

**NO:**
- Out of age/school
- Wants elite-only competition
- Cannot attend after-school window at all

### Sales steps

#### Mode A — Checkout-close (no call)
- Every email ends with one CTA + "what happens next"
- Click but don't buy → trigger "main concern" email

#### Mode B — Closer assist (5–10 min)
Closing's core: give them power to decide (1) understand what they want, (2) put next to options, (3) make sure they reach a genuine decision.

**Call skeleton:**
1. Confirm basics (age/school/sport)
2. Ask: what do they want for their kid? (fun, friends, confidence, activity)
3. Ask: what's stopping them from enrolling today?
4. If they stall: ask **main concern**
5. Handle the concern → ask for decision → enroll

### Objection handling

| Objection | Bucket | Response |
|---|---|---|
| "Will my kid like it or quit?" | Fear | "What's your main concern about them trying it?" → risk-reversal |
| "Is it safe / is this for beginners?" | Fear | Identify what "unsafe" means → map to safe-to-learn structure |
| "We're busy / we'll miss practices." | Time | Confirm what would need to be true to say yes → on-campus convenience |
| "It's too expensive." | Money | "If this were free, would you do it?" — if yes, it's not money |
| "I need to check with my spouse." | Permission | Decision makers on the call before investing time; reschedule if not |

### Inbound Call Close SOP (Assisted Checkout)

**Goal:** End call with enrollment completed (registration + payment).

#### Data capture
- Parent name
- Parent email (so registration link can be sent live)

#### Call Script

**1. Confirm + name (10 sec)**
Parent: "Is this the program at [School]?"
You: "Yes — this is **Discipline Rift at [School Name]**. Happy to help. What's your name?"

**2. Capture email early**
You: "Perfect, [Name]. What's the best email to send you the **registration link** right now?"
If they ask why: "So you have it in writing, and I can walk you through it on the phone."

**3. 30-sec value explanation**
You: "Here's the simple version: it's **right after school on campus**, **beginner-friendly**, and **fun-first**. We handle the setup — coaches, equipment, and drills. After dismissal, we gather the kids, run practice at the school facility, and at the end we direct **car riders to pickup** and **aftercare kids to aftercare**."

If reassurance needed: "We run a structured practice so kids can learn without pressure, and our coaches are trained and consistent."

**4. Season structure + price**
You: "We run on a **6-week season**. Everything is included. Tuition is **$X per season**."

**5. Assisted Checkout close**
You: "The easiest way is to knock it out right now while we're on the phone. I'm going to email you the registration link — once you open it, I'll walk you through step-by-step. Sound good?"

If hesitate: "Totally fair — what's your **main concern**?"

#### Live walkthrough

1. **Send link + confirm receipt:** "Do you see the email from Discipline Rift?" (If no: check spam/promotions; resend.)
2. **Click link + find school:** "Type **[School Name]** and select it."
3. **Select season/sport:** "Choose the **[Sport] season** at [School]."
4. **Fill fields:** "Fill out the fields. I'll stay on the line. Tell me when you're at the payment screen." (You shut up here.)
5. **Payment:** "Complete payment. Tell me when you see the confirmation screen."
6. **Confirm + expectations:** "Awesome — you're all set. We're excited to have [Child Name] with us. They'll learn a lot, and we keep it fun and beginner-friendly. You'll receive a confirmation email with the details." → coach credibility line.

#### If they delay ("I'll do it later")
- **Tool A:** "No problem — what's your main concern about registering today?"
- **Tool B (money objection):** "If this were free, would you do it?"

**Do not discount on the call** — Closing playbook warns this turns closing into haggling and destroys value.

### FAQ Modules

**"Does my kid need experience?"** "No — it's beginner-friendly. We teach from scratch and build confidence through reps and games."

**"What about pickup / aftercare?"** "At the end, **car riders go to pickup** and **aftercare students go to aftercare**. We coordinate that flow."

**"What's included?"** "Coaching, equipment, drills, and the full 6-week season structure are included."

**"What if we miss a practice?"** "Totally ok — most families miss occasionally. They can jump right back in at the next session."

**"Is it safe / are coaches legit?"** "Yes — we run structured sessions designed for beginners, and coaches are trained and consistent."

## 7) Fulfillment DNA

### Activation point candidates
- **AP1 (attendance):** family whose child attends first session + at least 1 more in first 7 days finishes season at higher rate.
- **AP2 (confidence):** family whose child reports "I want to come back" by session #2 enrolls next season more.
- **AP3 (parent engagement):** parent who completes "setup" homework (calendar + pickup plan) shows up more consistently.

### Onboarding Day 0–7

**Day 0:** Welcome + Where-to-start (email/text) — exact dates + times + location + pickup/dismissal flow + what to bring (minimal) + beginner welcome expectation + Homework: "Reply 'READY' and I'll send your child's first-day intro."

**Day 1–2 (48-hour forced win window):** Progress/clarity update even if nothing changed ("You're all set. Here's what week 1 looks like.") + activation-driving homework (add to calendar, confirm pickup, child picks 1 goal).

**Day 3–7 (drive to first attendance):** Reminder cadence focused on actions that predict success. Personal nudge (text/call) if no confirmation.

### In-season delivery

**Session structure (non-negotiable):**
- 5 min: arrival + name/connection ritual (belonging)
- 10 min: warmup game (fun-first)
- 20 min: skill ladder (safe-to-learn progression)
- 20 min: guided play/scrimmage (use the skill)
- 5 min: "win moment" + preview next session

**Attendance SOP every session:**
- Mark attendance live.
- Trigger: if attendance drops (or miss session #1), intervene immediately.

**2x/week parent touchpoints:**
- A: praise participation
- B: progress update

### Save plays
- If miss first session: personal outreach within 24h; reframe ("no problem — here's how to jump back in next session").
- If attendance drops: reach out at the drop, not after 2–3 missed. Solve friction (pickup confusion, kid anxiety, schedule conflict). Re-sell value in context of why they bought.

### Roles
- **Program Operator (Admin):** enrollment confirmations, onboarding messages, parent comms cadence, attendance reporting
- **Head Coach:** session quality, safe-to-learn delivery, "win moments," kid confidence
- **Assistant Coach / Floater:** equipment flow, beginner support, keeps experience stable as headcount rises

### Quality control — weekly dashboard
- Show rate session #1
- % attendance by week
- Intervention outreaches sent
- Re-enroll intent (yes/no) final week

## TO ANALIZE (Risks log)

### Risk #2: Measurable promise isn't tight enough
**Fix:** Define "win conditions" you can control:
- Sessions guaranteed (if you cancel, you make it up)
- "Beginner-to-game" progression (clear milestones)
- "Confidence moments" tracked (simple rubric)

### Risk #3: Guarantee needs to be conditional to actions (not vibes)
**Fix:** Make it conditional on attendance / participation basics (whatever your best customers do).

### Risk #4: Too much "new offer surface area" too early
Starter pass + season + playbook + progress pack + home club + downsells is a lot.
**Fix:** Keep it to 3 steps first:
1. Starter (first practice / first week)
2. Season
3. One upsell (order bump) **or** one continuity bridge — not both

### Highest-ROI implementations

#### A) Force "wins" inside 48 hours of purchase
- Instant "Welcome + what to expect" page
- Same-day coach intro (short video)
- Gear checklist + "first practice success" script
- Day-1 "your kid's first win" text template

#### B) "Card on file" / 2-step Starter → Season upgrade
- Starter pass checkout collects card
- Auto-offer: "Apply starter fee to season if you enroll by midnight after practice #1"

#### C) Progress updates into Perceived Likelihood machine
- Weekly micro-score ("comfort / participation / skill") + one highlight

#### D) Payment plan: reward pay-in-full
- Payment plans can lose money if full-pay buyers switch and cancel.
