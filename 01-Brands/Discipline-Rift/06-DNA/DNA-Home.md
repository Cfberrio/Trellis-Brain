## Purpose
The strategic skeleton of DR: where it plays, what pain it solves, what it sells, how it makes the offer known, how it converts, how it captures money, how it delivers, and what it measures.

## How To Read This Folder

1. **Market** — where DR plays (submarket, winnable wedge, competitor set, "we win here because...")
2. **Problem** — what pain we solve
3. **Offer** — what we sell + why it's compelling
4. **Message** — the core message
5. **Funnel** — how leads flow
6. **Lead** — how we make ourselves known + what to count
7. **Conversion** — how we close (nurture + close + objection handling)
8. **Money Model** — customer journey ladder (entry / core / upsells / continuity / downsells)
9. **Delivery** — how results are produced
10. **Metrics** — what we measure

> The short notes here are strategic skeleton. Operational scripts and SOPs live under [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/01-Systems/Sales/Sales-Home|Sales Home]] and [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/05-Operations/SOPs/|Operations / SOPs]].
