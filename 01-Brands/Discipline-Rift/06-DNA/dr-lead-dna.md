# DR Lead DNA

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: A lead is a unique parent record in the CRM with Name + Email captured via QR, website form, or manual entry.
- **definition**: An engaged lead is a lead that clicks the enrollment link, replies to a nurture email, or calls the business.
- **rule**: Primary weekly metrics to track are leads created, engaged leads (count and %), and enrollments (count and %).
- **process**: Daily operations require same-day response to inbound inquiries and immediate entry of call-in leads into the CRM.
