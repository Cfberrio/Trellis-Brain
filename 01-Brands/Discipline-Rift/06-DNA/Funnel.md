## Purpose
End-to-end parent + student funnel for DR's on-campus season offering.

> [!info] DRF (franchise/licensee) funnel is **separate** — see [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/04-Projects/DRF/Funnel-DRF|DRF Funnel]].

## High-Level Path
```
Flyer / QR / school distribution
    ↓
Lead capture (name + email + optional phone, child age/grade, school, sport interest)
    ↓
Nurture (7-email sprint over 30 days)
    ↓
Enrollment (self-checkout or closer assist)
    ↓
Onboarding (week 1 first-session)
    ↓
Attendance (weekly)
    ↓
Retention (visible progress + parent comms)
    ↓
Next season
```

## Strategic Constraints (per [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/00-Brand-Core/Brand-Home|Brand Home]])
The funnel wins when four things hold simultaneously:
1. School experiences low operational burden
2. Parent feels offer is simple, safe, worth the fixed seasonal commitment
3. Child feels program is beginner-safe and fun enough to return
4. Delivery produces enough progress/belonging/consistency that re-enrollment is natural

Attendance is not just a delivery KPI — it's a leading indicator of retention and future season conversion.
