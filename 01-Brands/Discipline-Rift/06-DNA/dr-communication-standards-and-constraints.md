# DR Communication Standards and Constraints

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Partner-school communities must never be described by race, national origin, culture, religion, or wealth in interviews, training, or on any recording.
- **rule**: Never name a player, or any child, in an interview or external recording; use generic terms like 'one of our players' instead.
- **rule**: When answering safety questions, state the formal protocol rather than using anecdotes about past incidents.
- **rule**: Describe young people and their behavior in terms that would be acceptable for a parent to hear; avoid contemptuous or judgmental language.
- **rule**: Coaches and staff are prohibited from bringing non-enrolled children (including family members) to school sites during sessions.
- **rule**: Assume every recorded call may be quoted; make no throwaway remarks about child welfare, parents, or safety.
