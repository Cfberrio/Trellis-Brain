## Purpose
How DR captures money. The customer journey ladder.

## 1. Entry / Acquisition Offer
**Name:** *First-Practice Pass* (or *2-Practice Starter*)

**What it is:** A low-commitment start that gets the kid playing immediately on-campus.

**Trigger:** Parent interested but unsure ("Will my kid like it?")

**Upgrade rule:** If they attend the first practice (or first 2) and the child wants to return → present full season enrollment.

**Primary value:** Removes risk + reduces decision friction.

## 2. Core Offer (main profit center)
**Name:** *Discipline Rift Developmental Season (No-League)*

**What it is:** On-campus after-school developmental program (fun-first, safe-to-learn, coached, gear included).

**Price model:** **$21.40 per practice × total practices in the season** (season length set by school schedule). Canonical posted price: **$129** per season — see [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/00-Brand-Core/Brand-Home|Brand Home]].

**Trigger:** Parent wants full structure + consistency.

**Upgrade rule:** Immediately after season purchase → present at-home reinforcement upsell.

**Primary value:** The main transformation — consistent coached reps + skill growth.

## 3. Upsells (increase spend, no more weeks)

Pick 1–2 max so it stays clean.

### Upsell A — Order bump at checkout
**Name:** *DR Home Playbook* (digital)

**What it is:** Simple "sports-from-home" drill system parents can do with kids (10–15 min) to reinforce skills.

**Trigger:** Parent enrolling, wants kid to "get better faster" without extra driving.

**Upgrade rule:** Offer at checkout + again after practice #2 if the kid is excited.

**Primary value:** More touches per week → faster confidence + skill-building at home.

**Components:**
- Weekly micro-drills by sport (volleyball / tennis / flag football)
- Printable "challenge cards" (gamified)
- Parent script: how to coach lightly + keep it fun-first
- Progress tracker (stickers/badges)
- "No-equipment substitutions" (use home items)

### Upsell B — Mid-season add
**Name:** *Season Progress Pack* (digital)

**What it is:** End-of-season progress summary + skill checklist + "next steps" plan.

**Trigger:** Parent wants proof / clarity of improvement.

**Upgrade rule:** Offer mid-season when parents ask "how are they doing?"

**Primary value:** Increases perceived results + makes re-enrollment easier.

## 4. Continuity (recurring revenue, between seasons)
**Name:** *DR Home Club* (monthly membership)

**What it is:** Ongoing at-home training + community that keeps kids playing year-round.

**Trigger:** Season ends OR parent wants the child to keep momentum.

**Upgrade rule:** Offer during final 2 weeks + immediately after season ends ("keep playing at home until next season").

**Primary value:** Retention bridge + recurring revenue without needing more on-campus weeks.

**Components (lightweight):**
- Weekly drill drop (per sport)
- Monthly "coach-led" mini challenge (family-friendly)
- Parent Q&A / office hours (group, short)
- Priority enrollment notifications (optional perk)

## 5. Downsells (salvage non-buyers / reduce churn)

### Downsell A — Payment plan
Same season, split payments.
**Trigger:** "Too much right now."
**Rule:** If they don't buy full pay → offer split-pay.

### Downsell B — Digital-only
**Name:** *DR Home Playbook Lite*

**Trigger:** Schedule/logistics stop them from joining on-campus.
**Rule:** If they can't attend → sell the at-home version so DR still helps the kid play.

## "We Get Paid" Clarity
Revenue does not depend on adding more weeks. Revenue expands through:
1. **Season enrollment** (core)
2. **Home Playbook** (upsell)
3. **Home Club membership** (continuity)
4. **Payment plans / digital-lite** (downsells)
