## Purpose
How DR makes itself known + what to count. Lead definitions, scoreboard, response metrics, sources, cadence.

## Lead Definitions

### Lead
A unique parent record in your CRM with **Name + Email** captured (via QR → pop-up, website form, or manual entry from a call).

> No "touched once" requirement — the funnel creates leads before contact happens.

### Engaged Lead (binary, trackable)
A lead that does **at least one** of:
- **Clicks** the enrollment link (or "check your school / register" link)
- **Replies** to any nurture email
- **Calls** (inbound questions count as engagement)

### Paid Lead
Completed registration + payment.

## Weekly Scoreboard

### Primary metrics (can't be gamed)
1. **Leads created** (Name + Email)
2. **Engaged leads** (# and %) — Engagement Rate = engaged / leads
3. **Enrollments (paid)** (# and %)
   - Lead → Enroll Conversion = enrollments / leads
   - Engaged → Enroll Conversion = enrollments / engaged

### Per-school performance
1. **Enrollments per school per season**
2. **Lead → Enroll conversion by school** (which schools to scale)

## Response / Follow-Up Metrics
These are the controllables that drive conversion. They replace "reachouts" which can be spammed and meaningless.

1. **Time-to-first-response** — minutes/hours from parent inquiry → first reply
2. **Follow-up count per lead before they die** — # of nurture touches delivered before either: they enroll, or the season closes and they roll to the next-season list

> If you only have email, track follow-up volume via sends + opens + clicks.

## Lead Sources

### Primary source (now)
- Flyer → QR → website → pop-up Parent Guide → **Name + Email**

### Secondary sources
- Inbound calls (capture name+email during call)
- PTA / school email blasts (lands in same pop-up)
- School events / try-it days

## Operating Cadence

### Daily (non-negotiable)
- **Same-day response** to any inbound parent email/call
- Enter any call-in leads into CRM (name+email) immediately

### Weekly (lead engine)
- **Flyer distribution cadence** (per school, per week) — current "inventory"
- **One proof post** shipped (minimum) + **one email** shipped (minimum)
- Review scoreboard: leads, engaged, enrollments, conversion by school

### Per season (review)
- Top 3 schools by conversion and enrollments
- Bottom 3 schools — fix offer clarity / distribution or drop

## Creative/Content System (right-sized to current distribution)

You don't need a full "marketing museum". You need a minimum shippable loop that supports flyers and increases conversion.

### Content capture (every practice)
- 3 clips: arrival → drill → smiles/game
- 1 "win moment" note (one sentence from coach)
- Attendance count (social proof)

### Ship weekly (realistic minimum)
- **1 short reel** (15–30 sec)
- **1 email** (proof + CTA)
- Optional: refresh flyer headline/QR CTA monthly (not weekly)

## School-Side Leads (if doing partnership outreach)

Only track if actively doing school outreach.

- **School lead** = decision-maker record (name + email) at a school
- **Engaged school lead** = replied OR scheduled OR approved next step (pilot date / flyer approval / rollout window)
- **School conversion metric** = schools approved → schools live
