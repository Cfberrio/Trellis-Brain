## Purpose
How DR speaks. Operative across parent communication, school outreach, coach-to-kid language, and ad copy. Distilled from Luis's authentic voice in the founder Q&A weekly series.

## Core Voice Posture
Direct. Simple. Neutral. Warm. Friendly. Not authoritative-superior.

> "Habla con ellos de manera directa, simple y neutral, sin usar palabras complicadas ni un tono de superioridad. La idea es que lo sientan cercano, no como alguien lejano o autoritario en exceso." — Luis

## Three Voice Modes

### Coach-to-Kid Voice
Warm, encouraging, energetic, not lecturing. Earn trust before correction.
- Coach is on the kid's side, not their enemy
- Build credibility through cariño, claridad, accesibilidad
- Connection precedes instruction

**In-practice phrases (used hundreds of times per session):**
- "Go" (repeated ~400 times per practice per Luis)
- "That was awesome"
- "Keep going"
- "Just one more"
- "You're one step away"

**Step-teaching format:** "Paso 1 / Paso 2 / Paso 3 / Paso 4" with complexity escalating as the child can absorb more.

**Refocus cue (internal):** "esto no es TikTok" — but never said in superior tone.

**Coach-to-kid teaching sequence (added 2026-09-17):** the "Paso 1 / Paso 2" format above is the voice; the method underneath is **Teach → Rep → Challenge → Apply → Connect** — demonstrate with one or two cues, controlled reps, then add one layer at a time. Never ask "who knows how to serve?" and use the volunteers as the lesson. See [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/05-Operations/Training/Method/Method-Home|DR Method Home]].

### Parent-Facing Voice
Operator-led. Clear. Commercially aware. Confident without flair.
- Mobile-readable, short sentences
- One message per asset
- Never invent prices, dates, locations, or coach names
- "Let me check and get right back to you" is always allowed if the answer isn't certain
- Always move toward a next step

**Progress language (added 2026-09-17, from [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/02-Communication/DR-Parent-Communication-Philosophy|Parent Communication Philosophy]] §12):** describe development as *what we worked on → what improved → what comes next*. "Practice went great today!" is not DR voice; "Today we progressed from passing into setting — players are starting to target their passes, so next we connect the pass into the set" is. Explain layers in everyday words; no curriculum jargon to parents.

### School-Facing Voice
Familiar, like-an-aunt-at-the-front-office. Approachable + warm + confident.
- Not corporate
- Not salesy
- Posture: "We're already working through the school facility process"
- For private schools: relationship-first, lower-school-developmental framing

## What DR Avoids
- Generic agency language
- Empty hype
- AI buzzwords
- All-caps subject lines or shouting punctuation
- "Will revolutionize / transform your business / unlock your potential"
- "Cutting-edge solutions / synergy / end-to-end excellence"
- Soft generic CTAs
- Long over-explanations

## Words DR Prefers
Systems, workflows, leverage, execution, conversion, reporting, structure, bottleneck, signal, next step, beginner-friendly, fun-first, safe-to-learn, on-campus, predictable commitment.

## Anti-Positioning In Voice (vs club academies)
DR explicitly does **not** sell:
- "College scholarship pipeline" promises
- "Elite for-elite-only" framing
- Tournament-and-travel intensity language

DR sells the **tangible**: collaboration, discipline, real skills, real growth NOW. (Source: Luis Q&A 53.)

> "Las academias venden oportunidades para que sus hijos vayan a la universidad: conexiones, rutas directas. Pero, realmente, nosotros pensamos que, por más que hay una probabilidad de que nuestros jugadores terminen siendo becados en la universidad, preferimos enfocarnos en lo real y lo tangible que son los resultados que podemos conseguir durante el tiempo que estén con nosotros." — Luis

## Cultural Refrains (use in content + parent comms)
- "Siempre hay una oportunidad de comenzar de nuevo"
- "Siempre podemos dar un poco más de lo que pensamos"
- "Perder también es ganar"
- "El método es el multiplicador" (paraphrased)
- "Safe to learn, fun to play, easy to say yes to"
- "The gap isn't interest — it's access"
- "Convenient enough to start. Fun enough to love. Structured enough to learn. Deep enough to continue. Flexible enough to pursue further if they want." — core DR parent message (Parent Communication Philosophy, 2026-09)
- "Teach well. Keep it simple. Make it engaging. Progress when they are ready. Build bridges between skills. Adapt to the players in front of you. Communicate the progress." — core coach philosophy ([[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/05-Operations/Training/Method/DR-Coach-Philosophy-Methodology|Coach Philosophy & Methodology]] §25)
- "We do not test players before we teach them." ([[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/05-Operations/Training/Method/DR-Training-Philosophy|Training Philosophy]])

## Operating Rule
If the voice would not feel right in a one-on-one conversation between Luis and a parent he respects, it doesn't belong on a DR asset.
