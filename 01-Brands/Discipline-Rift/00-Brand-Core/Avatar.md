## Primary Avatar — Parent Payer
Parent of a child ages 6–12 at a participating school who wants their child active, confident, and socially connected, and prefers a low-friction, on-campus, beginner-friendly season over off-campus sports logistics.

### Parent Segments (3) — with detection signals + message angles + closes

#### Segment 1 — New-to-sports / anxious kid (Fear / self-consciousness)
**What they're really buying:** psychological safety + belonging.

**Detect signals (in name+email funnel):**
- Reply asking: "Is this for beginners?" / "What if my kid is shy/nervous?"
- Click **"what to expect"** / "beginner" content more than price/schedule
- On calls, ask about embarrassment, skill level, coach vibe

**Primary obstacles (ranked):**
1. Kid fear / embarrassment
2. Trust in coaches / environment
3. "Will they quit?"

**Message angle:**
- "Beginner-friendly / safe-to-learn / fun-first"
- "Small wins" + "no pressure" + "they won't be singled out"
- "Most kids start with zero experience"

**Standard close:** "The only way to know is to try it in a safe environment — on campus, with coaches who teach from scratch."

#### Segment 2 — Logistics-overwhelmed parent (Time / friction)
**What they're really buying:** convenience + reduced decision fatigue.

**Detect signals:**
- Click schedule/pickup logistics first
- Call with: "What time does it end?" "Where do I pick up?" "Aftercare?"
- Don't ask "is it elite?" — they ask "how easy is this?"

**Primary obstacles:**
1. Pickup timing + dismissal flow
2. Calendar fit / missed sessions
3. Signup friction / unclear steps

**Message angle:**
- "On-campus right after school"
- "Clean dismissal: car riders to pickup, aftercare to aftercare"
- "Fixed season = predictable routine"

**Standard close:** "This is designed to be the easiest 'yes' you'll make for after school — no driving, no weekends, same place your child already is."

#### Segment 3 — Price-sensitive but willing if simple (Money + risk)
**What they're really buying:** certainty + predictable commitment (not 'cheap').

**Detect signals:**
- Ask price early, or click price/registration link repeatedly
- Reply: "What's included?" "Any extra fees/gear?" "Can we miss practices?"
- Compare to clubs/aftercare cost

**Primary obstacles:**
1. "Is it worth it?"
2. Fear of hidden costs (gear, add-ons)
3. Fear kid quits early

**Message angle:**
- "Fixed per-season commitment"
- "Everything included" (if true)
- "Low-barrier entry vs club escalation"

**Standard close:** "You're not signing up for 'club life' — it's a fixed season with a clear start/end, and you know exactly what you're getting."

### Segment Routing Rule
If you can't collect more fields, route by **behavior**:
- **Anxious/beginner questions → Segment 1**
- **Schedule/pickup questions → Segment 2**
- **Price/included/value questions → Segment 3**

Tag in [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/01-Systems/Sales/GHL-CRM-Pipeline-Spec|GHL]] based on whichever link they click most OR which question they ask first.

## Secondary Avatar — School Gatekeeper
Principal, AP, enrichment lead, or administrator who wants more student participation and better after-school options without creating supervision, dismissal, communication, or staff workload problems.

### School Risk Language (what they fear — what your pitch addresses first)
1. **Dismissal chaos** (who goes where, supervision, parent confusion)
2. **Safety incidents** (injury, inadequate supervision, liability concerns)
3. **Staff workload creep** (front office flooded, teachers pulled in)
4. **Vendor unreliability** (no-shows, inconsistent coaching, poor comms)
5. **Equity perception** (exclusive/elite vibe, parent complaints)

### What Schools Want To Hear
- Structured supervision
- Defined dismissal flow
- Clear parent communication handled by DR
- Coach reliability
- Beginner-friendly + inclusive positioning
- Low lift for school staff

### Anti-Headache Promise (verbatim school pitch)
> "We run an on-campus after-school sports season that increases participation while keeping operations clean: structured supervision, defined dismissal flow, clear roles, and parent communication handled by us — not your staff."

### Proof Package (minimum trust kit)
1. **Dismissal flow one-pager** (who you pick up, where, how released, where dropped)
2. **Safety + supervision standards** (coach ratios, supervision rules, emergency procedure)
3. **Coach credibility** (training standard + consistency plan)
4. **Communication boundaries** ("Parents contact us, not the front office" + response SLA)
5. **Program structure overview** (season dates, days/times, facility needs)
6. **Equity statement** (beginner-friendly, inclusive participation)

### School Objections + Standard Responses (risk-first)

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Will this create headaches for staff?" | "We provide a defined dismissal plan + handle parent communication. Staff involvement is limited to space approval + a single point of contact." |
| "How do we know this is safe and well-managed?" | "We run structured sessions with trained coaches and documented supervision procedures." |
| "Will families see it as inclusive?" | "It's beginner-friendly by design and positioned as fun-first — participation over elite selection." |
| "How will comms work?" | "Parents contact us directly; we send standardized updates; we don't route questions through your office." |

## Deliverables (by segment)

**Parent deliverables (by segment):**
- Segment 1: beginner safety + confidence structure + reassurance language
- Segment 2: logistics clarity + clean pickup flow + easy registration
- Segment 3: fixed commitment + what's included + risk reduction

**School deliverables (risk control):**
- Clean dismissal plan (documented)
- Safety + supervision standards (documented)
- Coach consistency plan
- Communication boundaries + SLA
- Reliability plan (coverage, contingencies)
