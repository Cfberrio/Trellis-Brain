# Value Proposition — Evidencia

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: Volleyball inherently teaches teamwork because the rules require three players to touch the ball before it crosses the net, preventing 'hero ball'.
- **fact**: Volleyball mandates verbal communication and fast emotional recovery from mistakes, as the team owns points collectively rather than individuals.

## Evidence Log

- 2026-10-15T21:48:18.054Z — `clickup_doc_page:8cqnrff-16057/8cqnrff-18597`
  - `e1` (source_body): You can't win alone — by rule. The ball has to touch three players before it crosses the net. Every point is a built thing. Every player's role is required.
  - `e2` (source_body): Apologize fast, recover faster. A bad pass is followed instantly by your teammate's set. You don't get to sulk. You reset.
  - `e3` (source_body): No hero ball. Volleyball doesn't reward the player who tries to do it all — it punishes them. So players learn to trust someone else's hands.
  - `e4` (source_body): Communication is non-negotiable. You have to call the ball. Out loud. Every time. Quiet players get exposed fast — and grow fast — in volleyball.
  - `e5` (source_body): Mistakes belong to the team, not the individual. A missed point isn't "your fault." The team owns it. That's the closest a sport gets to teaching emotional fairness.
