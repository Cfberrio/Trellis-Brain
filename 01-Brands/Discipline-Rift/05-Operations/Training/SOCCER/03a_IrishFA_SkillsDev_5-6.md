# Irish FA Skills Development Programme (5-6 Years)

Skills Development Programme Coaching Manual (5-6 Years)

Irish FA Skill Development Programme 5 - 6 Years (Week 1 - Week 6)

The Coaching Standard

Start and end the practice with fun. Ensure you have a ball to each player when possible or relevant. Simple organisation and lay out of the session. Simple communication/rules of the practice. No "bus queue" of players waiting to play/shoot etc. Develop individuals, not teams. Inspire players with the session, your personality, energy and enthusiasm. Don't over coach, give the players "free" playing time in the session. Give the players questions to answer and choices in technique and decision making. Encourage attacking football. Encourage players to work hard. Smile and have fun!

## Week One - Dribbling & RWTB

**Warm Up Activity - Bubble (Spatial Awareness)**
Aims: develop head up, eyes forward, look for space; combination of movements using balance, co-ordination, range of movement, strength and speed. Area: 20x20 yards. Numbers: 10-20. Layout: players spread around the area, arms-reach apart. Rules: players move to find personal space, protecting an imaginary "bubble" - if others get too close, it bursts. Development: shrink space, add cones/poles as obstacles, then add a ball to dribble while maintaining space. Coaching points: heads up, eyes forward, push off the outside foot to change direction, watch balance and body position.

**BALL WORK - Mastery (Toe Taps)**
Aims: confidence, touch and coordination with both feet; instinctive ability to move the ball in relation to stimulus. Layout: coloured cones spread in the grid, more cones than players. Rules: dribble from free cone to free cone, no queueing. Development: move to cones in a set colour sequence; different foot/task per cone colour. Coaching points: tap the ball with the inside of the right foot across the body, then the inside of the left foot back; weight on the balls of the feet, knees slightly bent.

**Skill Practice - Dribbling & Running with the Ball (Lives)**
Aims: correct dribbling/RWTB technique and touch strength for the space; coordination of dynamic body movements. Rules: dribble keeping close control; using hands or losing the ball out of area costs a life. Development: add cones (touch = lose life), add a foam ball defender, or have players target each other's balls.

**Skill Practice - Open/Closed Gates**
Rules: dribble around the area; gates are called open or closed (e.g. yellow open, red closed); count gates dribbled through, changing direction away from closed gates. Development: coach varies open/closed gates throughout; increase speed.

**Warm Up Activity - Car Horn (Spatial Awareness)**
Rules: players "drive" using a cone as a steering wheel; if another driver gets too close, make a horn sound. Development: shrink space, add obstacles, increase speed, then add a ball.

## Week Two - Dribbling & RWTB

**BALL WORK - Mastery (Sole Roll)**
Coaching points: roll the right foot over the ball so it moves across the body toward the left foot; repeat starting with the left foot.

**Skill Practice - Number Calls**
Rules: dribble and respond to coach's number calls - 1. On the Run (dribble) 2. Sole of the Shoe 3. Take a Knee (touch ball with knee) 4. On the Floor (touch ball with head on floor) 5. Stay Alive (tackles) 6. Show your Tricks 7. Up to Heaven (head the ball) 8. Find a Mate (pair up).

**Skill Practice - Steal from the Rich**
Layout: groups spread around the area; balls piled in the middle (roughly one per player). Rules: one player at a time runs in, takes a ball, dribbles back; next player goes once teammate returns. Development: once the middle is empty, players steal balls from other groups ("steal from the rich to give to the poor").

**Warm Up Activity - Shrinking Space (Spatial Awareness)**
Rules: find personal space in the area; coach halves the area repeatedly (4 squares to 2, to 1) as players succeed at maintaining space.

## Week Three - Dribbling & RWTB

**BALL WORK - Mastery (Inside, Outside)**
Coaching points: tap the ball across the body with the inside of the right foot, then push it away with the outside of the left foot, shifting feet sideways so the ball sits between them; repeat to the other side.

**Skill Practice - Leader and Follower**
Rules: pairs, one ball. Leader dribbles, follower trails behind. On "change," leader passes to follower and roles reverse. Development: follower tries to tap the leader's back; give the follower a ball too.

**Skill Practice - Pass Through the Legs**
Rules: one team dribbles and passes the ball through the legs of stationary players from the other team as many times as possible in the time given, then teams swap. Coaching point: don't repeat the same pair, keep head up to find a free pair of legs.

**Warm Up Activity - Traffic Lights (Spatial Awareness)**
Rules: red cone = stop but keep scanning for space; green cone = move; yellow cone = change direction.

## Week Four - Passing & Receiving

**Ball Work - Turns (Inside Hook)**
Aims: change direction (180°) while protecting the ball; instinctive ball movement in relation to stimulus. Coaching points: hook the foot around the back of the ball, use the instep to stop and turn 180°, push the ball forward and accelerate away. Practice with both feet. Development: add a passive/active defender for a turn-and-protect scenario.

**Skill Practice - Pass Through the Gate**
Aims: passing accuracy over 5-10 yards; positive movement into space after the pass. Rules: pairs pass through a gate to each other, then move as a pair to a new gate and repeat, within a time limit. Development: shrink the gates for accuracy, use the weaker foot, reduce time. Coaching points: short passes through the middle of the ball with the inside of the foot, strong ankle, standing foot beside/behind the ball, steady head, arms out for balance, high tempo.

**Skill Practice - Cross-Gate Passing**
Layout: four gates (red and yellow) positioned across the grid. Rules: receive at the red gate, dribble to the yellow gate, pass through to the red gate on the opposite side, then return to receive. Development: reverse yellow/red roles to use both feet/inside-outside passing; increase distance.

**Warm Up Activity - Chain Tig (Spatial Awareness)**
Rules: one or two catchers; caught players join the chain, which must work together to catch everyone. Development: chain splits once it exceeds four players.

## Week Five - Passing & Receiving

**Ball Work - Turns (Outside Hook)** - same mechanics as the Inside Hook, executed to the outside.

**Skill Practice - Triangle Passing**
Aims: passing accuracy over 5-10 yards; movement into space to maintain passing triangles. Rules: three players around a square with one side free; the player on the ball passes to either of the other two, then moves to the free side. Development: no pass-back to the passer; add two-touch/one-touch; add a defender for 3v1 (loser of possession swaps in). Coaching points: short inside-of-foot passes, receive on the back foot on the half-turn to open the body and limit touches.

**Skill Practice - Numbered Overload**
Layout: two grids of 4-6 players each, numbered 1-5, 5-10m apart. Rules: players pass and move inside their grid; when the coach calls a number, that player runs to the opposite grid and defends a 4v1 - the defender who wins the ball scores a point. Development: pass in number sequence; shrink the area for accuracy; increase to 3v2 once completion rate is high.

**Warm Up Activity - Flush the Toilet (Spatial Awareness)**
Rules: 2-4 catchers tag players, who then stand still with an arm out; teammates free them by pushing the arm down. Development: split the grid in half with catchers in each half.

## Week Six - Passing & Receiving

**Ball Work - Turns (Drag Back)**
Layout: players spaced around a circle, centre cone 10-15 yards away. Rules: on command, run toward the centre, then turn back toward the next cone clockwise. Coaching points: foot on top of the ball to stop it, drag backward while turning the body 180°, push the ball forward and accelerate away. Variant: instead of the full 180°, flick the ball with the inside of the foot behind the standing leg for a 90° turn.

**Skill Practice - Avoid the Landmines**
Layout: pairs spread through the area, one ball each, cones scattered as "landmines." Rules: move from one end of the grid to the other by passing and moving (no dribbling), avoiding the landmines. Development: shrink the area for accuracy, add more landmines, reduce the least-preferred-foot handicap or the time limit.

**Skill Practice - Safe Zone Passing**
Layout: two teams; one player per team stationed in a "safe zone." Rules: the team in the middle keeps possession and scores by passing to their teammate in the safe zone; dribbling into the safe zone is not allowed and only one player may occupy it at a time. Development: shrink the middle zone and safe zone width, add players to the middle, add gates in front of the safe zone for accuracy.
