# Irish FA Skills Development Programme (7-9 Years)

Skills Development Programme Coaching Manual (7-9 Years)

Irish FA Skill Development Programme 7 - 9 Years (Week 1 - Week 6)

The Coaching Standard

Start and end the practice with fun. Ensure you have a ball to each player when possible or relevant. Simple organisation and lay out of the session. Simple communication/rules of the practice. No "bus queue" of players waiting to play/shoot etc. Develop individuals, not teams. Inspire players with the session, your personality, energy and enthusiasm. Don't over coach, give the players "free" playing time in the session. Give the players questions to answer and choices in technique and decision making. Encourage attacking football. Encourage players to work hard. Smile and have fun!

## Week One - Dribbling & RWTB

**Warm Up Activity - Flush the Toilet (Agility)**
Aims: control of the body in a dynamic environment (move quickly and efficiently, start, stop and start again while maintaining stability); combination of movements using balance, co-ordination, range of movement, strength and speed. Layout: 2-4 catchers. Rules: catchers tag players, who then stand still with an arm out; teammates free them by pushing the arm down. Development: split the grid into two halves with catchers in each half.

**BALL WORK - Mastery (Toe Taps Roll Over)**
Layout: coloured cones spread in the grid, more cones than players. Rules: dribble from free cone to free cone, no queueing. Development: move to cones in sequence with a different task per colour. Coaching points: tap the ball with the inside of the right foot across the body, inside of the left foot back, then roll the right foot over the ball (slap) across the body to the left foot; repeat starting with the left foot.

**Skill Practice - Small Goals**
Layout: 6-8 small goals (1-2 yards apart) spread around the area. Rules: attackers score by dribbling through any small goal while 3-4 defenders try to prevent it; defenders may only block a goal then must move to the next (no goalkeeping). Development: dribble through goals in a colour sequence. Coaching points: correct touch strength for the space, use both feet, reverse/change direction under pressure, use ball mastery and feints to beat defenders.

**Game Practice - 1v1 Race to Goal**
Rules: the defender passes to the attacker, who engages and attempts to score in either goal; after a goal, both players race to their corresponding cone at the opposite goal (also the shooting line) - if the defender touches their cone first, the goal is cancelled. Development: progress to 2v2/3v3 with the scorer and nearest defender racing. Coaching points: works ball control, dribbling, improvisation (mastery, turns, feints) for attackers; closing space, denying, tackling and interception for defenders; in larger formats, also width, mobility, penetration, support, pressure and cover.

**Warm Up Activity - Rats & Rabbits (Agility)**
Layout: two parallel lines about a metre apart, one team "rabbits," one "rats." Rules: on the call of a team's name, those players sprint to their end line before being tagged. Development: award points for catches and safe arrivals; add "rhinos" and "raccoons" as more team names; add positions of readiness or a ball. Coaching points: reactions to commands, balanced positions, sideways movement, movement from the ready position.

## Week Two - Dribbling & RWTB

**BALL WORK - Mastery (Inside, Outside)**
Coaching points: tap the ball across the body with the inside of the right foot, push away with the outside of the left foot, shift feet sideways so the ball sits between them; repeat to the other side.

**Skill Practice - Islands**
Layout: five 5x5m islands (one per corner, one in the middle). Rules: players dribble to each corner island via the middle island, returning to the middle once all four corners are visited (max two players per island). Development: visit all five islands in any order; add "sharks" who move in the water (not on islands) trying to kick balls out of the grid - players evade onto islands but cannot stay more than 5 seconds; losing the ball means completing a task (e.g. keepie-ups) to rejoin. Coaching points: correct touch strength for the space, both feet, heads-up scanning for open islands and defenders, ball mastery and feints to escape sharks.

**Game Practice - Zone 2v1 to Goal**
Layout: two zones (10 yards long, 20 yards wide), goals at the end of Zone 2. Rules: a defender starts on the Zone 1/2 line and can move once the ball enters Zone 1, where attackers play 2v1; if they retain the ball into Zone 2 they play 2v1 plus a goalkeeper. The Zone 1 defender cannot enter Zone 2. Development: add defenders, restrict touches, or add a time limit per zone. Coaching points: attacking width and support angles, overlapping runs, mobility and disguise to drag defenders, purposeful decision-making, pass accuracy/weight/timing, positive shooting attitude.

**Week Three - Passing & Receiving**

**Warm Up Activity - Freeze & Thaw (Balance)**
Aims: understanding that balance is achieved with the centre of gravity over the supporting base. Rules: 2-3 "freezers" tag players who then freeze in a balanced position until "thawed" by a teammate's touch. Development: vary the freeze position (one leg, arms overhead); add "thawers" who can also be frozen. Coaching points: correct balanced positions, body alignment.

**Ball Work - Turns (Inside Hook)**
Aims: change direction (180°) while protecting the ball. Rules: pairs take turns dribbling to the end of a 5x5 grid, turning, and dribbling back; add a defender for a turn-and-protect scenario. Coaching points: hook the foot around the back of the ball, use the instep to stop and turn 180°, push forward and accelerate away; practice both feet.

**Skill Practice - Find a New Marker**
Layout: pairs, one player inside a circle/square, one outside, ~10 yards apart. Rules: inside player receives a pass, returns it, then moves to find a new outside player. Development: inside player receives, turns, dribbles, returns the ball, then moves on; further progression - keep the first ball received and find new open outside players for it. Coaching points: short inside-of-foot passes, strong ankle, standing foot beside/behind the ball, steady head, arms out for balance; positive movement to lose the marker (away then toward the ball), timed so players aren't static too long; receive on the back foot/half-turn to limit touches; scan before receiving to move the ball in the right direction.

**Game Practice - Zone Passing (3v3/3v4)**
Layout: three equal zones; the middle zone team defends. Rules: teams at either end keep possession and try to pass through the middle zone (no passes above head height); an interception swaps the intercepting team into the losing team's zone. Development: no pass-back to the passer; add two-touch/one-touch; add a defender for 3v1 (two defenders remain in the middle). Coaching points: short accurate passing, receiving on the back foot/half-turn, high tempo passing across the width to shift and split the defensive block for through passes.

## Week Four - Passing & Receiving

**Warm Up Activity - Body Part Clusters (Balance)**
Rules: coach calls a number and body part (e.g. "4, right leg") - players form groups of that size balancing on that body part; best cluster earns a point. Coaching points: balanced positions, body alignment.

**Ball Work - Turns (Drag Back)**
Layout: players spaced around a circle, centre cone 10-15 yards away. Rules: on command, run toward the centre then turn back toward the next cone clockwise. Coaching points: foot on top of the ball to stop it, drag backward turning the body 180°, push forward and accelerate away; variant - flick the ball behind the standing leg with the inside of the foot for a 90° turn instead of the full 180°.

**Skill Practice - Around the Mannequin**
Layout: two players at bottom cones with a ball each, one player between mannequins at the top of the grid. Rules: the top player moves side to side to receive, controls the ball offline behind the mannequin, and passes back on the inside; rotate after 60 seconds. Development: add two-touch/high tempo passing, driven passes (extend the grid), different parts of the foot to control. Coaching points: short inside-of-foot passes, strong ankle, standing foot beside/behind the ball, steady head, arms for balance; control the ball offline to present it to the other foot, opening the pitch and forcing defenders to move offline.

**Game Practice - 2v2 to End Zones**
Layout: 4 corner end zones (10x10) with a 20x30 middle zone; each team has a player in each of their end zones plus two players in the middle. Rules: 2v2 in the middle, scoring by passing into the correct colour end zone, which restarts the attack for the other team. Development: limit touches; reduce to one end-zone player per team for a 3v3 middle. Coaching points: short accurate passing, positive movement to lose the marker, timing so players aren't static; end-zone and middle players can swap after a scoring pass to create a temporary overload/recovery scenario.

**Warm Up Activity - Twister (Co-ordination)**
Rules: coloured cones scattered around the area, each colour tied to a coach command (e.g. yellow = left hand, blue = right hand, red = jump and head); players move and respond to called colours. Development: colours can trigger more than one command; add a ball to dribble while moving. Coaching points: balanced positions, body alignment, heads-up awareness of others.

## Week Five - Shooting & Finishing

**Skill Practice - Diagonal Feint & Rotate**
Layout: 10x10 grids, four players per grid, one at each cone. Rules: players move diagonally to meet in the centre, feint, dribble onto the cone behind their opponent, then move clockwise to the next cone. Development: sequence or combine feints; create opposition with only one player having a ball on the diagonal.

**Ball Work - Feints (Fake)**
Coaching points: with the ball between the feet, lift the preferred foot to push the ball outside with the small toe, miss the ball so the foot plants past it, then push the ball sideways with the outside of the other foot to beat the opponent; add an extra fake for "fake, fake, take."

**Skill Practice - Staggered Goals Shooting**
Layout: goals staggered on either side of a 30x30 area, groups 20-30 yards out. Rules: players dribble in one at a time and shoot from a coach-specified distance, then join the other line. Development: add target zones in the net, a goalkeeper, a defender, or dribble cones before the shot. Coaching points: for accuracy, side-foot strike, non-kicking foot alongside the ball, contact the middle of the ball, power from the hip, body over the ball; for power, use the instep, knee over the ball on approach, power from the knee straightening and hip driving forward, body over the ball to keep it low.

**Game Practice - Numbered Shootout**
Layout: goals on either side, teams numbered and positioned at the halfway point across from each other. Rules: when a number is called, those players dribble around their coloured cone and shoot; first to score gets the point. Development: add dribble cones, a defender, or goalkeepers. Coaching points: same shooting technique notes as above (accuracy = side foot; power = instep).

**Warm Up Activity - Co-ordination (Mirrors)**
Rules: pairs face each other; one performs an action (lifting limbs, moving directions, jumping/hopping) and the partner mirrors it. Development: combine multiple actions at once; add a ball to dribble, juggle, bounce, catch. Coaching points: accuracy and speed of the mirrored action, body alignment, fair play.

## Week Six - Shooting & Finishing

**Skill Practice - Slalom Feints**
Layout: cones in the middle of the grid (~1m apart) plus a line of cones down two sides. Rules: players start at the bottom, dribble diagonally through the centre cones using feints at each cone to the corner, then slalom down the side cones using coach-specified feints, then join the back of the other group. Development: both directions, both feet, increased tempo, double feints.

**Ball Work - Feints (Scissors)**
Coaching points: with the ball between the feet, circle the preferred foot around the front of the ball back to its original position, then push the ball sideways with the outside of the other foot to beat the opponent; add a second scissors for extra disguise.

**Skill Practice - Three-Station Shooting**
Layout: three stations 10 yards outside the 18-yard box. Rules: Station 1 dribbles in and shoots, then moves to provide a layoff for Station 2; Station 2 dribbles to the edge of the D, passes to Station 1, receives the pass back inside the box and shoots, then moves to defend Station 3; Station 3 dribbles in and must beat Station 2 before shooting; players rotate stations after each sequence. Development: add feint cones on the run-in; add a 2v1 between Stations 1 and 3. Coaching points: high tempo with correct sequencing; shooting technique notes as above.

**Game Practice - Split-Field 3v1 Shooting**
Layout: 30x30 split into two halves, each a 3v1 plus a goalkeeper (scalable to 5v3). Rules: goalkeepers always pass to the defender in their half; the 3 attackers shoot in their half or switch play to set up their teammate in the other half for a long-range effort. Development: reverse the numbers to encourage more long-range shooting and link-up play from the lone attacker. Coaching points: shooting technique notes as above.
