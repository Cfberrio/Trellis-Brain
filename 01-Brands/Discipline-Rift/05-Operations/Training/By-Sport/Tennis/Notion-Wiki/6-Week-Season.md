## Tennis tiers (levels of players)

### Beginner

| Stroke | Tier 1 | Tier 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Forehand | Low to high rainbow motion | Swing through the ball |
| Backhand | Stack hands, pulling a wagon motion, low to high | Swing through the ball |
| Volley | One hand for forehand, two hands for backhand, blocking motion | One hand for both strokes, show the strings, split step |
| Serve | Underhand serve (drop the ball), similar to a forehand | Feet position, toss above head, start with racquet behind head, hit at highest point |

### Intermediate

| Stroke | Tier 3 | Tier 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Forehand | Split step, shuffle side to side | Brush up on the ball, split step, shuffle |
| Backhand | Split step, shuffle side to side | Brush up on the ball, split step, shuffle |
| Volley | One hand for both strokes, show the strings, split step, know the zones, open spots | Continue mastering split steps and volleys in open spaces |
| Serve | Feet position, toss above head, racquet behind head, hit at highest point, aim at the correct zone | Focus on consistency and precision in aiming for correct zones |

### Advanced

| Stroke | Tier 5 | Tier 6 |
|---|---|---|
| Forehand | Footwork, split step, shuffle, playing tactics | Rotate towards the ball, swing through the ball, always return to the middle |
| Backhand | Footwork, split step, shuffle, playing tactics | Rotate towards the ball, swing through the ball, always return to the middle |
| Volley | Refine volley placement and learn defensive tactics | Refine tactics, know all zones, anticipate opponent moves |
| Serve | Continue to perfect serves, with emphasis on speed, spin, and placement | Master all serves (flat, spin, kick), return to the middle after each serve |
