# DR Team — The 10 Coach Responsibilities

## Parent
- [[Notion-Wiki-Home]]

## Related

## 1. Be #FUELED
**Embody the spirit.** Always bring your best energy and attitude to every session.

## 2. Be punctual
**Arrive on time.** Get to your school early to prepare and be there for your players.

## 3. Be responsive
**Stay connected.** Check and respond to team communications promptly.

## 4. Be show ready
**Prepare the space.** Set up all equipment and ensure the court is ready for action.

## 5. Be the energy outlet
**Lead with enthusiasm.** Your energy sets the tone. Stay upbeat and your team will follow.

## 6. Control the team
**Lead with confidence.** Keep the group focused and in control, even during tough situations.

## 7. Track class attendance
**Keep records.** Make sure attendance is accurate and up to date.

## 8. Manage the equipment
**Inspect and maintain.** Check all equipment before use and ensure everything is safe and in place.

## 9. Create an irresistible environment
**Engage creatively.** Make the class visually appealing and fun for all students.

## 10. Manage your time
**Stay on schedule.** Make sure every part of your lesson plan is covered efficiently.
