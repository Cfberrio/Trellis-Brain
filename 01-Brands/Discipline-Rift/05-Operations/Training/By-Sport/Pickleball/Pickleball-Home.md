## Strategic role
Pickleball is an active DR sport and it fills. Until the 2026-08-11 Notion pull this folder was an empty placeholder — no PDFs were ever provided for pickleball, so unlike tennis there is no `Source-Docs/` layer and never was. The DR PICKLEBALL coach wiki is now mirrored here and is the only pickleball curriculum the vault has ever held.

## Canonical source
[[Notion-Wiki-Home]] — start there. It mirrors the DR PICKLEBALL Notion database and is the source of truth for pickleball coaching material.

**The season is half-built.** The 6-week map is defined, but only Weeks 1–3 exist as sessions in Notion. Weeks 4 (Serves), 5 (Drop shots), and 6 (Rallying + Assessment) have no content at all. If pickleball is sold as a six-week season, coaches are improvising the back half.

## How to use this library
Use [[Notion-Wiki-Home]] and the `Notion-Wiki/Curriculum/` sessions for coaching and AI context. There is no superseded source layer to fall back on — the mirror is all there is.
