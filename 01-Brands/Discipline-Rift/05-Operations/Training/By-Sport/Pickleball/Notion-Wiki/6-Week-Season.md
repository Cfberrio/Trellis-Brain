# 6 Week Season — Pickleball

> Coach! This is your resource for your game plan in every practice.
> 1. Learn it
> 2. Practice it
> 3. Adapt to it

## Season Table

| WEEK | SKILLS | LIFE LESSONS |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Forehand strokes + Basic paddle grip | Teamwork + Discipline |
| **Week 2** | Backhand strokes + Forehands (Review) | Consistency + Humility |
| **Week 3** | Dinks + Forehands & Backhands (Review) | Confidence + Adaptability |
| **Week 4** | Serves + Dinks, Forehands & Backhands | Resilience + Passion |
| **Week 5** | Drop shots + Serves, Dinks, Forehands & Backhands (Review) | Focus + Integrity |
| **Week 6** | Rallying + All Skills (Assessment) | Growth + Fun |

## Standard session shape
Every built lesson follows the same block structure:
1. Introduction (10 min)
2. Warm-up (5 min)
3. Skill breakdown (5 min)
4. Skill repetition / drills (30 min)
5. Game of the Day (10 min)
6. Life Lesson (ongoing)
