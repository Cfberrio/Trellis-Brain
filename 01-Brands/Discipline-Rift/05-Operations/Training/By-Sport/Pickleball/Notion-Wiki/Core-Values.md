# Core Values

Our core values answer the following questions:
- Why do we exist?
- What do we do?
- How do we behave?
- When do we win?

---

### HUMILITY — "HUMBLE"
We acknowledge that there is always more to learn, even from the players themselves. Our approach: coaching with an open mind, sharing knowledge with fellow coaches, and taking initiative toward collaboration.

### PERSEVERANCE — "HUNGRY"
We consistently encourage players to push through challenges, whether it's a tough practice or personal matters, with relentless drive to improve both our coaching skills and those of our players.

### ADAPTABILITY — "SMART"
We apply emotional intelligence in all interactions. That means being aware of your own emotions, resolving conflicts in a way that strengthens relationships, and reading players' emotional states to understand their motivations.
