# DR Team — 10 Coach Standards

1. **Be #FUELED** — Always bring your best energy and attitude to every session.
2. **Be Punctual** — Get to your school early to prepare and be there for your players.
3. **Be Responsive** — Check and respond to team communications promptly.
4. **Be Show Ready** — Set up all equipment and ensure the court is ready for action.
5. **Be the Energy Outlet** — Your energy sets the tone; stay upbeat, and your team will follow.
6. **Control the Team** — Keep the group focused and in control, even during tough situations.
7. **Track Class Attendance** — Make sure attendance is accurate and up to date.
8. **Manage the Equipment** — Check all equipment before use and ensure everything is safe and in place.
9. **Create an Irresistible Environment** — Make the class visually appealing and fun for all students.
10. **Manage Your Time** — Make sure every part of your lesson plan is covered efficiently.
