# Week 2 — Backhands + Forehands (Review)

## INTRODUCTION (10 MIN)

**WELCOME**
- Greet all students and welcome them to the second week of the pickleball season.
- Reinforce the program's mission — **Pickleball Pathways**: using pickleball to teach life skills like discipline and teamwork.
- Recap: "What did we learn last week about teamwork?" Highlight positive examples shared by players.

**LIFE LESSON RECAP**
- Word of the Week: **Consistency**.
- Ask players to share one way they demonstrated teamwork during the week (school, friends, home).
- Discuss briefly how teamwork applies to pickleball, such as working together during doubles play.

**TEAM EXPECTATIONS**
- Reinforce rules: be on time, respect teammates, listen to coaches.
- Add a fun twist: let players suggest one team rule for the week that everyone follows, like "cheer for your partner."

## SKILL — BACKHANDS + FOREHANDS (5 MIN)

**DEMONSTRATION — Stance and Ready Position**
- Begin with a balanced stance: feet shoulder-width apart, knees bent, paddle held in front with both hands (two-handed backhand) or the dominant hand (one-handed backhand).
- Emphasize staying low and keeping weight evenly distributed.

**IMITATION — breakdown steps for players**
- **Stance**: stand in ready position and practice rotating the shoulders.
- **Grip Practice**: let players experiment with both grips to find what feels natural.
- **Swing Practice**: use shadow swings to guide the low-to-high motion without hitting a ball.
- **Controlled Repetition**: use slow feeds so players focus on technique. Provide immediate corrections on grip, swing path, or foot positioning.

**CORRECTION**
1. **Stance and Alignment**
   - Feet too close together → adjust to shoulder-width for better balance.
   - Knees locked → encourage a slight bend for mobility.
2. **Grip Adjustments**
   - Secure grip without excessive tightness.
   - Adjust non-dominant hand placement for control (two-handed backhand).
3. **Swing Issues**
   - Swing too flat → emphasize the upward brushing motion for proper lift.
   - Late contact → encourage earlier paddle preparation and swing.
4. **Follow-Through**
   - Short or abrupt finish → extend and finish high for better control and power.

## BACKHANDS (30 MIN)

**Coach Demonstration**
1. **Stance and Ready Position**: balanced stance — feet shoulder-width apart, knees bent, paddle held in front with both hands (two-handed) or dominant hand (one-handed). Stay low, weight evenly distributed.
2. **Grip Explanation**
   - *Two-Handed Backhand*: dominant hand on the handle, non-dominant hand above, gripping lightly for support. Neutral or slightly rotated grip for better control.
   - *One-Handed Backhand*: continental grip (a "handshake" with the paddle).
3. **Body Rotation**: pivot on the back foot while turning the shoulders so the paddle aligns with the side of the body. In a two-handed backhand, the non-dominant hand leads the initial motion.
4. **Swing Path**: start the paddle low, below the level of the ball. Use a low-to-high motion, brushing up and forward to lift the ball over the net.
5. **Contact Point**: strike the ball at waist height, slightly in front of the body. Keep eyes on the ball until contact.
6. **Follow-Through**: finish with the paddle above the opposite shoulder, pointing toward the target zone. Demonstrate a smooth recovery to ready position.

### ADVANCED PLAYER CUES
1. **Footwork**: move to the ball with small, quick steps, balanced before the swing. Recover quickly to ready position after each shot.
2. **Topspin Focus**: brush up on the ball to create topspin, keeping it in bounds. Practice brushing drills with a coach feeding at a controlled pace.
3. **Anticipation**: read the incoming ball's height and speed, preparing for the backhand well in advance.

### DRILL BASE SETUP
- **Set Cones as Targets**: place cones on the opposite side of the net in different spots.
- **Baseline Position**: line players up at or behind the baseline and feed balls one by one.
- **Hit Targets**: players aim at the cones with their stroke.
- **Scoring**: players who hit targets earn points, which can lead to rewards.
- **Vary the Target Locations**: some cones down the line, others cross-court, to improve accuracy and simulate match scenarios.

### DRILL — BACKHAND TARGET CHALLENGE
**Setup**: place 5–7 cones on the opposite side of the net, spaced across zones — near the baseline, mid-court, near the NVZ.
**Instructions**
1. Players line up behind the baseline on one side of the net.
2. Coach feeds the ball to each player, one at a time.
3. Players hit backhands, aiming for specific cones.
4. **Scoring**: 3 points for hitting a cone directly, 2 for landing within 1 foot of a cone, 1 for a successful hit into the correct zone.
**Progression**: *Beginner* — larger, brightly colored cones and slower feeds. *Intermediate* — alternating targets and medium-paced feeds. *Advanced* — specific backhand types (cross-court, topspin) or faster feeds.
**Coaching Tips**: emphasize low-to-high motion for controlled lift; eyes on the cone until striking the ball; consistent foot positioning before each shot.

### DRILL — PARTNER BACKHAND RALLIES
**Setup**: pair players on opposite sides of the net, about mid-court distance apart.
**Instructions**
1. *Warm-Up Phase*: partners rally using only backhands, aiming for 10 continuous shots without an error.
2. *Scoring Phase*: each successful rally earns the pair a point. Introduce combo points for rally counts (2 points for 15 hits, 3 points for 20 hits).
**Progression**: *Beginner* — closer together, slower rallies. *Intermediate* — increase distance, require shots to clear the NVZ. *Advanced* — cross-court backhand rallies or targets.
**Variation Twist**: *Time Challenge* — how many rallies in 3 minutes. *Moving Rally* — partners shuffle laterally after each shot.
**Coaching Tips**: highlight proper grip and follow-through; encourage partner communication; reinforce controlled, consistent strokes over power.

### DRILL — ZIGZAG BACK
**Setup**: place cones in a zigzag pattern from the baseline to the NVZ line, about 5–7 feet apart.
**Instructions**
1. *Movement Phase*: players move laterally around each cone in a zigzag pattern, hitting a backhand toward a target area at each cone.
2. *Shooting Phase*: coach or partner feeds balls as players reach each cone. Players return to ready position after each shot and keep moving.
**Scoring**: 1 point for landing in the correct zone; bonus points for hitting cones in the target area.
**Progression**: *Beginner* — stationary backhand shots after each cone. *Intermediate* — faster feeds, alternate cone placement. *Advanced* — topspin backhands and quick recovery to the middle.
**Variation Twist**: *Relay Race* — two zigzag lanes, teams compete for speed and accuracy. *Directional Focus* — alternate down-the-line and cross-court shots.
**Coaching Tips**: focus on quick recovery to ready position; smooth controlled movement rather than rushing; proper alignment before each shot.

## GAME OF THE DAY (10 MIN)

### KITCHEN BATTLE ROYALE
**Objective**: build confidence in forehands and backhands while emphasizing court positioning.
**Setup**: divide players into two teams. Players rally from the NVZ line (kitchen) using forehands and backhands.
**Rules**
1. Each team starts with a set number of points.
2. Players earn points for successful, controlled rallies and lose points for unforced errors.
3. A team wins on reaching a pre-set score (e.g. 10 points) or holding the highest score when time runs out.
**Variation**: advanced players must alternate between forehands and backhands; add a bonus rule for hitting marked zones.
