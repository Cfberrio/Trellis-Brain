## SKILL — DINKS (5 MIN)

**DEMONSTRATION — Why dinks?**
- **Purpose**: dinks are soft, controlled shots designed to land in the opponent's kitchen (non-volley zone), forcing them to play low and preventing aggressive returns. Critical for slowing the pace of play, maintaining precision, and creating opportunities to outmaneuver your opponent.
- **Strategy**: emphasize patience and control during dink rallies. Highlight how dinks turn a defensive situation into an offensive advantage.

**Stance and Ready Position**
- **Balanced Stance**: feet shoulder-width apart, knees bent, weight distributed evenly for stability. Paddle held at chest height, angled slightly forward for quick reactions.
- **Staying Low**: bend at the knees to stay low, aiding the controlled upward motion on dinks.

**IMITATION — breakdown steps for players**
1. **Stance**: start in ready position and keep the paddle neutral.
2. **Grip Practice**: relaxed grip for better control and finesse.
3. **Swing Practice**: short, upward stroke from below the ball, with a soft touch.
4. **Follow-Through**: finish with the paddle pointing toward the target.

**Controlled Repetition**
- **Shadow Practice**: shadow swings without a ball to reinforce the low-to-high motion.
- Provide immediate feedback on paddle angle, swing length, and posture.

**CORRECTION — common errors**
1. **Stance and Balance** — *Error*: feet too close together or knees locked. *Fix*: shoulder-width stance, slight knee bend.
2. **Grip** — *Error*: grip too tight or paddle angle too flat. *Fix*: relaxed grip and slight paddle tilt.
3. **Swing Technique** — *Error*: swinging too flat or hard. *Fix*: brush the ball softly upward with minimal motion.
4. **Follow-Through** — *Error*: stopping the paddle abruptly. *Fix*: extend through the shot toward the target.

## REPETITION (30 MIN)

Reinforce technique and consistency in dinks through structured repetitions with constant feedback and gradual progression.

### DRILL — DINKING CHALLENGE
**Objective**: simulate a dink rally to reinforce soft, controlled shots into the opponent's kitchen. Lays the foundation for dinking in live gameplay and strengthens precision and consistency.
**Setup**: 4 participants on court, 2 per side. Players start just behind the kitchen line. 1 ball per rally. Additional players wait on the sidelines to swap in.
**Instructions**
1. *Start the Rally*: players dink the ball softly over the net, aiming for the kitchen area on the opponent's side.
2. *Maintain Control*: emphasize smooth, upward strokes and keeping the ball low. Shots must bounce in the kitchen to count.
3. *Rotation*: when the rally ends, sideline players swap in, maintaining continuous play. Encourage active observation from the sidelines.

### DRILL — AROUND THE WORLD
**Objective**: enhance coordination, teamwork, and awareness by directing the ball across the court in a rotating pattern.
**Setup**: 4 participants — Players A and B on one side, C and D on the other. All begin just behind the kitchen line.
**Instructions**
1. *Clockwise Rotation*: A → C → B → D. Each player dinks to the next in sequence.
2. *Reverse Rotation*: D → B → C → A. Adds variety and challenges directional control.
3. *Goal*: see how many successful rotations the group can achieve without losing control. Encourage steady communication to anticipate the next shot.
**Extension — Volley Dinks**: players behind the Non-Volley Line can take the ball on the full (volley) if reachable, but the shot must still bounce in the opponent's kitchen. Adds quick decision-making and control.

### COACHING TIPS
- **Focus on Fundamentals**: "Stay balanced, and use soft, controlled strokes — power isn't the goal here."
- **Encourage Communication**: "Call out where the ball is going next to keep everyone engaged and ready."
- **Sweet Spot Awareness**: "Try to hit the ball with the center of your paddle for consistent control."
- **Build Confidence**: "Mistakes happen — focus on keeping the ball in play, not perfection."

## GAME OF THE DAY (10 MIN)

### KITCHEN BATTLE ROYALE
**Objective**: build confidence in forehands and backhands while emphasizing court positioning.
**Setup**: divide players into two teams. Players rally from the NVZ line (kitchen) using forehands and backhands.
**Rules**
1. Each team starts with a set number of points.
2. Players earn points for successful, controlled rallies and lose points for unforced errors.
3. A team wins on reaching a pre-set score (e.g. 10 points) or holding the highest score when time runs out.
**Variation**: advanced players must alternate between forehands and backhands; add a bonus rule for hitting marked zones.
