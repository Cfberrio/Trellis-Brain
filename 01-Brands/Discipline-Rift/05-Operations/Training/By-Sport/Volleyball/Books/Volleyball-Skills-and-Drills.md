## What the book covers
Official AVCA compendium, one chapter per author (top NCAA/pro coaches), 10 chapters: Serving (Tom Peterson), Receiving Serves (Marilyn Nolen), Setting (Sean Byron), Attacking (Jim McLaughlin), Blocking (Don Hardin), Digging (Joan Powell), Playing Offense (Joe Sagula), Playing Defense (Julie Backstrom & Mike Schall / Russ Rose), Transitioning (Stephanie Schleuder), Practicing (Paul Arrington). Goes deeper than pure drill lists — covers mechanics, footwork, formations (5-1, 6-2, 4-2 offenses; perimeter/middle-up/rotation defense), serve-receive patterns, and a full chapter on practice planning (goal setting, feedback, positive/negative reinforcement, drill design).

## How DR uses this book
Reference for coaching depth beyond drill names — offensive/defensive systems, serve-receive formations, and the practice-planning chapter (goals, feedback structure, reinforcement) are strong source material for DR coach training and the T-tier curriculum.
