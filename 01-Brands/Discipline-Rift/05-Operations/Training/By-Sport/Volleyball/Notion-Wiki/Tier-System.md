# Tier System

## Parent
- [[Notion-Wiki-Home]]

## Related
- [[DR-Practice]]
- [[Terminology]]

Six-tier player progression based on observable learning, not age alone.

## Tier 1
- Ready position
- Passing & setting form
- Motor coordination
- Court orientation

## Tier 2
- Passing & setting control
- Serving basics
- Basic rules
- "Mine!"

## Tier 3
- Serve receive
- Free-ball transitions
- Attacking approach
- Footwork coordination

## Tier 4
- 3-touch flow
- Intro overhands
- Consistent pass
- Game scoring awareness

## Tier 5
- Hitting power
- Defensive play
- Coverage & tips
- Game strategy

## Tier 6
- Setting combinations
- Serve consistency
- Offensive play
- Leadership
