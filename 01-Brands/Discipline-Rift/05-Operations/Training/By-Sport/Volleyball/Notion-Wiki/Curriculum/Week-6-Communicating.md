**North Star skill:** Pass/Set to a target with control
**Culture outcome:** Discipline = commit to the decision + follow through
**Week lens:** control before power, target before speed, feet before arms

The sources strongly support three ideas that fit this week:
- passing should always be practiced **to a target**
- passing mechanics start with **feet to ball**, then platform to target
- overhead passing / setting should shape the ball at the **forehead or higher** and go square to the target

## Block 1 — Culture Routine + Belonging
**Learning Target:** "Today we learn to **PASS and SET to a target**."
**Cue:** **Platform early**

**Activity:** quick huddle · cue call-and-response · effort norm: reset fast on "Freeze"

**Practice method classification:** Retrieval Practice

**Micro-CFU:** Coach: "What's today's cue?" — Team: "Platform early!"

**Next-block progression:** Move into court-learning retrieval with low-complexity movement.

## Block 2 — Retrieval Warm-Up
**Activity: The Lines Game** (optional add-on: **Volleyball Tag**)

**Why it fits:** retrieves prior court knowledge and basic movement without new teaching — revisits court lines, posture, and fast response.

**Cue:** **Platform early**

**Practice method classification:** Retrieval Practice

**Micro-CFU:**
- "Point to the **attack line**." (coach scans for fast visual identification)
- freeze scan: ready posture + arms not late to position

**Coach note:** The **Job Zone** upgrade keeps belonging high and avoids idle elimination, which fits the developmental philosophy better than sit-and-watch.

**Next-block progression:** From court awareness into explicit passing install.

## Block 3 — Teaching Block — Passing install
**Learning Target:** Pass with a strong platform to a partner/target
**Cue:** **Flat platform**

**Activity:** 2 demo reps (bad vs good) · partner mirror · toss-catch-pass for beginners if needed

**Source emphasis for passing:**
- **move feet to ball**
- **create platform**
- **contact on forearms**
- **freeze to target**

Drill-book forearm-passing cues: beat the ball · contact away from body · platform to the target.

**Practice method classification:** Blocked Practice

**Micro-CFU:** "Show me **flat platform**." Observable evidence: hands together · thumbs even · flat forearms.

**Exit criteria:** player can show **hands together + thumbs even + flat forearms** on command.

**Next-block progression:** Add repetition and target control.

## Block 4 — Skill Acquisition Reps Engine
**Learning Target:** Pass and set with control to a target
**Cue:** **Face the target**

**Activity:** run the 2-lane station model.

### Lane 1 — Passing reps
Drills: **Passing Challenge** · **Target Drill** · **Shuffle and Pass**

Source-backed logic: passing should be trained **to a target**; pass quality can be measured by how accurately the ball reaches the setter/target. The source recommends scoring passes and many repetitions early in the season.

### Lane 2 — Setting reps
Drills: **Setting to Wall** · **Setting Challenge** · **Through the Hoop**

Source-backed logic for overhead passing / setting: shape the ball at the **forehead or higher** · **square to the target** · extend as needed.

**Practice method classification:** Blocked Practice → Serial Practice

**Tier system:**
- **Tier 1:** catch → pass/set to target
- **Tier 3:** move across 3 spots
- **Tier 5:** call "mine" + "target" before contact

**Micro-CFU:** "Say the cue + show one rep." Observable evidence: shoulders/hips face target · ball reaches target area · controlled contact, not panic contact.

**Next-block progression:** Players choose pass vs set based on a simple read.

## Block 5 — DR Teaching (Guided Decisions)
**Learning Target:** Choose pass vs set based on ball height
**Cue:** **Above forehead = set. Below chest = pass.**

**Activity: Read & React Shuffle**
- low toss → shuffle and **pass**
- high toss → shuffle and **set**
- vary only one thing at a time: left/right/short/deep

**Practice method classification:** Interleaving

**Micro-CFU:** "What did you notice that told you to set?" Expected: "It was **above my forehead**." Observable evidence: correct choice · player points to the evidence · ball goes to the intended target area.

**Coach note:** **Job Zone** re-entry as "Instant Coach" keeps reps flowing and reinforces cue language without idle time.

**Next-block progression:** Take the same decision into a competitive constraint game.

## Block 6 — Application (Constraint Game)
**Learning Target:** Under pressure, pass/set safely to stay alive
**Cue:** **Safe to target**

**Activity: Jailbreak**

Players must: read the feed → choose pass or set → send a controlled ball → stay alive under pressure.

**Source-backed logic:**
- passing quality should be tied to whether the ball reaches the setter/target with options
- setters should provide a visible target and arrive in the target area near the net
- as players improve, add movement, live reads, and variability gradually until they can perform in more game-like situations

**Practice method classification:** Randomized Practice

**Metrics that matter:**
- team streak = number of **safe to target** reps in a row
- lose a life only for: (1) unsafe ball that doesn't cross, (2) wild out ball, (3) wrong pass/set choice by today's rule

**Micro-CFU:** Between rounds: "See → Decide → Act in one sentence." Example: "I saw it was high, I decided set, I set to the hoop."

**Next-block progression:** Close the loop with retrieval from memory and preview the revisit.

## Block 7 — Debrief + Retrieval + Spacing
**Learning Target closure:** Discipline = reset fast + commit to the cue
**Cue:** **Safe to target**

**Practice method classification:** Spaced Practice

**Retrieval question:** "What tells you **set vs pass**?" Expected: "Above forehead = set; below chest = pass."

**Exit criteria:** 8/10 athletes can:
1. freeze in **ready platform** on cue
2. complete **3 controlled contacts** to a partner/target

**Spacing preview:** "Next week warm-up: we'll re-test **flat platform** and **above/below** in the first 3 minutes."
