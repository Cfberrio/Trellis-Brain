## Practice outcomes
**Primary Learning Target (today's "north star"):** *Serve the ball in with control (to space / to a zone).*

**Why Week 2:** Serving is the easiest "start of rally" success lever for mixed-experience elementary groups, and it creates tons of reps + fast feedback.

## Block 1 — Culture Routine + Belonging (2–3 min)
**Learning Target (say it):** "Today we're learning to **serve in** with control."
**One Cue:** "**Freeze your finish** (hold your arm/hand where you want it to go)."
**Effort norm:** "Reset fast—next rep mindset."
**Micro-CFU:** "What's the cue today?" (choral response)

### Add-on options (Block 1) — 20–40 second serving-specific culture boosters
- **Option A: Call-and-response (serving version)** — Coach: "Serve on three—1,2,3…" → Team: "SERVE!" Then: "What's the cue?" → "Freeze your finish!"
- **Option B: Target commitment line (discipline tie-in)** — Coach: "Point to your target." (everyone points) Coach: "Commit." (everyone says "Commit.") Then run Block 2.

## Block 2 — Retrieval Warm-Up (5–7 min)
**Goal:** retrieve last week's ball-control skill *without turning it into a lecture.*

### Warm-up choice (book-sourced, simple, lots of touches)
**Four-Person Pepper (warm-up)** — a repeatable "touch rhythm" warm-up that reinforces control without heavy instruction.

**Micro-CFU (10–15s freeze):** coach scans for *ready posture + "platform/finish control"* (observable evidence).

**Tiering (same activity):**
- **Tier 1:** catch–toss version to keep rhythm (if needed for total beginners)
- **Tier 3:** bump only
- **Tier 5:** bump + move (players must adjust feet before contact)

### Add-on options (Block 2) — more retrieval warm-ups that still support serving
- **Option A: Pepper with "Finish Freeze" (serving cue preview)** — during pepper, on whistle everyone freezes in an "extended finish" shape for 2 seconds (arm up/forward). Rehearses "freeze your finish" before serving begins.
- **Option B: Partner "Toss–Contact" Rhythm** — partners 10–15 feet apart; one holds ball still; other practices a *gentle* contact to partner and freezes finish; swap every 5. Contact confidence without net pressure.
- **Option C: "Target Point" retrieval** — coach calls a zone ("Zone 1!"); players point instantly; then resume pepper. Fast court mapping for the "serve to space" target layer.

## Block 3 — Teaching Block (6–8 min)
**Purpose:** install the serve with **one cue** and **blocked reps**.

### Teach the serve with one "why"
Use the drill book's coaching emphasis that servers should have a **purpose before each serve** (where is it going?) — that's the first decision layer you'll build later.

**One Cue (keep it consistent):** "**Freeze your finish** (aiming arm/hand points to target)."

**Blocked reps:** 1–2 quick "shadow reps" → 1 serve each → immediate replay.

**Micro-CFU:** "Show me the cue" (everyone freezes finish for 2 seconds after contact).

**Exit criteria (ready to add complexity):** player can get **3/5 serves over** (distance scaled as needed) *while holding the finish.*

### Add-on options (Block 3) — serve teaching build-up without changing your cue
- **Option A: "Purpose before serve" prompt (2 seconds)** — before each rep: "Where is it going?" (player points) → serve → freeze finish.
- **Option B: Underhand open-hand contact (for beginners who need consistency)** — if a player can't contact consistently using a fist, cue **open hand contact** (heel/meaty part of hand) and still use **freeze finish**. *(Supports long-term progression to overhand contact consistency.)*
- **Option C: Shadow rep "3 freezes"** — Freeze 1: starting stance; Freeze 2: contact moment shape; Freeze 3: **finish freeze**. Then one live serve.
- **Option D: "Move the line" success scaling rule (non-negotiable)** — if they can't get 3/5 over from baseline, move them up until they can. Success comes first; then back them up.

## Block 4 — Skill Acquisition Reps Engine (10–12 min)
**Purpose:** high-volume serving reps with confidence.

### Reps engine option A (fast pace + accuracy): Minute-to-Win-It Serving
Kids serve for points into assigned zones for 60 seconds (competitive, short rounds, high reps).

**Tiering (same drill, different demand):**
- **Tier 1:** shrink court (move serve line up) + count "in-bounds over net" as success
- **Tier 3:** serve to "big zone" (half court)
- **Tier 5:** serve to a **specific zone** (cone/tape zones)

**Micro-CFU:** "Name the cue + show one rep." (one athlete per group, rotate)

### Add-on options (Block 4) — more reps engines (same purpose: volume + confidence)
- **Option B: Serving "Lanes"** — split court into 3–4 lanes with cones/tape; each player serves only into their lane; retrieve quickly. +1 in-lane serve; +2 if it lands past attack line (depth control). Focus: freeze finish + purposeful target.
- **Option C: Serve-to-Targets Circuit (stations)** — 3 stations: (1) serve over/in, (2) serve to big zone, (3) serve to hoop/cone; 90 seconds per station → rotate; beat-the-clock totals.
- **Option D: Partner "Serve & Score"** — pairs: one serves, partner is a "score judge" on the other side; 5 serves each, then switch; partner calls immediate "IN / OUT" and tracks points. Instant feedback without coach stopping reps.
- **Option E: "Make 3 then Move Back"** — everyone starts closer; make 3 serves in → step back one big step; miss → stay; first to baseline wins. Automatic scaling for mixed levels.

## Block 5 — Teaching Acquisition (guided learning under decisions) (10–12 min)
**Purpose:** connect serving technique to **See → Decide → Act** with one variable added.

### Decision-based serving (still controlled): Serving Ladder
Pair format with scoring + location demands. Adds the decision of *where* to serve (and keeps accountability through scoring).

**Decision/perception focus (ask it fast):**
- "What did you choose—deep or short / which zone—and why?"
- "What did you notice about your last serve that made you adjust?"

**Fast teaching moves (no long freezes):** perception question → recreate the same target → replay immediately.

**Micro-CFU:** athlete answers: "I chose ___ because I noticed ___." (and points to the target/zone)

### Add-on options (Block 5) — more guided decision serving games (still controlled)
- **Option A: Color-Zone Serve** — 3–4 colored cones representing zones; coach calls a color as player begins routine; player must serve there and freeze finish. Micro decision: "I chose ___ because coach called ___ / I aimed to space."
- **Option B: Deep vs Short (one variable)** — tape line at attack line; coach calls "SHORT" (in front of attack line) or "DEEP" (past attack line). +2 correct depth + in; +1 in only. Teaches purposeful serving without needing perfect zone accuracy yet.
- **Option C: "Fix It" Replay** — player serves, then must answer fast: "What did you notice?" (too far left/right/short); immediately serves again to the adjusted target. The "perception → replay" model packaged into a repeatable game.
- **Option D: Serving Ladder "Levels"** — Level 1: in-bounds · Level 2: big zone · Level 3: called zone · Level 4: deep/short call · Level 5: hit the "special target" for bonus.

## Block 6 — Application (Constraint Game) (10–15 min)
**Purpose:** transfer under game-like pressure *without turning into chaos.*

### Constraint game option (still serving-focused): Run to Serve
Adds time + movement pressure ("gamelike feel") while still keeping serving reps central.

**Constraint:** must serve within the time limit after running back (scaled for age).

**Metrics that matter (what you track):**
- **Decision success:** served to the called/assigned target area
- **Process success:** held the "freeze finish" cue

**Micro-CFU between rounds:** "See → Decide → Act" in one sentence.

### Add-on options (Block 6) — more serving-first application games
- **Option A: Hula Hoop Serving Challenge (team scoring)** — hoops spread on opposite court; two teams alternate serves. Inside hoop = 2; rim/touch = 1; in-bounds = 1 (if you want high success). Focus: purposeful target + freeze finish.
- **Option B: Mission Impossible (serve → sprint → catch)** — each successful serve sends server to opposite side to sit and become the catcher for the next serve; first team to cycle everyone through wins. High reps + fun movement + immediate consequence.
- **Option C: Join the Blob (targeting + engagement)** — servers aim to land near or tag the "blob" area; a successful "tag" joins the blob on the opposite side. Keeps even hesitant servers motivated and repeats targeting.
- **Option D: Cops and Robbers (serve under time + movement)** — "safe zone" hoop for serving; outside hoop = tag risk; servers complete X serves in time; taggers try to catch them outside the safe zone. Serving + speed + fun pressure.
- **Option E: Simon Says Serving (listening + technique)** — teams lined up with balls; coach gives commands; only some allow an actual serve; points only for correct "Simon" serves that land in. Reinforces attention, routine, and self-control.

## Block 7 — Debrief + Exit Criteria + Spacing (2–3 min)
**Meaning tie (DR):** "What did it look like today to reset fast and try again?"
**Retrieval question:** "What's our serving cue?"
**Spacing preview:** "Next week we'll keep serving, but we'll add *serve-receive passing* back in."

**Exit criteria (observable):**
- Can serve **over the net and in-bounds** from an appropriate distance, and
- Can **hold the finish** after contact (shows control + intention).

### Add-on options (Block 7) — fast scoreboard + retrieval (30 seconds)
- **Option A: 10-Serve Team Test** — whole team gets 10 total serves; count how many are "in + finish freeze"; announce score and "beat it next week."
- **Option B: Retrieval lightning** — "Cue?" → "Freeze your finish!" · "What's the target?" → "Space / Zone!" · "What's discipline?" → "Commit + follow through!"

## Coach option menu (quick pick list for faster-paced sessions)
- **If the group needs confidence first:** Make 3 then Move Back (Block 4 Option E) · Serve & Score partners (Block 4 Option D)
- **If the group needs accuracy to space:** Serving Lanes (Block 4 Option B) · Color-Zone Serve (Block 5 Option A)
- **If the group needs game energy:** Mission Impossible (Block 6 Option B) · Join the Blob (Block 6 Option C) · Cops and Robbers (Block 6 Option D)
- **If the group needs listening + discipline:** Simon Says Serving (Block 6 Option E) · Run to Serve (Block 6 anchor)
