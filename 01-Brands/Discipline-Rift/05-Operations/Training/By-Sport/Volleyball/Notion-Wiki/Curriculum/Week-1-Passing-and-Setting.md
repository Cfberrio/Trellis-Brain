**North Star:** *Pass/Set to a target with control*
**Culture outcome (Discipline Rift):** *Commit to the decision + follow through (fast listening, fast reset, try again)*
**Daily cue:** **"Platform early."** *(you can swap to "Angle = direction" later in the session)*

## Block 1 — Culture Routine + Belonging (2–3 min)

### What the coach DOES / SAYS (script)
1. **Welcome (20 sec):** "Coaches, names, welcome to DR Volleyball."
2. **Discipline Rift definition (30 sec):** "Discipline isn't punishment. Discipline is the power to **commit to a decision and follow through**."
3. **Team expectation (20 sec):** "When I give a cue, you respond fast. When you miss, you reset fast."
4. **Call-and-response (30 sec):**
   - Coach: "Mine on three. 1–2–3…"
   - Team: "MINE!" *(repeat twice)*
5. **Belonging rule (20 sec):** "If you're out in a game, you go to **Job Zone** for 10 seconds and come right back."

### Micro-CFU (choral)
Coach: "What's discipline?" — Team: "Commit and follow through."

**Next progression:** Retrieval warm-up where they must listen fast + move fast.

### Add-on options (Block 1) — fast culture + communication boosters (30–60 sec)
Use *only if you need more energy or more buy-in* without adding time.

**Option A: "VOLLEY / ATTENTION"**
- Coach: "VOLLEY!" → Team: "ATTENTION!"
- Coach: "Mine on three—1,2,3…" → Team: "MINE!"

**Option B: Name Pop**
- Coach: "Say one teammate's name." **Wait** until you hear names. Then: "Mine on three…"

## Block 2 — Retrieval Warm-Up (6–8 min)

### A) The Lines Game (court awareness + listening)

**Setup**
- All players start on the **center line**, spread out (no pushing).
- Coach stands where everyone can see/hear.

**Court overview (60–90 sec max)** — walk to each line and point:
- **End line / baseline**
- **Sidelines**
- **Center line**
- **Attack line (10-foot line)**
- *(Optional later: "net line" / "under the net" is not a line — don't teach that as a line.)*

**Instructions (practice rounds first)**
1. Coach says: "When I call a line, you sprint to it and **touch it with your hand**. Then freeze in athletic stance."
2. Coach calls 3 practice rounds: "Attack line!" → "Sideline!" → "End line!" (run/touch/freeze each)

**Game version (choose ONE)**
- **Version 1: Elimination (original)** — last player to touch the line goes to **Job Zone** (not "out forever"). After 10 seconds, they re-enter next round.
- **Version 2: Team points (faster, fewer stoppages)** — split into 2 teams; everyone runs each call; **1 point** if the whole team touches the correct line and freezes in stance within 3 seconds. First to **7** wins.

**Add the retrieval twist (optional):** On "Freeze!" coach asks: "Point to Zone 1!" *(scan fast)*

**Job Zone (10 seconds, no idle):**
- 3 platform locks + "Platform early!"
- 2 step–shuffle reps in place
- 1 low freeze (ready position)

**Micro-CFU:** Coach: "Point to the attack line." (scan)

**Next progression:** Connect posture + footwork to volleyball stance (Crab Position).

#### Add-on options (Block 2A) — Lines Game variations
Pick **one** to keep it simple for coaches.
- **Option 1: "Call + Action"** — coach calls a line **plus** an action: "Attack line + platform freeze!", "End line + hands up!" Adds volleyball posture retrieval without new skills.
- **Option 2: "Two-Line Combo"** — coach calls two lines in a row ("Sideline—Attack line!"); players touch both, then freeze. Faster brain + faster feet.
- **Option 3: "Zone Point Check"** — after touching a line, coach yells "ZONE!"; players point to Zone 1/5/6 instantly. Court mapping under tempo.

### B) Shuffling Crab Game (posture + platform + movement)

**Teach "Crab Position" with the 3 P's (30 sec):**
- **Posture:** low, knees bent, chest tall
- **Platform:** hands together, arms straight (ready to pass)
- **Position:** feet wider than shoulders, weight on toes

**Setup:** players spread out facing the coach (enough space to shuffle without collisions). Coach holds a volleyball as the visual cue.

**Instructions:**
1. Everyone starts in Crab Position.
2. Coach moves the ball: right / left / forward / backward.
3. Players must **shuffle** the direction the ball moves while staying low.

**Challenge rule (keeps it fun):** Coach may **say "right"** but move the ball left. Players must follow **the ball**, not the voice.

**"Out" rule (keep it light + fast):** Out = stands up, crosses feet, or follows the wrong direction. Out → Job Zone 10 sec → re-enter.

**Micro-CFU:** On whistle: "Freeze—show Crab Position + platform."

**Next progression:** Skill install (passing), because platform is already built.

#### Add-on options (Block 2B) — Crab Game upgrades
Pick **one**.
- **Option 1: "Crab + Call"** — players must call "MINE" on every move cue. Out = silent OR breaks posture.
- **Option 2: "Crab + Platform Late Trap"** — coach says "NO PLATFORM!" while moving ball (hands relaxed), then "PLATFORM!" and they snap it in 1 second. Directly reinforces "platform early" *on command*.
- **Option 3: "Crab Mirror Race"** — two teams; team point if everyone stays low and moves correctly for 5 consecutive cues.

## Block 3 — Teaching Block (6–8 min)

### Passing install (fast + unified language)
**Learning target:** "Pass with a strong platform to a repeatable target."
**Cue:** **"Platform early."** *(or "Angle = direction" if that's your focus)*

**What the coach demos (2 reps only):**
- Bad rep: platform forms late, arms swing
- Good rep: platform early, quiet arms, legs lift

**Imitation (30 sec):** Coach calls numbers for the passing sequence: "1 Ready" → "2 Pancakes" → "3 Low" → "4 Up". Everyone freezes each position quickly.

**1-rep check (everyone sees it):** Coach tosses to 3–5 athletes quickly (not everyone). Ask the group: "Was platform early?" thumbs up/down.

**Micro-CFU:** Coach: "Show me platform early in 2 seconds." (freeze)

**Next progression:** High-rep engine with targets + scoring.

### Add-on options (Block 3) — fast teaching "checks" (30–60 sec total)
- **Option A: "Target Call Freeze"** — coach calls: "Angle to Zone 2!"; athletes freeze platform angle and point where the ball would go.
- **Option B: "One Word Fix"** — coach says only one cue: "SURFBOARDS!"; athletes instantly correct elbows/arms without more explanation.

## Block 4 — Skill Acquisition Reps Engine (14–18 min)
Run **two main lanes** plus an optional connection lane if you have enough balls/coaches.

### Lane 1 — Passing (pick 2 games, 5–6 min each)

#### Option 1: Target Hoops (Target Drill, clarified)
**Setup:** 2–4 hoops on the other side of the net (or cones if no hoops); one line of passers at the attack line (easier) or deeper (harder); coach feeds from the opposite side or same side.
**Run:** Athlete steps up, coach tosses, athlete passes over net aiming for hoop zone. Athlete gets ball, returns to line fast.
**Scoring:** Over net in bounds = 1 · In a hoop = 2 · Called "special hoop" = 3
**Coaching focus:** Platform early · quiet arms · Low → Up

#### Option 2: Shuffle & Pass (tightened)
**Setup:** Coach and athlete start center; mark 3 spots: left/middle/right.
**Run:** Coach toss → pass; coach + athlete shuffle to next spot together → toss → pass; 3 passes then rotate athlete out.
**Gamify:** "Clean 3" = athlete earns a point for their line/team.

#### Add-on options (Lane 1 — Passing)
- **Option 3: 3-Person "Pass-to-Target" (triad)** — Tosser → Passer → Target (near net); rotate every 5 reps. Score: +1 for each "no-move catch" at target; first to 10. Focus: platform early + angle. *(Diagram in Notion source — not mirrored.)*
- **Option 4: Partner "Pepper Pass" (forearm only)** — pairs 10–15 ft apart; must "freeze platform early" on coach whistle. 20 in a row = win; drop resets. Focus: quiet arms, legs lift.
- **Option 5: "Serve-Receive Lite" (coach roll/toss)** — 3 passers + target; passers call "mine," pass to target, rotate after 5 balls. Target gives 0–3 rating; team tries to reach 20 points. Focus: movement before platform, platform early. *(Diagram in Notion source — not mirrored.)*
- **Option 6: Seam Saver** — two passers with a cone between them ("seam"); coach tosses into seam; one must call and take it. +1 clear call + controlled pass; −1 silent/confused. Focus: communication + decisive movement.
- **Option 7: "Beat the Clock" Passing** — target + hoop; 60 seconds of rapid tosses to passer line; count hoop hits; beat previous best. Focus: fast reset, repeatable platform.

### Lane 2 — Setting (pick 2 games, 5–6 min each)
**Learning target:** "Move under, stop, legs up, fingertip control to target."
**Cue:** **"Stop then push up."** *(or "Finger tips… up!" depending what's breaking)*

#### Option 1: Through the Hoop (clarified)
**Setup:** basketball hoop OR held hoop target; line of setters.
**Run:** Coach tosses to forehead height; athlete moves, stops, sets through hoop.
**Scoring:** Through = 2 · Hits rim = 1 · Miss = 0
**Coaching focus:** feet stop before contact · legs push up · fingertips only

#### Option 2: Setting to Wall (if wall space exists)
**Setup:** pairs at wall, one ball per pair.
**Run:** continuous sets to wall, recover, set again; count consecutive clean fingertip contacts.
**Gamify:** "Beat your best" scoreboard (pairs record high score).

#### Add-on options (Lane 2 — Setting)
- **Option 3: "Hershey Kiss Freeze" + Set** — on toss, athlete must show "Hershey Kiss" shape *before* contact. +1 for visible shape + clean set; streak wins. Focus: early hands, calm contact.
- **Option 4: Move–Stop–Set Squares** — 4 "setter squares" (cones) around center court; coach points to a square; setter moves there, stops, receives toss, sets to target. +1 if feet stop + target hit; first to 8. Focus: stop before contact, legs up.
- **Option 5: Front/Back Call** — two targets (front = near net; back = deeper); coach calls "front" or "back" mid-flight; setter adjusts. 1 for correct depth, +1 for clean technique. Focus: legs create distance, not arm flick.
- **Option 6: Partner Set & Catch** — pairs 8–12 ft apart; setter sets to partner who catches at forehead height (not above head). 10 clean catches = win; drop resets. Focus: controlled release, fingertip contact.
- **Option 7: "No Spin Challenge"** — any setting rep; sets must have minimal spin. +1 "quiet spin"; −1 palm/slap. Focus: fingertip pads, even pressure.

### Optional Lane 3 — Pass → Set connection (North Star lane)
**Setup:** tosser/coach sends ball in; Athlete A passes to a marked setter target; Athlete B sets to hoop/pin target.
**Scoring:** pass hits setter target = 1 · set hits hoop/pin target = 1 · both = bonus point.
**Tier without splitting groups:**
- Bigger target / slower toss for newer athletes
- Smaller target / faster toss for advanced athletes
- Add "Mine" + called target for top tier

**Micro-CFU (10 sec):** Coach: "What's today's cue?" → "Platform early!" Then one live rep from one athlete immediately.

**Next progression:** Guided decisions (hands vs platform).

#### Add-on options (Lane 3 — connection)
- **Option 1: Pass–Set–Catch Relay** — two teams; passer + setter + catcher/target; toss in → pass to setter → set to target → target catches = point. First to 10. Focus: controlled contacts, fast reset.
- **Option 2: 2-Ball Connection** — two balls running (second starts 2 seconds later); forces quick transitions and communication. Count "clean sequences" in 60 seconds. Focus: tempo + organization.
- **Option 3: Called Target Connection** — two hoops (left/right) for the set; coach calls target late ("Left!") after the pass leaves platform. +2 if setter still hits correct hoop. Focus: decision + composure.

## Block 5 — DR Teaching (Guided Decisions)
**Learning target:** "Choose hands vs platform based on ball height/cleanliness."
**Cue:** **"High/clean = hands. Low/fast = platform."**

### Game: Pass-or-Set Gate
**Setup:** create two zones with cones:
- **Hands Zone:** balls tossed higher and cleaner (above forehead)
- **Platform Zone:** balls tossed lower/faster (below forehead)

Athlete starts in ready stance.

**Run:**
1. Coach tosses randomly (high or low).
2. Athlete must **say the decision first**: "HANDS!" or "PLATFORM!"
3. Then execute.

**Win condition:** 3 correct decisions in a row = "level cleared." Wrong decision = Job Zone 10 sec → re-enter.

**Micro-CFU:** Coach asks: "Why hands?" Expected: "High and clean."

**Next progression:** Application game under score.

### Add-on options (Block 5) — more guided decision games
Still **guided** (coach controls input), not full chaos.
- **Option 1: Two-Ball Reveal** — coach holds two balls (one high toss, one low toss), reveals at last second; athlete must call "hands/platform" then play.
- **Option 2: Color Decision** — red cone = platform, blue cone = hands; coach flashes color and tosses accordingly; athlete calls the decision out loud.
- **Option 3: Late Call "Tight / Off"** — coach calls "tight" (near net) or "off" (away from net) mid-flight; same high/clean vs low/fast rule but athlete must move accordingly. Keeps footwork tied to decision.

## Block 6 — Application (Constraint Game) (12–18 min)

### Game of the Day: Jailbreak (clarified + paced)

**Setup:** split into Group A and Group B on opposite sides; each group lines up behind the baseline; coach has balls at the net or attack line for quick feeds.

**Run (fast cycle):**
- First athlete steps to attack line.
- Coach feeds: early rounds = toss; later rounds = faster toss / light serve.
- Athlete must **pass OR set** (coach calls which, or athlete decides if you want DR).
- Ball must go over and in.

**Lives + jail:**
- Everyone starts with **3 lives**.
- Miss (into net/out/not over) = lose a life.
- At 0 lives → go to jail (other side).

**Breaking out (keep it safe + skill-based):**
- Catch = allowed **only if coach calls it safe**
- OR breakout option: "2 clean platform catches" (self-toss → platform pop → catch) in jail area, then re-enter.

**Gamify upgrades (optional):**
- **Streak bonus:** team earns 1 bonus life if they hit 5 safe-to-target balls in a row
- **Decision bonus:** if athlete correctly calls "hands/platform" + executes, team gets +1 point

**Micro-CFU (between rounds, 10 sec):** Athlete: "I saw __, chose __, did __."

**Next progression:** Debrief + retrieval.

### Add-on options (Block 6) — alternate application games
- **Option A: 2v2 Mini Volleyball (short court)** — shrink court (attack line to end line width); 3 contacts = bonus point. Forces pass → set behavior without lecturing.
- **Option B: "Beat Your Best Rally"** — whole group on one side vs coach toss; achieve X pass→set sequences in a row; scoreboard best streak; try to beat it twice.
- **Option C: King/Queen of Control** — 3 on, others in line; only points if first contact goes to target area; winner stays; others rotate quickly. Pressure + reps.
- **Option D: Texas Star (ball-control version)** — players rotate through spots after each contact; point only if set hits target. Chaotic movement + controlled contacts.

## Block 7 — Debrief + Retrieval + Spacing (2–3 min)

**Discipline reflection (60 sec):** Coach: "Today discipline was committing to the decision and resetting fast."

**Retrieval (choral):**
1. "Cue?" → **Platform early**
2. "When hands?" → **High/clean**
3. "When platform?" → **Low/fast**

**Spaced preview:** Coach: "Next time we re-test in the first 3 minutes: platform early + decision rule."
