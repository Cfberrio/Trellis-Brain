## Origin of the word "coach"
Comes from a Hungarian town called **Keszthelyi**. Hungarians from Kocs made wagons named *kocsi szekér* (wagon of Kocs) for transportation purposes.

> [!note] Image in Notion source (not mirrored — expiring URL): "KOCSI SZEKER (wagon of Kocs) — an enclosed vehicle with four wheels which is pulled by horses, and in which people used to travel."

1. Kocsis were imported to other countries and called "coach" in English.
2. Today, the word coach is commonly used as a synonym for cars and buses as well.
3. Nowadays a "coach" is known as a bus that carries passengers.

## You are the coach
- From the origin of the word coach
- Just like the wagons and buses
- You have the responsibility of taking the players:

> [!note] To where they want to go

> [!note] To where you know they can go

> [!note] When they can't get there themselves

## Moral responsibility
1. To take your player somewhere
2. You need to go
3. When you can't get there yourself
