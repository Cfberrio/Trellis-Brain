## What core values answer
- Why do we exist?
- What do we do?
- How do we behave?
- When do we win?

## Humility — "Humble"
We acknowledge that there is always more to learn, even from the players themselves. Our approach is coaching with an open mind, sharing knowledge with fellow coaches, and taking initiative towards collaboration.

## Perseverance — "Hungry"
We consistently encourage players to push through challenges, whether it is a tough practice or personal matters, with relentless drive to improve both our coaching skills and those of our players.

## Adaptability — "Smart"
We apply emotional intelligence in all interactions. This means being aware of your own emotions, resolving conflicts in a way that strengthens relationships, and reading the emotional states of players to understand their motivations.
