# SEASON DESIGN — 60-Minute Blocks + Six Sessions

## Same every week — 60 min

**Block 1 (3 min) — Belonging + Routine**
- "Name + norm + today's why" (quick, not speeches).
- Script: "Sport is the vehicle. Today we're leveling up one thing together."

**Block 2 (8 min) — Retrieval Warm-up (last week's cue + movement)**
- Tag / relay / partner toss with **one cue** you're reviewing.

**Block 3 (7 min) — Skill Clarity (new cue)**
- 20–30s demo, then *1 cue only* + 1 quick "show me" CFU.

**Block 4 (12 min) — Fluency Reps (high reps, low talk)**
- Stations or partners; set a rep target ("10 quality throws each").

**Block 5 (13 min) — Decision Reps (add read, time, or defender)**
- Still coached by the *same cue*, now under pressure.

**Block 6 (14 min) — Application / Constraint Game**
- Small-sided game where scoring **rewards the cue**.

**Block 7 (3 min) — Wrap + Exit Ticket**
- 1 question + 1 shout-out tied to the cue.

---

## "Elimination" that still teaches (use in every game)

**When a player is "out"** (tag/flag pull/drop/out of bounds): they don't sit — they go to the **JOB ZONE** (2 cones on the sideline).

### Option A — Quick Job (10 seconds)
- **Instant Coach (primary):** give the next player **1 cue** + 1 quick demo. **Re-entry:** done → back in **on the next reset/whistle**.
- **Skill Sprint (backup):** 10s: 3 squeezes + 2 switches + 1 freeze "safe window". **Re-entry:** finish clean → back in **on the next reset**.

### Option B — Stay-On Job (1 possession or 30 seconds max)
Pick ONE role for the day:
- **Ref + CFU Collector:** watch 1 drill, answer: "What cue did you see?"
- **Target Scanner:** call "space left/right" or "pressure" **twice correctly**
- **Reset Captain:** return ball/reset cones fast + call "Ready!"

**Re-entry:** complete the role requirement → sub back in on the next reset.

# Six 60-minute Sessions (Lesson 1–6) — DR-style + Weekly Gamification

## Week 1 — Throwing + Catching Foundations (Lesson 1)
**Curriculum anchor activities:** Flag Tag + Partner Poly Pass
**Cue of the week:** "**Step to your target**"

- **B1:** Team name + "how we treat people" norm.
- **B2:** **Flag Tag** warm-up (JOB ZONE instead of sitting) + *retrieval cue*: "Eyes on hips" for flag pulling.
- **B3:** Throwing clarity: *Side to target → ball to ear → **step**.* (Only coach "step".)
- **B4:** **Partner Poly Pass** reps (start kneeling → stand).
- **B5:** Add a moving target: "QB hits WR jogging to a cone" (accuracy under light movement).
- **B6:** Constraint game: "**5-Pass Finish**" (4v4): 1 point per completed pass + **bonus point** if passer steps to target.
- **B7 Exit ticket:** "Show me with your body: what does 'step to target' look like?"

**Gamify it:** "Rookie Combine Card" — earn a stamp for 10 stepped throws + 10 "diamond hands" catches (if ready).

---

## Week 2 — Catch Levels + Communication (Lesson 2)
**Curriculum anchor activities:** Hot Potato; Catching Low/Medium/High; Touchdown 4v4
**Cue of the week:** "**Diamond hands at chest**" (medium/high) *or* "**Rake hands low**" (choose one)

- **B2:** **Hot Potato** intervals (20s) with partner communication pauses.
- **B3:** Catching clarity: choose **diamond** *or* **rake**; everyone freeze-shows the hand shape.
- **B4:** **Low/Medium/High** partner ladder (step back after success).
- **B5:** Add a rule: thrower must call partner's name; receiver must call "ball!" (communication under reps).
- **B6:** **Touchdown (4v4)**: hidden ball/object in a few players' hands; defense pulls flags; reveal in end zone. *Scoring tweak:* +1 "communication point" when a team uses a clear huddle plan.
- **B7 CFU:** "Why do hands change low vs high?"

**Gamify it:** "Communication Captain" — teams earn a bonus if every player uses *name + direction cue* ("Jayden, now!"). Track with a simple tally.

---

## Week 3 — Offense: Pass Patterns + YAC Finish (Lesson 3)
**Curriculum anchor activities:** Pass Patterns (Hook/Now/Slant/Go); Touchdown City
**Cue of the week:** "**Lead your partner on the slant**"

- **B2:** Retrieval warm-up: 2-min Hot Potato + 2 "diamond hands" demos.
- **B3:** Pattern clarity: teach **Hook + Now + Slant** (skip Go until ready); cue is only "lead on slant."
- **B4:** Partner route stations (poly spots = LOS); rotate QB/WR.
- **B5:** Add a defender "shadow" (walk pace, no steals) forcing better leads.
- **B6:** **Touchdown City**: QB calls route; WR catches then runs into end zone and drops ball in hoop (YAC).
- **B7 Exit:** "Which route was easiest/hardest and why?"

**Gamify it:** "Route Runner Badges" — Hook Hero / Now Ninja / Slant Specialist, earned on 3 clean reps **and** giving a teammate a "glow + grow."

---

## Week 4 — Ball Carrying + YAC Chase (Lesson 4)
**Curriculum anchor activities:** YAC; Snake Run
**Cue of the week:** "**Tuck + lock it away**" (ball security)

- **B2:** **YAC** with music-stop chase (15–20s chase).
- **B3:** Clarity: show "lock it away" (outside hand away from defense if ready for that extension).
- **B4:** **Snake Run** lanes through cones (general space), focus on secure carry.
- **B5:** Add a read: "If defender closes space, switch ball to outside arm" (advanced group).
- **B6:** Constraint game: "**YAC Points**" (4v4): 1 point catch + 1 point if runner keeps 4 points of contact through the run.
- **B7 Exit:** "Name one skill-related component you used today."

**Gamify it:** "Ball Security Shield" — teams earn a shield when **every player** demonstrates "tuck + lock" on the whistle in under 2 seconds.

---

## Week 5 — Handoffs + Running Lanes (Lesson 5)
**Curriculum anchor activities:** Box Drill; Running Lanes
**Cue of the week:** RB: "**Elbow up — let QB place it in your pocket**"

- **B2:** **Box Drill** review (defender has 15 seconds; switch on flag pull).
- **B3:** Handoff clarity (RB cue above; QB focuses on "place firmly").
- **B4:** **Running Lanes** reps: QB calls lane ("1 left", "4 right") + "Set-Hut."
- **B5:** Add defender at the lane entrance (walk pace) forcing correct first step.
- **B6:** Constraint game: "**Lane Bingo**" — score only if the RB hits the called lane *and* shows elbow-up pocket receive.
- **B7 CFU:** "Which side are 5-3-1 vs 2-4-6?"

**Gamify it:** "Playcaller Level-Up" — every time a QB calls the right lane + cadence and the RB executes correctly, the pair earns a "Playcaller token."

---

## Week 6 — Offensive Strategy: Play Action + 4th Down (Lesson 6)
**Curriculum anchor activities:** Strategic Flag Tag 4v4; Play Action Pass; 4th Down (Offense Only)
**Cue of the week:** "**Sell the fake**" (RB/QB acting)

- **B2:** **Strategic Flag Tag 4v4** (quick review; flagged player resets and returns).
- **B3:** Play Action clarity: fake handoff → RB out for pass; cue = "sell the fake."
- **B4:** Play Action reps in groups (rotate roles).
- **B5:** Add a defender "read": defender can only cover RB after the fake (forces convincing fake timing).
- **B6:** **4th Down (Offense Only)**: groups of 4 rotate QB/WR/RB/Center; QB chooses play; 4 plays practiced. *Scoring:* +2 for a clear huddle plan + correct rotation; +1 for a successful "sell the fake."
- **B7 Exit:** "How do you know the strategy worked? What would you change?"

**Gamify it:** "Offensive Coordinator Challenge" — each team earns a "Coordinator Patch" by (1) running 1 pass + 1 run + 1 play action, (2) rotating positions fairly, (3) using one piece of peer feedback (glow/grow).

# Season-long Gamification Layer

### 1) Team XP scoreboard (belonging + reps)
Teams earn **XP** for:
- **Cue reps** (observable): "10 stepped throws," "5 clean pocket handoffs"
- **Teammate help** (Instant Coach job done well)
- **Fast transitions** (cones/balls reset)

### 2) Weekly "badge" tied to the cue
Rookie Combine → Communication Captain → Route Runner → Ball Security Shield → Playcaller → Offensive Coordinator.

### 3) "Constraint bonuses" in Block 6
Make the game reward the learning, not just speed: bonus points only when the cue shows up (step, diamond, lead, lock, elbow up, sell fake).

---

## Differentiation defaults (keep everyone in)
Adjust equipment/targets, simplify instructions, flexible rules, reduce space, peer modeling, and reward effort/participation.
