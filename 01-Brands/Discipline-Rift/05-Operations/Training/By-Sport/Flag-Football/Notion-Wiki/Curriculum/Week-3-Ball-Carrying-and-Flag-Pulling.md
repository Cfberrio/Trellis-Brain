# Week 3 — Ball Carrying + Flag Pulling

**Theme:** *Defense Drives Confidence*
**DR Focus:** one main cue, high reps, fast resets, no sitting

## Cue of the Day (choose ONE)
- **Option A (Defense):** **EYES ON HIPS**
- **Option B (Movement):** **SINK HIPS TO STOP**

**Season Defensive Mantra (say it once, reuse all year):**
> Slow down → Stay between → Pull the flag

## Purpose (coach-facing, not a speech)
- At this age, **defense builds confidence**.
- If we can **stop runners safely and consistently**, everything else grows faster.
- Today we train: **movement control** (start, stop, re-go); **defensive containment** (protect the end zone first); **reliable flag pulling in bounds**.

## Space & Grouping
- Rectangle for tag + games; 2–3 cone lanes for reps; partners + groups of 4
- **Equipment:** cones, flags, pinnies (optional)

---

# DR Practice Spine (60 minutes)

## Block 1 (2–3 min) — Belonging + Routine + Retrieval

**Coach script (tight):**
> "Team in. Week 3 is about **stopping runners**. At this age, **defense builds confidence**. Today's cue is **(EYES ON HIPS / SINK HIPS TO STOP)**. If you're out, you're on a job — **quick job, back in fast**. Let's wake up what we already know."

**30–45s Retrieval Burst (Weeks 1–2):**
- "Week 1 throwing cue?" → **STEP to your target** (quick step-foot point)
- "Week 2 catching cue?" → **Diamond hands** (flash)
- "Before you throw?" → **Name!**
- "Catcher says?" → **BALL!**
- Bridge: "Defense — where are your eyes?" → **HIPS!**

**Norm (pick one):** "Fast resets = more fun." / "We coach cues, not people."
**Micro-CFU:** "What's today's cue?" → choral response

---

## Block 2 (5–7 min) — Retrieval Warm-Up Game: Flag Tag

**Setup:** cone rectangle; everyone wears flags.
**How to play:** everyone moves — chase, flee, dodge. Goal: pull flags **in bounds**. Both flags pulled → **JOB ZONE (10 sec)**.

**Coaching focus (ONLY today's cue):** Eyes on hips *or* Sink hips to stop. Safety: no diving, no contact.

**JOB ZONE (choose ONE job):**
- **Quick Job A (10 sec): Reset Burst** — high knees / fast feet
- **Quick Job B (10 sec): Instant Coach** — tell a teammate the cue + show it

**Micro-CFU (5 sec):** "Where are your eyes?" → "HIPS!"

---

## Block 3 (6–8 min) — Teaching Block: Defensive Principle

**Install the principle (30 seconds):**
> "Protect the end zone first."

**Coach language:** "We don't chase the runner. We **stay between** the runner and the end zone. Angle first. Flag second."

**Cue-specific focus:**
- **Eyes on hips day:** "Hips tell you where they're really going."
- **Sink hips day:** "To stop fast, you bend to brake."

**CFU:** defense cue → point to hips; movement cue → show stopping shape (hips back, knees bent).
**Exit Criteria:** 8/10 athletes show the cue before speed increases.

---

## Block 4 (10–12 min) — Skill Acquisition Reps Engine: Finish-the-Flag Lane

**Setup:** cone lane (5–7 yards); runner goes straight; defender starts 3 yards away.
**Task:** close → stay square → pull flag → freeze.

**Tier System (same drill, different demand):**
- **Tier 1:** runner jogs straight
- **Tier 3:** runner changes speed once
- **Tier 5:** runner gets **one cut** inside the lane

**Coach corrects ONE thing only:** eyes on hips *or* sink hips to stop.
**Micro-CFU:** "Say the cue + show one rep."

**Common fixes:** lunging → "Slow down first." · fly-by → "Stay between." · standing tall → "Sink."

---

## Block 5 (10–12 min) — Teaching Acquisition (Decisions): Angle Tag

**Setup:** runner at cone A; end zone at cone B; defender starts inside.

**Goal:** train **See → Decide → Act** — see hips / first step; decide angle; act: contain → flag pull.

**Add ONE variable only:** wider gates *or* runner starts closer.

**Perception Question (fast):** "What told you which way?" (Expected: hips / first step)
**Micro-CFU:** player answers and points to evidence.

---

## Block 6 (10–15 min) — Application Constraint Game: Stop-the-Run 4v4

**Key reminder (say once):** "This is not about scoring. It's about **stopping the run**."

**Rules:**
- Offense: run-only (or one pass max)
- Defense: **1 point per in-bounds flag pull**
- Bonus point if defender clearly shows the cue
- Offense: 1 point for first down or TD

**Metrics the coach tracks:** in-bounds flag pulls; stayed between runner and end zone; used the cue.
**Between-round CFU:** "Slow down → Stay between → Pull the flag."

---

## Block 7 (2–3 min) — Debrief + Exit Criteria + Spacing

**Meaning tie (10 sec):** "How did defense help our team today?"
**Retrieval question:** "What's today's cue?" · "What comes first — flag or angle?" → "ANGLE!"
**Exit Criteria (observable):** close under control; stay between runner and end zone; pull flags **without diving**.
**Spacing statement:** "We'll hit this again next practice during warm-up."
