# Week 5 — Handoffs + Running Lanes

**Theme:** *Play Fast, Stay Organized*
**DR Focus:** one cue, high reps, fast resets, no sitting (JOB ZONE)

## Cue of the Week (RB cue — 1 cue only)
**"INSIDE ELBOW UP — let QB place it in your pocket."**
(We keep QB coaching minimal: "place it firm.")

**NFL FLAG skill cues (handoff + receive) support this week:**
- QB handoff: turn/face sideline, extend both arms, place ball firmly in RB stomach
- RB receive: step diagonal toward the lane, **inside elbow up / outside elbow down**, let QB place ball in pocket, then tuck and run

## Equipment / Setup
- Cones or spots to mark **running lanes (holes)** and a line of scrimmage
- Flags on everyone
- 4–8 footballs (or foam)
- Optional: hula hoops for scoring targets (Team Touchdown game)

---

# DR Practice Spine (60 minutes)

## Block 1 (3 min) — Belonging + Routine

**Script (tight):** "Team in. Sport is the vehicle. Today we're leveling up **handoffs + running lanes**. Our ONE cue: **INSIDE ELBOW UP — let QB place it in your pocket.** If you're out, you're on a job — quick job, back in fast."

**Micro-CFU:** "Show me 'inside elbow up'… freeze."

---

## Block 2 (8 min) — Retrieval Warm-up (last week's cue + movement)

**Use: Team Flag Tag (curriculum-aligned)**
- Everyone wears flags, two teams, everyone is "it."
- When you pull a flag, sprint it to your "home base" (cone/hoop) and drop it, then rejoin. (No standing.)

**Retrieval cue (from Week 4):** "TUCK + LOCK IT AWAY" on any ball carry (quick reminder only).

**JOB ZONE (10s) when out:** Instant Coach — show a teammate **inside elbow up** (we keep the week's language alive even during warm-up).

*Why this works: small-sided, constant reads/movement, no lines.*

---

## Block 3 (7 min) — Skill Clarity (new cue)

**20–30s demo: "Pocket Receive"** — RB body picture:
1. Step **diagonal toward the lane (hole)**
2. **Inside elbow UP**, outside elbow down
3. **Do NOT reach** — QB places it in the pocket
4. Tuck + run

**Coach only says (every rep):** 👉 "Inside elbow UP… pocket… go!"

**CFU (5 seconds):** "Freeze — show me inside elbow up."

---

## Block 4 (12 min) — Fluency Reps (high reps, low talk)

### Drill A: "Handoff Fit" Reps (pairs)
**Setup:** 1 QB + 1 RB at cones (LOS), lane cone ahead.
**Reps:** 8–10 clean exchanges each, then rotate.
**Constraints to boost success:** start at walking speed; RB *must* show elbow-up pocket before QB can move the ball.
**Coach feedback (1 cue only):** if RB reaches → "Elbow up — pocket still." If exchange is sloppy → shorten distance + slow down (rebuild success).

### Drill B (optional 2-min add-on): "Cadence Freeze"
QB says "Set… Hut!" → RB freezes pocket shape before the handoff happens.

---

## Block 5 (13 min) — Decision Reps (add read/time/defender)

### Drill: Running Lanes (curriculum anchor)
**Setup:** lanes/holes labeled (use the lane diagram concept).

**How it runs:**
- QB calls the lane: "**1 left!**" (or 2/3/4/5/6)
- On "Set-Hut," RB takes **diagonal first step** toward that lane and receives handoff (elbow-up pocket), then runs through the lane.

**Add pressure (light, one variable):**
- Put a **walk-speed defender** at the lane entrance. Defender can only pull flags *after* the handoff (no contact).
- RB decision: hit the lane fast OR bounce one step to the next lane if blocked (advanced group only).

**Coach keeps it to the SAME cue:** 👉 "Inside elbow UP."

---

## Block 6 (14 min) — Application / Constraint Game

Pick one depending on your space.

### Option 1 (most on-theme): Box Drill (curriculum Lesson 5)
**Setup:** "Box" / split field with two sides. Players divided offense/defense.
**Rules:** offense tries to carry into opponent territory; defense can pull flags once offense enters; if RB flag is pulled, RB gives ball to the other team and returns to their side to rejoin. Roles switch after a round.
**Scoring that rewards the cue:** 1 point = clean handoff (coach sees elbow-up pocket) · 1 point = runner hits the called lane · defense earns 1 point for a clean flag pull.

### Option 2 (more "scoreboard fun"): Team Touchdown (small-sided game)
**Setup:** hoops placed left/center/right. End zone hoops worth 6, sideline hoops worth 3.
**Rules:** 2–3 on offense vs 2–3 on defense. Offense runs the ball and **places it into a hoop** to score. Defense pulls flags only in opponent territory.
**Constraint bonus:** scoring only counts if the possession started with a **clean elbow-up pocket** handoff.

**JOB ZONE stays active** (no sitting).

---

## Block 7 (3 min) — Wrap + Exit Ticket

**1 question (fast):** "On a handoff, do we **reach** for the ball or let the QB place it?" Expected: "Let QB place it."
**1 show-me:** "Freeze pocket: inside elbow up."
**1 shout-out tied to cue:** "I saw ___ keep inside elbow up and get a clean pocket — that's why the run was smooth."

# Week 5 Gamification: "Playcaller Level-Up"
Every time a **QB calls the correct lane** and the **RB executes elbow-up pocket receive**, that pair earns a **Playcaller token**. (Simple tally on a clipboard.)

## Extra drill ideas you can rotate in
A quick defense-themed "runner vs sharks" style game as a 2-minute finisher — the sharks-and-minnows concept reinforces staying in bounds + dodging while defenders pull flags.
