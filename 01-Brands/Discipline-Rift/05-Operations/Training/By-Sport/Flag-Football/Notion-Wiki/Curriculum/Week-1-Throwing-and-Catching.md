# Week 1 — Throwing + Catching

**Cue of the Week:** STEP to your target (opposite foot)
**Support Cue:** Eyes on hips (flag pulling)
**Theme:** Discipline creates freedom. Scrimmage is earned.

### Purpose
- Build a repeatable throw: **side → ball to ear → STEP → throw**
- Start catching foundations: **diamond hands (mid/high) + rake hands (low)**

---

# DR Practice Structure (60 minutes)

## Block 1 (3 min) — Belonging + Boundaries + Buy-In

### Step 1 — Introduction (command presence first)
**Coach script (confident, calm, short):**
> "Welcome to DR — Disciplined Rift. I'm Coach ____. This is Coach ____. We're here to help you get better — fast. If you listen and work, you will scrimmage more. If you waste time, scrimmage gets shorter. I don't control the clock. We have 60 minutes. How we use it? That's on us."

*(Pause. Eye contact. No rushing.)*

### Step 2 — Define discipline (20 seconds)
> "Discipline means doing what you need to do — even when you don't feel like it. Sometimes practice is fun. Sometimes it's repetitive. Both make you better. Better players = better scrimmage."

### Step 3 — Communication system (attention callouts)
We do not yell over chaos. We use signals. Pick 1–2 and keep consistent:
- **Option A: Clap Reset** — Coach: *clap-clap* → Players: *clap* → Coach: "Eyes!"
- **Option B: Echo Call** — Coach: "DR!" → Players: "DISCIPLINE!"
- **Option C: If You Can Hear Me** — "If you can hear me, clap once." (layer until silent)

**Teach it once. Practice it once. Expect it always.**

**Standard:** eyes on coach; ball still; mouth closed; body still.
**Language:** "Fast resets = more scrimmage."

### Step 4 — Ground rules (clear + binary)
**Our 4 practice non-negotiables:**
1. When coach talks → you listen
2. No clowning teammates
3. Stay in boundaries
4. Fast resets

**Define boundaries physically:** show field limits, sideline area, Job Zone.

### Step 5 — Scrimmage is earned (make it concrete)
> "Scrimmage is your free time. You want more scrimmage? We move fast. If it takes 10 seconds to get quiet → we keep our time. If it takes 1 minute → we lose 50 seconds of scrimmage."

Make it visible: "We're supposed to scrimmage 20 minutes. If we waste time, it might be 5." Let silence sit. "It's not my choice. It's the clock."

### Step 7 — Sideline etiquette (prevent chaos early)
When off-field: partner pass only; no tackling; no running across drills; stay inside cone boundary.
> "If you're not engaged, you're partner passing. No loose cannons."

*(Note: the source page skips Step 6 — numbering gap is in Notion.)*

### Terminology to introduce (use all season)
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Reset** | Immediate stop + eyes |
| **Job Zone** | Short task area before re-entry |
| **Fast Reset** | Under 5 seconds |
| **Earned Scrimmage** | Time built through focus |
| **DR Standard** | What we expect every rep |

### Micro-CFU (30 sec)
- "What happens if we waste time?"
- "How do we get more scrimmage?"
- "What does discipline mean?"

Players answer in 1 sentence.

### Close Block 1 with authority
> "We don't coach people. We coach cues. We don't waste reps. We earn scrimmage. Let's get better."

## Block 2 (8 min) — Retrieval Warm-Up Game: Flag Tag

**Setup:** cone rectangle boundaries; everyone wears flags.
**How to play:** everyone moves, dodges, flees, chases; players try to pull flags.

**Coaching cue (defense):** **Eyes on hips** · stay **low** and **square** · reach for the **hip**

**"Out" rule (DR Job Zone):** if **both flags** are pulled → **JOB ZONE** (2 cones on sideline)
- **Option A — Quick Job (10–15 sec):** dynamic burst (choose 1): high knees / jumping jacks / mountain climbers. *Re-entry:* clip flags back on → jump right in.
- **Option B — Stay-on Job (30 sec max):** Reset Captain — help one teammate re-clip / return a loose flag. *Re-entry:* say "READY" → back in.

**Micro-CFU (5 sec):** "Where are your eyes when pulling flags?" → "Hips!"
**Safety:** no contact, no diving, two feet down on pulls.

---

## Block 3 (7 min) — Skill Clarity: Throwing (NEW cue)

**Setup:** partners facing each other; start close (5–7 yards), expand only if successful.

**Teach (30 seconds)** — demonstrate once, then everyone mirrors. Throwing checkpoints (say them fast):
- Side to target
- Ball to ear
- **STEP to target (opposite foot)** ✅ *(today's cue)*
- Rotate chest/hips
- Follow through

See [Throwing](../Skills/Throwing.md) for the full mechanics reference.

**Coach script:** "Your **step is your aim**. Step with your **opposite foot** right at your partner."

**CFU (show-me):** "Freeze: show me your **step foot** pointed to your target."

---

## Block 4 (12 min) — Fluency Reps: Partner Poly Pass

**Setup:** 1 ball per pair; two poly spots per pair about **5 yards apart**.

**How to run (3 mini-rounds):**
1. **One-knee throws** (build arm-leg opposition feel) — 4 min
2. **Stand + boxer step** (turn sideways for power) — 4 min
3. **Distance ladder** (1 step back after 2 accurate throws) — 4 min

**Rep goal:** "10 quality throws each."

**Catching reminders (quick, not a new coaching focus):** mid/high = **diamond hands**; low = **rake hands**; catch → **squeeze** → tuck it.

**Micro-CFU (10 sec):** "Why do we turn sideways?" (power/aim) · "What does arm-leg opposition mean?" (opposite foot steps)

---

## Block 5 (13 min) — Decision Reps: "Call the Catch"

Same partners, same spots — now add a tiny decision.

- Thrower calls **LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH** before throwing
- Receiver must show the correct hands: LOW = **rake**; MED/HIGH = **diamond**
- Switch roles every 3 throws

**Coach focus stays the same:** **STEP to your target** (don't add more throw cues)
**Success criteria:** thrower steps to target; receiver shows correct hands + squeezes.

---

## Block 6 (14 min) — Application Game: Mascot Ball (groups of 4)

**Setup:** groups of **4**; **QB in the middle**, 3 receivers around (triangle).

**How to play:** QB throws to a receiver → receiver catches with correct hands, **squeezes**, **tucks it away**, and yells the mascot/team word → receiver throws back to QB → continue until **all 3 receivers** catch → rotate QB each round.

**Scoring (gamified + cue-based):**
- **Touchdown = 3/3 catches** in a round
- **Bonus point** if QB shows **STEP to target** on every throw that round

**"Out" rule (DR Job Zone):** if a pass is dropped or off-target → **JOB ZONE** for **10 seconds**
- **Quick Job (primary): Instant Coach** — tell the next thrower "**STEP to your target**" + quick demo step. *Re-entry:* done → back in on next turn.
- **Backup Quick Job: Catch Hands Demo** — show **diamond** and **rake** hands once each. *Re-entry:* done → back in.

**Coach script:** "JOB! Coach the cue. Back in fast."

---

## Block 7 (3 min) — Wrap + Exit Ticket

**Exit ticket (fast):**
- "Freeze and show me: **STEP to your target**."
- Partner share: "Tell 2 throw cues and 2 catch cues."

**Shout-out:** "I saw ___ step to target and ___ use diamond/rake hands the right way."

# Week 1 Gamification — Mascot Touchdown Tracker

Write group names on the board.
- +1 for every Mascot Ball touchdown (3/3 catches)
- +1 bonus for a "perfect step round" (QB stepped on every throw)

**Optional personal challenge:** "Earn your Rookie stamp when you hit **10 step throws** + **10 catches**."

---

# Differentiation

**Easier** — foam ball, shorter distance, slower throws; receivers closer to QB in Mascot Ball; allow a "bounce catch" on low throws at first.
**Harder** — increase distance; require receiver to move 2 steps to a cone before catching; bonus point only if catch is clean + immediate tuck.

# Safety + Belonging
- No contact, no diving
- Celebrate effort + improvement
- "We coach cues, not people."
