# Week 2 — Catching + Communication

**DR Focus:** one clear catching cue + tons of catches + communication baked into every rep + "out" = quick job (no sitting)

**Cue of the Week (choose ONE):**
- **Option A (recommended):** **"Diamond hands at chest"** (medium/high catches)
- **Option B (if lots of low throws):** **"Rake hands low"** (scoop the ball)

**Culture line (say it once, then run):** "If you're out, you're on a job. Quick job = back in fast."

### Equipment
- 10–12 footballs/foam balls
- Cones/poly spots (12–20)
- Flags/belts (for Touchdown 4v4)
- Whiteboard/clipboard for team points ("Communication Captain")

### Space & Grouping
- Warm-up space: open rectangle
- Skill work: partners (1 ball per pair if possible)
- Game: 4v4 (6 teams of 4) or 3v3 if tight

---

# DR Practice Spine (60 minutes)

## Block 1 (3 min) — Belonging + Routine

**Coach script:** "Today we're leveling up catching + communication. Our cue is **(DIAMOND hands at chest / RAKE hands low)**. The best teams talk: name first, then the cue."
**Norm:** "We build each other up. Fast resets."

---

## Block 2 (8 min) — Retrieval Warm-Up: Hot Potato

**Goal:** quick hands + quick talk

**Setup:** partners 5–7 yards apart (or small circles of 4 for more energy); 1 ball per pair/circle.

**How to play:** on "GO," toss and catch quickly (soft throws). Work in **20-second rounds**, then freeze for a 10-second coaching point.

**Communication rule (starts now):** thrower says **receiver's name**; receiver calls **"BALL!"**

**"Out" rule (if drop happens):** drop = **JOB ZONE (10 seconds)**
- **Quick Job (primary): Instant Coach** → tell your partner the cue ("Diamond hands!") + show the hand shape. *Re-entry:* done → right back in.

**Micro-CFU (5 sec):** "What do you say before you throw?" → "Name!" · "What does the catcher say?" → "BALL!"

---

## Block 3 (7 min) — Skill Clarity: Catching Cue (ONE cue only)

Pick **ONE** for the whole practice.

**Option A: Diamond Hands (medium/high)** — teach in 20 seconds:
- "Show me a **diamond**." (thumbs and index fingers)
- "Hands out front, **at chest**, watch it in."
- "Catch → **squeeze** → tuck."

**Option B: Rake Hands (low)** — teach in 20 seconds:
- "Hands low like a **scoop**."
- "Pinkies close, thumbs out."
- "Catch → squeeze → tuck."

**CFU (show-me):** "Freeze — show me your hands for a **(medium/high OR low)** catch."

---

## Block 4 (12 min) — Fluency Reps: Catching Low / Medium / High Ladder

**Goal:** lots of successful catches at different levels
**Setup:** partners on spots 5 yards apart; 1 ball per pair.

**How to run (3 rounds × 4 minutes):**
1. **LOW round** (below waist) — rake/scoop or "hands ready" depending on your cue
2. **MEDIUM round** (chest) — **diamond hands** if that's your cue
3. **HIGH round** (above chest/face) — **diamond hands** + soft throws

**Ladder rule (game feel):** 3 catches in a row → both take **one step back**. 2 drops in a row → **one step in**.

**Coach focus:** only correct the **cue of the week** + "soft throw."

**Micro-CFU (10 sec):** "When do you use diamond hands?" (medium/high) · "When do you use rake hands?" (low)

---

## Block 5 (13 min) — Decision + Communication Reps: Name + Ball Challenge

Same partners, now add pressure (still lots of reps).

- Thrower calls: **"[Name]… READY!"**
- Receiver answers: **"BALL!"** and shows target hands
- Thrower chooses the level (low/medium/high) and throws softly

**Scoring (per pair):** 1 point = catch with correct hands · +1 point = perfect talk (name + ball) · first to **10 points** wins, then switch partners.

**"Out" rule (drops):** drop = **JOB ZONE 10 seconds**
- **Stay-on Job (30 sec max): Target Scanner** → stand at side and call "LOW / MED / HIGH" for your partner's next throw twice correctly. Rejoin immediately.

---

## Block 6 (14 min) — Application Game: Touchdown 4v4

**Goal:** communication + teamwork under game speed
**Setup:** teams of 4 with flags on; small field with an end zone.

**How to play:** offense huddles quickly and chooses a plan (who might have the ball / who's the decoy). On "GO," offense tries to get into the end zone. Defense pulls flags to stop progress. Once someone reaches the end zone (or play ends), offense reveals who had the ball.

**Scoring (gamified for Week 2):**
- **1 point** = touchdown
- **+1 Communication Point** if the offense uses a clear huddle plan AND you hear **name + ball** at least 3 times in the play

**"Out" rule (flag pulled / stopped / drop)** — JOB ZONE, choose one and keep it consistent:
- **Quick Job (10 sec): Instant Coach** → give a teammate the cue ("Diamond hands!") + show it. *Re-entry:* done → back in next play.
- **Stay-on Job (1 play): Ref + CFU Collector** → answer "Did we use name + ball?" *Re-entry:* correct answer → back in.

**Coach script:** "JOB! Coach the cue. Back in on the whistle."

---

## Block 7 (3 min) — Wrap + Exit Ticket

1. "Show me **diamond hands** (or rake hands)."
2. "Why do our hands change low vs high?" (because the ball arrives at different levels)

**Shout-out:** "I heard ___ use name + ball, and I saw ___ use the right hands."

---

# Week 2 Gamification: "Communication Captain"

**Team bonus goal:** every player completes **3 perfect talks** in practice — thrower says **name**, receiver calls **"BALL!"**

**Easy tracking:** put team names on the board. Each time you hear **5 perfect talks** from a team, give them **1 Captain Point**. Winning team earns the title **Communication Captains** (and leads the cheer/line-up next week).

---

## Differentiation

**Easier** — foam ball, closer distance, slower tosses; allow "body catch" for first 2 minutes, then back to hands; use circles of 4 for Hot Potato (short throws).
**Harder** — add a defender "shadow" (walk pace, no steals) during Block 5; require "catch → squeeze → tuck" before the next throw counts.
