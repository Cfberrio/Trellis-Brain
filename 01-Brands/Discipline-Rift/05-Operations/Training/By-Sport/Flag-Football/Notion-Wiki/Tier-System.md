# TIER SYSTEM

## Tier 1 — Ready

**What players can do**
- Stand in a ready athletic position.
- Move on signal without freezing.
- Hold the ball correctly and attempt basic throws or catches.
- Run, stop, and change direction without losing balance.

**Core skills** — Ready stance (knees bent, eyes up); basic carry position; two-hand catch attempts; simple run and stop.

**Coaching cues** — "Eyes up." / "Hands ready." / "Freeze feet, then go."

**Common errors + fixes** — Freezing → "Small steps first." Looking away → "Watch it in." Dropping ball → "Two hands first."

**CFU** — 3 clean reps in a row with no stop.

**Cool Joes vs Cool Jess** — Joes: follow the coach and copy. Jess: explain what "ready" looks like.

---

## Tier 2 — Control

**What players can do**
- Pass and catch under no pressure.
- Carry the ball while moving.
- Pull flags safely at slow speed.
- Understand basic rules and boundaries.

**Core skills** — Step-to-throw; secure catch then run; flag pull with eyes on hips; calling "Mine!" on catches.

**Coaching cues** — "Step to target." / "Squeeze then tuck." / "Eyes on hips."

**Common errors + fixes** — Rushing throws → "Slow feet, smooth arm." Reaching for flags → "Get close first."

**CFU** — 5 successful reps with one simple constraint (distance or time).

**Cool Joes vs Cool Jess** — Joes: short distance, no defenders. Jess: add light shadow defender.

---

## Tier 3 — Connector

**What players can do**
- Catch and throw while moving.
- Create space to get open.
- Transition from catch to run.
- Defend space, not just chase.

**Core skills** — Catch on the move; simple lead passes; speed change to get open; angle pursuit on defense.

**Coaching cues** — "Show hands." / "Run away from the crowd." / "Take the angle."

**Common errors + fixes** — Standing still → "Move first." Forcing throws → "Find the easy one."

**CFU** — Correct choice 3 out of 5 reps.

**Cool Joes vs Cool Jess** — Joes: two clear options. Jess: three options, faster reps.

---

## Tier 4 — Flow

**What players can do**
- Play in continuous 2- or 3-touch sequences.
- Reset quickly after mistakes.
- Use basic overhand throws consistently.
- Understand scoring and possession.

**Core skills** — Pass → move → receive again; quick decision after catch; defensive spacing and recovery; score awareness (where/when).

**Coaching cues** — "Catch, decide, go." / "Next play." / "Help your teammate."

**Common errors + fixes** — Stopping after mistake → "Reset fast." Holding ball too long → "One look, then move."

**CFU** — Complete a clean 3-touch flow without stopping.

**Cool Joes vs Cool Jess** — Mostly Jess. Advanced Joes only with heavy structure.

---

## Tier 5 — Strategist

**What players can do**
- Use power and placement intentionally.
- Anticipate defenders.
- Support teammates off the ball.
- Adjust play based on the situation.

**Core skills** — Strong throws or runs with purpose; defensive reads; coverage and support positioning; simple game strategy awareness.

**Coaching cues** — "What did you see?" / "Where's the space?" / "Protect the middle."

**Common errors + fixes** — Hero plays → "Take the free one." Ball watching → "Find your help spot."

**CFU** — Explains the choice made after the rep.

**Cool Joes vs Cool Jess** — Jess focus tier. Joes rarely reach this level.

---

## Tier 6 — Leader

**What players can do**
- Organize teammates.
- Communicate clearly.
- Adjust strategy mid-play.
- Model effort and composure.

**Core skills** — Calling resets; leading spacing; consistent serve/throw reliability; peer support and correction.

**Coaching cues** — "Say it early." / "Lead with actions." / "Fix it fast."

**Common errors + fixes** — Over-talking → "One cue, then play." Controlling others → "Guide, don't boss."

**CFU** — Peer-led rep that improves team outcome.

**Cool Joes vs Cool Jess** — Almost exclusively Cool Jess. Rare and celebrated.

---

## How this ladder is used
- Players **move between tiers by behavior**, not age.
- Different skills can sit in **different tiers at the same time**.
- Coaches teach **toward the next tier**, not the top.
