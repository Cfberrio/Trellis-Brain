# PRACTICE DESIGN — FOR YOU

## DR Practice Write-Up Template (use this exact structure each week)

### 1) Week of Practice
- Week #: 0 / 6
- Age band version: **6–8 / 9–10 / 11–12**
- Practice length: 60 min (or chosen range)
- Equipment assumptions (minimal list)
- Safety priority of the day (collision risk / stopping mechanics / spacing)
- Coach roles for the day (if multiple coaches): Lead / Station / Culture

---

### 2) DR Identity Anchors (short + repeatable)

**A) DR Meaning (15-second script)**
- "DR = Discipline Rift."
- "Discipline = the power to commit to a decision and follow through."

**B) Word of the Week (life lesson)**
- Word: ___
- One-sentence definition: ___
- One behavior we'll practice today: ___

**C) Inclusion Promise (the DR standard)**
- "Everyone gets coached."
- "Everyone gets reps."
- "Everyone belongs."

*(This section replaces long introductions with a repeatable weekly identity block.)*

---

### 3) Weekly Outcomes (tight, coach-usable)

**A) Skill outcomes (1–2 only)** — Primary skill domain: ___ / Secondary support skill (optional): ___
**B) Decision outcome (1 only)** — "Players will learn to **see** ___, **decide** ___, then **act** ___."
**C) Culture + Inclusion outcome (1 only)** — Inclusion focus: ___ (e.g. "everyone gets touches," "everyone takes turns leading," "everyone gets a coaching moment")
**D) Effort norm of the day (1 only)** — the one behavior you will praise all practice: ___ (e.g. "fast reset," "help your teammate," "eyes up," "next rep mindset")

---

### 4) Skill Acquisition Map Links (A)
For this week's primary domain, include:
- Micro-skills list (tiny teachables)
- Step ladder (easy → harder)
- 1–2 cues per step
- Common errors + quick fixes
- "Ready to level up" check (observable)
- Safety note (if relevant)

**Author rule (non-negotiable):** every station and every game must map to a micro-skill step on the ladder.

---

### 5) Deep Practice Rules for This Week (B) — "How We Run Reps"
- Station timing + rotation (how long + how to rotate)
- Line rule (max line length or max wait time)
- Constraint rule (1–2 focus points only)
- Feedback rule (1 cue → immediate retry)
- Self-check / peer-check option (age-appropriate)
- Scaling rule (beginner vs advanced inside the same station)
- Inclusion rule (no elimination; everyone stays active)
