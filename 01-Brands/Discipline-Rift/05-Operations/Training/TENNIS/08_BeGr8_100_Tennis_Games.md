# BeGr8 - 100 Tennis Games

Fun Tennis Games for Coaching & Camps

No. Game Name Description

FUN WARM UP & CHASING GAMES

1 Pacman - The Pacman starts at the net with the other players (ghosts) starting at the baseline. Pacman has 2 minutes to try and catch all the ghosts. If a ghost is caught, they go down on one knee and the other ghosts can't pass them.

2 Sharks & Fishes - The Shark (chaser) starts at the net with the Fish (other players) starting at the baseline. The Shark has 2 minutes to try and catch as many fish as possible. If a fish is caught, they have to go into the shark's pit and can only be set free by another fish. If all fish are caught and in the pit, then the Shark wins.

3 Lines of the Court - Each player gets 3 lives. The coach calls out a line of the court and the players have to run to it. The last player to get to a line loses a life. When there are 2 players left the first person to the line is the winner.

4 Snatch the Bacon - Players are divided into 2 teams. Each player gets a number. The coach calls out a number. The players run to the opposite inside tramline, then back to their inside tramline and then go and get the ball. First team to 7 points wins.

5 Corner Run - This chasing game can be over 2 or 3 courts. One team starts in one corner and the other in the corner furthest away. The goal is to catch one member of the other team by chasing them around the outside of the courts.

6 Racquet Worm - When the coach says START, the first player in line places the racquet on the floor from the baseline, then runs back to high five the next teammate, who places their racquet in front of the last, forming a "Racquet worm".

7 Mountains & Valleys - Also called "Cups and Saucers" or "Cone Flip". Cones are placed up or down on the court; a player or team turns them all one way within a time limit.

8 Tidy / Messy - Similar to Mountains and Valleys but using ball cans, turning them upright or upside down within a time limit.

9 Catch the Middle - Players switch tennis balls from one cone to another before running to grab the ball in the middle of the court. First to the middle ball wins. Can be played in teams.

10 Cone Ball - Players start next to racquets on the floor. Cones spread around the court have balls hidden in some. Players run to flip one cone at a time, returning to touch their racquet before flipping the next. Most balls collected wins.

11 Stealer - Steal tennis balls from other players without letting the ball drop from your racquet.

12 Tiger Tail - Each player tucks a ribbon/fabric "tail" in the back of their pants; others try to grab it.

FUN ABC'S GAMES

13 Snowballs - Coach starts with one less ball on their racquet than players. After launching the balls, players run to get one; miss out and you're out. Last remaining ball wins it for whoever grabs it.

14 Snowballs V2 - Two teams throw balls overarm onto the opponent's side; fewest balls on your side at time's up wins.

15 Flip the Hoop - Roll a ball toward a hoop; if it goes in, flip the hoop over. First to get their hoop past the center service line wins.

16 React - Coach calls body parts to touch, then calls "Ball" - first to react and grab the ball scores.

17 Circle Racquet Catch - Players in a circle balance a racquet on one finger; on "3,2,1,GO" they move clockwise to catch the next racquet before it falls.

18 Spin the Hoop - Spin a hula hoop on the ground without it falling while placing a cone from one hoop to another.

19 Eggs in the Basket - Bring balls one at a time from the middle to your own racquet; once the middle is empty, steal from others. Most balls wins.

20 Cone Feet - Jump with a cone between your feet toward a hoop on the ground.

21 Bullseye - Take turns throwing a ball into a bucket; first to 10 points wins.

22 Crossy Road - Coach rolls a ball across the court; players try to hit it with theirs. First to 3 points wins.

23 Precision - Kneeling behind the baseline, roll a ball toward the net; closest without touching it scores. First to 5 wins.

24 Hoop Challenge - Roll balls toward hoops: 1 point yellow, 2 points red, 3 points blue. First to 15 wins.

25 Mini Tennis Rolling Challenge - Roll the ball between each set of cones and retrieve it quickly; first through all 4 sets wins.

26 Send and Receive - Throw the ball underarm into the opponent's cone-marked circle; progress to throwing and hitting.

27 Collision - Pairs roll balls simultaneously from their tramlines trying to make them collide; first to 20 collisions wins.

28 Caterpillar - Coach rolls a ball to different areas; players move together to let it pass through their legs. Touch it or miss it and lose a life (3 lives each).

29 Middle Ball - A large rubber ball sits mid-court; players throw tennis balls from the tramlines to roll it to the opponent's side.

30 Pyramid - Knock down cones stacked in a pyramid; most knocked down in the time limit wins.

31 Tic Tac Toe - Get 3 cones/bibs of the same colour in a row.

32 Mini Tennis Race - Coordination race to hit the Red, Orange, Green & Yellow balls first, with forehand, backhand, or hand-rolled ball.

33 Mini Tennis ABC - Coach calls a colour; players run to that ball and perform an exercise, first 3 to finish and shout "Stop" score.

34 Agile Escape - Players face off at the tramlines; goal is to get past the opponent within the tramlines.

35 Tennis Hockey - Two teams roll a ball along the ground through gaps in the opposing line to score a goal.

36 Reaction Ball - Play points in the service boxes with a reaction/agility ball; catch after one bounce for a point. First to 10 wins.

37 Royal Court - Coach feeds a high ball; catch it after one bounce for a point and place a ball on your team's racquet. Most balls on the racquet wins.

38 Racquet Catch - Like Circle Racquet Catch but played in pairs facing each other.

39 Nose Run - Crawl from the baseline to the service line while rolling the ball with your nose.

40 Floor Tennis - Hit a large rubber ball along the ground with your racquet toward cone "goals" to score.

41 Statues - One player/coach at the net, others at the baseline try to tip the net without being caught moving or dropping the ball.

FUN POINTS GAMES

42 Around the World - 3 lives each; after each shot players run around the net. Miss the return and lose a life. Final two play up to 3 points without running around the net.

43 The Racquet Game - Two teams; after each shot players rotate. Miss the ball and lose your racquet, passing it up the line. Last racquet lost with a miss eliminates the team.

44 Play to 21 - Two teams play out a point; winner scores and stays on for one more point before rotating. First to 21 wins.

45 Graveyard (aka Sick, Dying, Dead) - 3 lives each; get the ball over to rejoin the line, lose all lives and you must catch the ball from the feeder's side to get back in.

46 King / Queen of the Court - Win 2 points in a row to become King/Queen; first to 10 points as ruler wins.

47 Volley Beat the Champ - Champ at net, others feed from baseline in half-court, no lobs; win 2 in a row to become champ. First to 10 wins.

48 Beat the Champ Over 2 Courts - Same as above, over two courts.

49 Serve Challenge - 10 balls on the service line each; land a serve to take one of your own balls, miss and take one from a basket instead. First to empty their service-line balls wins.

50 The Dropshot Game - Coach feeds a dropshot; reaching it and returning it in play scores a team point. First to 15 wins.

51 The Lob Game - Coach lobs over the player's head; must let it bounce before returning. In-play return scores a team point.

52 Touch Boxes V1 - Play out points in the service boxes, no hard shots, 10 lives each; can be 4 players/1 ball.

53 Touch Boxes V2 - Ball starts on the net, tapped off to either side then played out in the boxes, no hard shots, 10 lives each.

54 Self Rally Challenge - Each player self-rallies a forehand trying to move their opponent; win by double bounce, ball out of box, or a shot below head height. First to 10 wins.

55 Net Roller - Aim to clip the top of the net and have it go over: +1 point; hit the net without going over: -1 point. First to 5 wins.

56 All Touch Volleyball Tennis - Large-group team game (8-20 players); every teammate must touch the ball before it's hit over the net, no hard hits, no bounce.

57 Kill it! - Fun 8-16 player drill on one court fostering communication and control.

58 Djokovic Ball - Service box points where every shot must be volleyed into the ground on your own side before crossing the net; land it in the opponent's box to win the point.

59 Doubles Scramble - Like Touch Boxes but you must pass to your partner before the ball crosses the net.

60 Bump - One-on-one points in mini/half/full court; win and move up a court, lose and move down.

61 Wipe Out - Advanced; 3 players per side play out the point, rotating with more players.

62 Four Square - Four players in the service boxes can also hit into a partner's adjacent box; one bounce off the net keeps the point alive.

63 Beach Ball Tennis - Play points hitting a large light beach ball over the net, giving more time to get into position.

64 Baseball Tennis - Improves footwork/ball judgment; thrower must throw within 3 seconds, max 3 steps; beginners may block and pick up before throwing over.

65 Grip Game - In the service boxes, bump the ball to yourself with the strings, then the next shot must be hit over the net with the grip of the racquet.

66 Bungee - Small drop-line square; hit the ball down into the square with your hand, miss and the opponent scores.

67 Double Racquet - Each player holds a racquet in each hand and plays out points with either. First to 5 or 10 wins.

68 Hit and Move - 4 discs in a square on each end; after hitting, move clockwise to the next disc and continue the point.

69 Throw and Play - Racquet placed on the baseline; throw the ball overarm in a service action, quickly grab the racquet, then play out the point.

70 Racquet Game V2 - Like The Racquet Game but the whole team shares one racquet, passed to the next player after each shot.

71 Bobsleigh - Two teams; players face off in rallies, losers sit near the net forming a "bobsleigh" line. Last standing player wins for their team.

72 Head to Head Serve Game - Cones placed in the service box as serve targets; first to knock down all cones wins (individually or by team).

73 Horse Serving Game - Service box split into T/Body/Wide zones by drop lines; server calls their target zone, next player must match it or gets a letter toward "HORSE".

74 Two Touch Volley Game - Bump the ball to yourself before volleying it over the net in the service box; straight or crosscourt.

75 Balloon Tennis - Hit a balloon over a net with your hand (or racquet) to win points.

76 Alternate - 2 teams of 3 on half court without the alley; coach always feeds the same team first each set, then players must alternate hitting one ball each during the rally.

77 The Doubles Game / Chicken - Challengers feed to champs at baseline or net; win at net and stay, lose and the other team takes the net. First team to 10 wins.

FUN RELAY RACE GAMES

78 Slalom Run - Fun warm-up combining tennis coordination drills; fastest team to complete every task correctly wins.

79 Tip the Lines - Teams start at the outside tramline; run to tip the far tramline with your foot and run back.

80 Sidestep and Run - Sidestep to the centre line and back, then sprint to the far tramline and back before the next player goes.

81 Cone Ball (relay) - Run to a middle cone, take the ball, place it on a cone on the far tramline, run back to your line.

82 Mini Tennis Disc Race - Hop onto discs placed in the tramlines in a set order before the next teammate goes.

83 Bean Bag on Head - Walk/run around a cone with a bean bag balanced on your head without dropping it.

84 Bounce - Bounce a tennis ball with your hand in and around a row of cones.

85 Switch - Move one tennis ball from one hoop to another and run back to your team.

86 Switch V2 - Move all the tennis balls from one hoop to another before running back.

87 Hoops - Move a ball from the first hoop to the second, tip a cone, repeat on the way back, then tag a teammate.

88 All Fours - Crawl while rolling a ball with your hands around a cone, then stand and run back.

89 Sidestep Bounce - Sidestep while bouncing a ball around a cone and back to your team.

90 Plank - Get into a plank position to move a ball from one cone to an empty cone before returning.

91 Racquet Bounce - Bounce a ball on your strings from the baseline to the net and back; variations include weaving cones or balancing the ball.

92 Roll Ball with Racquet - Roll the ball with your racquet in and around cones and back.

93 Tennis Knees - Hold a ball between your thighs just above the knees and hop with both feet together without dropping it.

94 Potato Race - Run from the baseline, take a ball from the net, get it back to the service line/over the net, then return to the baseline and repeat.

95 Throw into Cone - Run a ball to a nearby cone and throw it overarm into the cone.

96 Hoop Race - Run to place a hula hoop over a teammate's head; first team to fit 10 hoops wins.

96b Tennis Ball Relay - Move balls into and out of cones using varied locomotion (running, skipping, lateral shuffle), switching hands as desired.

97 Cone Relay Race - Run around a cone, pick it up, stack it at the start while tagging the next teammate; first team back and seated with all cones wins.

98 Bean Bag into Hoop - Take a bean bag from a filled hoop, run to the next cone, throw it underarm into the next hoop.

98b Pass the Ball - Line up with racquets and pass the tennis ball from one racquet to the next without dropping it.

100 Hurdle - Split-step over a mini hurdle, slalom through cones, then grab a different coloured cone from the end of the court.
