## Session 1: Being a Coach
*Presenter: Coach Andy · 15 min*

**Coach Andy — Intro**
- Started playing tennis since 2007
- Coaching tennis since 2018
- Reflection prompt: How has coaching others impacted my personal life?

**Being a Coach** — What does it mean to be a coach?

**Origin of the Word**
- Comes from the name of a Hungarian town called "Kocsi"
- Kocsis people made wagons for transportation; in the 1500s "Kocsi" entered Britain and France as "Coach"
- Nowadays a "coach" is known as a bus that carries passengers

**To Take You Somewhere**
- From the origin of the word coach — just like the wagons and buses
- We have the responsibility of taking the players somewhere

**Coach Bland (cautionary example)**
- Little engagement, minimal communication, lacked a connection with his coaching style
- Discussion prompt: Do you think "Coach Bland" took me somewhere?

**Three-Part Definition of Coaching**
1. **To take you somewhere**
2. **You need to go** — Every coach will take you somewhere; the difference is WHERE a coach takes you. A great coach makes sure you feel comfortable along the way. Impact starts from genuine care.
3. **When you can't get there yourself** — No one can reach their dreams and goals alone, because we all need people — that's what being a coach is all about. (Discussion: Was Michael Jordan's coach a better basketball player than he is?)

**Conclusion**
- Only 38% of kids ages 5–11 stick to the sport they currently practice
- Statistically, only 6/20 players in a class will stick with the sport
- Prompt: What are you going to do about the 14 players who never touch a racquet or volleyball again?
- By caring, we'll make a bigger impact than we can imagine

**Closing Line:** *"Halfway is nowhere." — Thank you, team!*

## Session 2: The Hand of a Coach + DR Fundamentals
*Presenter: Coach Luis · 15 min*

**Coach Luis — Intro**
- Around sports since age 4; coaching tennis since 2019
- Realized the impact a sports lifestyle made in his life and decided to take it further

**The Hand of a Coach**
- A simple concept to make the coaching journey smooth
- A tool for all domains of life to help us grow; levels up our leadership as coaches
- Slide references a 5-finger framework — Pointer, Middle, Ring, Pinky, Thumb — filled in live during training

> **Gap flagged:** The specific meaning of each finger in "The Hand of a Coach" is never written down in any source file — it was spoken/live content only. Worth recovering from a coach's memory or a training recording if one exists.

**Locus of Control** (psychological concept)
- Explanation or cause of events that take place
- **Internal Locus of Control:** Outcome is on you
- **External Locus of Control:** Outcome is on external factors

**Empathetic Feedback**
- Encouraging feedback paired with a strategy to push players forward
- One-on-one approach: **Sandwich Method**
- Build emotional trust with empathy, slowly but surely

**Recognition**
- Being intentional about recognizing input from others — from players to coaches
- Prioritize the well-being of others above your own interests

**Negotiation** — Without the ability to negotiate, great leadership doesn't exist

**Psychological Safety**
- The "safe environment" concept — for players to be themselves, it starts with the coach
- Allows players to take risks and express their own opinions

**Our Formula / Our Method: "FUELED"**
- As the DR Team, something is different about how we do things — we have "it"
- Our way is being **FUELED** (full acronym breakdown appears in [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/05-Operations/Training/Shared-Coaching-Resources/DR-Annual-Staff-Training/DR-Staff-Training-2024|2024]])

**Closing — "Right Here" (2023)**
- Together we have the resources we need
- Collaborating as a team, we grow with and from each other
- Right here we can make a significant difference — Thank you, team!

## Session 5: Values & Applications
*Presenter: Coach Jason · 15 min*

**Coach Jason — Intro**
- Playing volleyball since 2016; coaching volleyball for 1 year
- Reflection prompt: How has coaching others influenced me?

**Core Values & How to Apply Them**

- **Discipline** — Steady progress along with accountability in spite of difficulties. *Apply it: Coming to practice even on the hardest days.*
- **Leadership** — Inspiring role model; elevating skills and personal growth. *Apply it: A player leads the setup of equipment and guides others.*
- **Respect** — Recognize team roles; value each person's significance. *Apply it: When players argue about their spot in line, redirect to respecting and honoring others on the team.*
- **Sportsmanship** — Positive support, fostering team unity and energy. *Apply it: Whether winning or losing a game, be a good sport.*
- **Team Work** — Playing for each other; shared goals and values. *Apply it: During drills and games, explain how players can play as a team.*
- **Accountability** — Take responsibility for our actions and other players' actions in a supportive way. *Apply it: Help each other improve after a loss and celebrate together after a win.*

**Closing — Remember (2023)**
- Find small windows and opportunities to plant the seed of a value
- Be the best first impression of the sport — Thank you, team!
