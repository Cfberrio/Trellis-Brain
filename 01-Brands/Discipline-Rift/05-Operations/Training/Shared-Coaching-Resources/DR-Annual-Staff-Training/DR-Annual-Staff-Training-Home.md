## Purpose
This is the cross-sport **coach onboarding / culture curriculum** — the annual all-staff training days (2023, 2024, 2025), not sport-specific skill curriculum. It sits at the coaching-quality and identity layer alongside the other Shared Coaching Resources, and should inform every sport's coach behavior, not just one program.

Split into one file per year because each year's content is substantial on its own. Start here, then open the year you need.

Source: Google Drive folder ["DR TRAININGS"](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/14TMgxG9kX4oMh-Xa9Gh1prBYqzJZvAOu) — 2023, 2024, and 2025 subfolders, converted from Google Slides/Docs and PPTX/DOCX.

## 📁 Organization Rules (how each year file is structured)

1. **Agenda/run-of-show first.** Each year opens with its event agenda/run-of-show before session content.
2. **One section per distinct session/topic**, in the order presented on the training day (not upload order). Where a source deck bundled multiple sessions into one file (e.g., "FALL TRAINING 2023"), it's split into per-session sections rather than duplicated.
3. **Supplemental material** (icebreakers, worksheets, handouts, games) is called out separately from core session content.
4. **Provenance preserved.** Every section states Year, Presenter(s), source Drive file title(s), and duration if known.
5. **Gaps flagged inline**, never silently dropped. When source material references something never actually defined in writing (e.g., "The Hand of a Coach" 5-finger framework is *named* but never filled in), it's marked with a `> **Gap flagged:**` callout.
6. **Non-text assets (video/photo) get an honest status note**, not a fabricated transcript. 2025's deck/doc only cover Sessions 1–3; Sessions 4–5 exist only as edited video, so that gap is documented rather than invented.

## 🔗 Cross-Year Themes & Recurring Pillars

These throughlines repeat across years — useful if building a master curriculum later:

- **Coaching identity & purpose** — "Being a Coach" (2023 Session 1), "Coach → Player" / "Row the Boat" (2024)
- **The DR Method: FUELED** — Friendly, Upfront, Enthusiastic, Leader, Efficient, Driven — named in 2023 (Session 2) without the acronym spelled out, then fully defined in 2024 and repeated in 2025. This is the throughline value system across all years.
- **Talent development mechanics** — the M+/M-/M+ demonstration-imitation-correction pattern, "irresistible environment," deep practice (2024, echoed in 2025 session titles)
- **Team expectations & operations** — logistics-facing content (2023 Session 3)
- **Group dynamics / classroom management** — 2023 Session 4
- **Core values & their in-practice application** — 2023 Session 5, 2024 "Coach → Player"
- **External reference material** — "Conscious Coaching" book notes (2024 FORMAT doc)
- **Etymology of "Coach"** (Hungarian *kocsi* → carriage → bus → transport → "takes you where you can't get alone") appears in both 2023 Session 1 and 2025 Session 2 — the throughline origin story for the org's entire coaching philosophy
- **1%-improvement / compounding-growth philosophy** — new in 2025 (Session 2's "ONE HUNDRED 1% Solutions"), but conceptually a direct extension of 2024's M+/M-/M+ deep-practice pattern
- **Gap resolved 2026-08-11:** "The Hand of a Coach" 5-finger framework was previously flagged here as never captured in writing. That is true of the Drive sources but not of the org overall — it is written up in the DR Notion wiki page "THE HAND OF A COACH": **Locus of Control · Empathetic Feedback · Recognition · Negotiation · Psychological Safety**. Notion is the source of truth for this framework; the Drive decks name it without defining it.
