Cross-sport coaching books, imported as full-text markdown so they are searchable in the vault and usable as AI source material. These shape **how a DR coach coaches** — not what drills a specific sport runs. Sport-specific books stay in each sport's own `Books` folder.

| **What standard** does a professional coach hold? | Becoming a Coach |

## Reading order for a new DR coach
1. **Every Moment Matters** — set the why and the youth-sports frame first.
2. **Conscious Coaching** — learn to read the athlete before trying to teach them.
3. **The Coach's Guide to Teaching** — build sessions that actually transfer to game day.
4. **Becoming a Coach** — hold the professional standard: ethics, mindset, presence, growth.

## Shared themes to pull into DR training
- **Coach the person, not the sport** — every book lands here from a different angle.
- **Feedback must be fast, focused, and solution-oriented** — one point at a time, used immediately.
- **Learning is not the same as performance** — retrieval, spacing, and interleaving beat massed repetition.
- **Culture is built in small habits and shared language**, not speeches.
- **Buy-in is trust** — earned by making athletes better, not by being liked.
