# Interviewer Standard — Handling Competitor Candidates

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: When interviewing candidates from competitors, name the competitor as a fact only; never characterize their quality, staff, directors, or culture.
- **rule**: Interviewers must state the 'standing line' unprompted: 'Don't hand in any notice, and don't change anything with your current job, until you have a written offer from us.'
- **process**: Differentiate against competitors using an unnamed category (e.g., 'A lot of after-school programmes supervise; we teach a lesson plan') rather than naming the specific employer.
- **rule**: A 2:30–5:30 schedule overlap with a current employer is a hard conflict that must be resolved with arithmetic, not enthusiasm, during the interview.
