# Interviewer Consistency and Conduct Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Interviewer consistency belongs to the content (facts, questions, gates), while warmth belongs to the delivery (stories, rapport, phrasing).
- **process**: Every interview must cover a fixed list: name/pronunciation, recording disclosure, agenda, logistics (times/arrival/minimum days), compensation (hourly/weekly/seasonal), safety/first-aid status, season dates, and the decision cadence.
- **rule**: Interviewers must state the 'standing line': 'Don't change anything with your current job or your class schedule until you have a written offer from us.'
- **rule**: Rapport building is time-boxed to a maximum of five minutes to ensure structural consistency across candidates.
- **rule**: Mirroring candidate language is a conditional step, not a requirement; it requires an explicit trigger (e.g., a candidate naming a specific discipline of study).
- **rule**: Interviewers must never express a leaning, rank a live candidate against the pool, or indicate a role tier verbally during the interview.
