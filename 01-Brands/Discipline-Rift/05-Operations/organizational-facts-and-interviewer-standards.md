# Organizational Facts and Interviewer Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: Discipline Rift operates in 55+ partner school sites across Central Florida, serving approximately 2,000 players per year with a team of around 20 coaches.
- **rule**: The staffing ratio is 1 coach per 10 players; teams of 11 or more require a head coach and an assistant. The hard cap for roster size is 20 players.
- **rule**: Seasons are six weeks long, with four seasons per school year (one per quarter) plus summer camps.
- **rule**: When describing the organization, 'schools', 'locations', and 'sites' are interchangeable and number 55+; 'teams' are a separate metric (approx. 9 per day) and must not be confused with the school count.
- **rule**: Banned phrasings for staff: do not say 'thousands of players' (use 'about 2,000') and do not hedge or guess the school count (state '55+' plainly).
- **decision**: Coaches are assigned specific teams for the full school year to ensure continuity; the annual schedule is published in advance and does not change.
