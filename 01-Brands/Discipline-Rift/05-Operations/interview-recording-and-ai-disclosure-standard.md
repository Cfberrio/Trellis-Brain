# Interview Recording and AI Disclosure Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Disclose the use of an AI note-taker at the start of every interview, explaining that it records, transcribes, and summarizes the call for the hiring file.
- **rule**: Obtain affirmative verbal consent for recording ('Is that okay with you?') before proceeding with substantive interview questions.
- **process**: If a candidate refuses recording: remove the tool, confirm its departure aloud, take manual notes, and log the refusal in the hiring file.
- **fact**: AI summaries are for convenience only; the transcript is the governing record. Summaries may hallucinate dates, tasks, or evaluative conclusions.
