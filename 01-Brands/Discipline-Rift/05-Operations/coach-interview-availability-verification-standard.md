# Coach Interview Availability Verification Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Coaches must be on site by 2:45pm for practices that run 60 minutes, typically starting between 3:00pm and 3:15pm.
- **rule**: The minimum availability requirement for coaches is 3 days per week (Monday–Friday), with 5 days preferred.
- **process**: Interviewers must verify availability by stating requirements first, obtaining a day-by-day schedule, computing drive time aloud against a specific school, and confirming the final schedule back to the candidate.
- **rule**: Interviewers must use the standing line: 'Don't change anything with your current job or your class schedule until you have a written offer from us.'
- **process**: Interviewers must ask about weekend availability and potential schedule changes when a student's new term starts.
