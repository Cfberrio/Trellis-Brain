# Interview Gap Logging and Commitment Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Sort every interview information gap into one of four categories: Never asked (interviewer follow-up), Asked not answered (re-ask), Conflicting evidence (owner decision), or Structurally unavailable (log and move on).
- **rule**: Every commitment made during an interview must include three components to be valid: a specific date, a named owner, and a defined communication channel.
- **process**: Maintain an 'Unresolved Items' block at the bottom of every interview write-up using status values: NOT ESTABLISHED (closable by asking), UNRESOLVED (evidence conflicts), or NEEDS OWNER DECISION (policy question).
