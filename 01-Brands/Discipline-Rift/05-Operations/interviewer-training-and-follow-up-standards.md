# Interviewer Training and Follow-Up Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: After asking a screening question, say nothing until the candidate has finished answering and you have asked one follow-up.
- **rule**: Avoid 'Answer-supply' (answering your own question) and 'Vocabulary-supply' (handing the candidate technical terms like 'adaptable' or 'sandwich method') to prevent data contamination.
- **process**: Use the Follow-up Ladder for philosophy answers: Get the claim -> Get the mechanics -> Get the failure case -> Get the exact words -> Get the scale.
- **rule**: Verify candidate claims by asking for 'mechanics' (physical steps/ball path) which cannot be answered from a memorized script.
- **rule**: Mandatory screening questions must include safety (injured child scenario), group management (scaling to 20 kids), and availability (weekends).
