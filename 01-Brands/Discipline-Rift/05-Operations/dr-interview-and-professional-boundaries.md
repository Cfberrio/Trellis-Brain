# DR Interview and Professional Boundaries

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Do not ask candidates about pregnancy, children, family plans, age, health, religion, or origin; if volunteered, thank them and do not include it in notes or decisions.
- **rule**: Maintain professional neutrality regarding other organizations; describe Discipline Rift's role rather than criticizing a candidate's current employer or local clubs.
- **rule**: Protect the privacy of students and staff: never name a child in interviews (use 'one of our players') and do not name staff to external candidates without cause.
- **rule**: Maintain candidate confidentiality by not discussing other applicants' situations or likely outcomes with current interviewees.
- **rule**: Only promise what can be delivered, specifically regarding hearing back by a certain time; do not promise specific schools, roles, or start dates during the interview.
