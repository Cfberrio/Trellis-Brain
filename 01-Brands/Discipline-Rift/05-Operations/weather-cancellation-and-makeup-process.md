# Weather Cancellation and Makeup Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: When weather forces practice cancellation, the season end date must be updated in GoHighLevel (Manage > Update session) to reflect the makeup date (e.g., extending the season by one week).
- **rule**: Parent cancellation notices must include the weather reason, an apology, and the specific makeup date. AI tools (GPT/Whisper) should be used to generate these, but dates must be manually verified for accuracy.
- **process**: To send cancellation SMS in GHL, use Contacts > Advanced Filters to filter by the specific season tag and school name to ensure only the affected teams are messaged.
- **rule**: In addition to parent SMS and email, the school front office must be called directly to coordinate early pickup changes for students.
