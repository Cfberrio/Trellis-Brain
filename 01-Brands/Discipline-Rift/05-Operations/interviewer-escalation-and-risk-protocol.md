# Interviewer Escalation and Risk Protocol

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: When an interviewer is asked a policy or admin question they cannot answer with certainty, they must use the standard holding line: 'That's an admin question — I'll get you the policy in writing.'
- **rule**: Interviewers must escalate and provide written follow-up for questions regarding background checks, fingerprinting, emergency protocols, private lessons, and refund guarantees, as these lack settled adjudicable standards.
- **rule**: Coaches are prohibited from bringing non-enrolled children to sessions; interviewers must answer this in the moment and then escalate.
- **process**: If a recording contains sensitive or protected-class information, the record must not be deleted unilaterally; it must be flagged the same day to the hiring owner for redaction.
