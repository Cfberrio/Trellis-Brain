# DR Fulfillment DNA

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The Discipline Rift fulfillment process centers on driving customers to a specific 'activation point' (e.g., attendance in the first 7 days) to ensure retention. Onboarding is structured as a 7-day sequence of 'forced wins' (updates, calendar setup, goal setting). In-season delivery requires a 60-minute session structure (ritual, warmup, skill ladder, guided play, win moment), live attendance tracking to trigger interventions, and 2x/week parent touchpoints focused on praise and progress.
