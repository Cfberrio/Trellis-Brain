# Interviewer Confidentiality and Conduct Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Interviews are confidential in both directions: candidate information belongs to them, and the internal hiring process belongs to the organization; neither should be shared with third parties.
- **rule**: Never reference other candidates (their stories, age, gender, or outcomes) during an interview. Use anonymous composites or personal stories for reassurance instead.
- **rule**: Do not disclose the state of the hiring pipeline or applicant volume to candidates, as this information is internal and can affect negotiations.
- **rule**: Referral relationships must be recorded internally and never mentioned to the candidate as a reason for their advancement in the process.
- **rule**: Interviewers must not leak their own leaning, rank candidates against the pool, or signal role tiers/pay rates before the formal decision meeting.
- **process**: Record schedule constraints as dates only in assessment records; avoid including personal disclosures or identifying details that could compromise confidentiality.
