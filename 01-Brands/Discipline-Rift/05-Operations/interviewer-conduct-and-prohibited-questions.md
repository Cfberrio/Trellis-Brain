# Interviewer Conduct and Prohibited Questions

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Interviewer rapport must be time-boxed to five minutes at the start of the call before moving to the question sheet.
- **rule**: Do not raise, extend, or comment on protected or personal characteristics including national origin, culture, accent, pregnancy, family status, appearance, age, religion, health, or personal financial circumstances.
- **process**: When a candidate volunteers personal disclosure: acknowledge briefly, do not evaluate or comment on it, do not connect it to the role, move to the next question, and record nothing about the reason for any scheduling changes.
- **rule**: Do not invite reciprocal hardship stories; if an interviewer shares a personal story to build rapport, they must not ask 'what about you?' regarding personal struggles.
- **rule**: Never suggest a candidate change their current job or class schedule until a written offer exists; use the scripted phrase: 'Please don't change anything with your current job or your class schedule until you have a written offer from us.'
