# Hiring Handoff Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: A clean handoff to a candidate requires stating four elements at the end of every call: what happens next, a specific date for follow-up, the name of the person contacting them, and the communication channel.
- **rule**: Handoff documentation for decision owners must answer what the candidate said (experience/method/availability), what they were told (figures/dates), blocking unknowns, internal information conflicts, and specific promises made.
- **rule**: Unknowns must be explicitly separated into 'Blocking' (e.g., availability/eligibility) and 'Non-blocking' (e.g., minor preferences). Blocking unknowns must be flagged in one sentence at the top of the handoff.
- **rule**: Handoff records must never include hire/decline recommendations, scores, rankings, comparisons to other candidates, or tidied-up quotes.
- **process**: When handing off to another interviewer, provide only the unresolved list of topics, the verbatim block of previous answers, and a one-line note on interviewer influence/interruptions.
