# Interview Opening Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: When opening an interview, explain the AI note-taker before it joins or starts, emphasizing that it allows the interviewer to listen rather than write.
- **rule**: If a candidate is uncomfortable with the AI note-taker, it must be turned off and notes taken by hand.
- **process**: State the interview purpose early: it is a two-way assessment to get to know the candidate's background and for them to understand the organization and coaching role.
- **rule**: Verify the correct pronunciation of the candidate's name at the start and use it exactly as provided; do not shorten or use nicknames without permission.
