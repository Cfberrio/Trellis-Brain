# Handling Sensitive and Personal Disclosures in Hiring

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: When a candidate discloses personal or protected information, interviewers must use a scripted two-move response: briefly acknowledge warmly, then explicitly state the information will not factor into the decision and return to role requirements.
- **rule**: Interviewers must never follow up on protected characteristics (pregnancy, nationality, religion, etc.), comment on a candidate's appearance, or attribute a candidate's ability to their culture or ethnicity, even as praise.
- **process**: Hiring records must exclude protected disclosures. Schedule constraints should be recorded as dates only with no reason attached. AI-generated summaries must be redacted by a human owner before entering the hiring file.
- **rule**: Availability must be discussed only in terms of the hours the role requires, never in terms of the reason a candidate is unavailable.
