# Interviewer Rules and Candidate Management

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Never characterize or disparage a candidate's current employer; contrast business models in the first person instead of assessing the competitor's quality.
- **rule**: Do not ask a candidate to change their current job, restaurant shifts, or course schedule until a written offer has been issued.
- **rule**: Never attach a status or pay incentive (e.g., head coach potential) to a request for schedule changes that the candidate has already declined.
- **process**: When discussing current employment, get the schedule in writing, perform travel arithmetic against arrival times (not practice start), and compare total take-home pay rather than just hourly rates.
