# DR Operational Facts and Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: Discipline Rift was founded in 2021 by Luis Torres and Diego Rivero in Central Florida; it serves over 55 schools and nearly 2,000 players annually.
- **rule**: Practices are one hour long, typically between 3pm and 4pm, with a roster cap of 20 players and a 1:10 coach-to-player ratio.
- **rule**: Head coaches are paid $70 per hour of practice, and assistants are paid $50 per hour, with a minimum commitment of three days per week.
- **rule**: Coaches receive a bonus of 3% of the revenue from their teams at the end of the season.
- **process**: Hiring decisions are made during team reviews at midweek and end of week; every applicant receives a response with a specific date.
- **rule**: Discipline Rift does not provide athletic trainers at practices; coaches follow first aid and emergency protocols (calling 911 for serious incidents) as outlined in the curriculum.
