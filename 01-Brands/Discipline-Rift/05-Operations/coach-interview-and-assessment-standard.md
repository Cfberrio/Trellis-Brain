# Coach Interview and Assessment Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Coach candidates are evaluated on passion depth, pedagogical thinking, and adaptability through specific diagnostic questions.
- **rule**: Strong answers for practice structure must demonstrate gamification, scaffolding, and a fun-first approach rather than just listing drills.
- **rule**: Adaptability is measured by the candidate's ability to diagnose why a drill isn't working and adjust in real-time rather than blaming players.
- **process**: Recruitment includes red flag detection and probing techniques to identify negatives early in the interview process.
