# Ghost School Waitlist Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Ghost schools (inactive or cancelled teams) are kept discoverable on the registration page to capture parent interest. Parents can join a waitlist for these teams, which collects player information without active enrollment or payment.
- **rule**: The threshold for reviewing a ghost school for potential reopening is 7 or more unique waitlisted players for the specific team and season.
- **process**: Waitlist management is manual; there are no automatic reopening triggers or automated emails to waitlisted parents. Admins review demand and decide whether to reopen registration.
