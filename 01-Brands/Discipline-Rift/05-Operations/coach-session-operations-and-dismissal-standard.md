# Coach Session Operations and Dismissal Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Coaches must arrive on campus by the scheduled arrival time and confirm the meeting spot with the front desk upon arrival.
- **process**: Attendance must be tracked via the Coaches Dashboard; if service is unavailable, coaches use a physical roster and sync data later.
- **rule**: Dismissal follows strict protocols: car riders are released one-by-one after visual confirmation of their ride; aftercare students are walked directly to the facility; walkers/bikers follow the specific method listed on the roster.
- **preference**: Coaches should greet parents at drop-off and pickup to learn names and build partnerships.
