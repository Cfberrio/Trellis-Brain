# Interviewer Self-Review and Data Verification Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Perform a ten-minute self-review immediately after every interview to correct automated summaries, remove protected personal data, and log unverified facts or promises.
- **rule**: Interviewers must verify automated summaries against the transcript; summaries are not accountable and have historically invented evaluative phrases or inflated figures.
- **rule**: Maintain specific interviewer performance targets: minimum 8 screening questions, 8 follow-up probes, candidate speaking time between 35%-50%, and interviewer turns under 250 words.
- **process**: Verify critical role details during the call: weekend availability, group management experience, safety judgment scenarios, and specific practice-design answers rather than philosophy.
- **rule**: Adhere strictly to the Canonical Facts Reference for rates, dates, and bonuses; improvised numbers are a frequent failure point in the hiring corpus.
