# Interviewer Execution Standard and Rehearsal Score Sheet

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: The Rehearsal Score Sheet measures interviewer process execution, not candidate quality; candidate assessment must remain a separate document based on role requirements.
- **process**: Interviewers must achieve a 'Ready' rating on all non-negotiable Part A behaviors (identity block, note-taker disclosure, name confirmation, canonical facts, availability, standing line, and next steps) before running live interviews unobserved.
- **rule**: Interviewer airtime must be controlled: candidates should have at least 35% of the words (50% target), and interviewers must not supply answers or vocabulary for the candidate.
- **rule**: Automated summaries must be human-verified against the transcript; no tasks, dates, or record entries should be created from a summary without checking for invented or incorrect data.
- **preference**: Interviewer improvement should focus on one change at a time (e.g., airtime or question count) rather than attempting to fix multiple behaviors simultaneously.
