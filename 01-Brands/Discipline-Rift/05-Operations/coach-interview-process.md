# Coach Interview Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The Discipline Rift coach interview follows a 10-part structure including opening the call, five specific questions, telling the company story, explaining the role/pay, and closing with next steps.
- **preference**: Interviews should avoid a checklist approach; instead, the interviewer should listen to the candidate's vocabulary and explain the company's methods using the candidate's own professional language.
- **rule**: The interviewer's role is to facilitate the conversation and document it; hiring decisions are made as a team by Admin after the call.
