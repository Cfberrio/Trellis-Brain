# Interviewer Training and Screening Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Interviewer turns must be kept under 250 words and end with a comprehension check to prevent topic capture and ensure the candidate absorbs critical logistics.
- **rule**: Interviewers are prohibited from reciprocal hardship disclosure; sharing personal financial or personal struggles that obliges the candidate to match them creates non-job-relevant yield.
- **process**: The interview agenda must be stated within the first two minutes and referenced by name throughout the call to facilitate warm redirects when a candidate over-shares.
- **rule**: Never reference other candidates (their stories, age, or performance) during an interview; use anonymized composites if a comparison is necessary.
- **process**: Verify candidate credentials with mechanical follow-up questions immediately after a claim is made (e.g., specific rankings or roles) rather than accepting unverified statements.
