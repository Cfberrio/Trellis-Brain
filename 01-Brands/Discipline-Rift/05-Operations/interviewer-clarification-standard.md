# Interviewer Clarification Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Interviewers must recognize and correct five signals of inadequate clarification: the enthusiastic close, blanket agreement, one-word turns, indeterminate referents, and unexamined claims.
- **process**: Availability must be verified through explicit arithmetic performed out loud: compute drive time to a named school for a 2:45 PM arrival against the candidate's stated schedule, rather than accepting leading statements.
- **rule**: Never state a candidate's availability to them; ask them to state their Monday to Friday schedule hour-by-hour in their own words.
- **process**: Verify material claims (rankings, certifications, methods) with at least one clarifying question regarding the issuer, date, or specific mechanics.
- **rule**: If a policy is unresolved (e.g., emergency protocols, private lessons, specific dates), do not improvise; use the approved response: 'That's an admin question and I'll get you the policy in writing.'
