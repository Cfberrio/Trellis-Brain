# Interviewer Preparation Checklist

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Interviewer preparation follows a three-pass structure: Pass 1 (Day Before) for reading applications and availability arithmetic; Pass 2 (30 Minutes Before) for confirming canonical facts and compensation; Pass 3 (Last 2 Minutes) for technical checks and name confirmation.
- **rule**: Interviewers must never tell a candidate how they compare to other applicants or share details about other candidates to avoid confidentiality failures.
- **rule**: Interviewers must state the 'no-risk line' in every interview: 'Don't change anything with your current job or your class schedule until you have a written offer from us.'
- **fact**: Canonical coach arrival requirement is on-site at 2:45pm for a practice window starting between 3:00pm and 3:15pm.
- **decision**: Interviewers must commit to a written outcome (advance or decline) for every candidate within five business days.
