# Flyer Sourcing and Printing Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: Current benchmark pricing for flyer printing (Anten Graphics) is $0.0380/flyer at 30k, $0.0346 at 40k, and $0.0331 at 50k, with a blended multi-sport cost of $0.0486.
- **rule**: Standard flyer specifications for quotes: 8.5 x 11 inches, 100lb text (approx. 150gsm coated art paper), 4/0 CMYK print, light gloss varnish one side, 300dpi PDF.
- **process**: Sourcing from China involves submitting RFQs on Alibaba (Trade Assurance/Gold Suppliers), Made-in-China, and Global Sources, requesting both sea and air freight quotes.
- **rule**: Quality control requires a physical printed sample ($30-50 budget) before placing bulk orders; allow 6-8 weeks lead time for sea freight.
