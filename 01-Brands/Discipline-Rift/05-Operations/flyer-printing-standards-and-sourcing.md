# Flyer Printing Standards and Sourcing

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Discipline Rift will source flyer printing from China (e.g., Alibaba) after local vendor research (Anten Graphics, VistaPrint, GotPrint) failed to produce lower per-unit costs for bulk runs of 30K-50K flyers.
