# Hiring Process and Interviewer Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Hiring decisions are made by the admin team during meetings held every Wednesday and Friday.
- **rule**: Every applicant must receive a written outcome (advance or decline) within five business days of their interview, promised by a named individual.
- **process**: Interviews must begin with a scripted, front-loaded disclosure regarding recording and AI note-taking to obtain explicit affirmative consent.
- **rule**: Offer letters are role-generic (Coach); specific placement as Head Coach ($70/hr) or Assistant Coach ($50/hr) is determined by experience and scheduling needs, confirmed in writing before starting.
- **rule**: Interviewer checklist requirements: request references from every applicant, ask for weekend availability, and state both pay rates without committing to a role verbally.
