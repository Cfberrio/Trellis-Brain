# Coach Compensation and Interview Rates

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Head coach rate is $70.00 per hour of practice; Assistant coach rate is $50.00 per hour of practice.
- **rule**: Coaches earn an end-of-season bonus of 3% of the season revenue for their assigned teams, capped at $300 per season (based on 5 full teams).
- **rule**: Interviewer Rule: Never state the bonus as a percentage without providing the dollar result ($60 per full team; $100-$150 typical; $300 max). Never state a bonus figure above $300.
- **rule**: Interviewer Rule: Do not assign Head or Assistant coach roles during the interview call; state both rates and explain that placement depends on experience and will be confirmed in writing.
- **fact**: A full team is defined as 20 players, generating approximately $2,000 in revenue per six-week season.
