# Interview Observation Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Managers observing interviews must disclose their presence to the candidate in one sentence, remain silent, and avoid taking notes on the candidate, focusing instead on the interviewer's execution.
- **rule**: Interviewers are considered 'Ready' to run calls unobserved when they meet all non-negotiables, maintain a candidate word share above 35%, and keep turns under 250 words across two consecutive observations.
- **process**: Observation metrics include counting screening questions (target 8), follow-up probes (target 8), and timing the candidate vs. interviewer talking share.
- **rule**: During the call, observers must not speak, react visibly, or rescue mishandled moments unless a legal or safeguarding problem occurs.
