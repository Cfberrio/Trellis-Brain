# Coach Compensation Interview Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Never state an hourly rate to a coach applicant without providing the weekly figure based on their stated availability.
- **rule**: Never state a bonus figure above $300, as this is the founder's ceiling and the arithmetic maximum for a 5-day coach.
- **rule**: Interviewers must disclose that the 2:45 arrival requirement and travel time are unpaid, as pay is defined strictly by the hour of practice.
- **fact**: Base pay for assistant coaches is $50 per practice; head coaches are $70 per practice.
- **process**: Interviewers must use the standardized compensation script to ensure applicants understand the difference between professional-service hourly rates and the actual weekly/seasonal earnings.
