# Interviewer Airtime and Question Metrics

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Interviewer performance is measured by four metrics: Screening questions asked (target 8), Follow-up probes (target 1:1 per screening question), Candidate share of words (target 35–50%+), and Longest single turn (target <250 words).
- **definition**: A screening question is defined as one directed at the candidate's experience, judgment, or circumstances that produces a unique, recordable answer; it excludes clarifiers, courtesies, or questions with universal 'yes' answers.
- **rule**: Interviewers must use a margin tally sheet to track questions and follow-ups in real-time, including a '20-minute check' where they must stop pitching and ask a question if the tally is under four.
- **rule**: Long turns must always end in a comprehension check (e.g., 'What part of that do you want more detail on?') to ensure the candidate is absorbing information.
