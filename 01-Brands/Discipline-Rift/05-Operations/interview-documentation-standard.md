# Interview Documentation Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Every post-interview write-up must contain five mandatory blocks in order: Call facts, What was said (quotable material), What I concluded (labeled as such), What remains unknown, and What needs follow-up.
- **rule**: Interviewers must quote candidates exactly, including awkward parts or transcription errors. Corruptions must be marked inline as [sic — likely 'X'] rather than silently cleaned.
- **rule**: Operational facts (like pay rates) must be presented in a table with columns for Fact, Value as stated, Verbatim quote, and Confidence level. If a figure was not spoken, it must be marked as NEVER STATED.
- **rule**: Write-ups must avoid narrative paragraphs in favor of bullets and tables, and must never include a score, rating, or comparison to other candidates.
