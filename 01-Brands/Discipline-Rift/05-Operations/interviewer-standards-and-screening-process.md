# Interviewer Standards and Screening Process

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Interviewer airtime should be no more than 50/50 by word count, with a hard floor of 35% candidate share to ensure sufficient screening signal.
- **rule**: Interviews must include at least eight scripted screening questions, each with at least one follow-up probe, to yield high-quality calibration evidence.
- **process**: Implement mechanical controls during calls: place a hard cap on single interviewer turns using comprehension checks, and perform a self-check at the 20-minute mark to ensure at least four questions have been asked.
- **rule**: Note-taking must capture the candidate's verbatim words for key claims, log every screening question/follow-up, and exclude personal or protected circumstances. Recording consent must be obtained and logged at the start of every call.
- **fact**: AI meeting summaries are not the governing record; they can invent evaluative phrases or reproduce incorrect figures. The transcript remains the primary source of truth for tasks and records.
