# Interviewer Training — Declining Mid-Call

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: When a candidate's availability closes a hard gate (e.g., school schedule vs. practice window), the interviewer should end the call immediately to respect their time, applying the gate within the first third of the interview.
- **rule**: Before declining a candidate for weekday availability, the interviewer must verify if the gate is truly closed by asking about weekend availability and potential timetable changes in future terms.
- **process**: A mid-call decline must include: the organization name (Discipline Rift), interviewer name/role, job title, pay range ($70/hr head, $50/hr assistant), and season shape, ensuring the candidate understands the opportunity they were declined for.
- **rule**: Declines must be stated plainly in 1-2 sentences using the requirement as the reason; interviewers must never explain a decline using a judgment or assessment of the person.
- **process**: If a door is left open for a candidate, it requires a 'hinge': a specific condition, a same-day written follow-up email, a named owner, and a system entry for follow-up.
