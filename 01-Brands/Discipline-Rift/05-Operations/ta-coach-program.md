# TA Coach Program

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: The TA Coach (or TFA) program allows middle school students to join the team as assistant coaches rather than players, focusing on leadership and volunteer experience.
- **process**: TA Coaches assist with operational tasks including managing dismissal, gathering players, monitoring behavior, reviewing forms, and ensuring team alignment during practice.
- **preference**: In exchange for the student's participation as a TA Coach, the parent receives free registration for their child.
