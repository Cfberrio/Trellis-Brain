# Coach Interview and Hiring Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The DR Coach Interview follows a 40-minute structured flow: Opening (2m), Discovery (6m), Scenario (4m), Pitch (13m), Logistics (10m), and Close (5m).
- **rule**: Candidates are scored on a 35-point rubric across 7 criteria: Passion for sport, Developmental mindset, Adaptability, Communication clarity, Values alignment (H/H/S), Availability fit, and Coachability.
- **rule**: Hiring decisions are based on rubric totals: 28+ is a Strong hire, 20-27 is Consider, and below 20 is a Decline.
- **process**: Interview modules must be adapted by candidate profile: experienced coaches receive deeper scenarios and skip method basics, while first-time coaches receive more encouragement and a full method explanation.
