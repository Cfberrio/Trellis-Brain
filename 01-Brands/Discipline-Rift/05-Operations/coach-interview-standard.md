# Coach Interview Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Every coach interview must capture mandatory identity, contact, and eligibility data, including full name pronunciation, reliable transport confirmation, and willingness to complete background screening.
- **rule**: Availability is a hard gate: candidates must have a minimum of three days (Monday–Friday) and be able to arrive on-site by 2:45 PM. Drive times must be calculated out loud during the interview.
- **process**: Coaching craft must be verified through mechanical probing of a specific drill, testing the candidate's ability to adapt mid-practice, and behavioral questions regarding struggling players.
- **rule**: Interviewers must not supply vocabulary to candidates, must prompt for candidate questions at the 60% mark, and must never record protected characteristics or reasons for schedule constraints.
