# Coach Hiring Interview Protocol

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Use the 'Seven Questions That Work' framework for all coach interviews to verify physical court experience and behavioral instincts rather than memorized scripts.
- **rule**: Verify technical claims by asking three mechanical follow-ups on a drill the candidate names (e.g., ball path, server, player count, failure modes).
- **rule**: Test developmental reasoning by asking candidates to name one drill they enjoy and one that actually develops skill, then forcing a comparison of the two.
- **rule**: Diagnose coaching adaptability by asking what they would do if a planned drill fails ten minutes in; do not offer vocabulary like 'adaptable'—let the candidate diagnose.
- **rule**: Treat availability as a hard gate: compute drive time to a named school for a 2:45 PM arrival and verify specific days and weekends in writing.
