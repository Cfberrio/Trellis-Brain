# Post-Interview Write-Up Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Interview write-ups must be evidence-only: they must not contain scores, ratings, grades, or a recommendation to hire or decline.
- **process**: The interview write-up template requires sections for call facts, verbatim candidate quotes, operational facts, interviewer inferences (clearly labeled), unknowns, and follow-up commitments.
- **rule**: Interviewer inferences must not use adjectives without a supporting quote and must never include a verdict on the candidate.
- **rule**: Every commitment made during an interview must be recorded with a date, an owner, and a communication channel to be considered valid.
