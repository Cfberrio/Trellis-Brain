# Interviewer Feedback Conversation Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The Feedback Conversation for interviewers must occur within 24 hours of the interview, lasting 20 minutes, focusing on the interview execution rather than the person.
- **rule**: The debrief sequence is fixed: interviewer self-reviews (3 mins), observer reads counts/numbers, both sit with the gap, observer shares two successes (quoted) and exactly one change to implement.
- **rule**: Feedback must be phrased as observable behaviors with observable consequences using quotes, avoiding comparisons to other interviewers or calls.
- **rule**: Hiring decisions, interviewer identity, and candidate personal disclosures are strictly off-limits during the process-focused feedback conversation.
- **process**: Serious findings (protected-class exchanges, record errors, or false commitments) must be escalated to hiring leadership and corrected in writing promptly.
