# Interviewer Conduct - Handling Personal Disclosures

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: When a candidate discloses personal circumstances (health, pregnancy, family, etc.), interviewers must acknowledge briefly and neutrally in one sentence without evaluation, surprise, or comments on appearance.
- **rule**: Interviewers must not disclose prior suspicion of a personal circumstance or link the disclosure to scheduling, staffing, or fit within the same exchange or call.
- **process**: Capture personal disclosures as dates of unavailability only (e.g., 'Unavailable Sept 7–11'); do not record the reason for the absence in the hiring record.
- **process**: Post-call, interviewers must review automated meeting summaries and remove any mention of personal circumstances or protected characteristics before the record is finalized.
- **rule**: Avoid 'kind' evaluations like 'that's totally fine' or 'we can work around it' during the interview, as these are still evaluative statements regarding protected characteristics on the record.
