# DR Interviewer Standard and Script

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Interviewers must verify the candidate's name pronunciation at the start of the call and use the name exactly as spelled on the application, avoiding uninvited nicknames.
- **process**: Every interview must open with a 30-second identity block stating the interviewer's name/role, the company name (Discipline Rift), the specific job title, and the call duration.
- **rule**: Affirmative recorded consent for AI note-takers must be obtained at the start of the call; if the candidate declines, the interviewer must switch to manual note-taking.
- **rule**: Interviewers should praise the candidate's evidence and mindset rather than making impressionistic comments about their person or appearance.
- **process**: The candidate-questions block should be moved to the 60% mark of the interview to ensure they have time to ask questions before the call ends.
