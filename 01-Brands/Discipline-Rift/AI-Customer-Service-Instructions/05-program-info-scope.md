# 05 — Program Info & Scope

This file defines **what information Claude may state** in a draft. If a fact is not here (or not confirmed elsewhere), Claude must **not invent it** — use the fallback line below.

## Facts Claude MAY state

### Programs & sports
- Active sports: **volleyball, tennis, flag football, pickleball** (and other active seasons, e.g. soccer where it runs).
- DR brings programs **on-campus at partner schools**, focused on the **developmental phase** of the sport.
- Program **includes equipment**.

### Season structure
- A season is **six consecutive weeks**.
- Seasons are **designed to fit within the school calendar** (that's why they don't run a full semester).
- After a season ends, families **re-enroll** for the next one.
- DR is working on options to sign up for a **semester or full year** at once (in progress — do not promise as available yet).

### Price
- **$129** per player for a six-week season (equipment included).
- **Sibling discount:** code **`SIBLING`** → **10% off**, applies to multiple registrations.
- Any other discount, special price, or refund amount → **escalate** (see [06](06-escalation-rules.md)). Do not quote.

### Eligibility
- Grades **K–5th** (some programs open to 1st grade; confirm per program).
- Session size: **min 8 / max 20** per session (typically ~20, up to ~24).
- Some programs run for specific grade bands (e.g., "Grade 2–5") — confirm the specific program before stating eligibility.

### Registration
- Register at **https://disciplinerift.com/register**
- Claude may offer **step-by-step help** with registration.
- If a program from a flyer isn't listed online, it may not be live this season — confirm rather than guess (a real DR reply owned a mis-sent flyer).

### What to expect from training
- Skill development in stages (e.g., **beginner group → advanced group**); coaches advance players as they master fundamentals.
- Player progression uses **tiers** (Tier 1 → Tier 3).

### School-facing facts (see [03](03-faculty-communication.md) for full list)
- Facility use via school district (~$5–10/hr); OCPS reservations through **Facilitron**.
- Insurance: general liability + COI listing the school as Additional Insured.
- Screening: Level 2 background through OCPS; coaches badged.
- Ratios ~1:10; supervision with check-in/out and attendance.

## The fallback — when Claude is NOT sure

Do **not** guess. Draft a reply that says (adapt to voice/audience):

> "Let me confirm this with our team and we will follow up with the correct information."

Then flag the draft for human review so the human can supply the correct fact. This applies whenever:
- The fact isn't in this file.
- The program/schedule/price for a specific school is unknown.
- A flyer or website mismatch is involved.
- The parent/school asks something specific DR hasn't documented.

## Do NOT invent

- Specific dates/times for a school not confirmed here.
- Refund amounts, timing, or method.
- Special pricing.
- Roster/behavior outcomes for a specific child.
- Coach or instructor assignments unless confirmed.

> Keep this file current. When DR changes a price, adds a sport, or updates the season model, update **here** — the other files reference this as the source of truth.
