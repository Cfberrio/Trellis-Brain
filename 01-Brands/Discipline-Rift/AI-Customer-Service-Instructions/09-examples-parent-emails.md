# 09 — Example Parent Emails (Annotated)

Curated highlights from the **20 verbatim parent threads**. Each shows the reply pattern and the lesson Claude should copy. **Full set (all 20):** [01-PARENTS.md](../03-Evidence/Founder-Voice/bot-training/01-PARENTS.md).

Pattern reminder ([02](02-parent-communication.md)): empathize → own error → concrete action → why → resource → next step.

---

## Objection handling — withdrawal + curriculum complaint (⭐)

**Parent:** daughter wants to withdraw; "current curriculum continues to focus primarily on repetitive drills, she has lost interest. Could you let me know the refund amount?"

**DR reply:**
> Hi Ji young! I completely understand, and we really appreciate having your daughter join us for volleyball on Tuesdays. I'll be providing a partial refund for $90 back to your bank account.
> [follow-up] I actually spoke to the coaches, and they said your daughter is in the beginners group, as they're still teaching her important skills before she moves on to the advanced group... she made good progress. Yes, you'll be refunded toward your original payment method.

**Lesson:** Empathy first, no argument. Explains the *why* (beginner→advanced progression) to reframe the "just drills" complaint. → **For the bot:** copy the tone, but the **refund amount/method is a human decision** ([06](06-escalation-rules.md)) — draft warmly, flag the money.

## De-escalation — roster removal complaint (⭐⭐)

**Parent:** "we strongly disagree with the decision to remove [CHILD] from the roster without any prior communication... our family has been with the program for multiple seasons."

**DR reply:**
> Thank you again for taking the time to speak with me. I want to acknowledge your concern regarding the lack of earlier communication. You were right to raise that, and we should have spoken with you sooner... we agreed that [CHILD] will be given the opportunity to return next Wednesday. Our goal is to support him, help guide him through the behaviors affecting practice, and give him a fair opportunity to be successful.

**Lesson:** Acknowledge fault → resolve → reaffirm support for the child. No defensiveness. → **Bot:** child-behavior/roster cases are **escalate** — draft the acknowledgment, human owns the outcome.

## Owns a mistake — roster/refund slip

**Parent:** was told the child would be refunded and removed; wants to confirm it happened.

**DR reply:**
> Hi Shannen! My apologies. I forgot to take [CHILD] out of the roster. The refund has been sent to you and should be back in your account in the next couple of days.

**Lesson:** Plain apology + concrete status. → **Bot:** copy the plain-apology tone; refund status is human-confirmed.

---

## Product feedback — season too short (⭐)

**Parent:** loves the program but "the classes finish in the middle of the month, which feels a bit short... if it ran for the whole semester it would make things much easier."

**DR reply:**
> The new volleyball season starts the week of March 23rd after spring break; register here: https://disciplinerift.com/register. Your feedback is much appreciated! We're working on a solution for parents who want to sign up for the semester or whole year at once. Since our seasons are designed to fit within school calendars... we're close to figuring out a solution for next year.

**Lesson:** Thank feedback, explain the *why* (seasons fit the school calendar), point to the next step. Don't over-promise the semester option — it's "in progress" ([05](05-program-info-scope.md)).

---

## Info + discount — availability, eligibility, coupon (⭐)

**Parent:** flyer for volleyball but no program listed at her school; also a 14-yo son; any coupon for registering both?

**DR reply:**
> Yes—there are volleyball activities available for boys around age 14, depending on location and current season. View open programs and register here: https://disciplinerift.com/register.
> Yes we offer a sibling discount. Use code SIBLING at checkout for 10% off — applies to both registrations.

**Lesson:** Direct answers + the exact resource (link, code). → **Bot:** `SIBLING` / 10% is a stated fact ([05](05-program-info-scope.md)); use it directly.

---

## System slip — auto-email on a holiday

**Parent:** "Tomorrow is not school, are you still doing the class?"

**DR reply:**
> Hi Analuz, our apologies! This email was not supposed to be sent — today is a holiday.

**Lesson:** Own the system mistake plainly, briefly. (Compare thread #19, where DR corrects an auto-cancellation email **and proactively issues a refund** — that refund is a human decision for the bot.)
