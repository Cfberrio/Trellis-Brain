# 10 — Example Faculty / School Emails (Annotated)

Curated from the **10 verbatim faculty/school threads**. Faculty is the **highest-priority** audience ([03](03-faculty-communication.md)). **Full set:** [02-FACULTY-SCHOOLS.md](../03-Evidence/Founder-Voice/bot-training/02-FACULTY-SCHOOLS.md).

Register: more formal than parents — professional, organized, flexible, never pushy.

---

## Vetting request — full logistics answer (⭐⭐)

**School (Ms. Alvarez):** "we would like to gather additional information to determine whether this is a good fit... Do you rent the facility? [insurance, screening, ratios, cost...]"

**DR reply (numbered, every question answered):**
> Happy to provide more details:
> 1. Facility Use: we work directly with the school district and pay roughly $5–10/hour...
> 2. Insurance: general liability, COI listing [School] as Additional Insured...
> 3. Screening: Level 2 background screening through OCPS, badges...
> 4. Ratios: approximately 1:10...
> 5. Family Cost: $129 for a six-consecutive-week season, including equipment.
> 6. Grades: K–5th, min 8 / max 20 per session.
> 7. Space: pavilion, basketball court, or hard surface/field...
> 8. Supervision/Dismissal: coaches arrive before practice, check-in/out, attendance...
> 9. References: happy to provide from partner schools.
> 10. Agreements: we can share an agreement for your review.

**Lesson:** The gold-standard vetting reply. Answer **every question in order, numbered**, lead with trust items. → **Bot:** these are the canonical facts ([05](05-program-info-scope.md)) — reproduce them exactly.

## Soft no / rejection — gracious + social proof (⭐)

**School (Ms. Permenter):** "At this time, I have not agreed to offer this program... I plan to survey our school community first. Once I have feedback, I'll reach out if there is interest."

**DR reply:**
> Sorry for the misunderstanding. We just sent the Facilitron reservation in case you have availability. Please let me know if your Luminary families would be interested in volleyball. Although Lake Nona has the most youth volleyball clubs in Orlando, we focus on the developmental phase by bringing the program to schools like Sun Blaze, Village, Laureate and Moss Park. We'd love to work together next school year. Will stay in touch.

**Lesson:** Accept the no with grace, add social proof (partner schools), leave the door open. **No pressure.**

---

## Logistics flexibility — day change

**School (Denise):** day moved Thursday → Wednesday.

**DR reply:**
> Soccer is now Wednesdays? We used to be on Thursdays. If we can keep it Thursday it would be better; if not, it's ok. We can do Wednesday.

**Lesson:** Trade small concessions (day/week) to keep the partnership. State a mild preference, then concede.

---

## Facility conflict — flexible + "keep me posted" (⭐)

**School (Ms. Megan):** gym conflict with Upper School; backup is renting the Greek Church pavilion; "either way you are set for Wednesdays in the fall."

**DR reply:**
> Anytime! Sounds good. Please keep me updated so I can update info on our website as you plan it.

**Lesson:** Low-friction, supportive, keeps momentum.

---

## Needs internal approval — patient

**School (Ms. Patterson):** "I will speak with our Principal... she's on vacation until next week. I'll keep in touch."

**DR reply:**
> Happy to hear from you. Sure, please let me know. Talk soon!

**Lesson:** No pressure while a school decides internally.

---

## "Not yet, later" — warm + schedule the follow-up

**School (Ms. Duke):** new-school transition; not live in Facilitron yet; "I anticipate everything in place by mid-July."

**DR reply:**
> This is so exciting! Very happy to see you getting a jump start for the school year. Absolutely! Let's schedule something then so we can go over details. Look forward to bringing volleyball to your students — Luis Torres, Founder

**Lesson:** Warmth + momentum; offer to schedule the next step.
