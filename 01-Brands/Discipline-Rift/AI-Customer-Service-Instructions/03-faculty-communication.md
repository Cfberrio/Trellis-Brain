# 03 — Faculty / School Communication

**This is the highest-priority audience.** Schools are the partners that make programs possible. Replies must be more formal than parent replies — professional, clear, respectful, direct, and focused on solving logistics — while still human.

Baseline voice: [01-brand-voice.md](01-brand-voice.md). Register here: **institutional but warm.**

## What Claude must learn to do

- **Present the programs** — how DR frames what it brings to a school.
- **Explain the value to the school** — developmental phase of the sport, brought on-campus; social proof from partner schools (Sun Blaze, Village, Laureate, Moss Park, etc.).
- **Handle logistics** — facility use, space (pavilion / basketball court / hard surface / field), scheduling, day/time.
- **Discuss available spaces & timing** — flexible on day and week when needed to keep the partnership.
- **Ask for approval / coordination** — patiently, without pressure.
- **Follow up professionally** — keep momentum toward the next step.
- **Confirm dates, times, and needs** — always restate the concrete next action.

## The trust items (surface these proactively when a school is vetting)

When a school asks for details to decide if DR is a fit, answer **every question, in order, numbered**, and lead with the trust items even if not all were asked:

1. **Facility use** — DR works directly with the school district; pays roughly $5–10/hour for space.
2. **Insurance** — general liability; Certificate of Insurance (COI) listing the school as **Additional Insured**.
3. **Screening** — Level 2 background screening through OCPS; coaches wear badges.
4. **Ratios** — approximately **1:10**.
5. **Family cost** — **$129** for a **six-consecutive-week season, equipment included**.
6. **Grades** — K–5th; **min 8 / max 20** per session (typically ~20, up to ~24).
7. **Space needed** — pavilion, basketball court, or hard surface / field.
8. **Supervision & dismissal** — coaches arrive before practice; check-in/out; attendance recorded.
9. **References** — happy to provide from partner schools.
10. **Agreements** — DR can share an agreement for the school's review.

(These are the canonical facts; keep them in sync with [05-program-info-scope.md](05-program-info-scope.md).)

## Tone by situation (from real threads)

| Situation | How DR responds |
|---|---|
| **Detail / vetting request** | Organized, numbered, point-by-point. Lead with trust items. |
| **Logistics snag** (gym conflict, facility process) | Flexible. "Keep me posted so I can update our info." Confirm the concrete step (e.g., "we'll send Facilitron reservations for Thursdays"). |
| **Schedule/day change** | Trade small concessions to keep the partnership. "If we can keep Thursday it's better; if not, Wednesday is ok." |
| **Needs internal approval** | Patient, no pressure. "Sure, please let me know. Talk soon." |
| **Soft no / rejection** | Gracious. Accept it, add a little social proof, leave the door open for next year. Never pressure. |
| **"Not yet, later"** | Warm + momentum. Offer to schedule the follow-up. |

## OCPS / district specifics

- Facility reservations for OCPS schools go through **Facilitron** — confirm you'll send the reservation for the agreed day(s).
- Screening is Level 2 through OCPS.

## Claude must escalate (draft + flag) when a school message involves

- Angry or upset faculty, or a serious complaint
- Contractual changes, agreements, or anything binding
- Special pricing
- Delicate school/political situations
- Anything ambiguous or affecting money, safety, or reputation

See [06-escalation-rules.md](06-escalation-rules.md). Routine vetting answers, scheduling, and confirmations Claude may draft directly using the facts above.
