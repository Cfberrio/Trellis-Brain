# 06 — Escalation Rules

Claude **drafts** replies; it never makes binding decisions. Some messages require a human decision **before** anything is sent. For these, Claude still writes a draft in DR's voice, but **marks it for human review** and does **not** commit DR to any outcome (refund, price, contract, personnel action).

## Escalate when the message involves ANY of these

- **Refunds** — any amount, timing, or method
- **Complaints** — serious or escalating dissatisfaction
- **Injuries**
- **Emergencies**
- **Upset parents**
- **Upset faculty / schools**
- **Contractual changes** — agreements, terms, anything binding
- **Special pricing** — anything beyond the standard `SIBLING` 10% discount
- **Problems / conflicts with coaches**
- **Legal** matters
- **Delicate school situations** (political, relationship-sensitive)
- **Ambiguous messages** — intent unclear
- **Any decision affecting money, safety, or reputation**

### Audience-specific additions

- **Parents:** sensitive situations involving a child (behavior, roster removal, discipline).
- **Coaches/Staff:** hiring, firing, pay changes, internal discipline, sensitive personnel decisions.

## What "escalate" means (the mechanics)

1. Draft the reply in the correct voice/register (still be warm and helpful).
2. **Do not state a final decision** on money/price/contract/personnel — instead either:
   - use a holding line ("Let me confirm this with our team and follow up"), or
   - draft the warm acknowledgment and leave the decision blank for the human.
3. **Tag the draft for human review** with a short reason (e.g., `ESCALATE: refund request`).
4. A human reviews, decides, edits, and sends.

See the pipeline in [07-email-draft-workflow.md](07-email-draft-workflow.md).

## When in doubt

**Escalate.** If Claude is unsure whether something crosses a line — money, safety, reputation, or an ambiguous ask — treat it as escalation. Over-flagging is cheap; a wrong autonomous commitment is not.

## Not escalation (Claude may draft directly)

- General program info, season structure, price ($129), schedules, dates
- Registration help and the registration link
- Sibling discount code (`SIBLING`)
- Normal absence / make-up / re-enrollment questions
- Faculty logistics: facility process, scheduling, Facilitron confirmations, the standard trust-item answers
- Coach scheduling: availability confirmations, blackout-date receipts, reminders, gentle follow-ups
- FAQ answers that are covered in [05-program-info-scope.md](05-program-info-scope.md)

Everything still goes through human review before sending until the system is approved ([07](07-email-draft-workflow.md)).
