# Private Lesson Booking Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The private lesson booking loop follows a specific sequence: check the roster sheet, tag contacts in GoHighLevel, send bulk texts, manage replies in the Team Inbox, and log confirmed bookings on the coach schedule.
- **rule**: A private lesson is only considered booked if it is logged on the coach schedule; this is the single source of truth.
- **rule**: When messaging parents for bookings, always offer specific day and time options rather than open-ended questions to increase response speed.
- **rule**: Bulk texts must include 'Reply STOP to unsubscribe' and avoid mentioning prices, as payment data lives on the school's roster sheet.
- **preference**: Parent communication should prioritize warmth first and logistics second, using short sentences and avoiding technical jargon.
