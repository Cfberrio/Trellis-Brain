# Social Media Publishing and Community Management Process

## Canonical Statement

- **preference**: Avoid publishing social media content exactly on the hour; use non-standard times like 2:07, 2:35, or 3:18 to stand out from typical posting schedules.
- **process**: Use GoHighLevel as the primary scheduling tool for Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok, but switch to manual iPhone uploads for TikTok carrusels (which require Live Photo format for movement) and Facebook carrusels that fail in GHL.
- **rule**: Ensure content is geolocated to Orlando by verifying VPN status (if remote) and using specific hashtags like #YouthSportsOrlando and #OrlandoSports to signal the target market to algorithms.
