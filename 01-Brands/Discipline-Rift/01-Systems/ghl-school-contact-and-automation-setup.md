# GHL School Contact and Automation Setup

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: To add a school point of contact (POC) in GHL, click the school name, navigate to the 'Man POC' section, and enter the contact's name, email, position, phone number, and extension before saving.
- **process**: For email automation (including FLAG automation) to function correctly, the recipient's email must be entered in both the main contact info field and the Main POC field.
