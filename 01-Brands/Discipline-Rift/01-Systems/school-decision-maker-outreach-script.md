# School Decision-Maker Outreach Script

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: When pitching schools, lead with the 'beginner-friendly sports program brought directly to campus' framing to identify if they have existing elementary-age offerings.
- **rule**: Volleyball is the primary spearhead sport for school outreach, positioned as a way for younger students to learn the sport before reaching middle school athletics.
- **process**: If a school reports the gym is full, pivot to smaller spaces (covered areas, grass, concrete) by emphasizing that the program is developmental and DR brings all equipment.
- **rule**: When a school already has a provider, do not criticize them; instead, ask if they serve lower school or suggest a different sport like flag football to fill a gap.
- **decision**: The primary goals for a decision-maker call are to confirm interest, obtain an email, send information, discuss sport/space fit, or book a logistics call.
