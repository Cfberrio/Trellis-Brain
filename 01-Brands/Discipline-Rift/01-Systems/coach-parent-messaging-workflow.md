# Coach-Parent Messaging Workflow

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Coaches are expected to reply to parent messages the same day; this is identified as the single biggest factor in family retention for future seasons.
- **process**: The 'Coach New Messages' workflow triggers an hourly notification to coaches (head and assistant) if they have unread parent messages on any of their teams.
- **rule**: Coach communication to parents should be kept simple; a one-line reply is considered sufficient to clear the dashboard and maintain engagement.
