# School Outreach and Follow-Up System

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: The follow-up standard for school outreach is 'Call and email'; if a contact is reached, send the email immediately; if not, still send the email if contact info is available.
- **process**: The normal follow-up rhythm for schools is every other day or every 2 days, as one-week intervals are considered too long.
- **rule**: Always attempt to call first to create movement and context, using email to create a record and the CRM to ensure continuity.
- **rule**: Daily activity expectations for outreach include at least 15 school calls and 10 emails, with every meaningful interaction documented in the CRM.
- **process**: CRM pipeline stages for school outreach are categorized as High Priority, First Outreach Sent, Follow-Up Needed, Interested, and Not a Fit.
