## Purpose
The DR sales system serves two parallel funnels that must not be confused:

1. **Parent funnel** — flyer/QR → site → pop-up → name+email → nurture → enrollment. Closing logic lives in [[Sales-Process]] and the [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/06-DNA/Conversion|Conversion DNA]].
2. **School funnel** — outreach to public/private schools to secure facility access (Facilitron / after-school programming). Two distinct SOPs per school type — see [[School-Outreach-SOP-Public]] and [[School-Outreach-SOP-Private]].

## Operating Posture
- The school funnel **never sounds like sales**. Posture is "we are already working through the school facility process" — familiar, calm, approachable.
- The parent funnel uses Hormozi-aligned objection handling (see [[Sales-Process#Objection Handling]]).
- All contacts and stages live in [[GHL-CRM-Pipeline-Spec|GHL]] — never rely on memory.

## Canon Decisions
- **No leaving voicemails** on school outreach. Log "no answer", move to Follow-Up Needed, try again.
- **No discounting on the call** (parent funnel). Use "if free, would you?" isolation instead.
- **Decision-maker required on parent close call** — prevent the "need to ask my spouse" stall.
- Sales calls should be recorded for SOP improvement (GHL / Whisper / ClickUp).
