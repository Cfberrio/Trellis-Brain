## Purpose
SOP for calling public schools, confirming who handles **Facilitron / facility rentals**, getting the right person, confirming availability, and moving each school correctly through Go High Level. For private/charter/Christian/Catholic schools use the [[School-Outreach-SOP-Private|Private SOP]] instead.

## 1. Main Objective
The goal is **not** to "sell the school". The goal is to sound like we are already working through the facility process and simply need to confirm the right person, day, and availability.

We are not calling like strangers. We are calling like:
> "Hey, we're already working through Facilitron reservations. I just want to confirm who's handling facility rentals this year."

Tone: familiar, calm, approachable. Almost like talking to an aunt at the front office.

## 2. Core Tone
Every call should feel: friendly, casual-but-professional, confident, not salesy, not over-explained, not robotic, like we already know what we are doing.

**Avoid:** "Hi, this is Luis with Discipline Rift, and we are a youth sports organization…"
**Use instead:** "Hi, this is Luis. How's it going?"

## 3. CRM Source of Truth — Go High Level
Every school needs: school name, phone, contact person (if known), email (if known), notes from call attempts, current stage, next follow-up action.

Do not rely on memory. If something happens, log it.

## 4. Who To Call
Only call schools in **High Priority**. Before calling, check:
- School name, phone, existing contact + email + notes
- Whether Facilitron was already submitted
- Whether this is volleyball, flag football, or both
- Whether this is a comeback or new school

## 5. First Call Script — Front Office

### Main opening
> "Hi, this is (Name). How's it going?"
> *Pause.*
> "I'm working through Facilitron reservations and wanted to confirm who's handling facility rentals this year."
> "Do you know who's handling Facilitron this year?"

Keep it short.

### If we have a contact name
> "Hi, this is Luis. How's it going? I'm working through Facilitron reservations and wanted to confirm if Ms. [Last Name] is still handling facility rentals this year. Is she the right person, or is someone else handling it now?"

### If they sound confused
> "Facilitron — the system used for school facility rentals. Do you know who handles that this year?"

### If they ask what it's about
> "We're just confirming the Facilitron process for next school year and wanted to make sure we're speaking with the right person. Would you be able to transfer me to whoever handles that?"

## 6. Keywords To Use
Primary keywords (use sparingly):
- **Facilitron** (strongest)
- Facility rentals / Facility reservations
- Volleyball program (or Flag football as backup)
- Next school year
- One day per week
- One hour after dismissal

## 7. If Transferred To The Decision-Maker

**Do not** sound surprised. Do not restart the introduction. Do not over-explain.

### If Facilitron already submitted
> "Hi, this is Luis. How's it going? We submitted the Facilitron reservation for next school year for the volleyball program, and I just wanted to confirm that [Wednesday] still works well for you guys. We're only looking at one day per week, about one hour after dismissal."

### If Facilitron not yet submitted
> "Hi, this is Luis. How's it going? I'm working through the Facilitron reservations for next school year, and I wanted to check availability before we submit everything. We're looking at one day per week, about one hour after dismissal, for the volleyball program. Would [Wednesday] work?"

## 8. Decision-Maker Approach Structure
1. Introduce by name
2. Mention Facilitron
3. Mention next school year
4. Mention volleyball program
5. Confirm the day
6. Ask if that works

Keep it human, funny, warm — auntie/cool uncle posture.

## 9. If They Ask "What Do You Guys Do?"

Go straight to mission:
> "We run a VOLLEYBALL PROGRAM through a national initiative to increase sports participation in children. The idea is to bring sports directly into the school so students can participate right after dismissal."
> "We only need one day per week, about one hour during that day, to run the program. Would that be too much to ask from you guys?"

## 10. If Not Interested In Volleyball
> "Totally understand. Volleyball may not be the sport that students are most interested in, depending on the demographic of the school."
> "Do you think flag football would be a better offering for students at the school?"

## 11. If They Say "Send Me An Email"

This is a good outcome. **Do not keep pitching.**
> "Okay, cool. Can you please give it to me and I'll send it to you?"

Then, depending on the situation:
- **Volleyball submitted:** "Would you be able to look over the facility reservation and let me know if that day works?"
- **Flag football may be better fit:** "I can also request to change it to flag football if that feels like a better fit for the school."

Close: "I'll send that over now. Thank you."

## 12. After They Give You An Email
Immediately update GHL with: contact name, role, email, phone, what they said, sport mentioned, whether they need to review the reservation, sentiment (interested / neutral / confused).

Send existing email template — do not create from scratch. Attach the flyer.

## 13. If They Don't Know Who Handles Facilitron
Stay patient. Do not make them feel dumb. Do not close as Not a Fit.
> "No worries. Do you know if that's usually handled by the AP, bookkeeper, office manager, or someone in the front office?"

If still unknown:
> "No problem. I'll check back later. Thank you."

Then: move to **Follow-Up Needed**, add CRM note, look up faculty/staff page, try calling again later.

## 14. If Nobody Answers
**Do NOT leave a voicemail.** Voicemails waste time for this process.

1. Log the call attempt
2. Add note: "No answer. No voicemail left."
3. Move/keep in **Follow-Up Needed**
4. Try again later
5. Send email only if we already have the right contact

## 15. Email Rules
- No all-caps subject lines
- Attach the flyer
- Personalize name if known
- Verify school name + sport correct
- Friendly + professional tone
- If we just called, mention that we tried reaching them

## 16. Subject Line Rules

**Good:**
- Volleyball at [School Name]?
- Facilitron reservation for next school year
- Facility availability for next school year
- Quick question about Facilitron
- Volleyball or flag football at [School Name]?

**Bad (all caps = aggressive):**
- VOLLEYBALL AT [SCHOOL NAME]
- FACILITY REQUEST
- URGENT
- PLEASE RESPOND
- PROGRAM APPROVAL NEEDED

## 17. Email After A Call

**Subject:** Facilitron reservation for next school year

> Hi Ms. [Last Name],
>
> I just tried reaching you and wanted to follow up here.
>
> We're working through the Facilitron reservations for next school year and wanted to confirm the best contact for facility rentals.
>
> We're looking at one day per week, about one hour after dismissal, for the volleyball program. I attached the flyer here for context.
>
> Would you be able to look over the facility reservation and let us know if the day works for the school?
>
> Thank you,
> [Signature]

## 18. Email If Flag Football Becomes The Better Option

**Subject:** Volleyball or flag football at [School Name]?

> Hi Ms. [Last Name],
>
> Thank you for speaking with me earlier.
>
> We're working through Facilitron reservations for next school year and wanted to confirm what would be the best fit for the school. We originally looked at volleyball, but if flag football would be a better offering for students at the school, we can request to adjust the reservation.
>
> We would only need one day per week, about one hour after dismissal.
>
> Thank you,
> [Signature]

## 19. CRM Stages

| Stage | When to move here |
|---|---|
| **High Priority** | Ready to be contacted. Only call from this stage. |
| **First Outreach** | Spoke to someone, sent first email, got the right contact, confirmed who handles Facilitron |
| **Follow-Up Needed** | No answer, they didn't know who handles Facilitron, person not available, email sent + waiting, contact not identified yet |
| **Interested** | Day may work / asked for flyer / open to volleyball or flag football / agreed to review reservation |
| **Not a Fit** | Right person confirmed: no space / no interest / no available day / has competing program / not eligible |

> [!warning] Do not move to Not a Fit just because the front office was confused.


## 21. Call Recording
Record when possible (GHL / ClickUp / Whisper / phone recording if approved). Purpose is SOP improvement, not micromanagement.

## 22. Call Quality
Before calling: AirPods, close to computer, clear mic, GHL open, school website open, ready to take notes, no multitasking.

## 23. What To Listen For
Who handles Facilitron / facility rentals, whether same person still in charge, whether AP/bookkeeper/principal handles it, whether they use Facilitron, available days, volleyball interest, flag football fit, email/flyer request.

Write it down immediately.

## 24. What Not To Do
- Start with corporate pitch
- Say company name too early unless asked
- Over-explain the program
- Sound like a stranger or salesperson
- Leave voicemails
- Use all caps in subject lines
- Forget to attach flyers
- Forget to update CRM or move the stage
- Mark schools Not a Fit too early
- Keep talking after they say "send me an email"

## 25. Best Call Flow
1. Open High Priority contact
2. Check school notes
3. Call the school
4. "Hi, this is Luis. How's it going?"
5. "I'm working through Facilitron reservations and wanted to confirm who's handling facility rentals this year."
6. "Do you know who's handling Facilitron this year?"
7. Get transferred or collect email
8. If transferred, confirm reservation/day
9. If asked what we do → national initiative explanation
10. If volleyball not a fit → ask about flag football
11. If they say send email → get email and stop pitching
12. Update GHL
13. Send the existing email with flyer
14. Move contact to correct stage
15. Add clear notes
16. Follow up later if needed

## 26. Final Standard

> "Hi, this is Luis. How's it going? I'm working through Facilitron reservations and wanted to confirm who's handling facility rentals this year."

Then:
> "We submitted the Facilitron reservation for next school year for the volleyball program. I just wanted to confirm if Wednesday still works for you guys."

If needed:
> "We run through a national initiative to increase sports participation in children. We only need one day per week, about one hour after dismissal. Would that be too much to ask from you guys?"

If volleyball is not the right fit:
> "Do you think flag football would be a better offering for students at the school?"

**Posture: friendly, familiar, confident, and direct.**
