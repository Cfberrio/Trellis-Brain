# School Outreach SOP Public — Evidencia

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: School outreach follows a specific CRM workflow in Go High Level, moving contacts from High Priority to First Outreach, Follow-Up Needed, or Interested based on call outcomes.
- **rule**: The communication posture for school outreach is 'familiar, warm, and direct,' described as 'talking to your aunt at the front office' rather than a formal sales pitch.
- **rule**: Outreach calls should prioritize confirming the Facilitron (facility rental) process and decision-maker rather than pitching the program details immediately.
- **rule**: Do not leave voicemails during school outreach; instead, log the attempt in the CRM and retry later.
- **rule**: Email subject lines for schools must avoid all-caps and focus on Facilitron or the specific sport (e.g., 'Volleyball at [School Name]?').
