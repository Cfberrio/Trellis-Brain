## Purpose
End-to-end parent funnel: entry → nurture → close → enrollment → onboarding handoff. This note governs both Mode A (self-checkout) and Mode B (closer assist). For deeper strategy detail see [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/06-DNA/Conversion|Conversion DNA]].

## Entry Point
1. Parent receives flyer → scans **QR**
2. Lands on site → pop-up offers **Parent Guide**
3. Submits name + email + (recommended) phone, child age/grade, school, sport interest

> [!tip] Add qualifying fields to enable segment routing and lead scoring (see [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/00-Brand-Core/Avatar|Avatar]] for 3-segment detection rules).

## 7-Day Conversion Sprint

### Cadence
- **Day 0:** immediate email + (if phone collected) text/call fast
- **Days 1–2:** 2 touches/day
- **Days 3–7:** 1 touch/day
- **Days 8–30:** weekly nurture touches (proof + reminders)
- **30+ days:** reactivation only (quarterly), not core conversion engine

### Welcome Series
| # | Theme | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Guide delivery + "what happens next" | Deliver promised asset, one clear CTA |
| 1 | Logistics friction | Address Segment 2 (logistics-overwhelmed) |
| 2 | Beginner-friendly / safe-to-learn | Address Segment 1 (anxious beginner) |
| 3 | Trust (coach quality + safety) | Proof clip or testimonial |
| 4 | Cost & commitment clarity | Address Segment 3 (price-sensitive) — "season = predictable" |
| 5 | Proof drop | Screenshots, testimonials, video-in-email |
| 6 | Decision email | "Spots close / season starts / enroll now" |

Every email = **1 CTA** ("what to do next / when / what they get / what happens next").

## Qualification (per call or inbound)

**YES (qualified):**
- Child age 6–12
- Attends served school
- Parent can pay per season (or accepts split-pay)
- Wants fun-first / on-campus convenience (not elite travel-club)

**NO (unqualified):**
- Out of age range or school not served
- Wants elite-only competitive structure DR doesn't offer
- Cannot attend the after-school window at all

**Lead scoring:** 1–5 or red/yellow/green. Route **green** to human assist when not buying quickly.

## Sales Modes

### Mode A — Checkout Close (no call)
- Every email ends with one clear CTA + "what happens next"
- If they click but don't buy → trigger a **"main concern"** email

### Mode B — Closer Assist (5–10 minutes)

**Call skeleton:**
1. Confirm basics (age / school / sport)
2. Ask: what do they want for their kid? (fun, friends, confidence, activity)
3. Ask: what's stopping them from enrolling today?
4. If they stall: ask for **main concern**
5. Handle the concern → ask for decision → enroll

**Follow-up rule:** No limbo. Either set the next decision point or disqualify.

## Inbound Call Close SOP (Assisted Checkout)

**Goal:** End the call with enrollment completed (registration + payment).

### Capture early
- Parent name (10 seconds)
- Parent email — sent registration link *during* the call

> [!example] Opening line
> "Yes — this is **Discipline Rift at [School Name]**. Happy to help. What's your name?"
> Then: "What's the best email to send you the **registration link** right now?"
> If asked why: "So you have it in writing, and I can walk you through it on the phone."

### 30-second value explanation
> "Here's the simple version: it's **right after school on campus**, **beginner-friendly**, and **fun-first**. We handle the setup — coaches, equipment, and drills. After dismissal, we gather the kids, run practice at the school facility, and at the end we direct **car riders to pickup** and **aftercare kids to aftercare**."

If they want reassurance: "We run a structured practice so kids can learn without pressure, and our coaches are trained and consistent."

### Season + price (value → price)
> "We run on a **6-week season**. Everything is included. Tuition is **$129 per season**."

### Assisted Checkout close
> "The easiest way is to knock it out right now while we're on the phone. I'm going to email you the registration link — once you open it, I'll walk you through step-by-step. Sound good?"

### Live registration walkthrough
1. **Confirm receipt:** "Do you see the email from Discipline Rift?" (check spam/promotions if no)
2. **Click link + find school:** "Type [School Name] and select it."
3. **Select season/sport:** "Choose the [Sport] season at [School]."
4. **Fill fields:** "Go ahead and fill out the fields. I'll stay on the line. Tell me when you're at the payment screen." *(Shut up here. Don't talk while they type.)*
5. **Payment + confirmation:** "Complete payment. Tell me when you see the confirmation screen."
6. **Confirm win + set expectations:** "Awesome — you're all set. You'll receive a confirmation email. We're excited to have [Child Name] with us."

### Stall handling
If they say "I'll do it later":
- **Tool A — Main concern:** "No problem — what's your main concern about registering today?"
- **Tool B — Money isolation (if they say price):** "If this were free, would you do it?"
  - YES → "Got it — so it's not the money. What's the real concern?"
  - NO → not qualified right now; end cleanly and keep on nurture.

> [!warning] Do **not** discount on the call. Closing warns this turns the close into haggling and destroys value.

## Objection Handling — Top 5

| # | Objection | Bucket | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Will my kid like it or quit?" | Self/Fear | "What's your main concern about them trying it?" → risk-reversal close |
| 2 | "Is it safe / is this for beginners?" | Self/Fear | Force "main concern" first → map to "safe-to-learn" structure |
| 3 | "We're busy / we'll miss practices" | Time/Circumstance | Confirm what would need to be true for yes → present simplest path (on-campus convenience, predictable season) |
| 4 | "It's too expensive" | Money | "If this were free, would you do it?" Isolate. Don't haggle. |
| 5 | "I need to check with my spouse" | Other-People | Prevent via qualification (decision-makers on call). If live: "What if they say no?" → route to what they'd object to |

## FAQ Modules (reusable, short)

- **"Does my kid need experience?"** "No — it's beginner-friendly. We teach from scratch and build confidence through reps and games."
- **"What about pickup / aftercare?"** "Car riders go to pickup. Aftercare students go to aftercare. We coordinate that flow."
- **"What's included?"** "Coaching, equipment, drills, and the full 6-week season."
- **"What if we miss a practice?"** "Totally ok — most families miss occasionally. They can jump right back in at the next session."
- **"Are coaches legit?"** "Yes — we run structured sessions designed for beginners, and coaches are trained and consistent."

## Bot Implementation Notes
- Bot's job is not to educate. It's to **answer → move to registration → stay on line until paid**.
- Same stall question every time: **"What's your main concern?"**
