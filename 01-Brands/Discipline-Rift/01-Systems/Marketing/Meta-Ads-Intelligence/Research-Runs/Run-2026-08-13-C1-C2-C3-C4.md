**C4 — ENOUGH_EVIDENCE.** Eight triggers produced. **Post-patch anchoring:** T1–T4 in first-party platform facts, T5 in a practitioner's own stated precondition, T6–T8 in DR context and structure facts. **No numeric threshold was manufactured** — T5, T7 and T8 are explicitly non-numeric, and the registry records that ~50 events/week, learning-phase status, and the 10× budget heuristic were considered and **rejected** as architecture gates in their own right.
