## Saturation decision

**E1 — ENOUGH_EVIDENCE (conditional).** Meta publishes the control/suggestion split explicitly; that decides the question. Locations hard, no interests, adult minimum age, expansion off. Residual items are DR-side configuration audits.

**E2 — ENOUGH_EVIDENCE (conditional); practitioner layer `NO_USEFUL_EVIDENCE_FOUND`.** The matrix follows from what the platform will enforce (location, minimum age, exclusions), what it cannot target (school attendance), and what is prohibited (under-13 data in payload, event names, **or criteria**). The creative-qualification hypothesis is labelled a DR HYPOTHESIS with a falsification path, not adopted as fact.

**E3 — ENOUGH_EVIDENCE (conditional).** Union of campus radii inside one ad set; the geometry needs no architecture change, so **C1/C2 REOPEN: NO**. Residents-only availability is a DR Ads Manager audit, not a research gap. The accuracy disclaimer permanently bounds geo claims.

**G1 — ENOUGH_EVIDENCE (conditional).** Supported windows retrieved first-hand; 7-day click is the ceiling, 1-day the only view/engage window. The recommendation coincides with a practitioner convention but is **not derived from it**. One stated dependency: availability by objective.

**G2 — ENOUGH_EVIDENCE (conditional).** Four-layer contract built on first-party metric definitions; the link/outbound/LPV distinction is preserved and a specific denominator error is prohibited. Layers 3–4 live in DR's systems, with availability marked honestly (AVAILABLE / UNKNOWN / PROHIBITED).
