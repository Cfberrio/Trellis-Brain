## Saturation decision

**D1 — ENOUGH_EVIDENCE (conditional).** Location settled by first-party mechanics: Meta's own creative-introduction feature operates inside the existing campaign and names relocation as what resets learnings; consolidation guidance agrees; a second ad set would fragment DR's budget and trigger a C2 reopen for no evidenced benefit. Batch size closed as a **derived capacity rule** from DR's own delivery arithmetic — no external number was adopted, and the derivation shows that **no batch size makes individual ads comparable at current delivery**, which is the more important finding. Further practitioner discovery would return the same content-farm corpus.

**D2 — ENOUGH_EVIDENCE (conditional).** The change-window procedure is fully specified and every prohibition traces to a platform fact. Meta prescribes no cadence, so the batching *preference* stays a **DR HYPOTHESIS** with a falsification path (framework §13 H2). The delivery-troubleshooting sentence — *"if changes are too frequent then your campaign will be constantly adapting and in flux"* — upgrades the principle from pure inference to partially platform-supported. **Numeric cadence: `NO_USEFUL_EVIDENCE_FOUND`.**

**D3 — ENOUGH_EVIDENCE (conditional) on the boundary; `NO_USEFUL_EVIDENCE_FOUND` on every numeric threshold.** The ladder, four kill types, and not-evaluable default are specified and anchored: Meta instructs ad-set-level evaluation with multiple ads in one ad set; Meta's own creative test ships with **no confidence level**; "no winner found" is a documented outcome; ≥7 days is a floor that Meta itself extends for long decision cycles. **No defensible spend or impression threshold exists and none was invented** — a relative exposure-comparability rule replaced them.

**D4 — ENOUGH_EVIDENCE.** Strongest question in the wave. Two first-party pages retrieved this run supply the mechanism (breakdown effect, with a worked example) and the cause list (delivery troubleshooting). The Nick tension is resolved by **splitting it**: principle transfers and is now platform-supported; the implementation does not.

## Stress-test results

20 scenarios run against the proposed method. **20 passed, 0 failed, 1 warning.**

**One rule was corrected by the stress tests.** An earlier draft allowed a dominated-performance kill on a **single** window. Tests 1 and 3 broke it — at DR's exposure, one window cannot separate weak creative from normal allocation. Corrected to require **repetition across more than one window**; the corrected form is what appears in the final method, and it changed the deliverable.

**Warning — Test 14:** the method survives a Wave 1A objective-branch change, but **State 4 (downstream business evidence) stays unreachable until registration-level signal is measurable.** The dependency is on measurement, not on which objective is chosen. Out of Wave 2B's scope; recorded.
