## Repeated patterns inside this advertiser

**One hook carries the whole parent campaign.** "Back to school, back to soccer." appears in 11 of 22 parent-facing ads, across VIDEO and CAROUSEL, across NYC and NJ variants.

**Geography written into the creative, not just targeting.** Separate variants name NYC, Manhattan, Jersey City, Hoboken and New Jersey ("Fall classes now open in Jersey City for ages 1 to 10"). A national page running city-named creative.

**Explicit age band in nearly every parent ad** ("ages 1 to 10", "ages 1–12").

**Season framing with an open-registration trigger:** "Fall classes now open", "Fall Season: September–December".

**Urgency is standard** (19/24 ads carry an urgency signal): "Spots fill fast", "Limited September spots available".

**Two distinct incentives run simultaneously:** `$45 off fall season (code IGFALL45)` on `881946464989123`, and a `100% FREE trial class` across 10 locations on `2270727753726204`.

**A carousel family that splits the message into one-benefit cards** (`1053712297304989`, `1522659398941940`, `1726936111639320`, `1063114502918048`, `2911988875807508`, `1354827263440270`, `1922947271735090`): "Every Star Starts Somewhere" / "Getting Started Is the Fun Part" / "Ages 1 to 10, All Skill Levels" / "Small Groups, Big Smiles".

**Separate B2B franchise family** (`2042597273277714`, `976092331922366`) pointed at soccerstarsfranchise.com — different buyer entirely.

## Potential relevance to DR

- **Observable:** a single seasonal hook ("Back to school, back to soccer") reused across formats and geographies. **Why:** DR's fall season sits on the same calendar trigger. **Limit:** this is NYC/NJ creative; nothing indicates it was tested in Orlando, and the phrase is generic enough that its lift is unknown.
- **Observable:** city names written into the creative itself rather than left to targeting. **Why:** DR is single-market Orlando and local clarity is a stated brand rule. **Limit:** SSS is a national brand *adding* locality; DR is already local, so the gain may be smaller.
- **Observable:** a free trial class running as its own creative line. **Why:** it is a low-friction first step for a hesitant parent. **Limit:** DR offer and guarantee decisions are governed by the DR domain, not by this file. Flagged, not recommended.
