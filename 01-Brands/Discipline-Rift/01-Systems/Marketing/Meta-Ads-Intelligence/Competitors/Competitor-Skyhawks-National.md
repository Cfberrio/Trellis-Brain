## Repeated patterns inside this advertiser

**One creative message, nine ads.** All 9 share the same title ("Sports Camps That Build Confidence") and the same body copy.

**What varies is media only:** 6 IMAGE, 3 VIDEO. CTA is `Sign up` on all 9; destination is the skyhawks.com homepage on all 9.

**Four proof/benefit elements repeat in every ad:** beginner acceptance ("picking up a ball for the first time"), social outcome ("lasting friendships"), scheduling ("flexible schedules"), and credential ("certified coaches").

**No offer, no urgency, no age band, and no sport named** — the least specific creative set in the sweep.

**No sport-specific or program-specific landing page**, in direct contrast to i9's `/ppc/<sport>` structure.
