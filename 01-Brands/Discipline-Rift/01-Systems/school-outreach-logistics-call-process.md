# School Outreach Logistics Call Process

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: When a school contact requests information, the goal is to immediately transition to booking a 'Program Logistics Call' for the following week to maintain momentum.
- **rule**: The logistics call must be framed as low-pressure and informational, explicitly stating it is 'not to finalize anything' but to explain how the program works, space needs, and student participation.
- **process**: Upon agreement, a calendar invite titled 'Discipline Rift x [School Name] — Program Logistics Call' must be sent immediately, including Luis, Yassine (as observer), and a Google Meet link.
- **rule**: If a contact refuses to book a call immediately, send the information and set a follow-up reminder for the following week.
