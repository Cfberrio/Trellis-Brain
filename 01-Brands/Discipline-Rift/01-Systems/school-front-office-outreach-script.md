# School Front Office Outreach Script

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: When calling a school front office, the primary goal is to identify the decision-maker for after-school programs, get transferred, or collect their direct contact information rather than pitching the program to the front office staff.
- **rule**: Use a neutral opening ('Hi, this is [Name]. How’s it going?') and immediately ask who handles after-school programs or clubs for lower school students to avoid being mistaken for a general solicitor.
- **rule**: If the front office asks for details, describe the program as a 'beginner-friendly sports program brought directly to campus for lower school students' that is 'one day per week' where DR 'brings the coaches and equipment.'
- **process**: If a transfer is not possible, request a specific email address and ask if the information should be addressed to a specific individual to ensure the follow-up reaches the decision-maker.
