# A. PARENTS

## A1 — Parent Nurture: "Here you go! 3-minute Parent Guide" ⭐ (signature voice)

- **Brand:** Discipline Rift
- **Channel:** Email (lead nurture / lead-magnet delivery)
- **Audience:** Parent
- **Subject:** Here you go! 3-minute Parent Guide
- **Fecha:** Jun 25, 2026
- **Contexto:** Sent to a parent who signed up for the DR Parent Guide. Delivers the promised guide + soft pitch to register.
- **Texto enviado (full):**
  > Here you go! 3-minute Parent Guide — From Coach Luis at Discipline Rift
  >
  > Hi [PARENT],
  >
  > What if this was the season your child did not just participate... But discovered something about themselves? The first time they understand a new skill. The first time they try again after struggling. The first time they feel like they belong in a group.
  >
  > Many children stop participating in organized activities before middle school... Not because they lack ability, but because they did not feel supported or capable early on.
  >
  > Here are five simple ways you can strengthen your child's experience starting this week.
  > 1. **Start Every Week with Curiosity** — ask open-ended questions after each session. Curiosity builds reflection. Reflection builds growth.
  > 2. **Create a 10-Minute Ritual** — move together twice a week. Connection, not correction.
  > 3. **Celebrate Small Wins** — notice effort, not just results. Confidence grows when progress is seen.
  > 4. **Let Them Teach You** — ask your child to explain what they learned. Teaching strengthens self-belief.
  > 5. **Talk About Growth** — perseverance, teamwork, character. Qualities that last long after any season.
  >
  > Our upcoming season is designed with that intention: Small groups. Intentional coaching. Progression-based learning. An environment where children feel capable.
  >
  > If this feels like the right next step for your child: **REGISTER FOR THE SEASON / FIND YOUR SCHOOL**
  >
  > What if this season everything shifts? — From Coach Luis, Discipline Rift
- **Intención principal:** Build trust through value, then convert to season registration.
- **Tono:** Warm, emotional, parent-empathetic, aspirational. "Coach Luis" personal voice.
- **Problema que resolvía:** Parent uncertainty about whether structured sports help their child; lowers the barrier to registering.
- **Info dada:** 5 actionable parenting tips, season design (small groups, progression-based), dual CTA (register / find your school).
- **Registro:** Commercial but soft — value-first direct response. Not administrative.

## B1 — Cold Outreach Template ⭐ (the workhorse — repeated 15+ times)

- **Brand:** Discipline Rift
- **Channel:** Email (cold B2B outreach to school)
- **Audience:** Faculty / School Admin
- **Subject:** "Volleyball at [School]?" / "Flag Football at [School]?" / "Volleyball or Flag Football at [School]?"
- **Fecha:** Recurring, Jun 18–24, 2026
- **Contexto:** Sent after a phone attempt ("I just tried to speak with you" / "I just spoke with your school"). Goal: get the school to host a DR after-school program.
- **Texto enviado (representative — Westpointe, jennifer.aprea@ocps.net):**
  > Morning Ms. Aprea! Hope you're great
  >
  > We're in the process of bringing volleyball as an after-school program for students at Westpointe.
  >
  > Every year, we partner with schools for thousands of students across Orange County to participate in our program and bring sports to the youth.
  >
  > Please let me know if you guys can accommodate any of your outdoor facilities to host our program!
- **Intención principal:** Open the door — secure facility access / a conversation to launch the program.
- **Tono:** Warm, brief, confident, professional-casual. Low-friction ask.
- **Problema que resolvía:** Gets past gatekeeping after a missed call; frames DR as established ("thousands of students," "every year").
- **Info dada:** Program (sport), that it's after-school, the facility ask, social proof.
- **Registro:** Professional but casual — relationship-opening, not formal.

## B2 — Logistics / Pricing Detail Reply ⭐ (the "qualified school" email)

- **Brand:** Discipline Rift
- **Channel:** Email
- **Audience:** Faculty / School Admin
- **Subject:** Volleyball at Potter's House Academy?
- **Fecha:** Jun 23–25, 2026
- **Contexto:** School asked for program details. DR replies with a full operational breakdown — the de-facto "school onboarding spec."
- **Texto enviado (full):**
  > Good morning Ms. Alvarez! Sure! Happy to provide more details
  > 1. **Facility Use:** We usually work directly with the school district and pay roughly $5–10 per hour of use; the agreement is based on our ability to use a specific area of the school.
  > 2. **Insurance:** We carry general liability insurance and usually provide a COI listing [School] as Additional Insured. We just need to confirm any specific wording you require.
  > 3. **Screening:** All coaches and staff undergo level 2 background screening through OCPS and will have badges to identify themselves.
  > 4. **Ratios:** Our ratio is approximately 1:10 for safety and quality instruction.
  > 5. **Family Cost:** Pricing is $129, for a six-consecutive-week season, including all necessary equipment.
  > 6. **Grades/Enrollment:** We typically serve K–5th grade students, with a minimum of 8 and a maximum of 20 participants per session.
  > 7. **Space Needed:** A pavilion, basketball court, or other hard surface/field is ideal for volleyball. Field/grass area would also work.
  > 8. **Supervision/Dismissal:** Coaches arrive before practice, meet students at a designated spot, handle check-in/out and record attendance (sent to parents as soon as done) and coordinate with aftercare as needed.
  > 9. **References:** Happy to provide references from schools currently partnering with us.
  > 10. **Agreements:** We can share an agreement for your review.
- **Intención principal:** Remove every objection a school administrator could have, in one organized message → move to agreement.
- **Tono:** Professional, thorough, organized, reassuring. Still warm ("Sure! Happy to...").
- **Problema que resolvía:** School needs to vet safety, insurance, cost, logistics before approving an outside vendor on campus.
- **Info dada:** Facility cost model, insurance/COI, background screening, 1:10 ratio, $129/6-week pricing, K–5 grades, 8–20 cap, space requirements, dismissal protocol, references, agreement offer.
- **Registro:** Administrative / institutional — but human, not stiff. This is the highest-leverage faculty email; should become a saved template/SOP.
