# DR — coach / staff: the bot must skip them (rule, 2026-09-14)

## Parent
- [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/03-Evidence/Founder-Voice/bot-training/2026-09-14-README-corpus-and-prompt-rewrite|DR prompt rewrite — corpus, decisions, validation]]


Decision: skip, including contextual coaches missed by the code pre-filter. Availability, Missing Days, blackout dates, payroll, direct deposit, employment documents, equipment returns, recruiting/interviews and shadowing belong outside parent/faculty drafting. A parent asking who the coach is, or a faculty request for DR's W9, is not a coach sender.

Verbatim GHL exchanges remain in [paired-exchanges.jsonl](paired-exchanges.jsonl), category `coach` (141 exchanges). Verbatim Gmail coach exchanges remain in `domains/ops/ghl/sms-draft/data/raw/bot-training-corpus/gmail_pairs.json`, indexed with dates, channel, counterpart, role and lesson in `domains/ops/ghl/sms-draft/data/raw/bot-training-corpus/gmail-classification.jsonl`, category `coach`. They count toward the corpus inventory, not customer voice. No employment/payment-document bodies are duplicated here.

Example: Gmail index 105, thread `1a06a6a116567b03`, “Missing Days”: sender is a coach describing absences at Hope Prep and TFA. Lesson for the bot: skip even though the historical human answered warmly.
