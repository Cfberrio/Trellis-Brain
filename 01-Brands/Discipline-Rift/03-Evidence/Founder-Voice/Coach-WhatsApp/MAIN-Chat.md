### 2025-10-31 — Luis Torres (Halloween + Conscious Coaching read)
Costume Challenge: Coach with the best outfit wins a $20 Amazon gift card. Drop a pic in this chat today.

I've summarized today's reading (~3 min). Focus: Conscious Coaching

1. Why Presence Matters — Kids read you before they hear you. Attention is the currency of learning. Intention directs repetition. Say: "My job today: calm, clear, connected."

2. During Practice — Micro-Loop (reset → encouragement tweak)
- Frame (15s): "Aim = first step. Watch my foot. You do."
- Rep (60–90s): They work; you circulate.
- Feedback (20s): "Saw 3 great first steps. Cue: knee over laces."
- Encouragement (10s): Quick boost, then go: "You're close.. just one more time taking a step." → Signal to next rep.

3. Post-Practice (2 min)
- Shout-outs (60s): 2 from peers, 1 from coach, tie to the goal.
- Exit Why (20s): One sentence: why today's focus matters.
- Tomorrow's Hook (40s): Share the next win word.

4. Micro-Tools (use all of these)
- 30-Second Reset (for you): Step out → breath → restate win word → re-enter with a name + cue.
- Catch & Release Feedback: Name → behavior → cue → let them try.
- Green/Yellow/Red Self-Check: If you're talking more than they're repping, do the 30-sec reset.

5. Behavior System: Escalation (calm, clear, brief) — If safety/respect breaks: remove from play → calm convo → return with one clear commitment.

TL;DR: Be calm, clear, connected. One win word. After feedback, add a short encouragement line, then play.
