## Standard operating rules (as communicated, recurring)
1. **Arrival** at exact scheduled time; bring ID; check in at front office; meet at spot in instruction email. Wednesdays early day (1:45–1:50 arrival, 2:15–3:15 practice).
2. **Attendance** on Coach Dashboard at gather-up + final at dismissal. No signal → screenshot roster, enter later (dashboard offline mode since Sep 2026).
3. **Missing player protocol**: verify with front office/aftercare/teacher → send responsible buddy pair (or one responsible player, 2026) → update attendance. Never assume.
4. **Dismissal**: players don't leave until released to a parent/guardian. Line up, "raise your hand if you see your parent", confirm adult, release one-by-one. Two coaches: one releases, one tracks.
5. **Court lines**: always mark a court (chalk → cones → existing lines). Care for equipment.
6. **Parent message after every practice** (from Jan 2026): within 30 min; one positive team note + one skill focus + one reminder. Dashboard "team messages" send individually; replies come back 1:1; email notifications both sides.
7. **Flyers** weeks 5–6 every season: every player gets one for home + one for a friend; scripted invite; friends are the #1 reason kids start/stay (Aspen/Project Play).
8. **End-of-season ceremony**: certificates (tier-based; move up one tier per season; expectations on back) + donut (one per player; Publix gift cards emailed to head coaches) + drum roll/shoutout per player.
9. **Weather**: rain → covered area, continue. Lightning/thunder → indoors, continue. Full policy on Ocean.
10. **Practice design**: Progression Ladder (Notion ladder is a SAMPLE only), teach in numbered positions/steps, shadow before ball, one cue per block, <30s talking, zero dead time, lines ≤3–4 kids, drills 8–10 min.

## Recurring coaching themes (Luis voice)
- Gamification + pattern interruption (K-pop Demon Hunters, Star Wars, FSU vs UCF): theme → roles → scoring → story
- Layers of difficulty / desirable difficulty (Jailbreak example)
- Micro-feedback: Name + Behavior + Cue; 3:1 positive:corrective
- Demo → Reps → Single Cue → More Reps → Celebrate
- Good cop / bad cop; "Lock in" call
- Third Circle Theory (Ghadimi) / Third Cycle (Me → We → Others)
- FUELED: Friendly, Upfront, Engaged, Listening, Efficient, Disciplined
- "Coaching is a skill set" / "We teach the sport" / "Planting seeds"
- Conscious Coaching micro-loop (Frame 15s → Rep 60–90s → Feedback 20s → Encouragement 10s)
- Split groups by age; design for every tier
