## Purpose

Hub for DR evidence + research + feedback artifacts. Each note here is **proof** — either:

- a **performance signal** (practice reports, feedback logs, workflow logs)
- a **founder voice source** (Q&A themes, interview transcripts)
- a **meeting record** (decisions captured from live conversations)
- an **AI feedback synthesis** (decision-grade analysis of real conversion threads)

Use this hub to find ground-truth before changing positioning, scripts, or playbooks.
