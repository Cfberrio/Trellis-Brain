# KnightShadow Funnel Validation Questionnaires

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The KnightShadow funnel validation process uses a five-stage questionnaire sequence (Instagram, Website, Pre-Application, Application, and Post-Application) to test attention, credibility, clarity, friction, and role expectations.
- **rule**: High-value validation questions to repeat across all funnel stages include: 'What do you think this opportunity actually is?', 'How attractive does it sound right now?', and 'What part feels unclear?'
- **process**: The final opportunity validation questionnaire focuses on overall attractiveness, real objections, motivations, and whether the target avatar (college students) would realistically consider the offer.
