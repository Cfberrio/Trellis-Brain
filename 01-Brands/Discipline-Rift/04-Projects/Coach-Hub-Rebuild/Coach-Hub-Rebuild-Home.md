## Rules that do not change with scope

1. Preserve the six-hub navigation and every canonical page ID. Resolve links by ID + sport/parent, never by "WEEK 1" alone.
2. Update specific blocks; never replace a whole page where unsupported blocks, media or unique content could be lost.
3. Preserve originals before consolidating (dated historical reference first, then restructure).
4. Do not convert examples, unverified game adaptations, individual family exceptions or missing-source topics into policy.
5. Do not contact coaches or parents, change enrollment, modify software, or delete legacy databases as part of a curriculum edit.
6. Return a change ledger: change ID, page ID, before/after summary, dependencies, outcome — kept in `INTERNAL — Curriculum Builder`.
