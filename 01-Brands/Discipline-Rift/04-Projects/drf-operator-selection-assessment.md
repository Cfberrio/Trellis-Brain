# DRF Operator Selection Assessment

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: The DRF Operator selection process uses a 15-question 'Swipe' assessment to measure initiative, ownership instinct, outreach comfort, operational stamina, and leadership maturity.
- **process**: DRF Swipe scoring logic: 8–10 points indicates a Strong Fit; 6–7 points is a Possible Fit; 4–5 points suggests a coach/intern path; 0–3 points is a Low Fit for an operator role.
- **fact**: The assessment measures specific traits: initiative (watching vs. stepping in), builder vs. supporter identity, persuasion instinct, and action bias (decisiveness with incomplete info).
