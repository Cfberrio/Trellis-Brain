## Purpose

Onboarding video script (Luis Torres voiceover). Delivered post-application to qualified candidates. ~3 minutes. Frame: this opportunity exists because Luis started where they are.

## Full script

> Congratulations!
>
> This is probably the first time you have applied to be an owner of your impact and your potential.
>
> Now, why does this opportunity exist?
>
> Because I started where you are today.
>
> I had a huge desire to do something great. The only difference is that I decided to start, and that was years ago.
>
> Just like you, there are a lot of young adults with talent, drive, sports experience, and leadership potential, but very few real opportunities that help turn those things into something meaningful.
>
> When I started building Discipline Rift, I had to learn through trial and error.
>
> I made mistakes. I learned the hard way. I had to figure out systems, operations, and execution over time.
>
> And after years of building and success, I realized something:
>
> What if we could take everything we have built and give the right person a way to step into ownership using everything we have already created?
>
> **That's why this opportunity exists.**
>
> Now, what would you actually do week to week?
>
> At the core, you would be using our playbook to lead youth sports programming in your area. That means planning seasons, running practices, and creating value for players and families.
>
> If selected, you would be stepping into a structure that already exists.
>
> Yes, you would still need to work hard.
> Yes, you would still need to be responsible.
> Yes, you would still need to lead.
>
> But you would not be doing it without direction.
>
> Now, who is this for?
>
> This is for someone who is **driven, coachable, and responsible**. Someone who wants to use their time wisely. Someone who cares about sports and making an impact. Someone who is hungry to grow.
>
> You gain real experience and real responsibility in business, entrepreneurship, and management.
>
> You gain leadership development that comes from leading people and developing coaches.
>
> You build skills in business structures, communication, organization, leadership, and decision-making.
>
> And yes, you would also be getting paid while building something of your own.
>
> We know you still have bills to pay.
>
> But I want to be clear:
>
> **The biggest value here is not just money. It is who you become through the process.**
>
> So if you applied, we may be reaching out soon to schedule a call with you.
>
> If you have questions, we are here.
>
> Maybe we will see you again soon.
