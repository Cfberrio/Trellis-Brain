# Avatar DRF — Evidencia

## Canonical Statement

- **definition**: The DRF Operator Avatar is a college-age student leader who wants ownership, structure, and a path to build something locally, rather than just a coaching job.
- **preference**: The ideal personality profile for a DRF operator is ENFJ (Primary), followed by ENTJ and ESTJ. The core formula is People-first + ownership-minded + system-friendly + action-oriented.
- **rule**: Filter candidates by their desire to build/grow rather than just coach. A 'high-fit operator' is a builder who also coaches, whereas a 'mission-aligned coach' (like the Julian archetype) lacks the appetite for outreach and admin.
- **rule**: Ideal operators must demonstrate Extraversion (for outreach/leadership) and Judging (for weekly execution and standards) over Introversion and Perceiving.
