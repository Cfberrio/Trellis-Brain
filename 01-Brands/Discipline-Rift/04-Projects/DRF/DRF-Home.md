## Purpose
DRF is the franchise/licensee program. It is a **second customer** of the DR brand — not the parent payer. DRF sells "ownership of a local DR program" to college-age student leaders.

> [!warning] Do NOT confuse DRF avatar with parent/school avatar. They are two separate funnels.
> - Parent funnel → on-campus after-school sports seasons (kids 6–12). Lives in [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/06-DNA/DNA-Home|DR DNA]].
> - DRF funnel → franchise/licensee program (college-age operators). Lives here.

## DRF Strategic Center Of Gravity
DRF wins when DR's existing playbook (curriculum, methodology, coach culture, brand consistency) can be deployed by a local operator who:
1. Owns the local execution (not just a coach)
2. Holds performance standards
3. Does outreach + builds parent trust locally
4. Stays inside HQ systems

A "great coach who tolerates building" is the failure case. A "builder who also coaches" is the win case.
