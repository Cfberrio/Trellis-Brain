## Purpose
End-to-end licensee acquisition funnel for DRF — from Instagram ad → operator-in-training. This is **not** the parent funnel.

## Funnel Stages

### Stage 1 — Instagram Ad
**Asset:** short-form video on IG.
**CTA in current form:** comment with name + sport background.

**Known issues (from Knight Shadow feedback):**
- Vague about which sports are offered
- Repetitive B-roll (same kids, coaches, practices appearing in every video)
- Lack of follow-on information in caption + bio leads to "scam vibe"
- Sound effects + changing font sizes create stimulation overload
- Trust gap — no clear "where this is coming from"

**Fix direction:** more specific positioning (six-figures / franchise / coaching opportunity), reduce repetitive clips, add visual brand consistency (font + style match website + ad → landing page).

### Stage 2 — Lead Capture Form
**Capture:** basic info (name, phone, email, sport experience with years), location.

**Reminder preferences (5 students surveyed):** email-leaning, ~2 reminders max, short clear message with link to finish.

> [!important] Always include applicant location at form submission. Used for territory assignment + follow-up routing.

### Stage 3 — Pre-Application Video
**Goal:** filter for interest with quick digestible info before asking for full application.

**Knight Shadow feedback:**
- Day-to-day duties were unclear
- "Add value before getting people to do anything" — provide a deliverable
- "I want to know what the blueprint is about and why I should fill out the form to find out"

**Fix direction:**
- Keep video around **1 min 30 sec**
- Add bullet points on the page for quick takeaways
- Reduce "Luis reaches for the camera" — add text + graphics for professionalism

### Stage 4 — Swipe Application + Short-Answer
**Knight Shadow feedback (5 students):**
- Swipe questions worded unclearly led to wrong-answer swipes (consider 1–5 Likert scale instead)
- Short-answer felt right amount — but consider optional resume upload
- Application UX broke on laptop/desktop (test cross-device)
- Wanted "specific sports" question for personalization

### Stage 5 — Post-Application Video
**Goal:** trust + position clarity + reduce drop-off.

**Knight Shadow feedback:**
- Wanted training process clarity
- Wanted role pay / time-commitment clarity (internship vs PT vs FT?)
- Wanted "why this won't fail" framing — most franchises fail

**Recommended video sequence (per Knight Shadow Final Feedback):**
1. **Philosophy & mission** — why the opportunity exists
2. **Commitment** — what's expected week-to-week
3. **Financials** — clear, straightforward breakdown
4. **Day-in-the-life** — TikTok-style real tasks + tools

Length: post-app videos can be slightly longer (3–4 min, **5 min max**), keep total content digestible.

### Stage 6 — Knight Shadow Day / Discovery Call
**Format:** structured simulation where the candidate runs through the operator process (review IG content → understand expectations → identify gaps).

**Used in Phase 1 to validate funnel.**

## Bottleneck Inventory (from Knight Shadow)

| Stage | Bottleneck |
|---|---|
| Ad | Trust gap, ambiguity about sport, visual overload |
| Form | Location not captured |
| Pre-App video | Day-to-day unclear, "give before ask" missing |
| Application | UX issues on swipe (wrong answer risk), no resume option, no specific-sport field |
| Post-App video | No structured 4-segment sequence yet |

Action items consolidated in [[Improvement-Log-DRF-Phase-1]].

## Coach Content Requirement
Per Knight Shadow synthesis: **make coaches a required content creator** for brand consistency. Each licensee should produce ongoing content that aligns with DRF philosophy and brand values. Standardize visual style (font, ad-to-website match) across all ads + landing pages.
