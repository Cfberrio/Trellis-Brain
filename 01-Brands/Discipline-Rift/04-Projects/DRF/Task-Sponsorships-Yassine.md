# Task — Secure Two Sponsorships (Food/Retail + Healthcare)

## Parent
- [[01-Brands/Discipline-Rift/04-Projects/DRF/DRF-Home|DRF Home]]

## Assigned to
Yassine

## Objective
Secure two sponsorships for Discipline Rift:

1. **Food/retail sponsor** — a local donut shop or Publix. Ties directly into the season-end donut awards we already give players.
2. **Healthcare sponsor** — AdventHealth or Nemours Children's Hospital.

## Why this matters
Season-end donut awards are a standing part of the DR player experience. A sponsor covering or co-branding that cost turns an existing expense into a marketing asset and a local credibility signal. A healthcare sponsor (AdventHealth or Nemours) adds institutional trust to a youth sports brand — parents read hospital-backed as safety-and-legitimacy, not just "another sports program."

## Standard
Time commitment: minimum 1 hour per day, every day, until one of these is closed. Log what you tried and what happened — dead ends count as progress if we know not to repeat them.

## Scope
- Sponsorship #1: any donut shop, or Publix (grocery/retail angle).
- Sponsorship #2: AdventHealth or Nemours Children's Hospital.

## What's already in your favor
- You're a UCF student — UCF has business school connections, alumni networks, career services, and community partnership offices that smaller operators don't have access to.
- DR already has a real, ongoing youth sports program with real kids in it — that's the credibility a sponsor is buying into, not a pitch deck.

## Brainstorm — not the plan, just starting fuel
These are angles to consider, not instructions. Find the one that actually moves and go deep on it instead of spreading thin across all of them.

- Donut angle: local shop sponsors the season-end award directly (product donation, discount, or co-branded box) in exchange for logo placement / mention at the event.
- Publix: community sponsorship or grant programs, store-level manager relationships, or their existing youth/community giving programs.
- AdventHealth / Nemours: youth sports safety, injury prevention, or community health angles — hospitals often have community outreach or marketing budgets specifically for youth programs.
- UCF-specific: business/marketing student orgs, alumni in sponsorship or hospital marketing roles, career center intro requests.

## Definition of done
A signed or verbally committed sponsorship (product, cash, or in-kind) from at least one donut shop/Publix AND meaningful traction (a real contact, a meeting booked, or a written proposal in front of a decision-maker) on AdventHealth or Nemours.

## Next step
Yassine picks the first angle and starts today. Report back with what's been tried and where it stands — no need to wait until it's fully closed to check in.
