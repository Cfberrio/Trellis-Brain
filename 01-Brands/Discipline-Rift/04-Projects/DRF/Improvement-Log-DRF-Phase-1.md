## Purpose
Structured action items from the Knight Shadow Day pilot (5 UCF students simulated being future DRF licensees). Each item is a recommended program improvement for the next Phase.

## Action Items

### 1. Capture Location After Form
- **Action:** Add a **location/address** field on the application form.
- **Why:** Capture applicant location for territory assignment + follow-up routing.

### 2. Video Content
- **Repetitive clips:** Replace repetitive B-roll of kids. Use more diverse footage.
- **Pre-app video:** Add bullet points on the pre-application landing page for quick digestible info. Keep video short and clear.
- **Detailed follow-up video:** After the application, give more detailed insights with segments on:
  - Philosophy and mission
  - Commitment expected from licensees
  - Financials — clear, simple breakdown
  - Day-in-the-life (TikTok-style)
- **Lengths:** First video ≈ 1 min 30 sec for engagement. Follow-up 3–4 min, **5 min max**.

### 3. Video Sequence (in this order)
1. **Philosophy & mission** — why the opportunity exists
2. **Commitment** — what's expected week-to-week
3. **Financials** — clear, straightforward breakdown
4. **Day-in-the-life** — show real tasks + tools

Each video builds on the previous one.

### 4. Engagement & Entertainment
- Make videos more entertaining for attention retention.
- Use short, entertaining clips (day-in-the-life style).
- Feature coaches comfortable on camera, sharing experiences + insights + why they believe in the program.

### 5. Coaches As Content Creators
- Make it a **requirement** for all franchisees to produce content for brand consistency + engagement.
- Set a standard for content type expected from each franchisee.
- Make content creation a core part of the operator role.
- Highlight coaches + their experiences.
- Ensure ads + DR/DRF content share the same style and font.

### 6. Application Process
- Keep the application simple — under **5 minutes**.
- Ask for basic info: name, phone, email, sport experience (with years).
- Use a simple lead-magnet strategy (small form → quick swipe application → text follow-up with next steps).
- Remove friction.

### 7. Brand Consistency For Franchisees
- Require franchisees to produce consistent content.
- Set a standard.
- Make content creation a core part of their role.
- Ads or any DR/DRF info should share the same style and font.

## Additional Insights

### Video Structure Principle
The first video should be short + simple — a filter for interest. Once they submit details, follow up with more detailed videos.

### Why This Matters
A clear video sequence combined with a simple, low-friction application increases conversion rates and attracts candidates who understand the opportunity — reducing unnecessary drop-offs.
