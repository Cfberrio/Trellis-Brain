# UCF RSO Registration and Youth Protection Standards

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: RSO registration requires 2–4 authorized officers, including a President, who must complete the RSO Authorized Officer Quiz on Webcourses.
- **fact**: Department sponsorship (e.g., Kinesiology or Health Sciences) is required to obtain UCF insurance and the right to use UCF marks; without it, the org has no UCF insurance or marks.
- **rule**: Youth Protection Policy 2-005 requires events with minors to be registered in Squire 45 days in advance, FL Level 2 background checks, biennial training, a safety plan, and proof of insurance.
