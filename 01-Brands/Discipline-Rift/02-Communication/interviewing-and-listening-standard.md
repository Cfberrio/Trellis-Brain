# Interviewing and Listening Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: When interviewing candidates, listen for their specific professional vocabulary (e.g., 'scaffolding', 'task analysis') and use those exact terms to explain the Discipline Rift method to build instant recognition and rapport.
- **rule**: Aim to talk less than a third of the time during an interview; if you have spoken for more than five minutes straight, stop and ask a question to ensure the candidate is heard.
- **process**: Use storytelling rather than abstract claims to land points: describe specific parent interactions or coach success stories to illustrate the program's impact.
- **rule**: Acknowledge when you are going off-script or talking too much out loud to disarm the candidate and reset the conversational balance.
