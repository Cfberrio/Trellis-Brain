# DR Third-Party and Competitor Communication Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Named organizations may be mentioned as a factual referral but must never be characterized or assessed regarding their quality, pricing, coaches, or culture.
- **rule**: Do not make negative claims about unnamed organizations; differentiate by describing Discipline Rift's own standards and written methods instead of highlighting others' failures.
- **rule**: Do not compare job applicants against current employees or use current staff as a benchmark to be beaten during interviews.
- **rule**: Only name third parties (such as former coaches) when their details are verified and necessary; otherwise, anonymize the story to focus on the teaching point.
