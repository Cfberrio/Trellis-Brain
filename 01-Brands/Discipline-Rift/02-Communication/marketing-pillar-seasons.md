# Marketing Pillar — Seasons

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: The 'SEASONS' marketing pillar must treat each sports season as a continuous narrative (start, development, and evolution) rather than just an enrollment announcement.
- **process**: The seasonal narrative scales through six stages: announcement, first moments, adaptation/difficulty, visible progress, team identity, and closing/next steps.
- **preference**: Content should prioritize transparency and humanization, such as 'Day 1 vs Reality' and 'Week 1 vs Week 3' comparisons to evidence evolution.
- **rule**: Seasonal communication must generate feelings of expectation, urgency, progress, belonging, and constant movement.
