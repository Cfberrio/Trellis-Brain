# DR Brand Story and Pitch Framework

## Canonical Statement

- **fact**: Discipline Rift was founded in 2021 by Luis Torres and co-founder Diego.
- **definition**: The name 'Discipline' refers to both the sport (e.g., volleyball) and the value carried into life; 'Rift' represents the 'before and after' transformation in a player.
- **preference**: The brand story should be told in five parts: 1. Origin, 2. Name Meaning, 3. The Problem (Access/Logistics), 4. Differentiation (Coaches who teach), 5. The Method (Step-by-step layers).
- **rule**: When explaining the method, use the '10-step ladder' analogy: teach the first step before moving to the 10th, ensuring students learn the basics (like catching) before complex drills.
- **rule**: Interviews and pitches should be kept under eight minutes, using pauses to check for engagement rather than speaking without breaks.
