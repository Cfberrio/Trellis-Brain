# Content Pillars and Strategy

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Discipline Rift content is organized into five specific pillars: Coaches (mindset/culture), Storytelling (micro-stories of growth), Education (sports correction/teaching), Seasons (narrative of the cycle), and Trends (contextualized viral dynamics).
- **rule**: The Education pillar follows a specific logic: show the mistake, the intervention, the correction, and the improvement.
- **process**: Content ideas are prioritized based on seasonal events, active promotions, immediate business needs, and maintaining a balance across the defined content pillars.
- **definition**: A 'strong idea' for Discipline Rift must be tellable as a story, suggest a powerful hook, serve a strategic purpose (educate/trust/positioning), lead to a natural CTA, and use language a parent understands.
