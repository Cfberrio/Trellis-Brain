# DR Lead Magnet Sequence

Every message from **a parent opting in** (popup / guide download / newsletter) through **their first registration**. Goal: move interest → registration by giving away genuinely useful parent guidance that also reframes what a good program looks like — and DR is the answer.

- **Capture:** Name + Email (phone optional). One guide offered per popup / QR.
- **Reciprocity first:** nearly every email hands over a usable [[../Templates/Parent-Guides-Library|Parent Guide]] before it ever asks for a registration.
- **Channel rule:** email-led. SMS only if the parent gave a phone + consent, and only twice (deliver + one CTA). Front-loaded, then drops to the monthly newsletter.
- **No public pricing** anywhere. Merge syntax `{UPPERCASE}`. Every email carries `{UNSUBSCRIBE_URL}`.

## 1 — The one question after practice · Email
Pure reciprocity — a tool they can use tonight. Guide 2 from the library. No ask.

**Subject:** The one question to ask after every practice
**Preview:** Works even on a rough day.

```
Hi {PARENT_FIRST_NAME},

If you take one thing from us, take this. After any practice — any sport — ask your child:

"What's one thing that felt easier today than it did last week?"

Why it works:
• It's specific, so they can't just say "fine."
• It's about progress, not winning.
• It works on a bad day too — something usually felt easier, even if it's "remembering my water bottle."
• It teaches them to notice their own growth instead of waiting to be told.

What to do with their answer? Don't fix it. Don't add advice. Just say: "That's growth. Let's see what feels easier next week."

Do it for three weeks and you'll have a kid who tracks their own progress.

Coaching happens at practice. Belief happens at home.

The Discipline Rift Team

```

## 2 — Reframe: how to spot overpromising · Email
Guide 3. Positions DR by contrast — the red flags are exactly the things DR doesn't do (no scholarship-pipeline talk, coach ≠ salesperson, real comms system, progress over trophies).

**Subject:** 4 red flags a youth program is overpromising
**Preview:** We'd rather lose your business than oversell.

```
Hi {PARENT_FIRST_NAME},

Before you commit to any program this season, here are four things worth watching for:

🚩 1. They mention a "scholarship pipeline" without numbers. Real numbers are checkable. Programs that pitch college outcomes without naming a single recipient are selling hope, not a pathway.

🚩 2. The coach is also the salesperson. The person who pitches you on price shouldn't be the one teaching on Tuesday. If they're the same person, there's no system — just a personality.

🚩 3. There's no parent communication system. If "we'll text you if there's a problem" is the whole plan, you'll know nothing for 12 weeks.

🚩 4. They brag more about wins than progress. Watch their Instagram. If it's all trophy photos and no practice, development is incidental, not intentional.

We hold ourselves to the other side of all four. We'd rather lose your business than oversell it.

The Discipline Rift Team

{UNSUBSCRIBE_URL}
```

**Preview:** A 6-question checklist.

```
Hi {PARENT_FIRST_NAME},

A 6-year-old and a 12-year-old learn completely differently. Here's a quick checklist to see if a program is built around your child's stage — or just the coach's schedule:

☐ Are players grouped by age, not just who showed up?
☐ Is the coach's language age-appropriate during practice?
☐ Do drills change format based on the age group?
☐ Is there a clear "fun-first floor" for the youngest kids?
☐ Is there a real "challenge ceiling" for the oldest?
☐ Can the coach explain — out loud — why a drill fits that age?

5 or more checked → built around your player's stage.
3 or fewer → built around the coach's schedule.
```

## Implementation notes
- **#0 fires instantly on opt-in** — delivery speed is the trust signal.
- Map one Parent Guide per email; the library has 5 (add "5 Life Lessons Volleyball Teaches" or "5 Questions" as swaps/A-B tests).
- SMS appears **twice max** (deliver + offer) and only with phone + consent. No phone → email-only sequence, unchanged.
- No dollar figures anywhere. "Low cost — everything included" only; price shows only at checkout.
- Every email carries `{UNSUBSCRIBE_URL}`. Honor STOP on SMS.
- If the contact **registers mid-sequence**, stop this sequence and hand off to [[DR-Registration-Sequence]].
- Segment by requested guide where possible — a parent who grabbed the "age-fit" guide is a different lead than one who grabbed "5 life lessons volleyball." Same touch count, more relevance.
