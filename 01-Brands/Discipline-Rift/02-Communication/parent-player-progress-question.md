# Parent-Player Progress Question

## Canonical Statement

- **preference**: Parents should ask their child 'What's one thing that felt easier today than it did last week?' after every practice to frame the experience around progress rather than performance.
- **rule**: Avoid vague or binary questions like 'How was practice?', 'Did you have fun?', or outcome-based questions like 'Did you win?', as they train children to value the wrong metrics.
- **rule**: When a child answers the progress question, parents should validate it simply ('That's growth') without adding advice or trying to 'fix' the experience.
- **fact**: Consistent use of progress-based questioning (3-12 weeks) helps players transition from tracking their own growth to becoming self-motivated.
