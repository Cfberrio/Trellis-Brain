# DR Content Strategy Framework

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: The DR Spring Season content framework follows a non-optional monthly distribution: 40% Visible Transformation (technical progress), 30% Coach Leadership (authority/Mic'd Up), 20% Parental Pride/Culture (emotional/learning from defeat), and 10% Community/Milestones.
- **rule**: DR content must prioritize 'Visible Transformation' (Before/After) because the brand sells visible progress; without showing clear technical improvement, there is no reason for parents to choose the program.
- **decision**: Confirmed recurring content series for DR include: 'Así empezó / Así va' (weekly transformation reels), 'Mic’d Up DR' (bi-monthly coach leadership reels), and 'Lo que aprendimos' (culture-building reels focused on process and re-framing defeat).
