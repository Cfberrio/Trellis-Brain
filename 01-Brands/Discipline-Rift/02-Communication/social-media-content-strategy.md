# Social Media Content Strategy

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: DR content must demonstrate three core elements: Visible Transformation (proof), Coach Leadership (authority), and Culture/Belonging (tribe/parental pride).
- **process**: The Spring content framework follows a specific monthly distribution: 40% Visible Transformation, 25% Coach Leadership, 20% Parent Pride/Emotional Proof, 10% Process Transparency, and 5% Culture/Tribe.
- **rule**: Content production rules: 70-80% must be Reels; no institutional carousels without narrative; every defeat must produce one piece of content (Lost -> What happened -> Correction -> Current state).
- **rule**: The 'Mic'd Up' series is a fixed requirement, always featuring a real-time correction followed by an immediate result with one clear teaching cue in text.
