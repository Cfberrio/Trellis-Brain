# Social Media Content Strategy and Structures

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: DR social media strategy requires five mandatory content structures: Identity Callout + Insider Fact (weekly), Contrarian + 5 Proofs (2x weekly), BTS Momentum (2x monthly), Preview Week (per milestone), and Postura DR (weekly).
- **rule**: Identity Callout reels must open by naming a specific tribe (Parent, Coach, or School Name) followed by an 'insider fact' to build status and belonging.
- **rule**: Contrarian content must use a polarizing hook (e.g., 'Is wall passing useless?') followed by a rapid 'avalanche' of 5 visual proof points to establish authority.
- **rule**: Postura DR content must take a firm educational stance on coaching values (e.g., 'punishment is not an educational strategy') to differentiate from traditional coaching.
