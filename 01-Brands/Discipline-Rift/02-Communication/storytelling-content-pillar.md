# Storytelling Content Pillar

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: The Storytelling pillar focuses on real micro-stories from the Discipline Rift universe (parents, kids, practices) to show the program's impact rather than selling it directly.
- **process**: Each storytelling piece follows a standard narrative structure: Hook → Base Situation → Development → Emotional Moment → Closure.
- **rule**: Storytelling is divided into three levels: Level 1 (Parents' emotional role/identification), Level 2 (Players' emotional experience/growth), and Level 3 (Practice intensity/authenticity).
- **preference**: Content must feel authentic and recognizable, avoiding fictional stories or generic 'DR is good' claims in favor of showing specific moments like a child finally succeeding or a coach correcting a drill.
