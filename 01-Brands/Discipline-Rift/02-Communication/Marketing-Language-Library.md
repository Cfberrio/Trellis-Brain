## Excluded — DO NOT USE (irrelevant or off-brand)
- human rights (use "Children's Bill of Rights in Sports" instead)
- equity toolkit
- Children's Rights (generic — use "all children" + "Bill of Rights in Sports")
- immigrant children
- government raids of parks
- private equity (as primary frame)
- growing influence of technology
- reshaping the youth sport landscape (too abstract)
- disjointed landscape / fragmented / uncoordinated (too abstract)
- municipal budgets cut / local recreation programs suffered / private providers flourished
- range of sources
- casual sport offerings buoyed participation
- recovered / rebound in participation (macro trend, not needed in DR ads)

## How To Use
1. Choose **one** parent outcome (Category 1) as the primary promise
2. Choose **one** pain (Category 2) as what we're escaping
3. Choose **convenience** (Category 3) as the unique unlock
4. Add **trust** (Category 4) only if the asset has room
5. Always end with a clear next-step (avoid soft generic CTAs)

For paid ads, lead with a hook-ready contrast phrase (Category 10) → meat → CTA.
