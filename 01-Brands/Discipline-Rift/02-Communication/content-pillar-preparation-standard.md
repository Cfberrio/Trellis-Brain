# Content Pillar Preparation Standard

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: Content editors must develop ideas using a 7-step structure: Pillar/Category, Content Idea, Hook, Reference, Execution, Brand Value, and Variation/Series.
- **rule**: Content pillars for the brand include Coaches, Storytelling, Team Culture, and Educational content.
- **preference**: Content should prioritize being relatable for parents, showing real practice dynamics, and humanizing the coaches through simple editing and POV shots.
