# Partnership and Sponsorship Strategy

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Corporate donut partners (e.g., Krispy Kreme, Dunkin’) are preferred over local shops for sponsorships due to scale (600 students), shelf-life, liability, and multi-location gift-card support.
- **rule**: Outreach emails for community partnerships must be short, warm, and non-salesy, using subjects like 'Community outreach help' and avoiding stats, asks, or partnership menus in the initial contact.
- **process**: Prioritize individualized emails (no BCC) to key partnership contacts, focusing the call to action on interest and advocacy rather than a direct sale.
