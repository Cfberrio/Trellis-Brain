# Education Content Pillar

## Canonical Statement

- **process**: The 'Education' content pillar converts sports teaching into dynamic content where tips feel like real training moments rather than a classroom setting.
- **rule**: Education content must follow a specific 6-step scale: show the error, coach stops the action, quick explanation, demonstrate correct form, retry, and show improvement.
- **preference**: Content hooks should create a sense of urgency or immediate correction, such as 'Stop. Do it again' or 'This is what everyone does wrong.'
- **definition**: The Education pillar includes eight sub-formats: Common Error, Real-time Correction, Before vs After, Game IQ, Mini Breakdown, Frustrating Errors, Misunderstood Skills, and Ignored Details.
