# Content Strategy and Messaging Pillars

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: DR content must lead with psychology and identity protection (confidence, fear of failure, process) rather than technical drills or logistics.
- **rule**: The content strategy is built on five mandatory pillars: Coaches (authority/criterio), Storytelling (emotional growth), Education (visible learning), Seasons (ongoing narrative), and Trends (humor/POV).
- **process**: Every practice must follow a capture checklist: error, correction, repetition, reaction, cinematic detail, and huddle/closing.
- **decision**: 70–80% of DR social content must be Reels, focusing on serial narratives rather than isolated clips.
- **rule**: Adopt 'Identity Protection Messaging': emphasize that the child isn't 'behind', confidence isn't built by pressure, and DR is a safe space to grow.
