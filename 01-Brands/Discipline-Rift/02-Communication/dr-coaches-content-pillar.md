# DR Coaches Content Pillar

## Canonical Statement

- **preference**: Discipline Rift uses a 'Coaches' content pillar to put coaches at the center of the narrative, showing the criteria, energy, and culture behind the practice rather than just highlights.
- **rule**: The Coaches pillar is divided into three levels: Level 1 (Mindset/Culture), Level 2 (Humor/Relatable Chaos), and Level 3 (Educational/Technical Authority).
- **rule**: Content style for coaches must feel organic and real; Level 1 uses close-up observational shots, Level 2 uses fast-paced skits/trends, and Level 3 uses clean instructional visuals like motion graphics.
