Subject: How Far They've Come in [Sport]. Final Practice Tomorrow

Hi [Parent First Name],

[Player First Name]'s final DR [Sport] practice of the [Season] season is tomorrow.

This last week is about bringing everything together: playing, recognizing progress, and celebrating how far the team has come.

Coach [Coach Name] will bring back favorite drills, activities, and games from throughout the season and give players plenty of opportunities to use what they've learned.

Tomorrow, [Player First Name] will:

- Revisit favorite drills and games from the season.
- Bring together skills learned over the past several weeks.
- Spend more time playing and applying what they know.
- Use their skills in game-like situations.
- See how much more comfortable and capable they have become.

The game becomes much more enjoyable when players understand what they're doing and feel capable participating in it.

Tomorrow gives [Player First Name] the opportunity to experience [Sport] with skills and knowledge they didn't have when the [Season] season began.

We'll also finish practice by celebrating the team.

Each player will be recognized for the effort and progress they made throughout the season. We'll award certificates, applaud our players, and give them a moment to be proud of what they accomplished together.

That celebration matters to us.

DR is an environment where [Player First Name] should feel recognized for their effort, supported by Coach [Coach Name] and their teammates, and excited about continuing to play [Sport].

It's also a chance for everyone to look back at where the team started and appreciate how much they've grown together.

Our goal isn't only for [Player First Name] to leave knowing more about [Sport].

We want them to leave wanting to keep playing it.

[Player First Name]'s Final Practice

Day: [Day]
Schedule: [Practice Schedule]
Location: [Location]

Thank you, [Parent First Name], for allowing Coach [Coach Name] and Discipline Rift to be part of [Player First Name]'s [Season] season.

We're excited to celebrate with [Player First Name] tomorrow.

Discipline Rift Coaching Team
