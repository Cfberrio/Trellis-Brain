## Purpose

Canonical 6-week parent email sequence for the DR volleyball season. Sent the day before each weekly practice. Combines **reminder** (practice details) + **education** (week's skill focus).

This is the **operational template family** that maps to the delivery rhythm — each email is anchored to a specific week's skill focus from the DR coaching curriculum.

## Structure (consistent across all 6 weeks)

```
Subject: Reminder: Volleyball Practice Tomorrow (Week X – Focus Area)

Hi [Parent/Guardian Name],

Just a friendly reminder that DR Volleyball practice is tomorrow.

Week X Focus: [Skill]
[1–2 paragraphs describing what players will learn this week]

What to bring (Week 1 only) / Weekly Reminder (Week 2+):
- Water bottle, athletic shoes, comfortable clothes, knee pads optional

Practice Details:
Date: [Day, Date]
Time: [Start – End]
Location: [Location]

[On-time arrival + prompt pickup paragraph]

[Reinforcement of value + Parent Dashboard contact instruction]

Discipline Rift Coaching Team
```

## Skill arc (curricular sequence)

| Week | Skill | Why this position in the arc |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Passing & Setting | Foundation — ball control + teamwork before anything else |
| 2 | Serving | Self-paced skill — start the rally with confidence |
| 3 | Attacking | Decision-making on offense |
| 4 | Defending | Effort + reading the play |
| 5 | Movement | Connective tissue — link all prior skills |
| 6 | Communication | Team identity — culminates the season |

## Adaptation rules

When adapting this sequence to a non-volleyball sport:
- Keep the **6-week arc** structure (foundation → individual skill → decision-making → effort → movement → team identity).
- Map sport-specific skills to each week.
- Keep the **subject line format** consistent: `Reminder: <Sport> Practice Tomorrow (Week N – <Focus>)`.
- Keep the **Practice Details + on-time/pickup + Parent Dashboard** anchor in every email.
- Keep the **What to bring** block in Week 1, then collapse to a "Weekly Reminder" line Weeks 2–6.

## Voice anchors

- "Help every player learn the game, grow in confidence, and enjoy being part of a team."
- "Learn how to attack helps players feel confident and capable on the court."
- "Defense builds effort, focus, and resilience, skills that carry beyond the court."
- "Improved movement often leads to confidence both on and off the court."
- "Watching your player grow this season, both in skill and confidence."

Tone is **operational warmth**: practical reminder + reinforcement of the developmental promise. Never sales-pushy.
