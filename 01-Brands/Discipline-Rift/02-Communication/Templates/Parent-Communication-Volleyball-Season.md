## Purpose

Canonical 6-week parent email sequence, sent **the day before** each weekly practice. Originally volleyball-only (see the two changelogs below for that history). As of 2026-08-31 this is Luis's final draft: one shared sequence for every DR sport, built around a **developmental-stage arc** (Getting Started → Recall & Rebuild → Advancement → Awareness → Making It Stick → Bringing It Together) instead of sport-specific skill names. That's what makes it genuinely sport-agnostic without being generic — every week still says something concrete about what happens in practice, it just doesn't depend on knowing that sport's curriculum.

## Developmental arc (why this order)

| Week | Stage | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Getting Started | Coach intro, foundational movements introduced |
| 2 | Recall & Rebuild | Recall last week, reinforce technique, layer in new related skills |
| 3 | Advancement | Precision, coordination, combining skills, less predictable situations |
| 4 | Awareness | Game awareness, decision-making, team mindset (effort, communication, resilience) |
| 5 | Making It Stick | Independence — players make decisions and combine skills without the coach prompting every step |
| 6 | Bringing It Together | Play, celebrate, season wrap-up |

This progression is genuinely sport-agnostic (foundation → recall → precision → awareness → independence → celebration is true of learning any physical skill), which is what lets one sequence cover every DR sport without needing a per-sport skill lookup the way the previous draft did.

## Voice anchors

- "The goal is for [Player First Name] to understand the movements, begin performing them correctly, and start seeing how they connect to [Sport]."
- "Sometimes a player is only one adjustment away from succeeding."
- "We want the learning to increasingly belong to [Player First Name]."
- "Our goal isn't only for [Player First Name] to leave knowing more about [Sport]. We want them to leave wanting to keep playing it."

Tone is **operational warmth** with more coaching-philosophy depth than the previous draft: practical reminder + a real window into how DR teaches, not just what time practice is. Never sales-pushy.
