## Purpose
Active template library for school outreach across public and private schools. Variables: `{{business.name}}`, `[Name]`, `[School Name]`, `[Last Name]`.

> [!warning] Always attach the flyer. No all-caps subject lines.

## A. Public School Cold Outreach

### Subject Lines
- `Volleyball or Flag Football at {{business.name}}?`
- `Volleyball at {{business.name}}?`
- `Flag football at {{business.name}}?`

### Template — Volleyball + Flag Football

> Good morning [Name]!
>
> It was great speaking with you, we're trying to check on your facility availability to make volleyball or flag football accessible for students.
>
> 1. We can make volleyball accessible for families by bringing equipment and skilled coaches directly to campus.
> 2. Practices are 6 consecutive weeks per season, with multiple developmentally appropriate seasons per school year. We actually teach and welcome for first time students.
> 3. Youth sports participation is becoming encouraged across the country, and parents are starting to ask to help children with confidence, health, and well-being.
>
> Every year, thousands of students across Orange County participate in our programs.
>
> We need space for one hour after dismissal, would one day per week be too much to ask? Are you the right person to coordinate this, or should I connect with another faculty directly?
>
> Take care,
> [Name]

## C. Public School Comeback Outreach

**Subject:** `VOLLEYBALL AT [School Name]` (use natural capitalization in email body)

> Hi Mrs. [Last Name]! Happy Thursday
>
> I wanted to let you know that we're planning to bring back volleyball for Fall 2026. We have already submitted our Facilitron reservations and are preparing logistics.
>
> Look forward to making volleyball accessible on campus again! With a beginner-friendly program that helps students build confidence, skills, and teamwork.
>
> 1. **Facility Reservations** — already sent for the fall season through Facilitron.
> 2. **Fall Season Start Date** — currently scheduled to start the week of **August 31st**.
> 3. **Meet the Teacher Event** — looking forward to joining you for the Meet the Teacher event. Once you have the information, please send it so we can plan accordingly.
> 4. **Support From Our Side** — we would love to participate, connect with families, and continue adding value to your school. Please let us know if you need any help from our side.
>
> Please let us know if there is anything else needed from our side as we prepare for the fall.
>
> Thank you, and we look forward to working with you this next year!
>
> Best,

## D. Private School Cold Outreach

**Subject:** `Volleyball for lower school students?`

> Hi Ms. [Last Name],
>
> I wanted to reach out and ask if [School Name] would be interested in bringing a beginner-friendly volleyball program for lower school students.
>
> At Discipline Rift, we make sports more accessible by bringing trained coaches, equipment, and structured practices directly to campus.
>
> For private schools, the program is usually simple: one day per week, about one hour after dismissal, with a focus on helping younger students learn the fundamentals and build confidence before middle school athletics.
>
> I attached the flyer here for context.
>
> Would this be something worth reviewing for your lower school students?
>
> Thank you,
> [Signature]
