# DR Social Media Content Pillars and Structures

## Canonical Statement

- **decision**: Discipline Rift content strategy for the next 2 weeks requires 2 pieces/week of Identity Challenges (quizzes), 2 pieces/week of Reframes (pain-to-power), and 2 pieces/week of Age/Situation Specificity (K-2, Week 1, etc.).
- **rule**: The 'Identity Quiz' content structure must use a hook to prove belonging (e.g., 'Madridista' or 'Beginner Parent'), a 3-question challenge, and a score-based CTA to drive engagement and status.
- **rule**: The 'Limiting Belief Demolition' structure must replace common excuses (e.g., 'not athletic') with a controllable technical path (sequence over strength) to eliminate shame and increase retention.
- **rule**: The 'Pain-to-Power Reframe' structure converts emotional pain (e.g., being picked last) into a signal of status or growth potential, providing immediate relief and a reason to return.
