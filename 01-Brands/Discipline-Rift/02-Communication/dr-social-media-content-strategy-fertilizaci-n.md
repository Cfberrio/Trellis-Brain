# DR Social Media Content Strategy - Fertilización

## Canonical Statement

- **preference**: DR social media content should prioritize 'Experience > Training', selling identity and memory through rituals like check-in lines and team chants rather than just drills.
- **preference**: Content should use 'Vulnerability + Hard Work' (Underdog real) to show behavioral change, such as a child moving from shyness to confidence (Week 1 vs Week 6).
- **preference**: DR uses 'Myth-busting with respect' to address parent/kid doubts (e.g., 'is my child too late to start?') using a binary question hook followed by 3 simple rules.
- **preference**: Marketing should include 'Democratic BTS' showing real campus life (high fives, mini-celebrations) to create social FOMO and show the program is insider and spontaneous.
