# Social Media Benchmarks and Strategy

## Canonical Statement

- **rule**: Discipline Rift social media output is set at 1–2 pieces per week, with a requirement that at least one piece builds 'real proof' of the program (volleyball, coaching, parents, or registration).
- **fact**: Weekly growth benchmarks for DR social media are: Followers +0.5% to +1.5%, Likes +1% to +3%, Reach/Views +3% to +6%, with 1–2 saves and 1–3 shares per week.
- **rule**: DR content strategy prioritizes strategic quality over volume; if the shared editor is overloaded with Trellis (the primary priority), DR can maintain the minimum of 1 strategic piece per week.
